**The Welch .45** is the name given by [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia) and Greek counterterrorism personnel to a single .45 caliber pistol used by [Revolutionary Organization 17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) to carry out every confirmed assassination in the group's twenty-seven-year operational life.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:53:38" />

The weapon was first used on December 23, 1975, in the assassination of [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch), CIA station chief in Athens, from which the weapon takes its name. It was thereafter used in each of 17 November's 28 confirmed murders, including the killing of [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders) in March 2000, until the group's rollup in 2002.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:29:29" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:38:59" />

The continued use of the same weapon across decades and operatives served as the primary forensic signature linking individual hits to the same organization in the absence of any successful identification of members.

## At the Welch killing

At the assassination of Richard Welch, the .45 was carried by one of two gunmen who exited a parked vehicle across from the Welch residence in Psychiko. A second gunman carried a .38 revolver. The .45 was the weapon used to fire the three rounds into Welch's chest after the formal announcement of his *"sentence"* by 17 November.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:53:38" /><Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:54:09" />

## At the Saunders killing

Twenty-five years later the .45 was used to fire the initial shots through the driver's-side window of [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders)'s vehicle. A second operative on the same motorcycle then fired an armor-piercing round from a long gun that severed Saunders's right hand from his body.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="1:40:05" />

Kiriakou has given the sequence the other way round as well: that the shooters blew off Saunders's right hand at the wrist and then shot him in the chest *"with the same 45 that they used to carry out most of their assassinations."* The name, in the same telling, is explained in a line: *"It was called the Welch 45 because it was used to kill CIA station chief Richard Welch on December 23rd, 1975."*<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="27:30" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="28:01" />

## Never recovered

Investigators never recovered the typewriter used for 17 November manifestos nor the firearm known as the Welch 45. <Cite s="2026-05-31-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-12-secrets-that-have" t="05:43" />

## Eighteen more killings (Jay Dyer)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the [Welch .45](/wiki/welch-45) — the pistol used to assassinate [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch) in 1975 — was used by [17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november) to kill 18 more people between 1975 and 2002. When the group was finally broken up, it was one of only two items the police never recovered, along with the typewriter used for the manifestos; the gun *"is out there somewhere."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="34:40" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="44:33" />
