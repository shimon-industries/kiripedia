**Gerald Bull** was a British inventor who designed the "big gun" — *"just a gigantic barrel, like a gun that was so big that they had to lean it against the side of a mountain."* Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), Bull *"went to the Israelis and he said, 'Hey, I invented this big gun. It's so big you have to lean it against a mountain.' And the Israelis said, 'Get out of here, we're not interested in your stupid gun.' And the Iraqis said, 'We'll take it.'"*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="1:21:29" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="1:21:59" />

## The Iraqi engineering problem

Per Kiriakou: every time the Iraqis tried to fire the big gun *"it would crack and they had to keep repairing it. Finally, they came up with this idea of using something that they called the condom — but instead of going over the gun, it went inside the barrel so that the barrel was smoother."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="1:22:29" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="1:23:01" />

## Mossad

*"As soon as they figured out that condom would work, the Israelis bombed the gun and they murdered Gerald Bull in Brussels."* The reason for killing Bull rather than the buyer: *"They don't want him making any more guns for the Iraqis. … Nobody else would buy this stupid gun. Only the Iraqis were willing to buy it."*<Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="1:23:01" /><Cite s="2026-02-26-bidoun-waraq-kuwait-invasion" t="1:23:31" />

## The supergun and the condom (JK Podcast)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says [Gerald Bull](/wiki/gerald-bull) and his *"big gun"* were among the very first things he learned about in his first week at the CIA. The gun was so enormous it had to be leaned against the side of a mountain to fire, sending a shell as far as a missile; nobody wanted it until Iraq bought it. When test-firing kept cracking the barrel, Bull devised a *"condom"* liner to smooth it — but the Israelis assassinated him before he could test it.<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="16:31" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="17:02" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="17:32" /> Kiriakou recalls asking his boss if it was a joke; *"No, this is deadly serious,"* he was told.<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="18:03" /> He has given the same account again in nearly identical terms, adding the detail that the gun's problem was that it had no market — *"What are we going to do with this giant gun? It's as big as a house"* — until Iraq took it, and reporting the boss's answer verbatim: *"No, no, this is deadly serious. It's a giant gun. They have to lean it up on the side of the mountain to fire it."*<Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="2:08:17" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="2:09:18" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="2:09:49" />

The context in which he raises it there is why Israel treated Iraq rather than Iran as the immediate threat in the 1980s: Iran had no nuclear programme at the time, while Iraq had nuclear, biological and chemical weapons programmes — and Bull's gun.<Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="2:07:46" />
