Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), the federal prison where he was incarcerated had a **sweat lodge** built for Native American inmates' religious services, but it was ultimately taken down after inmates began using it for sex instead of ceremony.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="1:05:20" /> Asked separately whether he had ever been in a sweat lodge himself, apart from that prison experience, Kiriakou said no.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="1:05:53" />
