The **Israeli strike on Qatar** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the period, the moment Israeli conduct stopped being a diplomatic irritant for Washington and became a direct problem. *"Benjamin Netanyahu went too far when he bombed Qatar. Too far."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="28:24" />

## Why Qatar is different

Kiriakou's objection is structural rather than moral. The Qataris are not merely an ally: *"They are a major non-NATO ally. They have the same status as Bahrain, as Japan, as South Korea, as Australia, and we are compelled to protect them."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="28:24" /> Beyond the treaty tier there is the physical stake, *"Qatar is the home of the world's largest air force base and it belongs to us."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="28:55" />

## The conversation Kiriakou imagines

He reconstructs the call between the Amir and [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) as an accounting of favours: *"What's wrong with you? We're friends. We're allies. We gave you this gigantic air base. We have great relations. We're friends. We just gave you a Boeing 747 for your own use, and you allow the Israelis to bomb us."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="28:55" />

## The warning that was not missing

Trump went public to say the Israelis had not given him enough time to warn the Qataris, which Kiriakou says was *"probably not true."* [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) then stated that he had given Trump two hours' notice before the strike, leaving Trump embarrassed and, on Kiriakou's reading, aware *"that this could ruin everything."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="29:27" />

The conclusion Kiriakou draws is the one he says Trump finally reached: *"Israel acts in the national interests of Israel, and the US must act in the national interests of the United States."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="29:27" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="29:58" />
