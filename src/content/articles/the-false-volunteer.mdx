**The false volunteer** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the one occasion he deliberately intimidated someone in the manner of the wrist-grabbing scene in *Meet the Parents*. He declines to say where it happened.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="19:49" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="20:19" />

A man walked into the American embassy volunteering information on someone the station was very interested in. He treated it as a social meeting; for Kiriakou it was a work meeting.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="19:49" /> Asked for the information, the man instead named his price: money, resettlement in the United States, and more. Kiriakou agreed to all of it if the access was real, took his driver's licence and passport for a name trace back at headquarters, and told him to come back in twenty-four hours.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="20:19" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="20:51" />

What the man did not realise was that surveillance was put on him for the rest of the day. He was a dishwasher at a restaurant with no access to the target at all — someone who thought he could lie his way into the United States.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="20:51" />

He returned the next day in a suit. Kiriakou took his wrist, would not let go, and threatened him; the man began crying, and was told never to come back to that embassy or to any other American embassy anywhere in the world. A colleague walked up and photographed him mid-sob, and he left as quickly as he could.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="21:21" /> Kiriakou's summary afterwards was of the cost rather than the theatre: "I can't believe I had to waste two hours on that guy" — an hour each day.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="21:53" />
