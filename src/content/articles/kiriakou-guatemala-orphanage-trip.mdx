[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) ends his account of the [1954 overthrow of the Guatemalan government](/wiki/united-fruit-arbenz-coup) with a personal visit rather than a historical judgement. Having described the installation of a military dictatorship that *"brutalised the population for the next twenty-plus years,"* he adds that the country *"still hasn''t gotten over that coup, it''s still one of the most dangerous places in the world."*<Cite s="2026-02-27-one-america-news-network-one-america-news-network" t="49:29" />

*"I went to Guatemala a couple of years ago to volunteer for a couple of weeks at an orphanage. And thank God that the orphanage had a twenty-foot-high concrete wall all the way around it, because I could barely get to sleep at night for all the gunfire outside."* His conclusion is causal and unqualified: *"It''s still a terrible place. And it''s a terrible place because we made it that way."*<Cite s="2026-02-27-one-america-news-network-one-america-news-network" t="50:00" />
