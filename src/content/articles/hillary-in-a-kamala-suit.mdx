Asked in late November 2020 what he expected from the incoming Biden administration on whistleblowers and foreign policy, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) answered in two phrases and then declined to soften either.

## The two phrases

On the president-elect: *"obviously I see him as Obama 2.0."* His interviewer said the words had come out of her own mouth simultaneously.<Cite s="2020-11-24-revolutionary-change-revolutionary-change-IozNdH" t="11:30" />

On the vice-president-elect: *"I see Kamala as Hillary in a Kamala suit."*<Cite s="2020-11-24-revolutionary-change-revolutionary-change-IozNdH" t="11:30" />

## The charge behind the phrase

The second judgement came with a reason attached, and the reason was prosecutorial rather than ideological. Kiriakou identified Harris as a career prosecutor and asked his listener to *"imagine the thousands of lives that she ruined by sending people to prison for long stretches for having, you know, personal use drugs."*<Cite s="2020-11-24-revolutionary-change-revolutionary-change-IozNdH" t="11:30" />

The complaint is the one he makes most consistently in his post-prison writing and speaking: that the American federal and state criminal systems produce draconian sentences for conduct that harms no one, and that the people who administered those sentences are rewarded rather than examined.

## The verdict

Asked directly whether he had any high hopes for the administration on anything, he answered with three words: *"No, I don't."*<Cite s="2020-11-24-revolutionary-change-revolutionary-change-IozNdH" t="11:30" />

## Context

The forecast was made by someone with a specific grievance against the previous Democratic administration. Kiriakou attributes his own prosecution to [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama)'s White House and has said elsewhere that he applied to Obama for a pardon and was ignored, knowing that he would be. He would later approach the Biden administration through a Greek Orthodox priest close to the president, assemble letters of support, and be passed over as well. The Obama 2.0 assessment predates that experience by two years.
