**Islam in America** was the subject of a segment [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) devoted in July 2026 to what he called a panic taking place in Philadelphia and elsewhere over the visibility of Muslim communities.<Cite s="2026-07-16-john-kiriakou-how-to-avoid-a-honey-trap-a" t="36:12" />

## The clip

The item under discussion was a viral post captioned *"thousands of American Muslims praying in Philadelphia,"* with the assertion that Islam is the fastest-growing religion in the United States.<Cite s="2026-07-16-john-kiriakou-how-to-avoid-a-honey-trap-a" t="36:44" />

Kiriakou's first move is evidentiary. That may or may not be true, he says of the growth claim — and, more to the point, the clip does not tell you what the event is, and does not even establish that it is in Philadelphia. He offers to give it the benefit of the doubt and assume that it is.<Cite s="2026-07-16-john-kiriakou-how-to-avoid-a-honey-trap-a" t="36:44" />

## Who is converting

His substantive point is about composition rather than numbers. Most of the new Muslims in the United States, he says, are African Americans who are converting to Islam — and this happens a lot in the American prison system.<Cite s="2026-07-16-john-kiriakou-how-to-avoid-a-honey-trap-a" t="36:44" />

The observation is first-hand rather than statistical: Kiriakou served twenty-three months at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), where the [Nation of Islam](/wiki/nation-of-islam-at-loretto) was one of the organised groups on the compound.

What he sees in places like Philadelphia, Detroit, and across Texas and Oklahoma is a fear that is sometimes fairly called Islamophobia and sometimes something less deliberate — a fear resting on a misunderstanding, or simply an absence, of any understanding of Islam at all.<Cite s="2026-07-16-john-kiriakou-how-to-avoid-a-honey-trap-a" t="36:44" />
