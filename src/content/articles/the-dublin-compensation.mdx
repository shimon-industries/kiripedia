**The Dublin compensation** is an episode [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounted in September 2025 as an example of what passengers are entitled to elsewhere and not in the United States.

## What happened

A mechanical problem in Madrid delayed his flight; the airline eventually repaired the aircraft and sent him to Dublin, where he missed his Washington connection. His view of the liability was straightforward: *"It's not my fault I missed the connection. Their plane was broken."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:32" />

At the information desk he found they were expecting him by name — *"Oh, yes, yes, we've been waiting for you. You're Mr. Kiriakou"* — and that the remedy had already been arranged. There was no flight to Washington until the following day, *"so we've booked you in this four-star hotel with buffet breakfast that could feed an army"*, and *"your ticket was $1,200. We're going to refund $600."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:32" /><Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:04" />

A shuttle bus was waiting for the passengers who had to stay the night, check-in was expedited, and the hotel was *"a gorgeous hotel in the center of Dublin"* with a gigantic buffet. He spent the afternoon sightseeing and flew out the next morning.<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:04" /><Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:36" />

The money followed: *"about six weeks later, I got my $600 transferred into my checking account."* His reaction was to ask why it does not happen at home — *"the craziest thing. How come we don't do that?"*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:36" />

## What he thinks the rule should be

Kiriakou and his host go further than the European standard. Compensation should extend even to weather delays, which are nobody's fault — the airline should not fly into a storm — with the cost met from a fund built out of a levy on fares. The standard need not be lavish: *"maybe you don't put them up at the four-star hotel, but they should be able to go to the Ramada or the Marriott or whatever."* His conclusion: *"everybody should do this."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="27:06" /><Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="27:38" />
