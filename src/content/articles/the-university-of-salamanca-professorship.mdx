The University of Salamanca professorship is John Kiriakou's academic post in Spain, and the institutional tie from which his later consulting venture grew.

## The institution

Kiriakou calls the University of Salamanca one of the most special educational institutions in the world, and notes that it is older than Spain itself: founded, in his account, in 1207 and continuously operating ever since.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="20:32" /> He describes it simply as a magnificent university.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="20:32" />

## The post

Kiriakou says he has a couple of ties to the university. He is a professor there in the graduate programme of intelligence studies, where he teaches an introduction to intelligence studies course as well as a course on the history of terrorism.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:03" />

The second tie is personal: he and the dean of that programme are good friends, and it was that friendship that led the two of them to establish the political risk consultancy described in [GeoRisk Solutions](/wiki/geo-risk-solutions).<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:03" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:37" />

The posting places Kiriakou's teaching alongside his other work in Spain; he has separately described being in Salamanca with the American privacy researcher Sean O'Brien on the travels the two publicised through their newsletter.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="19:57" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="20:32" />

## A generation born after 9/11

Kiriakou uses the Salamanca classroom as his measure of how far the events of his career have receded. Mentioning [Carlos the Jackal](/wiki/carlos-the-jackal) in a 2026 conversation, he noted that *"none of those kids have ever heard of Carlos the Jackal. None of those kids really even could pick out [Osama bin Laden](/wiki/osama-bin-laden) in a picture"*, because *"they're all born after 9/11."* His own sense of the distance runs the other way: *"in my mind, 9/11 was, you know, 2 weeks ago."*<Cite s="2026-07-28-dissidentklubben-a-conversation-with-john-kiriakou" t="11:13" />
