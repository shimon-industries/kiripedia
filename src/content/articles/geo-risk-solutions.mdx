GeoRisk Solutions is the consulting firm John Kiriakou co-founded with the dean of the graduate intelligence studies programme at the University of Salamanca, where Kiriakou [teaches](/wiki/the-university-of-salamanca-professorship).

## Formation

Kiriakou says he and the dean, who are good friends, decided to try their hand at establishing a consulting firm.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:03" /> They brought in the American privacy researcher Sean O'Brien, a professor at Yale, along with a professor from Chile, one from the United States, and a former assistant secretary of state who Kiriakou noted had joined the firm that same day.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:37" />

## What the firm sells

The stated business is to provide cost-effective political risk analysis to companies, firms, banks, mutual funds — in Kiriakou's phrase, everybody that needs a deep view of what is happening either around the world or very specifically about a certain area.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:37" />

Kiriakou reported that the venture was going well so far. It had been established only within the past year, had just completed its first contract, was moving quickly into a second, and was expanding, with offices both in Salamanca and in Washington, D.C.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="21:37" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="22:13" /> Asked to name it, he initially could not recall having given the name and had to check with O'Brien before confirming it as GeoRisk Solutions.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="22:13" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="22:43" />

The firm sits alongside Kiriakou's other post-agency work with O'Brien, including the seminars on surveillance and anti-surveillance the two were then advertising.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="22:43" />
