**Washington, rat central** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s description of the American capital's rat problem, offered as the counterpart to [the cats of Athens](/wiki/the-cats-of-athens).

## The ranking

*"And here in Washington, we're second only to New York,"* Kiriakou said, invoking the films *Willard* and *Ben* from his and his host's childhood: *"That's We're living it now."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="39:45" />

On size he was emphatic. *"The the places are overrun with rats. And I mean, they're big. I saw one the other day. I'm telling you, without any exaggeration, this thing was the size of a small dog. Never in my life ever have I seen a rat this big."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="40:16" />

## The one o'clock call

Kiriakou then told a story he says happened days earlier. Unable to sleep — *"cuz I'm old and I guess this is like life from now on"* — he was sitting up watching television at one in the morning when a friend of his father called.<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="40:16" />

The friend's son and a companion were stranded in Washington. They had gone into a 7-Eleven; someone got into their van and stole it, with the luggage, passports and laptops inside. *"Everything. They have nothing."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="40:47" />

Kiriakou asked where they were, and reacted to the address: *"What in the world would they do in that part of town? Like, I wouldn't go to that part of town in the daytime."* The explanation was that they had booked the cheapest hotel on a booking site — *"Yeah, well, it's the reason it's the cheapest hotel."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="40:47" /><Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="41:17" />

He said he would be there in thirty minutes, and arrived in Southeast Washington at about 1:45 a.m.<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="41:17" />

## What they were actually frightened of

The two were eighteen and panicked, and Kiriakou assumed he knew why. *"I thought they were panicked because they just had all their [expletive] stolen. They're in a terrible part of town. That was not why they were panicked. They were panicked because while they were waiting for me, they noticed a homeless guy sleeping on the ground and rats were eating his toes."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="41:49" />

His answer to them: *"Yeah. Welcome to Washington. We are rat central these days, and I don't know why nobody wants to do anything about it."* They told him they had never seen anything like it and never wanted to come back. *"I was like, Well, yeah, exactly."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="41:49" /><Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="42:19" />
