The **Athens bank fraud report** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of a whistleblowing effort that went nowhere. In May 2020 he took what he calls a *"job from heaven"*, chief operating officer of an Athens-based investment company, only to discover, about six weeks in, that the whole thing was *"an elaborate bank fraud."* On his lawyer's advice he downloaded 15,000 to 20,000 pages of documents proving large-scale fraud and quit.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:10:48" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:11:20" /> Unable to reach the FBI, told to *"call 311"*, he used a contact, a former FBI deputy director, to get a meeting at the Washington field office, where the agent put up his hands: *"if this doesn't have the words terrorism, China, or January 6th, we're not interested."*<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:11:51" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:12:53" /> He and his lawyer ultimately flew, at their own expense, to Athens and Israel, where the Israeli police took the case.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:13:24" />
