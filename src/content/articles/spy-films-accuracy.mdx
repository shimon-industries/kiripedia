Asked which espionage films resemble the job, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) named three.

*Argo* he calls "completely true. Completely, totally true," adding that the officers involved were heroes at the agency.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="18:45" /> *The Recruit* he rates almost completely accurate, with a single correction drawn from his own time at [the Farm](/wiki/the-farm): training was hard, "but they didn't beat us like they beat him."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="18:45" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="19:17" /> *Bridge of Spies* he calls 100 per cent historically accurate.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="19:17" />

The action genre he rejects: "the action ones, it's totally made up."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="19:17" /> Asked specifically whether he had ever had anything resembling a hidden room with a stash of weapons, in the manner of *Mr. & Mrs. Smith*, his answer was no — "nobody does."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="18:45" />
