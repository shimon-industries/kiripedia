**The Netanyahu death rumour** was a claim circulated during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war) that [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) had been killed and his death concealed. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounted it as an object lesson in how quickly credibility could be spent during the war.

The case, made by *"a couple of podcasters who until yesterday were highly respected,"* ran that Netanyahu had not been seen; that his son had gone quiet on Twitter for exactly seven days, which was read as sitting shiva; and that the son's return to posting marked the end of the mourning period.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="06:24" /> Kiriakou's account of the refutation is one word long: Netanyahu gave a press conference. *"Oops."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="06:24" />

His own explanation for the absence was mundane — *"There are plenty of bunkers in Israel. They're all over Israel. Everybody has a bunker."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="06:56" /> A fortnight later he was tracking reports placing Netanyahu abroad instead; see [Netanyahu in hiding](/wiki/netanyahu-in-hiding).

## The real decapitation campaign

The rumour sat alongside a genuine sequence of killings. The United States announced that it had killed the IRGC's spokesman, following the head of the IRGC and the minister in charge of it.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="05:52" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="06:24" />

Kiriakou's forecast for the war as a whole, given in the same passage, was that *"it's worse than it was yesterday. This is not going to end quickly. It's not going to end easily or nicely. It's going to be hideous."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="05:52" /> The theory behind the killings is treated at [the house of cards theory](/wiki/the-house-of-cards-theory).
