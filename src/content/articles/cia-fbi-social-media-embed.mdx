**CIA and FBI embedded at social media firms** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s claim, drawn from the Twitter Files that Matt Taibbi exposed, that active-duty CIA and FBI officers were placed — often undercover — inside Twitter, Facebook and other social-media platforms.<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="31:48" /> He asks why active-duty officers would hold such positions and answers that there can be only one reason: *"Because they're spying on Americans."*<Cite s="2025-12-08-judging-freedom-cia-tortures-and-crimes" t="32:19" /> In a fuller telling, Kiriakou says Taibbi's reporting, done just after Musk bought Twitter, found active-duty CIA and FBI officers working at the company, and that active-duty CIA, FBI and possibly NSA personnel were later found embedded at Facebook and Google as well.<Cite s="2026-01-26-tin-foil-hat-w-sam-tripoli" t="45:18" />

## The revolving door and national security letters

Kiriakou says national security letters — used by the FBI to compel information from tech companies without a warrant — are often received and processed by former FBI and CIA personnel who now work at those same tech companies via the revolving door. *"If you follow the revolving door between FBI, CIA, and big tech, that's where they go to cash out."*<Cite s="2026-03-10-former-congressman-matt-ga" t="56:11" />
