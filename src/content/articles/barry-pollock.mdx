**Barry Pollock** is the Washington defense attorney [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls *"legendary,"* who represented [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange). Kiriakou says Pollock never lost hope because he had the facts on his side and understood that public opinion plays *"at least as great a role"* in convictions as facts do.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="16:10" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="16:41" /> With Assange refusing to be brought to Alexandria, Virginia, fearing the Justice Department would not honor the deal and vowing to kill himself if locked up, Pollock negotiated the [plea](/wiki/assange-plea-deal) to be processed in Saipan, in the Northern Mariana Islands, the U.S. district physically closest to Australia.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="17:48" />
