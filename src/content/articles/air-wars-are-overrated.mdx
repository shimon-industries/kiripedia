**Air wars are overrated** is the general conclusion [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) drew from the simultaneous campaigns in Gaza, Lebanon and Iran, offered as *"the big meta headline"* of the entire period.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:53" />

His evidence is the gap between destruction and result. Two and a half years after October 7th, *"the Israelis flattened Gaza, but they didn't flatten [Hamas](/wiki/hamas). They're still the only game in town."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="16:50" /> With Israel committed in both Lebanon and Iran, Kiriakou judged Hamas to be *"in the catbird seat"*, unable to be forced into a disarmament deal because its adversary was preoccupied elsewhere.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:52" />

In Lebanon the same pattern: *"[Hezbollah](/wiki/hezbollah)'s putting up a tougher fight than the Israelis had expected."* He was careful not to overstate it, *"not to say that Hezbollah's winning, they're not. They're getting creamed. But they're fighting back"*, with almost a million Lebanese internally displaced and nowhere to go.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:52" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:23" />

## The corollary

The same reasoning gives Kiriakou his forecast for Iran. Asked what happens when the world's oil cannot get through [the Strait of Hormuz](/wiki/strait-of-hormuz), he pictured *"the mullahs down in their bunker"* playing the Rolling Stones on loop: *"They just have to hang tight. Because time is on their side."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:18" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:49" />

Then the summary: *"We know how to blow things up, but that doesn't mean we know how to win."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:49" /> The strategic version of the argument is at [the house of cards theory](/wiki/the-house-of-cards-theory).
