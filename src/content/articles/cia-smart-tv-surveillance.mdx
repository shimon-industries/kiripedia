According to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), the [CIA](/wiki/cia) can repurpose a smart television's speaker system as a covert microphone, enabling audio surveillance even when the TV is turned off.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="24:14" /> He has described the same capability elsewhere in stronger terms: the CIA can convert smart TVs and speakers into microphones to listen to conversations without a warrant, using technology he estimates is roughly 20 years ahead of general public understanding.<Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:19:32" />

Kiriakou frames this and comparable disclosures as only a small sample of what is actually known, drawn from leaks and whistleblowers rather than any systematic accounting: *"it's incalculable how much is out there that we don't know."*<Cite s="2023-01-11-real-progressives" t="41:58" />

## The clip's second life

By December 2025 the capability was circulating again as a viral clip, and Kiriakou was being asked whether it was real. His answer was flat — *"Oh, no. They very much can do that"* — followed by the observation that the material was not new: *"it's funny to me that this clip that you're talking about has taken on a second life, because that information was released in 2017 by Josh Schulte."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="06:15" />

He traces the chain of publication explicitly — [Joshua Schulte](/wiki/joshua-schulte) sent it to WikiLeaks and WikiLeaks released it, as part of [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) — and restates the mechanism: *"the CIA can turn on your smart TV without it looking like it's on. They can turn the speaker into a microphone and they can listen to everything that goes on in that room. All the while you are thinking that the TV is off."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-epstein-was-an-access-agent-for-mossad" t="06:46" />
