**Fort Gordon** is a U.S. Army installation in Georgia. NSA whistleblower Reality Winner, discussing her case on [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s show, says she was working at Fort Gordon in what she considered a temporary position, taken to renew a security clearance that was set to expire after she left the U.S. Air Force.<Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="03:01" />

Winner says she took the next available linguist position without knowing whether it would be at Fort Meade or Fort Gordon, because recruiters for a position in Bagram, Afghanistan would not hire her without an active clearance.<Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="03:33" /> She describes working at Fort Gordon "in the giant, shiny NSA building that was brand new at the time" as an NSA government contractor.<Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="06:12" /> In the same conversation, Winner describes the printer [watermark](/wiki/watermark) that ultimately let the NSA trace her leaked document back to a specific office.
