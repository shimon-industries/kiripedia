**Thin Thread** and **Stellar Wind** were competing NSA programs at the center of the post-9/11 mass-surveillance turn. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts the broader story in connection with NSA whistleblower [Tom Drake](/wiki/thomas-drake). In the documentary *Silenced*, Drake himself says that, tasked with surfacing dormant NSA systems after 9/11, he personally brought the Thin Thread program to senior leadership's attention shortly after the attacks.<Cite s="2025-10-14-jspione-silenced-2014-the-war-on-whistleblowers-feature-documentary-" t="15:24" />

## Thin Thread and Hayden

Per [Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), on September 11, 2001, NSA Director Michael Hayden implemented Operation Thin Thread. Kiriakou says Hayden called 9/11 *"the greatest possible gift to NSA"* — meaning, in context, that *"now that the gloves are off, we can intercept anything we want anywhere in the world."* Thin Thread, in his description, lets the agency *"vacuum up every phone call, every text message, every email, every bit of metadata from every American anywhere in the world"* — in violation of the NSA's own charter, which prohibits spying on Americans. The justification: Americans *"may be talking to terrorists, so we have to grab everything and then sort out the details later."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:24:58" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:25:30" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:26:03" />

## Stellar Wind — the road not taken

[Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says three NSA officers — Bill Binney, Kirk Wiebe, and Ed Loomis — had developed a competing program, Stellar Wind, that *"would be able to collect the same kind of data while protecting Americans."* Hayden, in his account, decided against it: *"No, we want to grab all of it."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:26:36" /> Kiriakou has given a different account of Stellar Wind elsewhere, describing it not as a rejected privacy-protecting alternative but as a companion technology the NSA developed alongside Thin Thread, using AI to discern which of the vacuumed-up communications represented a genuine threat.<Cite s="2024-12-10-pete-a-turner" t="39:25" />

## Tom Drake

The choice to collect everything is what led [Tom Drake](/wiki/thomas-drake) to try to report the illegality. He went to his boss (*"mind your own business"*), then to the Inspector General (who was not read into the compartment and *"never heard of any of these programs"*), then to the General Counsel (*"I advise you to keep your big mouth shut"*), then to the Pentagon Inspector General — who reported him to the FBI. Drake was ultimately charged with multiple felonies including espionage.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:26:36" /><Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:27:09" />

Kiriakou identifies Stellar Wind as the program [Edward Snowden](/wiki/edward-snowden) later leaked publicly.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="2:26:36" />
