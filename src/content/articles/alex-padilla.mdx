**Alex Padilla** is a U.S. Senator from California. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says video of a June 2025 incident clearly shows Padilla identifying himself — *"I'm U.S. Senator Alex Padilla"* — before being taken to the ground and handcuffed anyway. Kiriakou says DHS Secretary Kristi Noem's public claim that Padilla had failed to identify himself was false.<Cite s="2025-06-15-joe-dirosa" t="00:36" />

Kiriakou notes that because Padilla was present at the event in the course of his official duties as a senator, he has congressional immunity and cannot be charged with any crime — making the White House's subsequently stated interest in prosecuting him legally baseless. He says the administration ultimately backed off its account, including a separate, also-false claim that Padilla had "charged into the room."<Cite s="2025-06-15-joe-dirosa" t="01:06" />
