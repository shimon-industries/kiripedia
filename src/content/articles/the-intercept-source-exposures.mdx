**The Intercept's exposed sources** is the pattern [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says makes the outlet dangerous to the people who bring it material.

## The pattern

Introducing [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale)'s decision to take [the Drone Papers](/wiki/the-drone-papers) there, Kiriakou called the choice *"actually quite controversial, because many national security whistleblowers who have gone to The Intercept have found themselves to be sent to trial and then on to prison."*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="7:30" />

The cases he names are specific. *"NSA whistleblower Reality Winner, for example, went to The Intercept, they exposed her identity. FBI whistleblower Terry Albury went to The Intercept, and they exposed his identity."*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="7:30" />

His own is the third: *"the two national security journalists at The Intercept, Richard Esposito and [Matthew Cole](/wiki/matthew-cole), were the journalists responsible for sending me to prison."* And the fourth is Hale, *"the same thing that happened to all of us happened to Daniel Hale at The Intercept."*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="7:30" />

## The question he leaves open

Kiriakou does not assert a motive. He puts it as an alternative and lets it stand: *"what is going on there? Are they that unprofessional, or are they somehow connected to the national security establishment?"*<Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="7:30" /><Cite s="2023-03-05-enema-of-the-state-enema-of-the-state" t="8:02" />
