**Dead Drop** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s narrative podcast — a season-long retelling of his intelligence career, named for the [tradecraft technique](/wiki/dead-drop).

## Format and credits

Season 1 launched in October 2025 with "What Makes This Spy Tick," followed by episodes tracing his path chronologically — "The New Recruit," "Golden Boy" (Athens, *"a beautiful, almost kind of surreal place, while a guy named [Osama bin Laden](/wiki/osama-bin-laden) begins to make his first moves against America"*), "Good Spies, Bad Endings," "A Failure of Analysis," and onward into the post-9/11 hunt.<Cite s="2025-10-10-dead-drop-s1e1-what-makes-this-spy-tick" t="59:35" /><Cite s="2025-11-03-dead-drop-s1e3-golden-boy" t="51:14" /><Cite s="2026-02-16-dead-drop-s1e15-the-hunt-begins" t="13:38" /> Mini-episodes fill the gaps between chapters — among them "A Spy's Guide to Sofia."<Cite s="2025-12-01-dead-drop-mini-episode-3-a-spy-s-guide-to-sofia" t="15:45" />

Every episode carries the same credits: written by John Kiriakou and Alan Katz; produced by Costard & Touchstone Productions; executive producers Kiriakou, Katz, and Nick Mechanic.<Cite s="2025-11-17-dead-drop-s1e4-good-spies-bad-endings" t="45:46" /><Cite s="2025-12-08-dead-drop-s1e6-a-failure-of-analysis" t="38:52" />

## The pitch

The show's stated ambition: *"to give you the deepest possible perspective on the world of spying and what makes any spy tick — it's like living life with an additional layer to it, a layer only you and a handful of other people know about."*<Cite s="2025-11-17-dead-drop-s1e4-good-spies-bad-endings" t="01:02" /> The sign-off leans into the conceit: *"Now that you know where the drop box is, you might as well take advantage. Who knows what secrets you'll learn."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-dead-drop-s1e1-what-makes-this-spy-tick" t="59:35" />

## Reach

By July 2026 [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) put *Dead Drop* at number six in the world in the history category and 238th overall out of some six million podcasts, finishing its second season.<Cite s="2026-07-09-byrna-how-to-spot-danger-before-it-s-too-late" t="32:53" /> He has separately given the download figure as three million, describing himself as "crazy grateful" for it.<Cite s="2026-07-27-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-middle-east-on-the-brink-of-a-m" t="20:47" />

He distinguishes it from [Briefing Room](/wiki/johns-briefing-room) by genre rather than by status: *Dead Drop* is the storytelling programme, Briefing Room the news show. The distinction arose because he had described Briefing Room on air as his only official podcast, and an emailed correction from a listener prompted him to clarify that he had simply been thinking about news rather than storytelling.<Cite s="2026-07-27-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-middle-east-on-the-brink-of-a-m" t="20:47" /><Cite s="2026-07-27-national-security-files-john-kiriakou-middle-east-on-the-brink-of-a-m" t="21:18" />

## Format and credits

Each episode opens with Kiriakou's own framing of the show — *"Welcome to Dead Drop, what makes a spy tick?"* — and an address to the listener *"from storyteller to storyteller"*, followed by a request to like, review, share or comment, on the reasoning that for podcasts and podcasters reaching the widest possible audience is the mission.<Cite s="2026-01-19-dead-drop-s1e11-have-human-spies-become-obsolete" t="0:00" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-dead-drop-s1e11-have-human-spies-become-obsolete" t="0:34" /> The closing credits name it as written by John Kiriakou and Alan Katz, produced by Cosdar and Touchstone Productions, with Kiriakou, Katz and Nick Mechanic as executive producers.<Cite s="2026-01-19-dead-drop-s1e11-have-human-spies-become-obsolete" t="57:57" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-dead-drop-s1e11-have-human-spies-become-obsolete" t="58:44" />
