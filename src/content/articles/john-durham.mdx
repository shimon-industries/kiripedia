**John Durham** was the United States attorney for Connecticut appointed to investigate the origins of the Russia inquiry. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), discussing the investigation in December 2019, treated the documents Durham had obtained as the measure of how serious it was.<Cite s="2019-12-21-fox-news-former-cia-whistleblower-on-durham-probing-br" t="01:06" />

Durham, he said, *"has now asked for and will receive call logs from [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan), emails from John Brennan from the time that he was the CIA director, as well as contemporaneous notes that Brennan took when he was meeting with these other leaders."*<Cite s="2019-12-21-fox-news-former-cia-whistleblower-on-durham-probing-br" t="01:06" />

What that material would support, in Kiriakou's reading, was not a case about the underlying intelligence but about the accounts given of it afterwards: *"process crimes, making a false statement, perjury, which seems to be what John Durham is picking up."* He noted that Durham had gone out of his way to correct the record on the nature of the exercise: *"A couple of weeks ago, John Durham said that his investigation is not an inquiry. It's an investigation, and it's a criminal one."*<Cite s="2019-12-21-fox-news-former-cia-whistleblower-on-durham-probing-br" t="01:36" />
