The **Capitol Hill cocaine ring** was a 1983 federal investigation into drug use among members of the United States Congress, which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) encountered at eighteen while working as a paid intern in his congressman's district office in Beaver Falls, Pennsylvania.<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="45:21" />

## The phone call

Kiriakou's father called the office one day and asked whether he was in some kind of trouble. He said no, and asked why. *"And he said, because an FBI agent was here and he wanted to talk to you. And I freaked out. I was a kid. I was 18."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="45:21" />

The agent had left a card. Kiriakou called the number and said his father had told him the agent wanted to talk.<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="45:21" />

## The questions

The interview, as Kiriakou recounts it, escalated in three steps. *"Have you ever seen the congressman snort cocaine? And I was like, what? No. Have you ever seen anybody in the office snort cocaine? I said, no. Have you ever seen the congressman trade stamps for cocaine? And I was like, no."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="45:53" />

His composure did not survive it: *"I was so freaked out. I blurted out, they all smoke pot on Friday afternoons."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="45:53" />

## What the questions were about

The premise of the third question turned out to be correct. *"Well, it turned out that the congressman was trading stamps for cocaine and ended up going to prison for seven and a half years."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="45:53" /><Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="46:23" />

And he was not alone. In Kiriakou's account, four or five members of Congress *"ended up either losing their primaries or being prosecuted and going to prison because there was this coke ring on Capitol Hill that was in 1983."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="46:23" />

## The lesson he draws

The conclusion Kiriakou takes from it is not scandal but ordinariness, the same frame he later applied to [the cocaine in the West Wing](/wiki/the-cocaine-in-the-west-wing): *"I came to learn after, you know, 41 years here, that's just how Washington runs. It's just a fact of life."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="46:23" />
