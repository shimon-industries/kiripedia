**Running into former sources** is something [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says has happened to him twice, and he raises it immediately after describing [the vetting](/wiki/the-operational-test) that once governed those relationships — with the aside that his prize source might not have survived the surveillance detection route he ran across an eight-lane highway.<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="08:48" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:20" />

## The encounters

*"I've actually run into sources twice. uh once at at an airport and uh and once on the street."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:20" />

The intervals were long and very different. *"In one case it was it was four years after I left the agency in the other case it was you know, 20 years after I left the agency."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:20" />

## Who moved first

Asked on the programme whether the correct response was a nod or a greeting, Kiriakou's answer described a situation in which he had no say. *"They came up to me. I wasn't really paying attention and they came up to me and it was big bear hugs and oh my god how are you doing?"*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:20" />

The relationship, in other words, outlasted the operational reason for it, and the former agents felt no need for the discretion that had once been the condition of their survival.<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:20" />

## The line he remembers

The detail Kiriakou singles out is one that reveals how much of the original relationship had been managed. *"And one guy said I can't believe YOU SPEAK ENGLISH. I can't believe it."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:52" />

The source had run for years without ever learning that his case officer had a language in common with him — the meetings having been conducted, presumably, in the source's own. The concealment held so completely that its exposure came as a shock in an airport decades later.<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="09:52" />

## The wider point

The anecdotes sit directly against the question Kiriakou had just answered about whether officers regarded their agents as traitors. His position was that they did not — that a recruited source became *"one of the good guys"* and part of *"one big happy family"* — and that seven of the nine people he recruited were people he would gladly be friends with. The bear hugs are offered as evidence that the feeling was mutual.<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="06:45" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="07:16" />
