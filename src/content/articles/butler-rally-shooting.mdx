The **Butler rally shooting** is the attempt on [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump)'s life at an outdoor rally in Butler, Pennsylvania, which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) treats as an open question rather than a settled one. He advances no theory of who was behind it. His objection is narrower and, in his framing, harder to dismiss: the facts as published do not fit together.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="33:03" />

The reconciliation he cannot make is with the competence of the protective detail. The account works, he argues, only on an assumption he rejects — *"Unless we accept the notion that the [Secret Service](/wiki/secret-service) is just a collection of incompetent boobs, and I don't think that they are."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="33:03" />

What remains is a list of questions rather than an accusation: how an eighteen-year-old obtained a free shot at a president; how he got inside the event; how he got onto the roof; what the circumstances of his planning were; and, most pointedly, why he was not shot before he fired, when agents had already seen him on the roof and seen him preparing.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="33:35" />

## The local knowledge he brings to it

Kiriakou's reading of the site is not abstract. Butler is the town immediately adjacent to his home town, and he went to the Butler Fair and Farm Show every year growing up — western Pennsylvania, in his telling, offering little else to do. Politicians had long held events there, so the venue was not unfamiliar ground for a protective detail.<Cite s="2025-09-25-the-jimmy-dore-show-the-jimmy-dore-show" t="08:20" /><Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:18:15" />

What that familiarity supplies is the roofs. *"We all knew about the flatness of the roofs,"* he says; the building the gunman fired from is the one where the cows, hogs, goats and sheep are shown. *"Anybody who knows anything about a fairgrounds knows that you need to check the roofs."*<Cite s="2025-09-25-the-jimmy-dore-show-the-jimmy-dore-show" t="08:50" />

## From screw-up to open question

His position has hardened over time. In the days after the shooting his view was that the easiest explanation was probably the best one at that stage: *"a colossal, historic screw-up on the part of the US Secret Service"* — the more damning for the fact that the protectee was not merely a nominee but a former president.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:17:45" /> By 2026 he was placing Butler alongside other unexplained cases and asking a different question — why the investigation had produced nothing, and what looked to him like a rush to silence and to cover up: *"who are we protecting?"* He is careful to mark the shift as a shift, describing himself as normally the least conspiratorial person in the room and saying he has had to reassess, because in his experience everybody is lying.<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="31:45" /><Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="32:16" />

Kiriakou's answer to why it still matters is not about the living. He has, he says, long argued for complete and open investigations — and the audience he names is not the electorate but the record: the American people deserve an explanation of what happened that day, and so do historians and future generations.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="33:03" /> That the answers look unreachable does not soften the judgement. They are *"questions that we're just never we're never going to know the answers to,"* and that, he says, is unacceptable.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="33:35" />
