**Laura Loomer** is the American activist whose attack on [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) he recounts alongside an earlier one by [Louise Mensch](/wiki/louise-mensch), as the case where he chose not to act. He learned of it the way he learned of the first: *"I woke up one Sunday a few months ago to half a dozen emails from friends saying, buddy, Laura Loomer is just tearing you up on Twitter."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="22:22" />

The charges were specific. She *"called me a Russian spy, a Russian asset, and a common criminal."* He went back to the lawyer who had handled the Mensch letter, and got the opposite advice: *"nobody takes Laura Loomer seriously. She's a nobody."* The reasoning offered was that she is *"literally the only person in [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump)'s circle that even Trump thought was so crazy that she didn't deserve a government job."* Kiriakou let it go, and *"the very next day, she just moved on to somebody else."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="24:27" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="24:59" />

## The turn on Russia and Ukraine

Kiriakou reads her switch of position as a career calculation rather than a change of mind. She *"has been all about Russia, Russia, Russia since the very beginning until now. And now it's all about Ukraine."* His explanation is that she has finally understood the appointment is not coming, *"she's not going to get a job at the White House. She's not going to be named the head of some obscure federal agency somewhere"*, and so has fallen back on what serves her own following: *"switching sides on Russia Ukraine was the way to go."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="27:34" />

## The Kiev pilgrimage

Bout put the flip to Kiriakou in a slightly different frame, how the personnel around Trump had turned him back toward the war he had promised to end. Kiriakou's account of Loomer's post-flip trip is one line: *"she recently flipped. She was pro-Russian and last week she flipped to be pro-Ukrainian and she went to Kiev and she met with Zelensky and said that Trump should keep the war going."* He places her in that chain alongside the *"military-industrial complex"* voices telling Trump *"the Russians uh they supplied the North Vietnamese during the Vietnam War and they killed American soldiers and the Russians helped the Taliban to kill Americans in Afghanistan."*<Cite s="2026-08-27-victor-bout-cia-vs-pentagon-hidden-rivalry-and-unsavory-deals" t="43:31" /><Cite s="2026-08-27-victor-bout-cia-vs-pentagon-hidden-rivalry-and-unsavory-deals" t="44:34" />

## The defence he did not ask for

He tells one coda with evident pleasure. While the attacks were running, a stranger opened a new account solely to post a single comparison, then deleted it: the account had asked an AI chatbot which country benefited most from what Kiriakou says, the answer given was the United States, on the basis of his torture-programme whistleblowing, and then put the same question about Loomer's statements, where the answer given was Israel. Kiriakou's response was to thank whoever it was: *"thank you for defending me like that."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="26:32" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="27:04" />
