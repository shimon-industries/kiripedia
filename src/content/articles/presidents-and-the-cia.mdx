**Presidents and the CIA** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s framing of how different U.S. presidents have related to the agency — some commanding it, others, in his view, led around by it.<Cite s="2026-01-01-fair-observer" t="07:53" />

George H.W. Bush, who had served as CIA director for only 11 months before becoming president, maintained what Kiriakou calls an ongoing love affair with the agency: he would visit CIA headquarters simply to walk the halls, and staff would spontaneously break into applause at word that *"President Bush is walking the halls."*<Cite s="2026-01-01-fair-observer" t="07:53" /> Kiriakou says Richard Nixon likewise had a close relationship with the CIA — one that Kiriakou identifies as one of the factors that brought down his presidency.<Cite s="2026-01-01-fair-observer" t="08:24" />
