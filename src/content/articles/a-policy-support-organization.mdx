**"A policy support organization"** is the formulation [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reaches for when asked what the [CIA](/wiki/cia) workforce does about a war it does not agree with.

It came up on 28 February 2026, hours into the attack on Iran, when [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) asked how most of the agency would be reacting — *"Scared, excited, genuinely curious?"*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:39" />

Kiriakou called it a great question and answered from the last time. *"It was my experience um during the Iraq war that people put their heads down and did as they were told."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:39" />

## The speech

What he remembers is a specific address to a specific room, and he supplies the room's own politics in an aside. *"I remember my boss telling a an assembled group, 'We're not here to take a political position one way or another,' because we all opposed the Iraq war."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:39" />

The rest of it draws the distinction the phrase turns on. *"He said, 'We're not here to take a political position one way or another. We are not a policy organization. We're a policy support organization. And so, we are going to do what we are told by the White House to do.'"*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:39" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="48:10" />

## The same conversation

Kiriakou did not claim knowledge of what was said inside the building that morning, only that the institution repeats itself: *"And my guess is they've had the same conversation today."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="48:10" />

The account is one of the few places in his record where he attributes the agency's wartime conduct to an explicit instruction rather than to inertia — and where he places himself among the dissenters who complied.
