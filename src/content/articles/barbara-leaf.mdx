**Barbara Leaf** is an American diplomat serving as assistant secretary of state for the Near East, and a person [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identifies as *"an old friend of mine."*<Cite s="2025-11-20-american-exception-cia-veterans-deep-state-w-barry-eisler" t="19:14" />

She appears in his account of Syria as the instrument of an unusually fast reversal. On his telling, [al-Julani](/wiki/al-julani)'s first act after taking power was to invite Leaf and the assistant secretary of state for hostage affairs to Syria — *"and this is like days after he took power"* — with recognition following in exchange for the lifting of the ten-million-dollar American bounty on him.<Cite s="2025-03-26-truthovercomfort-truthovercomfort" t="26:47" /><Cite s="2025-03-26-truthovercomfort-truthovercomfort" t="27:19" />

Describing the same trip later, Kiriakou put it more drily: Leaf *"jets off to Damascus with the special envoy for hostage negotiations to make peace and bring gifts"* — after which the new Syrian president was invited to the White House *"to be fêted by all the Washington swells."*<Cite s="2025-11-20-american-exception-cia-veterans-deep-state-w-barry-eisler" t="19:14" />
