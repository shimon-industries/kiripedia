**The CIA and Hollywood** describes what [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says is a permanent, institutional relationship rather than an occasional courtesy.

## The branch

Asked how important the agency considers soft power through film and television, his answer was *"critically important — to the point where the CIA has set up a branch within the Office of Public Affairs whose job it is solely to cooperate with Hollywood studios."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="4:19" />

The precedent he gives is the [FBI](/wiki/fbi), which he says has done the same since the 1950s. The purpose is uniform: *"the CIA works with Hollywood studios to make sure that everything that comes out of Hollywood has a pro-CIA message."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="4:19" />

## The legal problem

*"Technically it's illegal for the CIA to propagandize the American people. But that's what happens every time a CIA related film is released. It's propaganda."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="4:52" />

The case he uses to show how far the cooperation runs is *[Zero Dark Thirty](/wiki/zero-dark-thirty)*, which in his account *"broke all kinds of laws"*: the director and writer were given classified briefings over a classified mock-up of the [bin Laden compound](/wiki/abbottabad) by counterterrorism centre analysts, and gave those analysts watches and premiere tickets in return. *"Well, they should all be in prison for that. Technically that's a violation of the Espionage Act. Instead, the film was celebrated as a victory at the CIA."*<Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="4:52" /><Cite s="2025-12-15-afshin-rattansi-s-going-un-cia-whistleblower-john-kiriakou-reveals-all-o" t="5:23" />

The detail of that production is treated at [Zero Dark Thirty](/wiki/zero-dark-thirty); the press side of the same apparatus at [CIA media relations](/wiki/cia-media-relations).
