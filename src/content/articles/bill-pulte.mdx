**Bill Pulte** is the home developer named acting Director of National Intelligence following [Tulsi Gabbard](/wiki/tulsi-gabbard)'s resignation. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes him as *"the Steve Witkoff of the intelligence community"* — an outsider parachuted into a specialist post.<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="28:34" />

His character assessment is secondhand and carefully separated from his competence assessment. Kiriakou, noting he has been in Washington for forty-four years, says he spent a weekend with people who know Pulte well: *"he's a a good guy. He's very smart. He is completely loyal to [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) and he could not find the office of the DNI if you gave him a map and a GPS unit."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="29:08" />

On confirmation he defers to the Senate majority leader: if John Thune is right, *"Pulte is not going to be confirmed. His name likely will not even be put in nomination."* Kiriakou's expectation for the interim is functional rather than institutional — as acting DNI *"he can do one thing that has been that has long been important to Donald Trump, and that is to fire all your enemies,"* and he suspects that between then and January that is precisely what will happen, after which *"a serious person will be named as the DNI — or not. I mean, it could just remain vacant."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="29:08" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="29:38" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="30:09" />

That last possibility is not a complaint in Kiriakou's mouth: he does not think the post should exist at all — see [Office of the Director of National Intelligence](/wiki/office-of-the-director-of-national-intelligence).<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="30:09" />
