The **International Spy Museum** is the Washington museum of espionage. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has an ongoing relationship with it, he mentions having gone two weeks earlier to give a family a tour.<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="08:50" />

## The torture room

His objection concerns a small room dedicated to the [CIA torture program](/wiki/cia-torture-program-whistleblowing). It runs a loop of videos, and Kiriakou's complaint is about whose voices are in it: *"the only people that they're looping are the torturers. And I'm never mentioned in that in that video. Only the pro-torture people uh are giving their side of the story. It's it's sickening."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="08:50" />

He raises it while answering a question about which of his former colleagues were complicit rather than merely silent, and it functions in his account as evidence of the same pattern: that the officers who ran the program have kept the ability to narrate it, several of them having written books arguing that torture was a good idea and that it saved lives.<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="08:19" />
