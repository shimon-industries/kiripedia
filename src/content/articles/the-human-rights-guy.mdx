**"The human rights guy"** is the nickname [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says CIA colleagues used behind his back for the officer who kept turning up on the side of the [Geneva Convention](/wiki/geneva-convention). It was told to him, inside the agency, as a warning, and he treats it now as evidence that the internal drift had already gone further than most Americans understood.

## The origin

Kiriakou dates the nickname to the [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) takedown, when captured al-Qaeda fighters, dozens of them, arriving in shifts of ten, were being processed at a CIA safe house. He asked why they had hoods on. *"And he said, 'I don't want them to see our faces.' I said, 'Are you seriously telling me that you have never read the Geneva Convention? You cannot put hoods on them. That is a war crime.' … And he says, 'Wait. If you take the hoods off, I'm reporting you to headquarters.' I said, 'Oh, I'm already reporting you to headquarters. You've committed a war crime and we have to report it to Congress.'"*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="08:56" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="09:27" />

The internal ruling ran against him. *"He took the hoods off, and we reported each other to headquarters and I got reprimanded."* Cheney had already declared the detainees non-state combatants; no court had endorsed that ruling, but no court had blocked it either. *"I realized then, like, okay, this is how they want to play it."*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="09:27" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="09:59" />

## Steve

The friend who told him what colleagues were calling him was named Steve, *"the consummate overachiever. We worked together at the CIA, plus he was a Brigadier General in the Army Reserve and a psychiatrist."* Steve's phrasing was direct: *"Hey, buddy, you know they call you the the human rights guy behind your back."*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="10:30" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="11:00" />

Kiriakou: *"Yeah, I know."* Steve: *"You know that's not a compliment."* Kiriakou: *"Steve, I'm not afraid of them. I'm right and they're wrong."*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="11:00" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="11:30" />
