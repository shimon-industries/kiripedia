The **Nobel Peace Prize** appears in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s story twice over — once as an honor he was himself nominated for, and repeatedly as the explanatory key he applies to presidents.

## Kiriakou's nomination

In 2016, a Swedish parliamentarian nominated Kiriakou for the Nobel Peace Prize — one of the signs, alongside a West Point invitation to lecture the freshman class on ethics in intelligence operations, that opinion had come around after prison.<Cite s="2025-01-19-eyes-wide-open-i-conor-ryan-ex-cia-spy-john-kiriakou-hunting-terroris" t="1:15:28" /><Cite s="2024-07-30-spartan-leadership-podcast" t="42:37" />

## Obama's prize

Kiriakou stood in the snow for two hours to vote for [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) and *"really believed... he deserved that Nobel Peace Prize."*<Cite s="2013-02-13-jayargonaut-rt-interview-with-cia-whistleblower-john-kiri" t="12:00" /> The belief didn't survive the record: Obama adopted most of Bush's counterterrorism policies, he says, while Americans *"just bought it"* because a Peace Prize winner was doing it.<Cite s="2013-02-13-jayargonaut-rt-interview-with-cia-whistleblower-john-kiri" t="06:49" /> His later formulation is flat: *"He didn't deserve one, but they gave him one."*<Cite s="2025-06-05-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-who-really-controls-u-s-intelligence" t="27:44" />

## Trump's obsession

Kiriakou reads much of [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump)'s foreign policy through a single motive. *"I have a friend at the White House who told me to never underestimate Donald Trump's desire to win a Nobel Peace Prize. He's still angry that Barack Obama has a Nobel Peace Prize."* Hence the North Korea talks, the Iran overtures, the promise to end the Ukraine war in a day.<Cite s="2025-06-17-mario-nawfal-ex-cia-agent-john-kiriakou-on-israel-iran-mossad-middle-" t="49:33" /><Cite s="2025-06-05-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-who-really-controls-u-s-intelligence" t="27:44" /> The endgame he sketches: expand the [Abraham Accords](/wiki/abraham-accords), bring in the Saudis, declare victory, *"and get your Nobel Peace Prize."*<Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="52:33" /> When Thailand and Cambodia announced a ceasefire after Trump's weekend phone calls, Kiriakou's explanation was the same: *"I think he wants the Nobel Peace Prize."*<Cite s="2025-07-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="31:24" /> Netanyahu's nomination of Trump for the prize he described with a term he declined to define on air: *"circle jerk is the best way to say it, actually."*<Cite s="2025-07-15-useful-idiots-useful-idiots" t="10:25" />

## Nixon to China, or Grover Cleveland

Explaining why Trump pressed for a Gaza deal, Kiriakou lists the prize alongside base politics. MAGA is against foreign wars *"and we're in this war up to our necks"*; and *"Donald Trump is obsessed with winning the Nobel Peace Prize. If he can bring peace even indirectly to Gaza with a deal that's acceptable to the Gazans, maybe that gives him a chance."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="26:16" />

He puts the ambition as a fork in the historical record. Trump *"can go down in history as just like Richard Nixon did when he went to China, as this cutting-edge leader in foreign policy. Or he can go down as Grover Cleveland, who was president in the late 19th century, served two non-consecutive terms, and nobody remembers who in the world he is. He wants to be the former."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="26:49" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="27:22" />

The same logic produces a prediction about Iran that Kiriakou concedes sounds unhinged: just as Nixon was the only president who could make peace with China in 1972, *"maybe Trump is the only one who can go to Iran"* — not literally, but to Riyadh, at Mohammed bin Salman's invitation, to meet President Pezeshkian. *"Stranger things have happened through history."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="27:53" />

## Why he will not walk away from Ukraine

Asked why [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) does not simply end American involvement in [Ukraine](/wiki/ukraine-proxy-war), Kiriakou gave three reasons and put the prize third and decisive. It would make him look weak — *"he would go down in history as the president who lost Ukraine."* He is the grand dealmaker. *"And there's a third reason, and we all know what it is. He desperately wants to win the Nobel Peace Prize."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="15:12" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="15:42" />

Kiriakou enumerates the deals already banked toward it — India and Pakistan, Congo and Rwanda, Thailand and Cambodia — and says a Russian–Ukrainian settlement is the one Trump believes would make him competitive. He adds the grievance he thinks drives it: *"he's furious that [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) won the Nobel Peace Prize without deserving it."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="15:42" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="16:13" />
