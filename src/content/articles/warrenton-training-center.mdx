The **Warrenton Training Center**, known inside the agency as the WTC, is a secret [CIA](/wiki/cia) communications facility in Warrenton, Virginia. It enters [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the [Aldrich Ames](/wiki/aldrich-ames) case not as a site of espionage but as the address [Soviet](/wiki/soviet-union) intelligence invented to keep the agency looking away from Ames.<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="10:39" />

In 1986 Ames told his handlers that he feared becoming a suspect. When the mole hunt eventually resumed, the KGB *"threw US investigators off of Ames's trail by constructing an elaborate diversion"*: a Soviet case officer told a CIA contact that the mole was in fact stationed at Warrenton.<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="10:39" />

The agency took the bait. Mole hunters investigated ninety employees at the facility for almost a year and came up with ten possible suspects, although, Kiriakou notes, the lead investigator recorded that *"there are so many problem personalities that no single one stands out."*<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="10:39" /><Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="11:13" />

What finally exposed Ames, on Kiriakou's telling, was not the counterintelligence apparatus at all: *"It wasn't so much the agency's work that eventually outed Ames as it was Ames's own complete stupidity. But then greed has never made anyone smarter."* The pattern analysis that did break the case belonged to [Sandy Grimes](/wiki/sandy-grimes).<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="11:13" />
