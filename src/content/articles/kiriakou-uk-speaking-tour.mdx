Asked in December 2025 what he was working on, John Kiriakou gave an inventory that ended with travel.

## The tours

He was going to start a speaking tour in February, he said, a nationwide speaking tour. He had done one in the United Kingdom at the beginning of that year, in January and February, all around England, Wales, Scotland, Ireland and Northern Ireland. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="41:31" />

So he was, he said, busier than he could maintain, adding immediately that it was a good busy. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="41:31" />

## What else was in the inventory

The tours were one item among several. He listed three podcasts: the daily programme he does with [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) live from nine to ten every morning on Rumble and YouTube; a twice-weekly deeper look at the news, which he said had taken off and was approaching a hundred thousand subscribers having started only that March; and [Dead Drop](/wiki/dead-drop-podcast), launched two months earlier on Apple Podcasts and then Spotify, which he said was in the top two per cent of all podcasts worldwide. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="39:26" /> <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="39:56" /> <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="40:26" />

On the books: [Remains of the Day](/wiki/remains-of-the-day-book), a guide to Washington's historic cemeteries, was due from Simon and Schuster on March 24; he had just sent his ninth book, [Whispers in the Dirt](/wiki/whispers-in-the-dirt-book), a guide to the mafia graves of New York City, to the publisher; and he intended to do another three after that. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="40:58" />

## What he credits

Asked what he attributes the growth to, Kiriakou pointed first to an algorithm, a year earlier, he says, he seems to have hit the YouTube algorithm just right, and went from videos getting five hundred or a thousand views to two shorts in a single week that got thirty million and thirty-eight million. He does not know how or why; it just happened. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="42:34" /> Pressed on whether it was really the stories or the hard work, he offered one other thing he hopes played a role, and said it is the thing he is most proud of: it has cost him a lot, but he always tells the truth, and he thinks people have come to respect it. <Cite s="2025-12-17-pete-a-turner-john-kiriakou-us-espionage-and-foreign-policy" t="43:06" />
