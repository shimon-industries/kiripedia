**Cheney's airstrip question** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s example of the kind of one-off demand that reached him as [executive assistant to the deputy director for operations](/wiki/executive-assistant-to-the-deputy-director-for-operations) during the Iraq war.

*"I got a request from Vice President [Cheney](/wiki/dick-cheney) one time, and he wanted to know what the longest airstrip in northern Iraq was made of."* Kiriakou's own reaction was bafflement, *"and I was like, what?"*, and it was shared by the operations directorate: *"nobody in operations knew. They were like, what?"* He kept restating the provenance to get anyone to take it seriously: *"it's from Cheney. Cheney specifically wants to know."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="07:20" />

The resolution came from an unglamorous quarter. *"I ended up having to enlist the CIA librarian, and she said, I can find this out."* Within twenty-four hours she had told him *"exactly what it was made of and what the mix consisted of"*, so much concrete, so much of everything else, and Kiriakou wrote the memo.<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="07:20" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="07:53" />

The reason for the question turned out to be a load calculation: *"they wanted to fly in tanks, and they wanted to know if the runway was solid enough to handle the weight of a plane carrying two tanks landed on it."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="07:53" />
