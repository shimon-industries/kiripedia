**The lapis lazuli paperweight** is a piece of unworked stone John Kiriakou says he removed from a house belonging to [Osama bin Laden](/wiki/osama-bin-laden) in Afghanistan and has kept ever since. He mentioned it on the DeProgram programme in August 2025, in the middle of describing his part in [the clean-up after Tora Bora](/wiki/cleaning-up-after-tora-bora).<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:04" />

## The object

Kiriakou introduced it as an aside and offered to produce it for a later broadcast: *"You know what? I'll bring it for the next show. I have a giant chunk of raw lapis lazuli."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:04" />

Its origin, as he states it, is specific: *"that I took from Osama bin Laden's house."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:34" />

## The paperweight

The function the object is named for was not asserted but arrived at on air. The suggestion — *"I like to think it was his paperweight"* — was met with agreement, and Kiriakou's own summary was hedged in the same way: *"I think it probably was, but I took it and I still have it. It's magnificent."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:34" />

The attribution is therefore speculative by the account's own terms. What Kiriakou states as fact is only that the stone came from the house and that he retains it.

## Lapis and the country it came from

Lapis lazuli is one of the semi-precious stones for which Afghanistan is known, and the country's mineral wealth is a subject Kiriakou has returned to on the same programme, where a viewer's question about Afghan raw materials produced a discussion of gemstones, rubies and rare earth minerals.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:09:18" />

Asked in the same exchange whether he had any of bin Laden's videos, Kiriakou said no, and answered instead with [his account of what was actually found on the computers](/wiki/the-porn-on-bin-ladens-computers).<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:34" />
