The Jerusalem harassment campaign is John Kiriakou's account of three break-ins suffered by a close CIA colleague and that colleague's wife while they were posted to Jerusalem, which he dates to a couple of years after his own [posting to Bahrain](/wiki/bahrain-1994-1996-posting) with the State Department.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="27:14" />

## The rotation

Both couples were on the same programme: a two-year rotation to the State Department. Kiriakou explains that to be promoted into the Senior Intelligence Service a CIA officer must complete a rotation in another directorate and a rotation in a policy position, and that these assignments satisfied the policy requirement.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="27:14" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="27:44" />

Both officers were declared to the Israelis, so, as Kiriakou puts it, there were no secrets: the station chief tells the Israelis that these are his people, that one will be doing State Department work and the other will be learning Arabic at the university.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="27:44" />

## Three homecomings

Kiriakou lists three incidents, each following a function at the American ambassador's residence.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="27:44" />

Shortly after the couple arrived they attended a party at the ambassador's residence, and when they got home somebody had rearranged all of their living room furniture. Kiriakou reads the message as deliberate: we can break into your house any time we want, and we are watching you.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="27:44" />

Several months later they went to the ambassador's Christmas party, and when they got home people had defecated in all three of the toilets in the house.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="28:15" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="28:51" /> Kiriakou describes the effect as feeling violated and the harassment as totally unnecessary.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="28:51" />

Before the couple left the post the ambassador held a going-away party for them. When they got home their dog was whimpering under the dining room table: the Israelis, in Kiriakou's account, had broken in and cut the dog's tail off, then wrapped it in gauze and medical tape.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="28:51" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="29:21" />

## Kiriakou's reading

Kiriakou frames the episode not as an aberration but as ordinary practice, observing that these are supposed to be friends and that this kind of harassment happens absolutely constantly.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="29:21" /> He offers it alongside [the "spell your name" briefing](/wiki/the-spell-your-name-briefing) and [the Israeli listening devices ban](/wiki/the-israeli-listening-devices-ban) as evidence for his broader verdict on the [liaison relationship](/wiki/cia-liaison-relationships) with [Mossad](/wiki/mossad).<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="24:23" />
