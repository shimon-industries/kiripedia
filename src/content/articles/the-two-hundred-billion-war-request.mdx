**The two hundred billion dollar war request** was the Defense Department's supplemental funding request for the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war), submitted without Congress having been consulted about the war itself.

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s interest was in how quickly congressional resistance collapsed. The hardcore Republicans, he noted, had begun saying *"we don't want to put our war fighters in harm's way, so now's not the time to be asking questions."* His assessment of that position was three words: *"Bunch of cowards."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="00:38" />

## Burden sharing

The scale was put to him as exceeding the entire cost of the Gulf War even after adjusting for inflation, and he agreed.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:08" /> What the comparison brought to mind was the financing arrangement of the earlier war, which he described as *"that thing called burden sharing, where we actually made the Kuwaitis pay for everything"* — that is, *"pay for the cost of their own liberation."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:08" />

He recalled that the same idea had been floated for Iraq, with a dry note on the outcome: *"but I don't know how that worked out."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:08" /> On whether Congress would pay up anyway, he was not optimistic — *"Congress is a bunch of lickspittles, but we'll see what happens."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:08" />

The daily running cost of the war is treated at [cost of the 2026 Iran war](/wiki/cost-of-the-2026-iran-war).
