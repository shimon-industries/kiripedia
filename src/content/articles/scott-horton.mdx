**Scott Horton** is an American antiwar journalist and interviewer, and one of the very few commentators [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) singles out by name for intellectual force: *"Scott Horton, who is one of the most brilliant people I've ever engaged with on these issues."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="22:58" /> The formulation recurs almost unchanged across appearances, *"one of the most brilliant people I I've ever met"*<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="1:19:00" />, and its fullest version is the least guarded: *"this guy is absolutely brilliant. I I wish my brain could work in such a linear fashion as his does. I I could sit and just listen to him talk all day."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:11" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:41" /> Horton has also written extensively on Afghanistan; when an interviewer raised his name in that connection, Kiriakou interrupted to say *"oh, he's wonderful."*<Cite s="2025-10-24-pakistan-nuclear-secrets-cia-lies" t="11:30" />

## The Piers Morgan panel

The occasion Kiriakou returns to is a televised panel hosted by [Piers Morgan](/wiki/piers-morgan) at which he and Horton took one side and [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz) and Danny Ayalon the other.<Cite s="2026-02-13-o-keefe-media-group" t="1:16:44" /><Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="1:19:00" /> Kiriakou describes it as *"the equivalent of a WWE Smackdown"*<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:11" /> and characterises the format the same way elsewhere, *"it's like smackdown on that show half"* the time, adding that Morgan keeps inviting him back *"because I don't interrupt people."*<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="1:18:04" /> Of the opposing pair he is blunt: *"when you're on with Duritz, Duritz never shuts his mouth."*<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="1:19:00" />

Neither he nor Horton was scheduled. Kiriakou, by his own account a semi-regular on the programme, was called at fifteen minutes' notice because a guest had dropped out; Horton was the other fill-in.<Cite s="2025-09-02-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="08:23" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:48:14" /> What he remembers is the relief of seeing who else was on the call: *"I log on and I see Scott and I was like, 'Oh, thank God Scott's here.' … as soon as I saw Scott Horton, I thought, 'Okay, this is going to be a serious intellectual conversation.'"*<Cite s="2025-09-02-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="08:56" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="1:48:46" />

Kiriakou's identification of the fourth panellist varies between tellings. He names him as Danny Ayalon, *"General Danny Ayalon … former Israeli general"*<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="1:19:00" />, in two accounts, but in two others describes the man opposite him only as *"the former head of Mossad"*<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:41" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:59" />. The variance is preserved here rather than resolved.

## What was argued

Two arguments came out of the panel, and Kiriakou has retold both.

The first was that [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) had been a [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) [access agent](/wiki/access-agent), *"somebody who gives a foreign intelligence service access to important people who have important information"*, a claim he says he made with 100% confidence, and one he grounds in the observation that a foreign service does not recruit Bill Gates or Bill Clinton or [Prince Andrew](/wiki/prince-andrew) but the man standing next to them.<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:41" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="57:13" /> Dershowitz, he says, *"practically had a stroke when I said it"* and answered that as Epstein's lawyer he would have known: had his client been working for the Israelis, he could have gone to the White House and obtained a more favourable sentence.<Cite s="2026-02-13-o-keefe-media-group" t="1:17:15" />

The second was that Israel spies on the United States as a matter of course. Kiriakou stated it as fact from the panel, *"[Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) was not an anomaly"*, and put the FBI's own count behind it: at least 187 undeclared Israeli intelligence officers spread across the United States, identified through the 1990s and lodged inside American defence contractors.<Cite s="2026-02-13-o-keefe-media-group" t="1:16:44" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="12:29" /> The Israeli side laughed; Morgan put it to the panel that there had been no spying since Pollard's arrest, because the prime minister had said so. Kiriakou's reply was that this was *"simply not true"*, and he offered his own first day at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) in 1990 as the evidence, he was warned about Israeli espionage against the United States on it.<Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:59" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="12:29" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="23:33" /><Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="24:03" />

A separate exchange from the same programme, which Kiriakou recounts as the moment he was cross-examined by the host rather than the guests, turned on [Hamas](/wiki/hamas): asked whether Hamas was a terrorist organisation and whether 7 October had been a terrorist attack, he answered yes to both, and was met with *"then what are you doing on this show?"* His answer was that a policy of killing *"women, children, the elderly"* and levelling every hospital, school and apartment block is genocide.<Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="1:19:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-05-theo-von-podcast-661" t="1:20:00" />

## As interviewer

Horton has interviewed Kiriakou repeatedly on his own programme, including on the pressure applied to independent journalists, the conversation in which Kiriakou grouped himself, [Max Blumenthal](/wiki/max-blumenthal) and MintPress News together as targets of the same campaign.<Cite s="2022-05-06-scott-horton-ep-5709" t="16:45" />
