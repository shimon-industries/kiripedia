**Find a Grave** is the online cemetery database that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) names as his favourite website, and which functions as a research tool for his books.

## The habit

Describing what he does while waiting for the ten-minute countdown before a broadcast, Kiriakou said: *"I go on what is literally my favorite website on the internet, and that is find agrave.com."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="10:02" />

## The working use

It is not idle browsing. *"I use find a grave a lot uh to to help guide the research in my book, right, to decide who to put in the book. And I know most of the people who are important enough to be on Find a Grave as a famous person."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="10:02" />

The claim to know most of them is what makes an exception worth reporting on air. On 8 September 2025 he found one listed as having died on that date whom he did not recognise, described in the entry only as a *"pop culture icon"*, see [Greta Zimmer Friedman](/wiki/greta-zimmer-friedman).<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="10:32" />

## Why the books exist

Kiriakou closed the segment with the rationale for the whole enterprise: *"this is why I write these cemetery books. I mean, the stories that people have are just fascinating to me. I love it."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="13:40" />

He returned to the show's actual agenda afterwards with a warning that nothing else that day would clear the bar.<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="13:40" />
