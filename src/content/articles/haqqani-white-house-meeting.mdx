In the mid-to-late 1980s, during the Reagan administration, a delegation of Taliban members was invited to the White House to meet with President Ronald Reagan, per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou); a former boss of Kiriakou's served as translator for the meeting. One member of that delegation was a man named Haqqani, who went on to ally himself with al-Qaeda and became the leader of what is now known as the Haqqani network.<Cite s="2018-06-19-real-progress-in-action" t="21:24" />

## Later consequences

Kiriakou says the Haqqani network was responsible for the capture of U.S. Army soldier Bowe Bergdahl, and separately for the kidnapping, rape, and torture of a Canadian couple.<Cite s="2018-06-19-real-progress-in-action" t="21:54" />
