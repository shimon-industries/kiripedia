**Lee Zeldin**, rendered "Lee Zeldon" in the source transcript, is discussed by Megyn Kelly, in her opening monologue on the episode that later featured [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), as the sitting EPA administrator reportedly considered by [Trump](/wiki/trump) as a possible replacement for Attorney General [Pam Bondi](/wiki/pam-bondi), per a Semaphore report that Trump had told Bondi her time as attorney general was "nearing an end."<Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:02" />

Kelly later reports, reading a Trump statement live on air during her interview with Kiriakou, that Deputy Attorney General [Todd Blanche](/wiki/todd-blanch), not Zeldin, was named to the role, seen as making "a lot more sense," both because of his job title and his reputation as well-liked within the department.<Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="1:24:45" />
