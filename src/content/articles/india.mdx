**India** is the country [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) names when asked for his least favourite of the places he has worked. He spent about six weeks there, based in Hyderabad, and is unequivocal: *"I really don't like India at all."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="33:42" /><Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="34:12" />

## Not the poverty

Kiriakou is careful to disqualify the obvious explanations. He describes it as the filthiest place he has been — *"I've been in filthy places before. I've never been in a place as filthy as India"* — but immediately sets that aside as the reason: *"I've been in worse places, poorer places, more dangerous places."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="34:12" />

What he objects to is social rather than material: *"the thing that I hate the most about India is the way Indians treat one another. It's shocking to me, this caste system that they have. It's shocking."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="34:12" />

## The driver

The example he gives concerns a driver assigned to him for the six weeks — *"a really sweet guy, wife and two little kids at home"* — earning perhaps a hundred or a hundred and fifty dollars a month, and available around the clock.<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="34:43" />

Kiriakou had been warned before leaving not to over-tip, on the grounds that it would *"ruin it for everybody else."* He over-tipped anyway, and accepts the charge that he set a standard others would then have to match.<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="34:43" /><Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="35:13" />

He then invited the man to lunch at the restaurant inside the hotel where he was staying. They agreed on one o'clock. Kiriakou waited — *"1:00, 1:15, 1:30. Where is this guy?"* — then went outside and found him standing there.<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="35:13" /><Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="35:43" />

*"I'm sorry, sir, they won't let me in."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="35:43" />

The reason given by staff was caste. Kiriakou objected that the man was his invited guest, and escalated to the manager, who repeated it. His final appeal — *"I'm a guest in this hotel and I've invited him to lunch, he's my friend"* — got the flattest possible answer: *"I don't care who you are. He's not coming in the hotel."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="35:43" /><Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="36:14" />

### The second refusal

Furious, Kiriakou got in the car and asked to be taken to the best restaurant in Hyderabad — the city being famous for its biryani. The driver took him to a converted Victorian mansion from the British period, the finest place in town.<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="36:14" /><Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="36:45" />

The manager's ruling was identical: *"You can come in. He can't come in."* After some argument, the compromise reached was a table set up outside.<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="36:45" /><Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="37:17" />

Asked whether this was really being done by Indians to other Indians, Kiriakou confirms it, and adds the part he finds least tolerable — that it is unremarkable: *"To their own people. And this happens every single day."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="37:17" />

He allows the country exactly one concession, offered without elaboration: *"The food is very good, though."*<Cite s="2026-07-21-one-night-with-john-kiriakou-exposes-which-celebrities-are-in" t="38:48" />
