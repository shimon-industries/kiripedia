**Mohammad Reza Pahlavi** was the last Shah of Iran. Asked on a January 2026 broadcast where the United States had gone wrong in its dealings with him, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) began with the man rather than the policy: *"Where do you even begin? The Shah was an absolute monarch, absolute dictator."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="38:35" />

The indictment that follows has three parts. The first is the security apparatus: the Shah *"had a bloodthirsty intelligence service called [SAVAK](/wiki/savak) that spent way more time investigating and murdering Iranian citizens than it ever did doing anything, you know, in any other country."* The second is alignment, he *"had formal official diplomatic relations with Israel, which really pissed people off."* The third is the oil: he was *"perfectly happy to send Iran's oil to the United States and to the UK."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="39:08" />

## The democratic interruption

In Kiriakou's telling the throne was not continuous. Iran *"very briefly had a democratic government"* under prime minister Mohammad Mosaddegh; the Shah's father had been in power, then the Shah briefly, then Mosaddegh won an election. [The United States and the United Kingdom overthrew Mosaddegh in 1953](/wiki/1953-iran-coup), and he *"spent literally the rest of his life under house arrest."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="39:39" />

The word Kiriakou uses for what happened next is the one that carries the argument: the Shah *"was forcibly reimposed on the Iranian people."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="39:39" />

He has given the same sequence elsewhere in a single sentence, and attributed it to the father rather than the son: the Shah *"overthrew the only democratically elected prime minister in the history of Iran, Mohammad Mosaddegh, 1953 with the [CIA](/wiki/cia) and MI6."*<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="47:21" />

## The ski vacation

The end, as Kiriakou dates it, was undramatic in form and total in effect. *"By 1979 it all fell apart. He went on a ski vacation in February of '79 and never went back."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="40:10" />

## The inheritance

Kiriakou treats the Shah's record as the reason his son [Reza Pahlavi](/wiki/reza-pahlavi) has no constituency to return to. Asked why the exiled crown prince *"wouldn't survive the walk from the plane to the terminal"*, his answer runs straight back to the father: *"Because they hate him. Why do they hate him? Because his father created [SAVAK](/wiki/savak)."*<Cite s="2026-04-06-danny-jones-iran-war-trumps-nightmare" t="46:51" />
