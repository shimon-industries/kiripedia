**"This desk kills fascism"** was the sign Kiriakou wrote on a page from a legal pad and taped to the desk in his cell at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), a play on "This machine kills fascists," which the folk singer Woody Guthrie had stencilled on his beat-up old guitar.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:33" /><Cite s="2020-05-15-choo-radio-network" t="41:51" />

## The manuscript

The sign was a response to an escalating attempt to deprive him of the means of writing. Roughly twelve hours after finishing one [letter from Loretto](/wiki/letter-from-loretto), Kiriakou was summoned to the Special Investigative Service office, where an officer returned an envelope containing the first 20,000 words of his book — which he had not realised was missing since a cell shakedown. The officer who had taken it claimed the manuscript had "made threats against staff"; the SIS officer said he had read it himself and believed it contained no such threats, and returned it in its entirety, though Kiriakou assumes a copy was made.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="36:58" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="37:29" />

## The desk

The staff were, in his account, furious about the letters he was writing, and the next move was against the furniture: they would take his desk off the wall so that he could not write.<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="24:47" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="37:29" /> He had warning. A friendly guard told him what was coming, and the bolts were stripped before the attempt was made — Kiriakou has said in his earlier tellings that he paid another prisoner a bag of tuna fish to strip the screws, and in a later one that he had stripped them himself as a preemptive measure.<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="24:47" /><Cite s="2020-05-15-choo-radio-network" t="41:51" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="37:29" />

Either way it held. Two hours after the warning the guards arrived with a drill, tried to take the desk off the wall, could not, and eventually walked away.<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="24:47" /> A work order was issued to chip the desk off the wall, and Kiriakou went to the unit manager, who promised the desk would stay. It did.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="37:29" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:02" />

## The sign

Kiriakou then took a legal pad, wrote *"this desk kills fascism"* on it and stuck it to the desk. It hung a couple of hours, until one of the officers making the daily count — a count run five to seven times a day — tore it off, crumpled it into a ball and threw it at him.<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="25:18" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:33" /> That reaction was the point: *"which is really what I wanted him to do."*<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="25:18" /> He flattened the page out and mailed it home to himself, and keeps it as a reminder that he was not alone in what he went through.<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="25:18" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:33" />

## The print

The page had an afterlife. In prison Kiriakou took a couple of art classes, found he had something of a knack for it, and made a screen print of the page — a hundred copies.<Cite s="2023-03-28-art-and-documentary-art-and-documentary" t="25:18" /> He sold the prints through his website to raise money for his attorney's fees.<Cite s="2020-05-15-choo-radio-network" t="42:22" /> By April 2022 he said he had nine or ten of them left.<Cite s="2022-04-09-will-turbitt" t="1:12:33" />

His broader characterisation of the episode is that crude, amateurish attempts to deprive him of his rights do not originate with a low-level correctional officer but come from higher up, from officials who should know better.<Cite s="2026-07-13-dead-drop-s2e9-killing-fascism" t="38:02" />
