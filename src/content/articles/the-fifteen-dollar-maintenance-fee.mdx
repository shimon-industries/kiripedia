The anecdote arrives in the middle of an appeal for viewers to move to a platform that pays the show more. Kiriakou interrupts it to explain, without euphemism, why the appeal matters to him. <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:18" />

## The appointment

He had been to the bank that morning, he says, on an appointment made "because I'm so broke." The bank had taken a $15 monthly maintenance fee, and the fee had put the account at negative fifteen dollars. <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:18" />

Kiriakou describes what he did about it in the terms of an ordinary customer with no leverage: "So, I had to go back and say, 'Guys, come on. Can you just give me back my $15?'" <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:50" />

The bank did. "Thank goodness," Kiriakou says. "So, I'm back up to zero." <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:50" />

## Context

The story sits inside a broader account Kiriakou gives of his post-conviction finances. In the same broadcast he and his co-host discuss the fact that the programme does not cover their bills, Rall introduces the platform pitch as a way to "help John and I be able to pay our bills, something that we currently cannot do." <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:18" />

Kiriakou has made the general claim elsewhere that whistleblowers die broke. The maintenance-fee story is that claim reduced to a single arithmetic fact, told against himself: a former CIA counterterrorism officer and published author negotiating with a teller over fifteen dollars in order to return his balance to nothing. <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:50" />

He does not dramatise it. The account is delivered as an aside, is over in a few sentences, and ends with him back at zero rather than ahead. <Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-LnuGCF" t="30:50" />
