The **Marble Framework** is a CIA code-obfuscation tool revealed in the [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) leaks. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes it as the practice of infecting computers domestically — itself illegal — and then embedding lines of code written in Farsi or Cyrillic, so that if the operation were discovered, the agency could claim *"it wasn't us, it was the Russians"* or the Iranians who *"forgot to translate"* their code. In fact, Kiriakou says, *"it was us."*<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:34:57" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="1:35:29" />
