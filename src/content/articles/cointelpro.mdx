**COINTELPRO** was the [FBI](/wiki/fbi)'s counterintelligence programme directed at Americans, and one of the four abuses [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) lists whenever he is asked what the [Church Committee](/wiki/church-committee) was for.

His description of it is short and unqualified: the committee was *"looking at the FBI's surveillance program and spying on Americans without any warrant or court order. They would just arbitrarily do it, through a program then called COINTELPRO."*<Cite s="2023-01-20-scheerpost-scheerpost" t="04:38" />

He sets it alongside the CIA's international assassinations programme, the Army's programme to infiltrate peace and anti-Vietnam-war groups, and [MKUltra](/wiki/mk-ultra) — the four together being what he means by crimes committed by the deep state, and the standard against which he measures [later committees claiming the Church Committee's mantle](/wiki/weaponization-subcommittee).<Cite s="2023-01-20-scheerpost-scheerpost" t="04:38" /><Cite s="2023-01-20-scheerpost-scheerpost" t="05:08" />

Its most consequential application, in Kiriakou's account, was the campaign against [Martin Luther King](/wiki/martin-luther-king) — an operation whose surveillance component he describes as running to two hundred agents, and which he treats as the demonstration of what the programme was ultimately for: *"they tried to label him first as some kind of communist agent. That didn't go over — then as some kind of pervert, or something. But destroy him."*<Cite s="2023-01-20-scheerpost-scheerpost" t="13:04" /><Cite s="2023-01-20-scheerpost-scheerpost" t="29:07" />
