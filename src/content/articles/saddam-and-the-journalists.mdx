**Saddam and the journalists** is a comparison John Kiriakou drew on the DeProgram programme in August 2025, after the host put to him the killing of the Al Jazeera correspondent Anas al-Sharif and four colleagues, which the host said the Israel Defense Forces had acknowledged as deliberate on the grounds that al-Sharif led a Hamas cell.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="16:41" />

## The claim to standing

Kiriakou grounds the comparison in his own career. *"I worked on Iraq for the most part of 15 years at the CIA, through two wars against Iraq."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:43" />

## The comparison

On that basis he offers [Saddam Hussein](/wiki/saddam-hussein) as a benchmark, and one that the present case fails: *"Saddam Hussein hated journalists, but he never went out and killed them."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:43" />

The contrast he draws is with the Israeli prime minister: *"Benjamin Netanyahu kills them — on purpose, en masse."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:43" /> His explanation of the motive is one sentence: *"He doesn't want the truth coming out."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:43" />

The host, an editorial cartoonist, told him the formulation was publishable as it stood — *"that's an op-ed right there"* — and separately observed that he did not remember Pol Pot or Saddam Hussein ever asserting a right to do it.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:11" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:43" />

## The case that prompted it

Kiriakou returned in the same passage to an earlier killing he says he remains angry about, that of [Shireen Abu Akleh](/wiki/shireen-abu-akleh) — the Al Jazeera correspondent he describes as a Greek Orthodox Palestinian Christian who was *"at every event in Washington"*, shot in the face by a sniper while wearing a marked press vest and helmet and sheltering behind a tree.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="17:43" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:15" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:45" />

He traces the shifting Israeli explanations — a mistake, then collaboration with Hamas — and describes the funeral at a [Greek Orthodox](/wiki/greek-orthodox-church) church in the West Bank, where he says Israeli forces entered mid-service and beat the pallbearers until the coffin was dropped and opened.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:45" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:16" />

What has changed since, in his account, is not the conduct but the pretence: *"now they don't even bother. They just kill the whole group ... because what's going to happen? Nothing ever does."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:49" />
