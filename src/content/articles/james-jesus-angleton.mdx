James Jesus Angleton headed the CIA Counterintelligence Center in the early 1970s, where he prohibited a new officer from accessing a wall of files on American citizens, highlighting the agency's illegal surveillance practices. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:05:22" />

Under James Jesus Angleton's leadership, the CIA Counterintelligence Center maintained a wall of files on American citizens, which was patently illegal. A new officer was prohibited from accessing these files, but later discovered their contents. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="01:05:54" />

## Files on Americans, and the Philby wound

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says old-timers were *"genuinely afraid"* of counterintelligence chief [James Angleton](/wiki/james-jesus-angleton). His first boss, who once worked in Angleton's office as a young grad fellow, was warned never to look in the wall of file folders, and when he did, found that *"every single one"* was on an American citizen.<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="30:35" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="31:38" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="32:10" /> Angleton, obsessed with hunting Soviet moles, was undone by the discovery that the mole was his own best man and *koumbaros*, [Kim Philby](/wiki/kim-philby); Kiriakou says he *"kind of lost his mind and never got it back."*<Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="32:10" /><Cite s="2026-02-12-austin-and-matt-us-destroying-itself" t="36:55" />

## The exhaustion he left behind

Kiriakou also treats Angleton's obsession as one of the reasons the next mole survived so long. Explaining how [Aldrich Ames](/wiki/aldrich-ames) went undetected for nearly two decades, he names a fear of moles that had tormented successive [CIA](/wiki/cia) directors and deputy directors, *"most famously deputy director for counterintelligence James Jesus Angleton"*, and the reaction it produced: *"a lot of people inside the CIA were just exhausted by this constant hunt for moles."* Alongside it ran the Directorate of Operations' refusal to entertain the idea at all, which Kiriakou calls *"this culture of misplaced secrecy [that] covered for its brethren regardless of their errors, failures, misdemeanors and outright crimes."*<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="10:07" />
