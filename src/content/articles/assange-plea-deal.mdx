The **Assange plea deal** is the June 2024 agreement under which [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange) pleaded guilty to conspiracy to commit espionage, was processed in Saipan, and flew home to Australia.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="13:29" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="17:48" /> [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), who made three trips to Iceland to strategize with WikiLeaks, relays the reform attorney Chip Gibbons's argument that because it was a plea to *conspiracy* rather than espionage, future prosecutors cannot use it to leverage the indictment of another journalist.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="15:05" /> He notes its odd terms: Assange had to swear an affidavit that WikiLeaks had purged all unpublished [Chelsea Manning](/wiki/chelsea-manning) material, of which there was none, while the CIA never demanded anything about [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) be purged; and he is barred from a U.S. green card for 25 years.<Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="18:20" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="18:51" /><Cite s="2024-07-29-news-beat-assange-israel-security-state" t="19:23" />
