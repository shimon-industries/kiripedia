**Lynn Buchanan** is described by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) as having signed non-disclosure agreements with the [NSA](/wiki/nsa) and [CIA](/wiki/cia), along with others, in connection with remote-viewing projects.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="57:31" />

Kiriakou identifies Buchanan and Paul H. Smith as Fort Meade-trained Army intelligence veterans who worked on the operational remote-viewing program under strict scientific protocols, with [General Albert Stubblebine](/wiki/albert-stubblebine) serving as a direct overseer. Kiriakou contrasts them with "television psychics," describing them instead as primary sources with documented operational and military records who signed non-disclosure agreements with the NSA.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="1:01:41" />
