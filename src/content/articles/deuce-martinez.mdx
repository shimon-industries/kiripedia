**Deuce Martinez** is a former [CIA](/wiki/cia) analyst who, on [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, *"had never, ever been undercover, ever."* He is nonetheless the subject of two of the three Espionage Act charges brought against Kiriakou.<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="28:31" />

## The business card

Martinez resigned from the CIA about a year after Kiriakou did. They were friends, and Martinez was *"excited to tell me about his onward position"*, so they had lunch and he handed over his new business card. It read: *"Deuce Martinez, Mitchell and Jessen LLC."*<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="29:03" />

Kiriakou's reaction was immediate — *"are you crazy going to work for these monsters?"* — [Mitchell and Jessen](/wiki/mitchell-and-jessen) being the two psychologists who designed the torture programme. Martinez's answer was *"no, no, they're good guys, they're misunderstood."* Kiriakou pressed: *"you've got to be insane to resign from the agency and go to work for these guys of all people. There wasn't one other job that you could have taken."* After Kiriakou went on ABC News, Martinez stopped speaking to him — *"which was fine."* But he still had the card.<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="29:03" /><Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="29:36" />

## The two charges

[Scott Shane](#) of the New York Times telephoned to ask how he could reach Martinez for a story. Kiriakou said they had not spoken in a long time, but that he had the business card — so he scanned it and emailed it. *"I was charged with espionage for that email."* When Shane's article ran, a reporter from ABC News called with the same question, and Kiriakou sent the card again. *"I got a second espionage charge."*<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="29:36" /><Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="30:07" />

The judge threw out all three espionage counts.<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="30:41" />

Kiriakou's standing objection to the charge is that Martinez's status was never secret in the first place: *"They charged me with espionage, saying that I outed Deuce Martinez. Deuce Martinez is on LinkedIn — Central Intelligence Agency Targeting Officer."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-8" t="02:05" />

## A second account of the same charge

Kiriakou has described the conduct behind his prosecution more than once, and the tellings do not match in their particulars — a variance worth recording rather than smoothing.

In a 2021 account he traces it not to the business card but to a book project. In 2008 an ABC News reporter told him he was writing a book on the [Abu Omar rendition](/wiki/abu-omar-rendition) — Abu Omar being a radical Egyptian cleric in Milan — and asked for an introduction to one of a dozen people on a list. Kiriakou replied that he knew nothing about the rendition and none of the people: *"kidnapping wasn't my thing at the CIA. I wasn't involved. I can't help."* A second list of a dozen names produced the same answer.<Cite s="2021-05-21-scheer-intelligence-a-former-cia-mideast-expert-s-view-of-the-isr" t="03:47" /><Cite s="2021-05-21-scheer-intelligence-a-former-cia-mideast-expert-s-view-of-the-isr" t="04:17" />

The reporter then changed tack and asked about a man Kiriakou had himself mentioned in his first book, giving only a first name. Kiriakou supplied the rest — *"oh, you're talking about John Smith. I don't know whatever happened to him, he's probably retired and living in Virginia somewhere"* — and, in his words, *"I confirmed the last name of this John Smith, and that's what got me in trouble."*<Cite s="2021-05-21-scheer-intelligence-a-former-cia-mideast-expert-s-view-of-the-isr" t="04:50" />
