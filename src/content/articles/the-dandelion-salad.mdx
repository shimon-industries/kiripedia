**The dandelion salad** is the meal [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) made for himself in the yard at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), and the last entry on his list of [the best meals he ever ate](/wiki/the-best-meal-overseas), offered with the acknowledgement that it does not belong on such a list. *"And then crazy as it sounds one of the best meals I ever had I made myself in prison."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="35:55" />

## Khaled

The idea came from another prisoner. *"There was an Iraqi Kurd in prison with me. He was an Imam of a mosque in upstate New York and this guy was innocent innocent innocent. They set him up on a terrorism charge, and then when he finally finished prison, they expelled him back to Iraq. It was awful."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="35:55" /><Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="36:26" />

Kiriakou found him walking the grass. *"Anyway, he was just kind of wandering around the yard one day, and I said, 'Khaled, I said, what are you doing?' Just wan- winding around, you know, the the grass. He said, 'I'm I'm looking for uh for um dandelions.' And I said, 'For what reason?' And he said, 'To eat them.'"*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="36:26" />

The answer landed on a Greek. *"And I was like, 'What a great idea! We make dandelion salad in Greece. We make dandelion jam, all different kinds of things.'"* He asked permission to pick at the far end of the yard, and got it.<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="36:26" /><Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="36:56" />

## The assembly

Kiriakou is specific about the washing and about why it mattered: *"You got to wash it cuz people are like spitting all the time or animals are peeing or pooping or whatever."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="36:56" />

The rest came from the commissary and from neighbours. *"So, I picked an entire bag full of dandelions. And then I went to the commissary and bought a bottle a bottle of olive oil and some sea salt. And then I I had some instant rice with a chicken bouillon cube. And I threw in some peanuts to give it a little bit of crunch and some Sazón uh spice that the one of the Mexicans gave me."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="36:56" /><Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="37:29" />

One ingredient carries an explanation about the institution rather than the recipe: *"Uh I had a bag of chicken. They sold bags of chicken there, not cans cuz you could make a make a shank out of the can and, you know, slash somebody with it. So, bags of chicken."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="37:29" />

## The verdict

*"I put it all together, and then I had my dandelion salad. It was the single most satisfying meal I ever had in 23 months in prison."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="37:29" /><Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="37:59" />

Kiriakou's own gloss on that judgement is two words long, and he supplies it himself: *"Sad, but true."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="37:59" />
