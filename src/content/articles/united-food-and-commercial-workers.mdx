The **United Food and Commercial Workers** (**UFCW**), at the time, by Kiriakou's account, the biggest union within the AFL-CIO, was [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s employer at the moment his life bent toward the [CIA](/wiki/cia).<Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="25:24" />

## The job

Kiriakou worked at the UFCW's international headquarters on K Street in Washington while studying Middle Eastern affairs at George Washington University, a natural berth for a man with *"a long history of labor involvement in my family."*<Cite s="2015-04-17-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:17" /><Cite s="2019-08-08-david-gornoski-archives-david-gornoski-archives" t="04:48" /> The post itself was junior: he has described himself as *"just an intern working for a polling unit that they had,"* a unit that ran public opinion polls for other AFL-CIO member unions.<Cite s="2016-01-12-the-sharin-hour-the-sharin-hour-1-11-16-john-kiriakou-ex-c-i" t="01:38" />

## The assignment

In [Dr. Jerrold Post](/wiki/jerome-post)'s class on the psychology of leadership, students were assigned to shadow their bosses for a week and write a psychological profile.<Cite s="2023-11-12-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-1" t="11:40" /><Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="25:24" /> Kiriakou's boss frightened him a little: an old-time tough-guy union organizer, rough and mean, whose back had been broken by scabs during a strike at a previous job with a garment workers' union.<Cite s="2022-07-23-the-darkened-hour" t="04:10" /><Cite s="2024-07-30-spartan-leadership-podcast" t="08:47" /><Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="25:55" /><Cite s="2021-09-08-primary-sources-jailing-the-messenger-the-cia-s-torture-whist" t="04:45" /> What frightened him were outbursts of temper, and of violence.<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="08:09" />

Halfway through the week they got into a serious argument, and Kiriakou called him a racist, *"which he was."* The man balled up his fists and took a stance.<Cite s="2021-11-21-dcm-tv-exclusivo-parceria-lava-jato-eua-foi-movida-p" t="02:53" /><Cite s="2019-10-03-kiriakou-on-the-cia-obama-vs-trump" t="01:35" /><Cite s="2021-09-08-primary-sources-jailing-the-messenger-the-cia-s-torture-whist" t="04:45" />

What happened next was not a punch. With both men squared up, Kiriakou putting his hands up and thinking *"dang it, I went too far this time"*, the boss instead said, twice, *"my penis is bigger than yours."* Kiriakou's reply was that he was crazy, and he quit and walked out on the spot, having concluded the man was dangerous. He wrote the paper immediately afterwards.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="17:01" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="18:33" /><Cite s="2021-09-08-primary-sources-jailing-the-messenger-the-cia-s-torture-whist" t="05:17" />

## The paper and its consequence

The paper Kiriakou turned in concluded that his boss was a sociopath, with psychopathic and possibly violent tendencies, and potentially dangerous, with evidence to support it; Kiriakou stresses that he footnoted the whole thing, so that it read as analysis rather than as him venting.<Cite s="2015-04-17-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:17" /><Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="17:01" /><Cite s="2021-09-08-primary-sources-jailing-the-messenger-the-cia-s-torture-whist" t="05:17" /> His own reading of why it worked is that the analysis was concise, to the point, and backed with facts.<Cite s="2026-07-08-voxera-production-official-voxera-production-official" t="18:33" /><Cite s="2015-04-17-the-real-news-network-the-real-news-network" t="01:17" />

It came back a week later with an A and a note in the margin. In one telling the note read: *"I've never done this before, but I urge you to quit this job. I agree with your assessment that he's a sociopath, and possibly a psychopath, and I think you should quit."*<Cite s="2016-01-12-the-sharin-hour-the-sharin-hour-1-11-16-john-kiriakou-ex-c-i" t="02:08" /> In others the margin carried the four words that mattered more, *"please see me after class."*<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="08:44" /><Cite s="2021-09-08-primary-sources-jailing-the-messenger-the-cia-s-torture-whist" t="05:17" /> By the time the advice to quit arrived, Kiriakou had already quit, a week earlier.<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="08:44" />

In the office afterwards, with the door closed, Post told him what the class had really been for: *"I'm actually undercover as a professor here. I'm a senior CIA officer acting as a spotter looking for grad students who I think would fit into the CIA's culture."*<Cite s="2015-12-11-scheer-intelligence-john-kiriakou-a-whistleblower-on-torture" t="08:44" /><Cite s="2021-09-08-primary-sources-jailing-the-messenger-the-cia-s-torture-whist" t="05:17" /> Post, who, Kiriakou later learned, had founded the [CIA's political psychology division](/wiki/cia-political-psychology-division), had taken notice. The union job ended; the intelligence career began.
