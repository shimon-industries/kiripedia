Responding to the [Mecca Agreement](/wiki/mecca-agreement), an Israeli official argued that Israel would have to deepen its relationships with Greece, Cyprus, the United Arab Emirates and Somaliland.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="33:07" /> Kiriakou singles out the last of these as the only part worth taking seriously.

His description of the territory is blunt: *"Somaliland is not a real country. It's the northernmost state in Somalia. It is recognized only by Israel, not by any other country in the world."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="33:07" /> The reason for the exception is geography, *"you can practically swim from Somaliland to Yemen"*, and the consideration is explicit: *"the Somalilanders are going to give the Israelis an air force base in exchange for this diplomatic recognition. Kudos to the Israelis. It was a smart move."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="33:07" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="33:37" />

The rest of the strategy he dismisses. Better relations with Greece, Cyprus, Somaliland *"even the Emirates, it's not going to cure anything."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="33:37" /> The Saudis and the Emiratis are in a diplomatic quarrel and on opposing sides in Yemen, but Kiriakou expects both to pass, because the underlying fact does not: *"they live in the same neighborhood. And so the Emiratis are not going to choose the Israelis over the Saudis. That's a pipe dream."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="33:37" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="34:09" />
