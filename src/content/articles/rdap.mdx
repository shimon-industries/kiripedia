**RDAP** — the Residential Drug and Alcohol Program — is the [Bureau of Prisons](/wiki/bureau-of-prisons) course that takes a year off a federal sentence on completion, and the centrepiece of the practical advice [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gives people who are about to be imprisoned.

His explanation of the arithmetic is straightforward: a federal prisoner earns good-conduct credit, and on top of that *"you get another year off for RDAP"* — and, he adds, you are in a low-security prison while doing it.<Cite s="2023-02-08-fortress-on-a-hill-john-kiriakou-ep-133-probe-dup-vs-6kmi7tkpf7q" t="28:06" /><Cite s="2023-02-08-fortress-on-a-hill-john-kiriakou-ep-133-probe-dup-vs-6kmi7tkpf7q" t="28:40" />

The same arithmetic underlies the counsel he gave [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale) when Hale was facing sentencing and the figure of five years came up. Kiriakou told him five years was *"a steal"*, and that there is a trick to doing the time.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="05:18" /> He applies the same reckoning generally: three and a half years feels like thirty, but once past the first year, with [halfway house](/wiki/halfway-house) time, house arrest and good-behaviour credit, three and a half years is really about two.<Cite s="2016-02-23-awakeradio1-awakeradio1" t="12:01" />

Kiriakou's own sentence produced none of these reductions. Because he was writing the [Letters from Loretto](/wiki/letter-from-loretto) about conditions inside, he received no halfway-house time at all and served every single day.<Cite s="2016-02-23-awakeradio1-awakeradio1" t="15:09" />

## What the counselling consisted of, and the all-or-nothing rule

Kiriakou describes the programme as ten or twelve months of drug and alcohol counselling worth up to eighteen months off a sentence — with the qualification that decides cases: *"you have to complete the whole year before you get the reward."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="19:52" /><Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="20:24" />

What the counselling actually was, in his own institution, he reports without comment: *"at least in the prison that I was in, you go every week and you watch videotaped episodes of *Intervention* from A&E — and that counts as your drug and alcohol counselling."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="20:24" />

The all-or-nothing rule is what makes removal from the programme a punishment in itself. [Jeremy Hammond](/wiki/jeremy-hammond) had two months left when he was pulled out and sent to the federal holding centre in Alexandria to face a grand jury — which, on Kiriakou's arithmetic, *"effectively added 18 months to his sentence."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="20:54" /><Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="21:26" />
