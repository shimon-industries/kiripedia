The name came up in passing in a viewer's comment about which politicians serve which interests. [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) had barely finished the list when John Kiriakou stopped him.

## The set-up

"Don't get me started on Crenshaw," Kiriakou said. "Oh my god."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="23:46" /> He then volunteered the reason: "Let me tell you about Crenshaw."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:19" />

He described briefly dating a woman "who was considerably younger than I, very, very attractive, a leader in her field."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:19" /> They were at her flat one night watching a film, it was about midnight, and her phone was on the floor near him.<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:19" /><Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:50" />

## The message

The phone buzzed with a text. "It's from Dan Crenshaw," Kiriakou said, "and he says, 'What are you wearing?'"<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:50" />

He read it out to her. Her reaction, in his telling, was weary rather than surprised: "Oh, that guy won't leave me alone."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:50" />

So Kiriakou answered it himself. "I took the phone and I wrote back. I said, 'Crenshaw, this is her boyfriend. Why don't you ask your wife what she's wearing?'"<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="24:50" /><Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="25:22" />

The reply came back in two words: "Sorry, scumbag."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="25:22" />

## What made it worse

Kiriakou's objection was not only to the message but to its timing in the man's life. "His wife just had — they had a ten-month-old baby at the time," he said, "but he's asking my girlfriend what she's wearing at twelve o'clock at night."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="25:22" />

He added that she then showed him other messages from the same sender. "They were filthy," he said — and, significantly, "she saved them just in case she needed to use them against him."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="25:22" />

Rall's reaction was two words of disbelief; Kiriakou's verdict was one sentence: "Yeah, he's a scumbag."<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="25:22" />

## Note

Kiriakou does not name the woman, give a date, or say whether she ever made use of the saved messages. He introduced the account unprompted, as an illustration of a judgement he had already delivered — that the subject "is in a league all of his own" among people he regards as contemptible.<Cite s="2025-11-10-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="23:46" />
