**Corporate competitive intelligence** is the private-sector application of intelligence collection that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) moved into after resigning from the [CIA](/wiki/cia) in 2004.

## Why he left

The reason he gives is domestic. *"I left the CIA, as funny as it might sound, to spend more time with my children."* An acrimonious divorce had taken his ex-wife and two sons to north-eastern Ohio to be near her parents, and he knew his career would shortly send him overseas again *"to some place not family friendly — Iraq, Afghanistan, Pakistan, Yemen, something like that."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="1:34" /><Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="2:05" /> A friend knew somebody at [Deloitte & Touche](/wiki/deloitte-consulting-career), which was looking for someone to head competitive intelligence in its Washington office. He applied, was offered the job within a couple of weeks, resigned from the agency and started the next day.<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="2:05" /><Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="2:37" />

## How the group was built

Kiriakou was *"actually the fourth CIA guy that Deloitte & Touche had hired."* The competitive intelligence group had been created on a simple design: hire former CIA officers, or others with experience in intelligence collection, and pair them with the firm's corporate research analysts *"to collect and analyze intelligence on their competitors."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="2:37" />

His own brief was collection rather than analysis: *"my job was to collect first-person human source intelligence on Ernst & Young, KPMG, PricewaterhouseCoopers and a myriad of consulting firms like IBM and Accenture and others."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="3:08" />

## The requirements

The targets are commercial but the shape is familiar. *"We tried to steal their pricing models, or how they determine discounts. Or — interestingly, at least to me — we tried to figure out which of their partners were disgruntled, so we could steal them, provided that they were top producers."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="3:39" />

The reason pricing matters he sets out as arithmetic: *"if you're going to go into a bid with a price of 24 million dollars, let's say, we want to know that, so we can go in with a bid of 20 million dollars and win the job — because a 20 million dollar contract of course is better than a zero dollar contract."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="4:11" /> The full requirement list he gives is *"the bidding process, it's discounts, it's disgruntled partners, it's plans to expand service lines or offerings — anything that might give you a corporate leg up."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="4:41" />

## Everybody does it

Asked whether the targets were doing the same back, his answer was *"absolutely — to each other. They all have the same groups."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="3:39" /> Asked whether he was any good at it: *"wildly, in some cases."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="4:41" />
