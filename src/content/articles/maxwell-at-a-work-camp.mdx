**Maxwell at a work camp** is John Kiriakou's objection to the security classification given to [Ghislaine Maxwell](/wiki/ghislaine-maxwell), raised on the DeProgram programme in July 2025 as what he called *"a minor point, but something that I've often wondered about."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="48:53" />

## The rule as he states it

Kiriakou says he has made the argument publicly for years. Convicted paedophiles, in his account, are *"almost always locked up in low security prisons"*, and the placement is bracketed at both ends.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="48:53" />

They are barred from the bottom of the scale by the nature of the offence: *"They're not eligible for minimums because they're paedophiles, which means they're dangers to society."* They are kept from the top of it for their own preservation: *"they don't go to mediums or maximums because they'll be attacked or killed or beaten or tortured or whatever"* by the prisoners already there.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="48:53" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="49:23" />

The result is a narrow band of the [federal system](/wiki/federal-prison-security-levels) in which such prisoners are held, which Kiriakou has described elsewhere in discussing the standing of [sex offenders](/wiki/chomo) among other inmates.

## The anomaly

Against that rule, Maxwell's assignment stood out. *"She's at a minimum security work camp,"* Kiriakou says. *"How the heck did that happen? Why were the rules waived for her? Why is she getting special treatment?"*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="49:23" />

## The refusal to speculate

Asked directly what his own answer was, Kiriakou declined to supply one: *"I don't know. I don't have an answer."* He would say only that *"it doesn't make any sense to me."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="49:23" />

The host drew the wider inference in his place — that unexplained facts invite invention, and that *"there may not be a conspiracy, but when things don't make sense, people fill in the blanks."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="49:23" /> Kiriakou did not extend the point.

On the underlying conviction he is not agnostic. Asked in the same conversation whether Maxwell had been railroaded, he answered *"oh, I don't think so. I think she was guilty,"* adding that others should have been prosecuted alongside her and Epstein but that *"she got what she deserved."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="49:55" />
