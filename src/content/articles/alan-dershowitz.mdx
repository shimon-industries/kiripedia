**Alan Dershowitz** is an American attorney and Harvard Law professor. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he debated Dershowitz head-to-head on Piers Morgan's show over the [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) case — an exchange Morgan himself called a "smackdown." Kiriakou told Dershowitz that, had Dershowitz gone to the White House seeking a lighter sentence for Epstein on the grounds that Epstein was spying for a foreign country, Kiriakou would have wanted to see him "hanging from a tree" after being convicted on espionage charges.<Cite s="2026-02-27-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="32:49" />

## Kiriakou's assessment of him

Kiriakou's characterisation of Dershowitz is deliberately two-sided. *"On the one hand he's a very kind and generous man,"* he says; *"on the other hand, when it comes to Israel, he's irrational."* Pressed on how, he sets out two things: that in Dershowitz's account *"there's literally nothing that the Israelis can do that's wrong"*, and that he will not let an interlocutor finish — *"if he thinks that you might possibly criticize Israel, he's going to interrupt you until you just give up and move on."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="20:42" /> Kiriakou concedes the antagonism is partly his own: *"I just can't help myself sometimes when Alan Dershowitz and I are in the same room."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="20:12" /> He also relays, as a piece of trivia, that Dershowitz named Ted Cruz the most talented law student he ever taught.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="1:06:11" />

## David Boies's characterization

Kiriakou cites attorney David Boies — whom he calls one of the greatest criminal defense attorneys in modern American history — stating on a later Piers Morgan appearance that Dershowitz's problem was that he had been far more than just Epstein's lawyer: he was also Epstein's publicist and friend, and had been a guest on Epstein's island, appearing in his underwear alongside a 16-year-old girl. Boies also mocked Dershowitz's continuing claim that he could have gotten Epstein a better deal had Epstein actually been a Mossad agent — noting Epstein, in Kiriakou's telling, was in fact a Mossad asset.<Cite s="2026-02-27-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="34:23" />

## The exchange on Piers Morgan

Kiriakou has described a televised argument with Dershowitz over whether [Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was working for Israel: *"I got into a spirited debate with Alan Dershowitz about this on the [Piers Morgan](/wiki/piers-morgan) show a few weeks ago, and I thought the man was going to have a stroke when I said that Jeffrey Epstein was most likely working on behalf of the Israelis."*<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="06:42" />

What interests him is the shape of Dershowitz's rebuttal, which he calls *"completely backward."* Dershowitz's argument was that he had been Epstein's attorney, and that if Epstein had been working for the Israelis he would have told him — and that Dershowitz *"would have used that information to get Epstein an even lighter sentence."*<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="07:12" />

Kiriakou's answer treats that as a claim about American law that cannot survive being stated plainly: *"wait a minute, professor. If you're saying that you would have gone to the White House to say, my client is committing espionage for the Israeli government — you think that would have gotten him a lighter sentence? I think he would have been hanging from a tree."* Pressed, he put it without the figure of speech: *"he would have been put in prison for the rest of his life, or put to death."* The yardstick he reaches for is [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard).<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="07:12" /><Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="07:42" />
