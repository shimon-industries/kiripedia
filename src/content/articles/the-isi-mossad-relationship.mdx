**The ISI-Mossad relationship** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to a direct question about how Pakistani and Israeli intelligence stand toward one another, asked in the context of [a rumoured Pakistani peacekeeping deployment to Gaza](/wiki/pakistani-troops-for-gaza) said to have been brokered between the two services.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="13:31" />

## Formal until 1979, and nothing since

*"There was a formal relationship as recently as 1979,"* Kiriakou says, *"but there is no formal relationship since then."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="13:31" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="14:01" />

Absence of contact is not, in his account, the same as hostility. *"They don't have a lot to do with each other in day-to-day world events. But they're not at each other's throats."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="14:01" />

## Why neither service has time for the other

The explanation he gives is one of allocated attention rather than of policy. [The ISI](/wiki/isi) is occupied elsewhere: *"The Pakistanis are worried about the Indians, and to a slightly lesser extent, they're worried about the Afghans. They don't have time to worry about the Israelis."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="14:01" />

The reverse is a matter of threat assessment. *"The Israelis have never seen Pakistan as a threat to Israel."* Pakistan's public alignment with the Arab position is real but bounded: *"they'll offer vocal support for the Arabs, but that ends there with vocal support. That's it."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="14:01" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="14:32" />
