**The gamma globulin shot** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the inoculation routine imposed on [CIA](/wiki/cia) officers travelling overseas, given in answer to a viewer who asked whether the vaccination rules for deployments were as strict as reported.

## The injection

Kiriakou dates the practice from his own arrival to the turn of the century. *"all of us Oh my god, you know when I first joined the CIA all the way up until like 2000. So, for that whole decade for yellow fever, you had to get a gamma globulin shot."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="07:44" />

The description is physical rather than clinical. *"it was like injecting maple syrup in your butt, and it would make this knot the size of a tennis ball."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="07:44" />

## The dispensation

What interests Kiriakou is that the agency built the side effect into its own timekeeping. *"they would give you dispensation to walk around the compound for an hour and stretch out your glutes so you didn't get that giant ball. It was like an hour of administrative time to work out that ball of gamma globulin that's pooling in your butt."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="07:44" />

A second accommodation covered the aftermath. *"another thing too is you'd get all these shots the same day, and the next day you're sick as a dog. And so, we would get that day off without it counting against your sick leave."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="08:16" />

## The frequency

Kiriakou attributes the volume of injections to his own posting pattern, in a formulation he attributes to Donald Trump rather than to himself: *"I only went to not only, but I almost exclusively went to um, the countries as Donald Trump called them. And so, uh, so I was getting shots right, left, and center every time I'd go."*<Cite s="2026-02-11-deprogram-ted-rall-bad-bunny-epstein-files-and-more" t="08:16" />

The limits of the protection this bought are the subject of [typhoid fever in Bangkok](/wiki/typhoid-fever-in-bangkok).
