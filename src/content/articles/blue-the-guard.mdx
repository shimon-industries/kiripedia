**Blue** was the nickname of a correctional officer at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes as arguably the most unpopular guard in the whole place.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:03" />

His distinguishing habit was a piece of masking tape covering his nameplate. In Kiriakou's account the reason was straightforward: he did not want anybody to be able to track him down on the outside once they were released, and knife him to death.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:03" />

Despite that reputation, Kiriakou says Blue never once caused him a problem, and he does not believe the man ever actually spoke to him.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:03" />

## The overheard exchange

Kiriakou learned the reason second-hand. One of his Italian friends asked Blue where he would be stationed for the coming quarter — assignments rotated every three months — and Blue said he was going to Central 1. The friend pointed out that Kiriakou lived in Central 1, referring to him as the CIA guy.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:03" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:36" />

Blue's answer, as relayed to Kiriakou, was that he never messed with that guy. Asked why, he said that was all he needed — to work eight hours at the job and then go outside and find CNN standing next to his car. No thanks.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:36" />

## Context

Kiriakou treats the exchange as confirmation of the leverage he had inside. The Bureau of Prisons had circulated a memorandum four days before his arrival warning staff that the incoming prisoner had access to the media, a document he later obtained through his own [Freedom of Information Act request from prison](/wiki/prison-foia-request).<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="22:14" /> He describes using the same leverage directly against the warden over the quality of the food, daring him to impose [solitary confinement](/wiki/solitary-confinement) in the knowledge that Kiriakou would go straight to CNN or the *Washington Post*.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="10:28" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="10:59" />

Kiriakou's general assessment of the staff at Loretto is unsparing — he says most of them were assholes, and that on his first day one whispered "traitor" as he walked past on his way to dinner.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="18:30" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="19:32" /> Blue is the counter-example, and Kiriakou's summary of him is simply: yeah, good, okay.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="20:36" />
