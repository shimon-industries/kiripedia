**The CIA Insider's Guide omnibus** is the single-volume reissue of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s three practical guides, proposed to him by Tony Lyons, the owner and publisher of Skyhorse Publishing.

The approach began with Lyons observing that Kiriakou was *"everywhere in my feed — you're on Instagram, you're on TikTok, you're on YouTube."* Kiriakou's own explanation was the one he gives everybody: *"this kid cut my videos up."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:03" />

The proposal was to republish [the guide to surveillance and surveillance detection](/wiki/cia-insiders-guide-to-surveillance) together with the guides to lying and lie detection and to disappearing and living off the grid, *"in one fat book,"* updated. The requested deadline was Saturday; Kiriakou's answer was *"550 pages, I can't do it by Saturday."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:03" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:34" />

[Tucker Carlson](/wiki/tucker-carlson) agreed to write the introduction and delivered it the next day. Kiriakou telephoned to ask how he had written it in a day; Carlson's answer was that it was not that long — *"I just dashed it off"* — with the instruction that the book needed to get out.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:34" /> Kiriakou's reply was that it did not need to, since the three books were all still in print and the exercise was repackaging and updating.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:06" />

## A big book year

The omnibus was one of several projects Kiriakou listed. Skyhorse also wanted the CIA Insider's Guide to the Iran Crisis updated, *"which is going to be a bigger work since we're at war"*; the cemetery book had been postponed; the Mafia book was finished and awaiting a release date; and he had begun a book of stories, on the reasoning that *"I've got to get all my stories all in one place."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:06" />

His ambition for what follows is a move upmarket. Now that he has *"a really great literary agent,"* he hopes future books will be published by one of the big five, his best relationship being with Simon & Schuster, which has done several of his others. The attraction is promotional: the large houses *"have PR budgets. And so they will send me on a book tour."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:37" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:07" />
