**Asian fuel reserves, 2026** describes the position of oil-importing Asian economies during the closure of [the Strait of Hormuz](/wiki/strait-of-hormuz), a subject [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) returned to as the least-covered consequence of the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war).

The figures raised on the programme were Myanmar, Vietnam and Singapore with about thirty days of fuel remaining, and Taiwan with about a hundred.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="54:17" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="54:47" /> Kiriakou's response was that the deadline was nearer than it sounded — *"that's going to come around sooner rather than later"* — and that the pressure was being underestimated: *"it's hard to overestimate how harsh this is."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="54:47" />

His analogy was domestic. *"Most Americans are like one medical disaster away from bankruptcy. It's the same thing with whole countries in this case."* For Singapore and Japan in particular, *"the economies will just screech to a halt."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="54:47" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:18" />

The constraint is that there is no alternative supply to buy. *"There's only so much oil available on the open market. And if it's not coming through the Strait of Hormuz, which accounts for at least 30% of the world's oil supply, then they're out of luck."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:18" />

He applied the same reasoning to the Philippines, which imports all its oil, agreeing that the war would *"push people into misery"* in a poor country and hit its light manufacturing; and to Japan, which imports all of its oil and has *"zero oil or natural gas."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="30:12" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="30:42" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="31:14" />

The price effects in the United States are at [the 2026 Iran war economic shock](/wiki/2026-iran-war-economic-shock).
