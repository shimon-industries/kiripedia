**The Russian soldier in Manama** is a brief encounter John Kiriakou recounted on the DeProgram programme in August 2025, at the close of a discussion about Chechnya prompted by a viewer's question about the disappearance of its leader from public view.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:15:38" />

## The conversation it arose in

The host had raised a claim from John Keegan's history of warfare that the Chechens are the most ferocious fighters on earth, *"completely indomitable, totally insane"*, and described the republic as a miniature North Korea inside the Russian Federation. Kiriakou's contribution was a piece of first-hand corroboration rather than an argument.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:15:38" />

## The encounter

Kiriakou's recollection of the place is not immediate. *"You know, I met a Russian soldier once. Where was I? Was it in Baghdad? Anyway, no, it wasn't in Baghdad. It was in Manama in Bahrain."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:15:38" />

The substance is a single sentence: *"And he told me that the Chechens were the only people on earth that he was afraid of."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:15:38" />

Kiriakou does not date the meeting, name the man or give his unit, and offers no assessment of his own about Chechen fighters.

## Manama

The city is one Kiriakou knows well. He was posted to the American embassy in [Bahrain](/wiki/bahrain-1994-1996-posting) in his late twenties, serving as its economic officer and its human rights officer, and has said he was twenty-nine when he arrived.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:32" />

It is the same posting that produced his account of finding [a listening device in his bedroom ceiling](/wiki/the-bug-in-the-bedroom), and of the intelligence chief [General Ian Henderson](/wiki/ian-henderson), whose name he says even the heir presumptive would not speak aloud.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="29:45" /><Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:34" />
