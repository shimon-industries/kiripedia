**The Taft joke** is the scene [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reaches for when asked what the two men at the top of the Bush administration were actually like in a room. His characterisation comes first: *"Cheney was a mean, cold, sociopathic son of a—. Nothing at all like George W. Bush. Bush was warm and friendly. Not terribly bright, but the nicest guy in the world."*<Cite s="2026-02-15-london-real-london-real" t="0:31" />

The demonstration is a briefing he attended with a colleague he describes as *"very portly"* and wearing *"kind of a handlebar mustache."* The president made a joke about the moustache, comparing him to some entertainer whose name Kiriakou no longer recalls, and the man answered with one of his own.<Cite s="2026-02-15-london-real-london-real" t="1:02" />

> *"Actually, I think I've got a William Howard Taft thing going."*

Kiriakou glosses the reference for the listener — the stories *"that William Howard Taft got stuck in a bathtub and it took six men to pull him out. It's probably not true, but this is the story in history"* — and then gives the two reactions that are the point of telling it: *"and W laughed to the point where I thought water was going to squirt out of his nose or something. And Cheney just stood there like a statue."*<Cite s="2026-02-15-london-real-london-real" t="1:34" /><Cite s="2026-02-15-london-real-london-real" t="2:04" />
