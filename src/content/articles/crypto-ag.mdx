**Crypto AG** was a Swiss manufacturer of encryption equipment which, as reported in February 2020, was covertly controlled by Western intelligence services and sold compromised ciphers to governments worldwide. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was asked about it days after the story broke.

## He did not know

Asked whether such an operation had been common knowledge inside the agency in his day, his answer was emphatic and repeated: *"no, no, no, no. This was highly classified, highly compartmentalized. You know, I was in a senior position at the CIA and I had no idea."*<Cite s="2020-02-25-cafe-weltschmerz-dutch-dutch-language-framing-confirm-john-speaks-no" t="19:29" />

## Not a scandal

Kiriakou had given an interview on the story to a Russian network the previous week, and described it as one of the funniest he had ever given, without intending it to be. The interviewer asked whether anybody would be prosecuted. *"I said no, but I think several people will probably win medals for it. This is a wildly successful operation. It's not viewed as a crime — it's viewed as something heroic and patriotic."*<Cite s="2020-02-25-cafe-weltschmerz-dutch-dutch-language-framing-confirm-john-speaks-no" t="19:29" /><Cite s="2020-02-25-cafe-weltschmerz-dutch-dutch-language-framing-confirm-john-speaks-no" t="19:59" />

Told that doing such a thing was not honest, he did not dispute the description, only its relevance: *"it's intelligence. It's an intelligence operation. It's not supposed to be honest, it's not supposed to be fair, it's not supposed to make for a level playing field. This is something that intelligence services do all around the world."*<Cite s="2020-02-25-cafe-weltschmerz-dutch-dutch-language-framing-confirm-john-speaks-no" t="19:59" />

## Where he draws the public-interest line

Pressed on whether the public should have known, Kiriakou made a distinction he applies consistently and which separates this case from the ones he has [blown the whistle on](/wiki/cia-torture-program-whistleblowing). Asked what the taxpayers' money is for, he answered that this is precisely what it is for: *"it's in the US public interest for the CIA to crack the codes of every other country in the world. Very much in the public interest."*<Cite s="2020-02-25-cafe-weltschmerz-dutch-dutch-language-framing-confirm-john-speaks-no" t="20:31" />

The contrast is with a crime, which in his account cannot lawfully be classified at all — the ground on which he defends [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange)'s publication of evidence of American war crimes in the same conversation.<Cite s="2020-02-25-cafe-weltschmerz-dutch-dutch-language-framing-confirm-john-speaks-no" t="11:06" />
