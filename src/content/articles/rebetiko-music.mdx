**Rebetiko** is the Greek musical genre that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) names, across several interviews, as one of the two things he listens to.

## The question that produced the answer

Asked in 2023 to name a piece of music that would give him memory recall if he ever ended up in *"the old whistleblowers home,"* Kiriakou replied that he did not have to think about it at all.<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="1:13:08" />

*"I listen to two kinds of music I listen to classic rock and I listen to Greek folk music that my parents and grandparents used to play when I was growing up so every morning as soon as I roll over in bed I put on 70s on 7 or a classic vinyl on Sirius XM."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="1:13:08" />

## What he plays when he is low

The second half of the answer named the genre. *"If I'm feeling particularly down I go to something called rebetiko music which is music that came out of the the opium dens of Constantinople in the 1920s and 1930s."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="1:13:43" />

## The same answer, elsewhere

He has given the description repeatedly and consistently, with small variations in dating. In one 2026 interview: *"I listen to a lot of classic rock, mostly early to mid70s classic rock. Um, I listen to folk specifically from the 30s and 40s and 50s. And more than anything else probably, I listen to Greek music."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-doug-bopst-escape-system-stop-being-manipulated" t="55:50" />

In the parallel telling he completes the sentence: a genre *"that came out of the prisons and the opium dens of Constantinople around the turn of the 20th century called Reetica. It's a Greek like blues is is the way most people describe it."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-doug-bopst-cia-whistleblower-escape-system" t="56:00" /> Elsewhere the auto-caption renders the name *Rebetika*, and he adds that mostly this is what he listens to — confirming, when pressed, that the classic rock means *"Zeppelin, the Eagles, stuff like that."*<Cite s="2026-05-26-covert-strategies-revealed-covert-strategies-revealed" t="55:08" />

The dating moves between the 1920s and 1930s and the turn of the century across his tellings; the origin — the prisons and opium dens of Constantinople — does not.
