**The Sahel church burnings** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, given on 31 October 2025 in answer to a viewer asking about Cameroon, of what he considers the graver situation next door.

Cameroon, he said, matters *"less so than Chad, Mali, and Nigeria"*, where the actors are *"al-Qaeda and its associated groups, Boko Haram. They're just slaughtering people, especially Christians."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="19:46" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="20:19" />

He describes two settled patterns. In the schools, the attackers are *"just going into schools and opening fire. They're kidnapping some of the children and opening fire on the adults, the teachers, the administrators."* In the churches, *"they'll herd people into churches and then set the churches on fire and then shoot anybody who tries to escape from the burning church."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="20:19" />

His closing observation is about the silence rather than the violence: *"it's not being covered in the American media for some reason."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="20:19" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="20:52" />

The exchange came in a run of viewer questions about Africa that led both hosts to commit the following week's programme to the region, Kiriakou having already said of [Sudan](/wiki/the-sudan-massacre) that they had been remiss.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="10:52" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="19:46" />
