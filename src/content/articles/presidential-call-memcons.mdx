**Presidential call memcons** are the internal records of telephone conversations between the president and foreign leaders. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described their handling in October 2019, when the disposition of one such record was the central fact of an impeachment inquiry.

## Who is actually on the line

Kiriakou dismissed the reported figure of twenty participants on the Trump–Zelensky call. *"Usually there are four; on rare occasions there are five people on that phone call."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="27:21" /> The standing cast is the secretary of state, the national security adviser, the chief of staff, and the [NSC](/wiki/national-security-council) expert for whatever the issue happens to be. For a counterpart of the rank of [Vladimir Putin](/wiki/vladimir-putin), the CIA director might be added.<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="27:54" />

The reason is volume rather than secrecy: *"these routine calls, they take place every day, all day. You can't have twenty people wasting their time on a call with a prime minister of XYZ country."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="27:54" />

## The document

*"These memos, they're called memcons, memoranda of conversation. They're write-ups of the call: the president said this, the prime minister said that."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="28:25" /> They are *"usually very tightly held and they do not leave the NSC."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="28:25" />

That handling rule is what made the Ukraine complaint anomalous to him. As a CIA analyst, *"we never ever had access to write-ups of the president's conversations with anybody, those were internal NSC documents, not to leave NSC."*<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="28:25" /> The fact that an analyst had read one told him the document had been passed to him by somebody at the White House, and that the underlying knowledge was second-hand, a status he treats as perfectly acceptable in a complaint, but as a fact worth establishing.<Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="2:36" /><Cite s="2019-10-03-rob-kall-bottom-up-show-discusses-ukraine-phone-call" t="28:57" />
