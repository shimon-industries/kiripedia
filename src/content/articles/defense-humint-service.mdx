**Defense HUMINT Service** is the Pentagon's human intelligence collection organisation, and [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) raises it as the load-bearing part of his argument that [the CIA](/wiki/cia) is redundant rather than simply unreformed. He introduces it with a warning about the initials — *"a human collection outfit at the Pentagon called DHS, not the Department of Homeland Security but Defense HUMINT Services"* — and rates it flatly against Langley: *"they recruit spies to steal secrets just as well as the CIA does."*<Cite s="2019-09-06-graham-elwood-uncovered-us-torture-program" t="12:58" /><Cite s="2019-09-06-graham-elwood-uncovered-us-torture-program" t="13:29" />

The argument it belongs to is one of duplication. On analysis, *"the best analysis in the intelligence community does not come from the CIA, it comes from the State Department's Bureau of Intelligence and Research."* On technical collection, *"in terms of tech issues and interception, that's what NSA does."* Which leaves the question he ends on: *"so why do you have this redundant organization at Langley, that is actually sort of in charge of the intelligence community, when they're the ones getting it wrong all the time?"*<Cite s="2019-09-06-graham-elwood-uncovered-us-torture-program" t="12:58" /><Cite s="2019-09-06-graham-elwood-uncovered-us-torture-program" t="13:29" />
