**Mike Rogers** is a former Republican congressman from Michigan who left the House, ran for the Senate and lost, and was running again in 2026.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="46:18" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="46:50" /> Kiriakou believes he chaired the House intelligence committee but flags his own uncertainty rather than asserting it: *"I believe he was the chairman of the House intelligence committee. I may be wrong on that."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="46:18" /> The 2024 race he narrowly lost was to Elissa Slotkin, whom Kiriakou identifies as *"a former CIA colleague of mine."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="40:02" /> On the [2026 primary](/wiki/michigan-2026-democratic-senate-primary), Rogers polls well against [Abdul El-Sayed](/wiki/abdul-el-sayed) and less well against Haley Stevens.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="46:50" />

## The Trojan horse advertisement

Rogers released an advertisement using generated imagery that called El-Sayed a Trojan horse — *"not for Islam but for communism."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="46:50" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="47:26" /> Kiriakou's verdict is that *"that ad counts on Michigan voters being morons,"* and his objection is definitional: he has *"a hard time believing that so many Republicans are so stupid as to not know that there's a difference between communism, socialism, and democratic socialism."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="47:26" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="48:00" /> To many Republicans, he argues, *"anything that is not Republican"* is communism — but Bernie Sanders *"is not a communist. He's a socialist, a democratic socialist, which really is capitalism light,"* meaning a robust social safety net.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="48:00" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="48:31" /> His conclusion about the advertisement's premise is unsparing: *"Mike Rogers is hoping people are stupid or reactionary."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="49:35" />
