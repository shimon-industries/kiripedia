**Kiriakou on diet and nutrition** collects the dietary positions [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) set out in a 2026 interview conducted, unusually for him, by a nutrition professional who disputed them as they were given.

Asked for the worst weight-loss advice he had ever heard, he named it twice for emphasis: *"to cut fat and cholesterol."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="00:00" /> His explanation is political rather than metabolic, protein is good and carbohydrate bad, especially refined sugar, but *"the sugar lobby in the United States is so powerful that for decades they convinced us that fat was the enemy."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="00:33" />

His interviewer pushed back on both halves, arguing that the harmful fats are specifically the saturated ones rather than fat as a category, that the response to dietary cholesterol varies between individuals, and that the real problem is overconsumption of calories, the average American, he put it, taking in 3,500 a day. Kiriakou agreed on the calories.<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="02:08" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="03:11" />

He defended the carbohydrate position on the narrower ground of his own age and circumstances: *"if you're my age, carbs are the enemy,"* because he will not get the exercise his body needs, his metabolism has slowed over the years, and the calories will be stored as fat rather than burned.<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="03:11" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="03:43" />

He also accepted correction. Describing the [tofu he had added to his diabetes diet](/wiki/kiriakou-type-2-diabetes), he added the aside that *"tofu gives you man boobs, now they're telling us"*; told this was untrue and that a recent meta-analysis found no such effect, his answer was *"Good. Thank you… Because I eat a lot of soy and I don't want to grow man boobs."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="05:20" />

When the interviewer proposed that the real driver of his post-surgical weight loss was simply a lower calorie intake rather than the operation itself, Kiriakou agreed without argument: *"Oh no, you're exactly right about that."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="11:32" />

## On chocolate

Asked on Halloween 2025 for his favourite candy, Kiriakou declined the premise: *"I don't really like candies, but I remain partial to chocolate."* Either kind will do, *"I like milk chocolate or dark chocolate the best"*, with one preference stated firmly: *"especially the heavy salted kind."* He notes that this was not available to him growing up: *"they didn't really do that in Ohio in the 1970s."* The other thing he liked as a child was Life Savers.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="07:13" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="07:46" />
