**David Hicks** is an Australian national captured by the United States in Afghanistan after the September 11 attacks and held at [Guantanamo](/wiki/guantanamo-bay). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts his case not for its own sake but as a control: it is the one instance he cites of Canberra forcing Washington's hand over a citizen in American custody, and he uses it to ask why the same effort was not made for [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange).<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="59:03" />

## The route to Kandahar

Hicks was, in Kiriakou's account, a troubled teenager who converted to Islam at eighteen and then made his way to Afghanistan to fight alongside the [Taliban](/wiki/taliban). He was recruited into al-Qaeda, but *"because he didn't speak Arabic, they just gave him a gun and told him to stand guard at the Kandahar Al-Qaeda training base."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="59:03" />

He happened to be in [Pakistan](/wiki/pakistan-station-2002) on the day of the attacks, and *"as soon as he saw the news, he ran back to Afghanistan to help Al-Qaeda fight the Americans."* He was captured almost immediately and sent to Guantanamo, *"where he was tortured mercilessly."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="59:34" />

## What Canberra did

The Australian government intervened directly, on the specific ground of the alliance: *"Whoa, wait a minute. You can't torture an Australian citizen. This is a [Five Eyes](/wiki/five-eyes) citizen. You have to let him go."* The result was a trial at Guantanamo, a conviction, a sentence of time served, and transfer into Australian custody.<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:00:05" />

## The comparison Kiriakou draws

Kiriakou does not present Hicks sympathetically. Having described the outcome he adds, flatly, *"Well, he was a genuine bad guy. What about Julian Assange? Why aren't the Australians in the streets over Julian Assange?"* He allows that the position had improved by the time of this 2023 conversation — *"it's getting better than it was 6 months ago even"* — while insisting that *"more needs to be done to impart to the White House that this is an issue worth falling on the sword for."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:00:05" /><Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:00:36" />
