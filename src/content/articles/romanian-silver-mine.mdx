The **Romanian silver mine** is the case [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) tells to explain what [private intelligence](/wiki/private-intelligence) analysis is, as distinct from private investigation — and, he says, *"a lot more fun."*

Working for himself between government jobs, he had three big clients: the ninth-largest hedge fund in the world, a mutual fund, and the largest mining company in the world, plus a contract for an oil company.<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="21:12" /><Cite s="2025-09-09-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="03:38" /> They asked very specific questions. One of them was: who is going to win the Romanian municipal elections in September?<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="21:42" />

## The method

Kiriakou's reaction was that he knew nothing about Romania. His solution was LinkedIn and a shared religion: he is [Greek Orthodox](/wiki/greek-orthodox-church) and, as he puts it, everybody in Romania is Orthodox, so he sent connection requests to everybody who looked interesting and happened to be Orthodox in Romania — about sixty people — and asked his question. One of them gave him private polling data.<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="21:42" /><Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="22:14" />

## Why they were asking

He then did what he says a good analyst does and asked the client why they cared. They told him: they were thinking of buying a silver mine, the silver lay underneath a village, and to mine it the whole village would have to be destroyed. If the socialists won they would not be allowed to do it; if the conservatives won, the villagers would simply be relocated.<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="22:14" /><Cite s="2025-09-09-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="04:38" />

Kiriakou told them the polls said the conservatives would win. They bought the mine at the pre-election price. The conservatives won, the village was moved out and destroyed, and the company took billions of dollars' worth of silver out of the ground.<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="22:45" />

He files the episode alongside the other questions clients put to him — should I do business in Yemen, should I buy that silver mine in Romania — as the everyday substance of commercial intelligence work.<Cite s="2021-03-04-consortium-news-lies-spin-propaganda-manipulation-w-suelette" t="11:07" />
