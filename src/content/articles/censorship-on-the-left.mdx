**Censorship on the left** is a proposition John Kiriakou advanced in January 2026, while defending the decision to broadcast on Rumble against viewers who objected that the platform is owned by conservatives.

## The occasion

The question put to the show was whether alternatives such as Rumble might displace the established platforms. Kiriakou thought there was a chance: a lot of people are tired of YouTube controlling what they get to see and what they get to say, and are looking for alternatives — *"That's why we're doing so well on Rumble."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="55:16" />

Rall reported being trolled for appearing there on the grounds of its ownership, and dismissed the objection.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="55:46" />

## Kiriakou's evidence

Kiriakou answered it from his own byline history rather than in principle. Having worked for conservative outlets, he said, *"I got I got censored a lot less on Forbes and for and at Fortune magazine than I ever did by The Nation and Mother Jones, who were brutal to me."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="55:46" />

Rall's generalisation followed — *"your own side tends to censor you more"* — and Kiriakou's assent was one word: *"Crazy."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="55:46" /><Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="56:18" />

## The mechanism

The explanation Rall gave, and which Kiriakou completed, is about categorisation rather than ideology. If the *Wall Street Journal* invites a leftist on, it knows what he is and does not try to squash him or turn him into a conservative. At *The Nation*, the reaction is that he is a leftist but not the right kind of leftist, and must be massaged — at which point Kiriakou supplied the missing phrase, *"You've got to be a DNC lefty,"* and Rall confirmed it twice.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="56:18" />

The exchange belongs with the show's running argument about why two self-described leftists were being paid by a right-wing video platform after being demonetised elsewhere.
