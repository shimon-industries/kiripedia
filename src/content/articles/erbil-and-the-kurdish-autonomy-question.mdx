**Erbil and the Kurdish autonomy question** is an inference [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) drew live on 28 February 2026 from a single line of wire copy.

Al Jazeera was reporting that Erbil airport in the Kurdish region of northern Iraq had been targeted twice overnight, and that air defences had intercepted and shot down a drone there.<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:12" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:43" />

## Why he thought it mattered

Kiriakou did not treat it as one more target on a long list. *"The reason that's important is because the Iraqi government has said repeatedly that it would not participate in any American attack on Iran. Obviously, it's close to the Iranian government."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:43" />

The complication is constitutional: *"The Kurds have autonomy in the north."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:43" />

From which he drew the conclusion. *"What we could be seeing here is the Kurds independently supporting US actions against Iran and allowing the US military, and by extent the Israeli military, to use their base in Erbil to attack Iran. That's an added little twist here."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:43" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:14" />

## The tension it creates

The reading sits uneasily beside Kiriakou's long-standing sympathy for [the Kurds](/wiki/kurds), whom he calls generationally pro-American and whose independence he says he supports. Here the same autonomy he would like to see made permanent is the thing that lets a war Iraq has declined to join be run from Iraqi territory.

He did not resolve the tension, and elsewhere in the same war he flatly rejected a viewer's suggestion that the CIA might be preparing the Taliban for a proxy invasion, *"Certainly not the Taliban"*, while declining to extend the same denial to the Kurds.<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="53:53" />
