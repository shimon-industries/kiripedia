The **CIA directorates** are the four internal divisions [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) uses when explaining to general audiences that there is no single job called working at the [CIA](/wiki/cia). Which one an officer enters, he says, decides what training follows.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="03:09" />

The Directorate of Intelligence is the analytical arm. The Directorate of Operations is the side *"out there actually recruiting spies to steal secrets."* Administration is the third — *"they're the ones that make the trains run on time, the accountants, the logistics people."* The fourth is the Directorate of Science and Technology, which he glosses as *"the scientists, the the forgers, the disguise makers."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="03:09" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="03:39" />

His own path ran across the line. He started in the Directorate of Intelligence and went through all of its writing training, then after seven and a half years made what was at the time a very unusual move to counterterrorism operations.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="03:39" /> Because half his career was already behind him, he was spared the introductory syllabus — *"what we used to call CIA 101"* — and went directly into defensive driving, then counterterrorist driving, then the accelerated version of the operations course that teaches the step-by-step of recruiting spies and stealing secrets.<Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="04:11" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-the-sharpe-exchange-reveals-the-dark-reality-of-intel-ops" t="04:43" />
