**The Piers Morgan panel** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of a recurring television appearance he uses both to explain why he changed his mind about [Piers Morgan](/wiki/piers-morgan) and to recount two of his sharper on-air exchanges.

He had loathed Morgan, *"I really did"*, until Morgan's producers began pursuing him for the show. He went on, and *"he treated me really well."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:22" /> He has since appeared repeatedly, including on the JFK files and, in December 2025, on *Piers Morgan Uncensored*.<Cite s="2025-03-26-truthovercomfort-truthovercomfort" t="46:41" /><Cite s="2025-12-22-piers-morgan-uncensored-piers-morgan-uncensored" t="35:19" />

## The panel

The appearance that matters put him alongside [Scott Horton](/wiki/scott-horton), the libertarian, he specifies, *"not the Atlantic Monthly journalist Scott Horton, although he's okay, too"*, against [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz) and a senior former Israeli official. *"There were four of us, Scott Horton and I on the one side, and the other side was Alan Dershowitz and"* the Israeli.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:53" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:28" />

Kiriakou does not identify the fourth panellist consistently. He calls him General Danny Ayalon and identifies him as being from [Mossad](/wiki/mossad); elsewhere simply *"the former head of Mossad"*; and once he corrects himself mid-sentence, *"it was Danny, was it Danny Ayalon? He was the head of Mossad… Danny Yat. Thank you"*, settling on a different name.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:53" /><Cite s="2025-07-24-deepstatebear-everybody-hates-israel" t="38:27" /><Cite s="2025-09-25-joe-mkhitaryan" t="1:19:50" />

The panel was hostile, *"it was harsh, man. And they were at each other's throats"*, and Kiriakou spent much of it watching.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:53" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:24" /> He says the format works against him: the show assumes guests will interrupt, and Morgan will break it up if it gets out of hand, *"but it's not really in my nature to interrupt. I have to force myself to do it on that show."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:18" />

## Hamas, October 7th and Gaza

Morgan eventually turned to him and asked whether he considered [Hamas](/wiki/hamas) a terrorist organisation. Kiriakou's answer distinguished periods: *"I didn't, until October 7th, when they attacked civilians. I believed that they were a liberation organization. But when they murdered civilians, yes."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:24" /> Asked whether [October 7th](/wiki/october-7-attack) was a terrorist attack, his answer was *"in part, yes."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:24" />

Asked then for his position on Gaza, he gave it without qualification: *"the Israelis are wrong and they need to get out of Gaza immediately."* The reasoning he offered was a rule about policy rather than a judgement about a party: *"you can't have as your policy kill everybody. That's the definition of genocide. And if anybody should know genocide, it should be the Israelis."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:56" />

## Epstein, Dershowitz and "who knows?"

The other exchange Kiriakou retells came when he said on air that he believed [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was an Israeli spy, an *"access agent,"* in the version he prefers, *"collecting information from American politicians."*<Cite s="2026-01-21-liberty-vault-john-kiriakou-is-certain-that-epstein-was-wor" t="07:49" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:06:37" />

Dershowitz, who had been Epstein's attorney, interrupted *"like attorneys do"*: *"that is outrageous. If he had been a spy, he would have told me, because I was his attorney and I could have gone to the White House and I could have gotten him a better sentence."* Kiriakou treats the objection as self-refuting: *"wait a minute. You could have gone to the White House to say, go easy on Jeffrey Epstein because he's an Israeli spy collecting information from American politicians? If I were the president, I would have hung him from a tree."*<Cite s="2026-01-21-liberty-vault-john-kiriakou-is-certain-that-epstein-was-wor" t="08:19" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:26:30" />

Morgan then put the question to the Israeli directly, *"was he a spy?"*, and got a laugh and *"who knows?"*<Cite s="2026-01-21-liberty-vault-john-kiriakou-is-certain-that-epstein-was-wor" t="08:19" /><Cite s="2026-01-19-diary-of-a-ceo-they-can-see-all-your-messages" t="1:26:30" /> Kiriakou reads that non-answer as the tell of the evening: the man *"was kind of into it in terms of having a fun time… he wasn't going to admit to anything,"* and when the claim that Israel had not spied on the United States since [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) was raised, Kiriakou answered that Israeli spies were exposed in Washington in 1991, *"and he just goes like, okay. I busted him. He doesn't care. It's all a big joke."*<Cite s="2026-01-21-liberty-vault-john-kiriakou-is-certain-that-epstein-was-wor" t="07:49" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:07:07" /><Cite s="2025-07-24-deepstatebear-everybody-hates-israel" t="38:27" /> Dershowitz, by contrast, *"kept interrupting and just coming in and hitting me over and over."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:07:07" /> Kiriakou's own assessment of the round is modest: *"I think I rattled him just a little bit."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:06:37" />

## Morgan's reversal

What Kiriakou reports next is the part he says heartened him. Morgan replied: *"I've finally come around on this issue and I have to agree with you"*, from a man who had been *"Joe Zionist from the very beginning."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:56" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="37:28" />

He accepted his co-host's qualification that one would have hoped for such moral clarity on 8 October 2023 rather than years later, and added the obvious rider: *"better late than never, seriously."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="37:28" />
