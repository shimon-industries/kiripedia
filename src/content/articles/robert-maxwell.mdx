**Robert Maxwell** was a British media proprietor and the father of [Ghislaine Maxwell](/wiki/ghislaine-maxwell). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s position is stated flatly: *"Ghislaine Maxwell's father was a prolific Mossad spy. That's a historic fact."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="53:22" /> Across retellings his term for Maxwell varies — spy, *"a known Mossad agent,"* *"a very well-known Mossad asset"* — but the claim of a long relationship with the Israeli service does not.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:48:16" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:57:14" /><Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="1:02:00" />

## "Kept it all in the family"

Kiriakou cites Maxwell's [MOSSAD](/wiki/mossad) connections as the probable origin of [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein)'s relationship with Israeli intelligence: *"It stands to reason that she would have introduced him to the Mossad or even her father would have introduced Epstein to Mossad."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower" t="53:22" />

The reasoning runs through what Kiriakou takes Epstein to have been. A service that wants information from figures it can never recruit directly recruits instead someone with access to all of them, and Epstein, in his assessment, *"bore all the hallmarks of an Israeli [access operation](/wiki/access-agent)."* It was the daughter's role that closed the circuit: *"his relationship with Ghislaine Maxwell, whose father was also a Mossad agent — a known Mossad agent — leads me to believe that they kept it all in the family."*<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:47:45" /><Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:48:16" /> Epstein's access improved as his wealth grew, and Kiriakou notes that the source of that wealth remains unclear — it could not all have come from Les Wexner.<Cite s="2023-11-19-dalton-fischer-kiriakou-part-2" t="2:48:16" />

Asked in 2019 to weigh the same connection, Kiriakou's answer was that *"there's so much smoke here that there has to be some fire."*<Cite s="2019-09-05-graham-elwood-graham-elwood" t="14:10" /> By 2025 he was putting it as settled background — *"we know all about Ghislaine Maxwell's father, having had this long relationship with Mossad."*<Cite s="2025-07-09-pbd-podcast-epstein-ghislaine-maxwell-israel" t="1:02:00" />

## The Kennedy flight log

Kiriakou uses the family's social reach to explain one name in the Epstein flight logs. [Robert F. Kennedy Jr.](/wiki/robert-f-kennedy-jr), he says, appears on at least one released log and has dealt with it head-on: Kennedy's wife at the time was a friend of Ghislaine Maxwell, mentioned that the family was flying to Palm Beach for Easter, and was offered a lift — so the whole Kennedy family flew down on Epstein's aircraft. Kiriakou calls the episode innocent, and offers it as a measure of the reach the operation had.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:56:42" /><Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="2:57:14" />
