**"Kill them all"** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s canonical scene for the mood inside CIA headquarters in the weeks after 9/11, a whispered instruction from his own boss during the farewell hug on the day before Kiriakou left for [Pakistan](/wiki/pakistan) to run counterterrorism operations there.

## The setting

Kiriakou had volunteered *"like everybody in the building"* to go somewhere and do something after 9/11. The leadership finally accepted: chief of counterterrorism operations in Pakistan, leaving tomorrow. He decided to stop at headquarters to say goodbye before flying out. His frame at the time was practical: *"not everybody's going to make it home, and I don't know what's going to happen out there."*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="06:48" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="07:21" />

## The hug

The boss he went to say goodbye to was, Kiriakou says, *"a very elegant, very bright guy. Always with the thousand-dollar suits. We always joked that he really belonged in the State Department. He would have been sort of the stereotypical ambassador."* His name was John, the same as Kiriakou's.<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="07:21" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="07:52" />

*"He gave me a hug and wished me best of luck. And when he leaned in, he whispered to me, 'Kill them all.'"*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="07:52" />

Kiriakou's answer: *"Really, John? We're already there."* His boss backed up, took him by the shoulders, and said it again: *"Kill them all."*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="07:52" />

## The reading

Kiriakou treats the exchange as compression rather than aberration. *"That was typical of the leadership at the CIA. Like, well, we can't bring back these 3,000 people, but we sure can kill a lot of the bad guys. And that's what they set out to do."*<Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="07:52" /><Cite s="2026-08-02-al-jazeera-english-cia-whistleblower-on-accountability-and-cia-s-history" t="08:23" />
