**Israeli non-extradition of Jews** is a rule [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes as absolute: Israel will not extradite a Jewish fugitive to another country, whatever the offence. *"You can be a mass murderer, a serial killer. You can be a pedophile. You can be the most notorious pedophile in the world. They're not extraditing you because they don't extradite Jews."*<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="53:10" />

## The Philadelphia attorney

Kiriakou illustrates the rule with a case he encountered through the federal prison system. A prominent Philadelphia attorney — Harvard Law School, lead mergers-and-acquisitions lawyer at an American firm in Moscow — molested a boy from the age of twelve to fifteen. When the boy filed a civil suit against him, he fled to Israel.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="52:37" /><Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="53:10" />

## The Cyprus ballet sting

The man was a great lover of the ballet, and the boy he had abused was a ballet prodigy in Moscow. Interpol took out advertisements in Israeli newspapers announcing a one-night-only performance in [Cyprus](/wiki/cyprus-green-line) featuring the greatest child ballerinas from Russia. Cyprus is roughly forty minutes' flight from Israel. He boarded a plane to see a ballet that did not exist, was seized on arrival, extradited to the United States, and sentenced to twenty years. Kiriakou calls the operation *"brilliant."*<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="53:44" /><Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="54:15" />

## The Epstein question

Kiriakou uses the rule to pose a question about [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein). Epstein owned multiple private jets and had billions hidden around the world; if the refuge was available, *"why in the world would I not flee to Israel?"* The only explanation Kiriakou can construct is that Epstein believed he was safe — that he held so much material on so many important people that nobody would dare move against him. *"And then they did."*<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="54:48" />

## Context: where sex offenders are held

In the same account Kiriakou notes that almost all sex offenders in the federal system are held in low-security prisons: maximum and medium institutions are too dangerous for them, and from minimum-security camps they would simply walk away.<Cite s="2026-03-24-michael-franzese-iran-israel-epstein-are-all-connected" t="52:37" />
