**South Lebanon displacement, 2026** refers to the mass internal displacement of Lebanese civilians during the Israeli campaign in the south that ran alongside the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war).

The conditions were put to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) in an interview: bombing that never stopped after the ceasefire, hundreds of civilian deaths, the use of white phosphorus with its long-term injuries and destruction of farmland, and an Israeli directive that fifty-three villages and towns in the south be evacuated — after which some 300,000 people left for Beirut and elsewhere, living in tents, in their cars, or with family.<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="00:00" /><Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="00:32" />

Asked how realistic the [Greater Israel](/wiki/greater-israel) project was as far as Lebanon was concerned, Kiriakou answered in terms of statehood rather than territory: *"I think that the Israelis have guaranteed that Lebanon will be a failed state. I don't think any Israeli government is going to allow Lebanon to flourish as a nation."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="01:07" />

## The scale

He expected the campaign to continue — *"Israeli attacks, Israeli forced displacement of civilians"* — and cited a statistic he had read the previous day: *"by the end of the week, as many as 700,000 Lebanese will be internally displaced."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="01:38" />

His framing of why it drew so little attention is the war next door: *"This is a humanitarian disaster and the world, most of the world isn't focused on it because they're all watching what's unfolding in Iran."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="01:38" /> The baseline made it worse — Lebanon already lacked *"enough food, enough medical care, enough clean water"* — and his forecast was that *"it's only going to get worse."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="01:38" /><Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="02:14" />

In a separate appearance he put the figure at almost a million internally displaced with nowhere to go, *"because they're surrounded by Syria on two sides, Israel on one, and the Mediterranean Sea on the fourth."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:23" />
