**Joe Rogan** hosts the biggest podcast in the world — and in October 2025, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) finally sat across from him.

## The taping

Kiriakou flew to Texas for the October 9 taping, going on from one to four with release expected the next day.<Cite s="2025-10-09-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:24" /> The protocol surprised him: *"You have to sign a non-disclosure agreement before you go on the show. So I couldn't bring anybody with me."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-Jl-e9y" t="01:33" /> Security was serious — the studio sits near where Alex Jones broadcasts, and one of Jones's reporters had been murdered a few blocks away that June.<Cite s="2025-10-10-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:32" />

He was driven to the airport early that morning by [Jeff Fahey](/wiki/jeff-fahey), the star of the film Kiriakou appeared in, and by Tyrel Ventura, Jesse Ventura's son.<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="05:17" />

Rogan arrived about ten minutes late, blaming traffic, and they went straight in. Kiriakou's read: *"nice guy, very down to earth, very normal, friendly, average."*<Cite s="2025-10-10-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-Jl-e9y" t="03:07" /> The very first thing Rogan asked as he walked into the studio: *"Hey, do you know my friend Mike Baker?"* — the ex-CIA regular whose firing story Kiriakou's own audience had begged him to tell Rogan.<Cite s="2025-10-10-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-Jl-e9y" t="14:40" />

The question survived into the recording itself, which opens on it. Episode #2392 was released the following day and runs two and a half hours; its first exchange is Rogan putting to Kiriakou that he had replaced Baker, and Kiriakou answering that Baker is a great guy who *"was the real deal"* and does not talk about his work much, and that he had replaced him in [Athens](/wiki/mike-baker) after Baker had done a great deal of preliminary legwork there.<Cite s="2025-10-10-joe-rogan-experience-2392" t="00:01" />

One exchange during the taping had an immediate sequel. Kiriakou complained that ChatGPT had lied about him, inventing a University of Maryland degree and a graduate school in Belgium he had never heard of, and that when he told the model it was wrong it told him he was wrong; Rogan replied that the studio uses Perplexity, which advertises on the show but is also what they use themselves. Kiriakou said he would switch — and at around ten the following night the chief executive of Perplexity emailed him a lifetime subscription.<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="20:54" /><Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="20:57" />

## Kiriakou on Rogan

Kiriakou pushes back on the standard framing: Rogan is *"unfairly characterized as a right-winger."*<Cite s="2025-08-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="4:20:23" /> He doesn't think Rogan is compromised or a bad person — and notes he treats working comics at his club unusually well.<Cite s="2026-04-06-due-dissidence-and-liberty-trump-s-unhinged-threat-kiriakou-spills-tea-o" t="3:06:34" /> Long before appearing, Kiriakou used the show as a reference point: it was to Rogan that Mark Zuckerberg admitted Facebook suppressed the Hunter Biden laptop story before the election at the FBI's behest.<Cite s="2022-09-20-the-dive-in-with-rattan-the-dive-in-with-rattan" t="1:30:16" />

## Who gets on the show

Asked by a listener whether he would ever appear on the show alongside [Mike Baker](/wiki/mike-baker), Kiriakou said probably not, and gave a reason that was not personal animus: he now has a manager who represents high-level people and who tells him he is more sought after than Baker is, so there is no reason to legitimize him.<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:53" /><Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:24" /> On another figure included in the same question he was categorical about Rogan's side of it: the show will not have him on, *"period. It will never happen,"* Rogan and his people having been crystal clear that the man is not taken seriously.<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="33:28" />

Kiriakou places the appearance in a sequence rather than treating it as a summit. He did [Patrick Bet-David](/wiki/patrick-bet-david), then Rogan, then *Diary of a CEO*, and describes each show as building on the ones before it, carrying him to audiences that had never heard of him — in particular young people who were too young to remember the events he was convicted over.<Cite s="2026-02-19-american-conservative-cia-propaganda-going-viral" t="02:37" />
