**Piers Morgan** is the British broadcaster whose panel show became a recurring — and combative — venue for [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) in 2025.

## The Dershowitz smackdowns

Kiriakou describes the marquee bout as *"the equivalent of a WWE Smackdown with [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz)"* — four panelists, with Kiriakou and Scott Horton (*"absolutely brilliant... I wish my brain could work"* like his) on one side, Dershowitz and former Mossad chief Moshe Yaalon on the other.<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="56:11" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:28" />

The flashpoint was Israeli espionage. When Kiriakou said the Israelis spy on the United States, Yaalon denied it; Morgan pressed, and the general answered that the spying stopped when [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) got caught and the prime minister forbade it. Kiriakou's reply: *"General, with all due respect, that's just simply not true. I joined the CIA in 1990..."*<Cite s="2025-07-11-useful-idiots-halper-mat-they-destroyed-epstein-files" t="35:28" /><Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="11:59" /> In another exchange, Morgan asked whether Israel has nuclear weapons — the general only smiled.<Cite s="2025-06-01-austin-and-matt-11-inside-the-cia-whistleblowers-war-crimes-and-blind" t="32:01" /> And when Kiriakou said he believed [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) was an Israeli [access agent](/wiki/access-agent), the Mossad chief chuckled — a reaction Kiriakou reads as telling.<Cite s="2025-07-25-gold-shields" t="1:06:37" /> He got into *"a little tiff"* with Dershowitz, who he believes lashed out from anger.<Cite s="2025-08-01-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="10:36" />

Describing a later appearance alongside Dershowitz and a retired Israeli general he names as Danny Ayalon, Kiriakou recalls arguing that Israel is *"prolific in their espionage work in the United States, especially as it relates to American defense contractors,"* until Morgan cut Dershowitz off and put the question to the general directly. The answer, Kiriakou says, was a laugh and a formula: *"My position is whatever the prime minister's position is."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="21:16" /> Morgan then asked whether Israel has nuclear weapons and received the same construction — the prime minister has not confirmed it, so the general would go with what the prime minister says. Kiriakou treats the repetition as its own answer on both counts.<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="21:46" /> *(The transcript's auto-captions render the name as "I alone"; the identification of the general as Mossad is Kiriakou's own.)*

Kiriakou also used Morgan's platform (and Fox News) to say he believes Epstein material was probably destroyed — noting that despite the Federal Records Act, the CIA destroys documents all the time.<Cite s="2025-07-15-useful-idiots-useful-idiots" t="01:01" /><Cite s="2025-07-15-useful-idiots-useful-idiots" t="08:52" /> A separate appearance covered the JFK files with Colonel Douglas Macgregor.<Cite s="2025-03-26-truthovercomfort-truthovercomfort" t="46:41" />

## Interrupting on command

Asked how one makes a point while being constantly cut off, Kiriakou admitted the show's format runs against his nature: his mother raised him not to interrupt. On Piers Morgan that has to go out the window — the producer explicitly told him not to be afraid to interrupt, and that Morgan would break it up if it got to be too much. *"I have to force myself to do it on that show."*<Cite s="2025-08-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:41" /><Cite s="2025-08-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:14" />

## The panel that agreed

Kiriakou has recounted an appearance in which Morgan pressed him on his premises and found no disagreement anywhere on the panel. Asked whether he believed [Hamas](/wiki/hamas) is a terrorist organisation, he answered *"of course"*; asked whether [October 7](/wiki/october-7-attack) was a terrorist attack, *"of course it was."* Asked then what point he was actually holding to, he said: *"The point I'm holding to is that Israel has to respect human rights. It really is as simple as that."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="12:22" /><Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="12:54" />

Morgan asked whether anybody disagreed. Everybody on the panel agreed with Kiriakou, and Morgan observed that it was the first time the programme had ever talked about Israel — an observation all four panellists accepted. Kiriakou's comment: *"What's so hard about that?"*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="12:54" />

He adds a detail about his own booking: it occurred to him only twenty minutes into the show that Morgan had put him on as one of the right-wing guests.<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="12:22" />

## The IDF spokesman

On a separate appearance Kiriakou responded to an IDF spokesman who said he did not respect Kiriakou's line of inquiry. Kiriakou — trying, he says, to make it a funny line — told him: *"Sir, you're the spokesman for a terrorist organization… the biggest terrorist organization in a region known for terrorist organizations."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="15:28" />

The framing behind it is one he attributes to Noam Chomsky: that fundamentally the difference between terrorism and counterterrorism is that *"it's terrorism when they do it and it's counterterrorism when we do it."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-covert-strategies-revealed-corpus-has-osxgmj89fwq-from-this-show" t="14:56" />

## The panel on Israeli espionage

Kiriakou has described a panel Morgan hosted in which he and [Scott Horton](/wiki/scott-horton) took one side and [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz) and a former head of Israeli intelligence — rendered in the auto-captions as "General Danny Ayalam" — took the other.<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="22:58" />

Kiriakou stated on air that the Israelis actively spy on the United States. Morgan put it to the general, who denied it. Asked about [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard), the general said that *"when Pollard got caught, that's when the prime minister ordered us to stop spying on the United States"* — which Kiriakou dates to 1985.<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="23:33" />

Kiriakou's answer was a document: *"that's funny, because in 1990 I got a briefing from the director of security at the CIA saying that Israel was one of the most active countries engaged in espionage against the United States in the whole world."* Morgan turned back to the general, *"and he just goes: no comment."*<Cite s="2025-08-30-jamarl-thomas-what-s-really-happening-in-israel-mossad" t="24:03" />

## Coming around

Asked for his opinion of Morgan in 2026, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) said he had changed it: *"I have to say that I've kind of come around on Piers Morgan. I loathed Piers Morgan. I really did."* What changed it was being pursued by Morgan's producers, going on, and being treated well.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:22" />

The appearance he singles out — a panel with [Alan Dershowitz](/wiki/alan-dershowitz), [Scott Horton](/wiki/scott-horton) and an Israeli general — ended with Morgan telling him he had finally come around on Gaza and agreed with him, which Kiriakou found heartening from a man who had been *"Joe Zionist from the very beginning."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:56" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="37:28" /> The episode is treated at [the Piers Morgan panel](/wiki/the-piers-morgan-panel).
