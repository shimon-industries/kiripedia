[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says Mark Zuckerberg was one of the first tech figures to fly to Germany and meet then-Chancellor Angela Merkel, *"either right before or after [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) was elected,"* to discuss hate speech and *"to create a domain of hate speech"* on Facebook. He notes that the episode is still findable online.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="46:08" /><Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="46:42" />

## The objection

Kiriakou's quarrel is not with the existence of limits. Offered the examples of child pornography and threats to someone's life, he agrees at once — *"I would oppose that, of course."* His objection is to who draws the line and how far it extends: *"otherwise it shouldn't be up to Mark Zuckerberg to decide what my position on Israel should be."*<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="46:08" /> In a separate conversation he raises [OnlyFans](/wiki/only-fans)' ownership as part of a broader claim about Israeli influence in online platforms.

He has been on the receiving end of the machinery. Suspended from Facebook — *"listen to how stupid this is"* — for replying *"crazy Brazilians"* to a zipline video sent to him by a Brazilian diplomat he was dating, he was told the offence was hate speech against Brazilians. He treats being *"thrown out of everything for a couple of weeks"* as an ordinary hazard of holding opinions on such platforms, and as an argument for organising offline: *"we have to speak and march and write and take to the streets when that's necessary."*<Cite s="2026-07-10-the-untold-stories-of-john-kiriakou" t="1:06:35" /><Cite s="2026-07-10-the-untold-stories-of-john-kiriakou" t="1:07:07" />

## Why he does not read Facebook as a private actor

The reason Kiriakou treats a corporate speech policy as a public matter is that he does not regard the company as independent of the government. He notes that Facebook *"launched the day LifeLog was shut down"* — the DARPA programme *"built to track every email, every photo, every location, every purchase"* — and that Facebook *"was incubated with the help of [In-Q-Tel](/wiki/in-q-tel) money."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="33:28" /><Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="50:37" /> Pressed on how much of a coincidence the timing is, his answer runs through capability rather than motive: since [Thomas Drake](/wiki/thomas-drake) went public, it has been known that the NSA intercepts *"every phone call, every text message, every email"* — so *"why not go farther than that and intercept everything else? If DARPA was doing it…"*<Cite s="2026-06-07-jack-neel-epstein-mossad-mi6-cia" t="51:10" />

He makes the same point about staffing: Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn *"and everywhere else"* are *"replete with former CIA officers who took the pension and then cashed in to make the big bucks with these tech firms"* — and *"so many of them, when they leave the CIA, don't really leave the CIA."* He adds that he is *"far closer to [Musk](/wiki/elon-musk) than to anybody else like Zuckerberg on issues of free speech."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="31:15" />

The clearest case of the government reaching through the company, in his telling, is Zuckerberg's own admission on [Joe Rogan](/wiki/joe-rogan)'s programme that Facebook suppressed the Hunter Biden laptop story before the election *"at the behest of the FBI"* — which Kiriakou considers worse than plain censorship, because it works *"to discredit it and make it vanish into the ether so that it just doesn't have a chance to percolate."*<Cite s="2022-09-20-the-dive-in-with-rattan-the-dive-in-with-rattan" t="1:30:16" />

He also uses Zuckerberg's congressional testimony as a measure of proportion: the Russians, Zuckerberg told Congress, spent one hundred thousand dollars on Facebook advertising — against the billion dollars apiece spent by the 2024 presidential campaigns and billions more by super PACs.<Cite s="2024-09-29-reason2resist-cia-co-opted-journalists" t="10:24" />
