**The Alaska glacier** is an image John Kiriakou returns to when the subject is climate change, drawn from a 2016 trip he made to Alaska with [Gary Johnson](/wiki/gary-johnson) during that year's presidential campaign. He offered it in August 2025 in a conversation about Paris preparing for days of fifty degrees Celsius.

## The visit

The party went to see a glacier. Visitors go to the centre first, are given a short briefing, and then find that the glacier is about a quarter of a mile away.<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:08" />

The detail that makes the story is the siting of the building. The glacier used to come right up to the visitor centre — that, in Kiriakou's account, is why the centre was put there. You could reach out and touch the ice from the observation deck.<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:08" />

## The joke

Somebody in the group asked why the planners would have put the visitor centre so far away from the glacier, and laughed.<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:08" /> Kiriakou's response is the point of the anecdote: *"Haha. Well, no, that's not that's not funny. That's not funny at all. This is exactly what we should be talking about."*<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:41" />

## The wider conversation

The story came at the end of an exchange about European heat. Rall observed that Paris — a low-prestige posting in Roman times because the weather was poor — had never had weather like the 122 degrees Fahrenheit now being planned for, and that Europe was at least preparing in a way the United States is not.<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:02:07" /> Kiriakou agreed, and noted with some interest that Tulsi Gabbard had come out saying the country faced a climate disaster, wondering aloud whether she had been the one chosen by the administration to begin bringing conservatives around.<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:02:38" />

He and Rall then pursued the same puzzle from the other direction — when, exactly, conservatives began caring about diet and health, which both of them had always taken for *"a crunchy lefty thing."*<Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:02:38" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:08" />
