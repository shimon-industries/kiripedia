**There are denials and then there are denials** is a rule of interpretation [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) applies to government statements, set out in October 2025 when Islamabad rejected reports that [Pakistani troops might be sent to Gaza](/wiki/pakistani-troops-for-gaza).

The distinction is between a denial that closes a question and one that does not. *"There are denials, and then there are denials. And this is not a denial denial."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="17:12" />

What Islamabad had issued was the second kind: *"we have no such plans — which of course means you could be working on it, indeed have plans, but you can still credibly say, until you've pulled the trigger and the prime minister has signed off, it's not really a plan. It's just something we're discussing."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="17:12" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="17:42" />

Kiriakou agreed the reading was correct and added a caution against over-reading the reports in the other direction: *"of course, Pakistan's probably the most rumour-tastic country in the world."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="17:42" />
