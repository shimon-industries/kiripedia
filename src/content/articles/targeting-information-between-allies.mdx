**Targeting information between allies** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s framing of the leak, during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war), that Russia had been giving Iran targeting information. His reaction on reading it was that it was expected: *"well of course they're giving the Iranians targeting information. They're allies."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="08:18" />

He places it in a series of equivalents, the United States giving Ukraine targeting information, Russia giving it to the North Vietnamese, and the United States giving it to the Afghan mujahideen along with [Stinger missiles](/wiki/stinger-missiles), and closes the list with the general rule: *"This is what enemies do to one another."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="08:18" />

The practical conclusion he draws is diplomatic rather than military. Asked how to stop it, his answer was to *"reach out directly to the Russians and say please stop. Help us negotiate some kind of a ceasefire."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="08:18" /> That is consistent with the reporting he was asked about the same day, that [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) had approached [Vladimir Putin](/wiki/vladimir-putin) to broker one.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="07:46" />
