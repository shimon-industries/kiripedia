**The CIA one-year leave** is the administrative fact about how [CIA](/wiki/cia) departures actually work — a twelve-month leave without pay during which the outgoing officer's security clearance stays live. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) uses it to answer the *"once CIA, always CIA"* line he says he gets from *"a lot of mental midgets out there, mostly on social media"*.

## How it works

*"When you resign from the CIA, you don't really resign. You take a one-year leave without pay. And that way your security clearance remains intact. So if during that one year there's an operation for which you are uniquely qualified or there's a source who will only speak to you — which believe me happens — that gives the CIA the right to pull you back in for that one year. If you don't get pulled in for that one year then your resignation becomes effective and you're no longer a CIA employee."*<Cite s="2026-09-05-john-kiriakou-would-i-run-for-president" t="10:02" /><Cite s="2026-09-05-john-kiriakou-would-i-run-for-president" t="10:33" />

## The badge-shredding ceremony

*"They make a very big deal about shredding your badge on the one-year anniversary of your request to resign."*<Cite s="2026-09-05-john-kiriakou-would-i-run-for-president" t="10:33" />

## The names Kiriakou pushes back on

He treats the *"once CIA, always CIA"* line as a factual mistake, not just a rhetorical one. *"There are a lot of dumb dumbs out there who apparently think that [Ed Snowden](/wiki/edward-snowden) is still in the CIA, that [Ray McGovern](/wiki/ray-mcgovern) is still in the CIA, [Larry Johnson](/wiki/larry-johnson) is still in the CIA — I somehow gave up my freedom, my pension, my career, my marriage, my children because I'm somehow still in the CIA. So that is a huge misconception, but just among the dum dums who are out there on the internet."*<Cite s="2026-09-05-john-kiriakou-would-i-run-for-president" t="10:33" /><Cite s="2026-09-05-john-kiriakou-would-i-run-for-president" t="11:06" />
