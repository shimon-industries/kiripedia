**The Libyan constitution unveiling** is an episode [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) offers as the clearest picture he has of how the United States handles a country after the government falls.

*"When the US and NATO overthrew [Muammar Gaddafi](/wiki/muammar-gaddafi) during the Obama administration, I happened to be invited to the unveiling of the new Libyan Constitution. It was unveiled at a lobbying firm here in Washington DC."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="23:21" />

## Who wrote it

*"So a group of wealthy Libyan exiles paid this lobbying firm and said: write us a new constitution."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="23:21" />

Kiriakou's assessment of the text is not sarcastic: *"this constitution was better than ours. It called for equal rights for everybody — women, gays, LGBT, whatever. Everybody was equal. They're going to share the oil money. Incredible constitution."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="23:54" />

## What happened to it

*"Did anybody ever do anything with it? Of course not — because nobody ever heard of these people in Washington DC, and nobody even wanted to read their silly constitution. Instead, we've had a period of what, 15, 16 years of fighting and civil war and chaos and death."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="23:54" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="24:24" />

The lesson he draws is general: *"the United States might think that it's the good guy all the time. The United States might think that it can impose a peace on a country like Venezuela. And it never works out that way. It never works out the way the policy makers think it's going to work out."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="24:24" />
