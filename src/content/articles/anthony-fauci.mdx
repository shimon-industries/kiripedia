**Anthony Fauci** is an American physician and public-health official. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) discussed him at length only once in the available corpus — a short Fox News segment on 14 May 2026, following testimony by an unnamed government witness — and the discussion turned entirely on the pardon Fauci received rather than on the conduct alleged against him.

## "You liar"

Kiriakou's objection is stated as a family one before it is stated as a political one. *"My cousin George was the second person in the state of Ohio to die of COVID back in very early 2020,"* he said. *"That death still stings all of us in our family."*<Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="01:01" /> He had recorded the same loss six years earlier, in the first weeks of the pandemic, naming a friend killed by the disease alongside his cousin.<Cite s="2020-05-15-choo-radio-network" t="06:46" />

From that he drew the personal verdict that gave the segment its title: *"I can't help but look at Doctor Fauci and think, you liar, look what you've done to our country."*<Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="01:01" />

## The pardon

Kiriakou's substantive complaint is procedural — that the pardon foreclosed any accounting. Fauci, he said, *"was auto-penned right into freedom and wealth and untold riches in his retirement. That is unacceptable."*<Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="01:01" />

He set that outcome directly against the position of the witness whose testimony prompted the segment. That man, Kiriakou said, had done *"a very brave thing"* and *"put his reputation on the line"*, and *"will probably never work in government again because of it"* — the asymmetry Kiriakou has described across many other cases of [whistleblower prosecution](/wiki/chilling-effect-of-whistleblower-prosecutions).<Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="00:31" /> His summary of the affair was a maxim rather than an allegation: *"At the heart of this story is that it's always best to tell the truth. The truth always comes out."*<Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="00:00" />

## What Kiriakou did not say

The host's framing of the segment included the claims that the virus had been *"cooked up in a lab"* and that Fauci *"may have killed 1 million Americans."* Kiriakou adopted neither. He offered no account of the origin of the virus, and no estimate of a death toll attributable to Fauci; his own assertions were limited to his family's loss, to the bravery of the witness, and to the pardon.<Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="00:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-14-fox-news-you-liar-former-cia-official-calls-fauci-s-pa" t="01:01" />
