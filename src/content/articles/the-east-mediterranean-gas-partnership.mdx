**The east Mediterranean gas partnership** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s explanation, given during the 2026 Iran war, for a divergence he treats as otherwise inexplicable: that the Greek public and the Greek government hold opposite positions on Israel.

Asked by a viewer whether Greek–Turkish tension would grow beyond its existing level, he said it would, and began with Turkey's standing in the war: *"The Turks are probably the most militarily powerful country standing in opposition to Israel. And the Greek government couldn't get any more pro-Israel."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="16:09" />

## The divergence

Kiriakou treats the split as one of the odder facts about his ancestral country. *"The funny thing in Greece the the Greek people are solidly anti-Israel because they're pro-Palestinian. But the Greek government is solidly pro-Israel because Cyprus is sitting on an ocean of natural gas."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="16:09" /><Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="16:43" />

The mechanism is a shared commercial interest rather than a shared politics: *"the Greeks, the Cypriots, the Israelis, to a lesser extent the Egyptians, are lifting the gas together and sharing the money from it."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="16:43" />

## Who is left out

The consequence he draws is about the country not in the consortium. The arrangement is *"driving the Turks crazy, but it's made the situation where as bad as things are between Greece and Turkey, they're sort of on track to get worse."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="16:43" /> He added that the war in Iran *"doesn't help anything at all."*<Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="17:13" />

The reading joins two of his standing positions: that Greek governments align with Israel for reasons the Greek electorate does not share, and that [Turkey and Israel](/wiki/turkey-israel-confrontation), once close, are now the region's next confrontation waiting to happen. The gas is what makes the alignment durable enough to survive public opposition to it.<Cite s="2026-03-03-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-regime-derange-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-jo" t="16:43" />
