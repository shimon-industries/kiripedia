**Meeting Elon Musk in 2009** is a story [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) tells against himself.

He had an idea for a news-based television series and mentioned it to a friend who was active in that world. The friend's best friend from school had just been named president of NBC, so a pitch was arranged: the executive would be in Washington for a fundraiser at the White House, and they would meet afterwards at a bar across the street. At nine he called to say he would be fifteen minutes late and asked whether he might bring a friend. *"The more the merrier."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="47:04" />

The two men arrived, and the executive introduced his companion simply as *"my friend Elon."* Kiriakou said hello, and sat while the old schoolfriends talked about high school and the girls they had dated.<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="47:35" />

## The question

Bored, Kiriakou turned to the stranger and asked what he did for a living. The answer, as he tells it, arrived like a switch being thrown: a passion for technology, a company called PayPal sold for about a billion dollars, the money put into another company, a company called Tesla bought, a space company started — *"and we're going to go to Mars. And we're going to colonize Mars."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="48:08" />

*"Wait a minute. Are you saying you're Elon Musk?"* He was. Kiriakou's response — *"the Thomas Edison of our time"* — got the answer *"I guess so."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="48:39" />

The rest of the evening was Mars: the first people up there probably would not make it, others would follow, and eventually the planet would be colonised. *"All night long. That's all he talked about was Mars."* Kiriakou's conclusion at the time, before anyone in tech knew the name, was that this was somebody special.<Cite s="2026-02-18-elizabeth-lane-tv-and-john-skinwalker-ranch-ufos-usos-and-secret-project" t="49:11" />

The assessment is one he has held to and complicated since. He describes [Musk](/wiki/elon-musk)'s companies as among those the [Directorate of Science and Technology](/wiki/directorate-of-science-and-technology) now works with at the cutting edge, and lists his organisations among the contracts the CIA's general counsel negotiates.<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="06:14" /><Cite s="2026-03-17-deep-focus-irans-future-cia-background-checks-us-revolution" t="21:30" />
