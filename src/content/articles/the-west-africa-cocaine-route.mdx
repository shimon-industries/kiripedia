**The West Africa cocaine route** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to the claim that Venezuela is flooding the United States with drugs.

*"The drugs that are in Venezuela are not Venezuelan. They come from Colombia and they come from Ecuador, and they're transiting Venezuela on their way to West Africa."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="02:36" />

From there the route turns overland and old: *"where they're then put on camels and taken up this ancient Tuareg dirt path to Morocco, where they're put on boats and sent to Spain."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="02:36" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="03:07" />

The conclusion is the point: *"these drugs are meant for Western Europe, not for the United States."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="03:07" />

Kiriakou raises the route as one of three reasons the stated case for American [strikes on boats](/wiki/venezuela-boat-strikes) and pressure on Caracas does not add up — the others being the absence of any economic upside and what he takes to be the real driver, [Chinese refining capacity in the Caribbean](/wiki/venezuelan-heavy-crude-refining).<Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="02:05" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-mario-nawfal-always-watch-the-naval-movements-ex-cia-offic" t="03:07" />
