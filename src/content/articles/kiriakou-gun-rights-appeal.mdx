Kiriakou lost the right to carry a firearm on conviction of a felony, and is pursuing its restoration by two routes at once.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="28:36" />

## The position

He states the underlying view knowing it will cost him with his own audience, *"What I'm going to say is probably going to be a little controversial. And if you want to hate on me in the comments, go ahead and hate on me"*, and does not soften it: *"I've always considered myself to be a progressive, a leftist. But I feel very, very strongly about my right to carry a gun."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="28:05" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="28:36" /> The occasion was a shooting at a fast-food restaurant in Idaho, which he argues was contained because an armed bystander was present.<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="28:05" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="28:36" /> His argument is from circumstance rather than principle: it would be *"wonderful if there were no guns for anybody"* and America could be *"like, I don't know, Sweden or whatever"*, *"But we're not like Sweden. That's not the reality,"* and what protects people from criminals, since the police cannot always do it, is *"honest citizens with their own weapons."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="29:07" />

## The suit

On his second inauguration [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) ordered the Justice Department to begin restoring the gun rights of felons whose cases involved no violence, no drugs and no organised crime, a description Kiriakou applies to himself: *"That's me."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="29:39" /> In April 2025 the department announced the order, promised a website with a form, and undertook that the ATF would investigate each application. *"They have not updated that since the day that they posted that message."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="29:39" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="30:10" /> His response is litigation, justified with a phrase borrowed from an attorney who once opposed him: *"I'm a litigious prick. And so, I'm going to file a suit against the Department of Justice to get my gun rights back."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="30:10" />

## The pardon

A presidential pardon would restore the rights directly, and he has applied for one, reporting *"both broad and deep support"* for it. He does not expect it to be enough: *"I can't count on a pardon. The CIA doesn't want me to be pardoned institutionally."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-john-kiriakou-trump-is-coming-for-the-man-who-put-me-in-pri" t="30:41" />
