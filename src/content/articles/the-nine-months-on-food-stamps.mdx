**The nine months on food stamps** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of his family's own dependence on public assistance, which he raised in October 2025 as 42 million Americans faced the loss of their food stamp benefits.

## The calculator at the dining room table

*"In 2012, in January, I was arrested in mid-January,"* he says, *"and just one week after my arrest, I was sitting at the dining room table with my wife, and she had a calculator, and then she looked up at me and she said, 'I don't see how we can afford to buy food next week.'"*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="42:04" />

He asked whether they were in that bad a shape already. *"And she said, 'Yeah, everything that we have we gave to [the lawyers](/wiki/the-legal-bills) last week, and I don't think we have enough money for food.'"*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="42:04" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="42:34" />

## The welfare office

*"So the next day we went to the welfare office in Arlington County, and we qualified for literally everything, including a $90 monthly cash supplement because she was breastfeeding."* There were two children at home, and two more from an earlier marriage for whom he was paying child support at the time.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="42:34" />

The assistance ran *"for 9 months, until she finally found a job in October of that year. And thank God for that."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="42:34" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="43:05" />

## Why he tells it

Kiriakou brought the episode up to answer a question about scale, one in eight Americans on food stamps, one in four in some states, and that only a subset of the poor, since the working poor who do not qualify or do not apply and those sleeping outside are not in the system at all.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="40:02" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="41:04" />

His specific target was Senator John Fetterman, who had told people to stop acting like children and vote for the budget. Fetterman, Kiriakou notes, *"was the mayor of Braddock, Pennsylvania, which is one of the poorest, most crime-ridden cities in the entire state of Pennsylvania. He knows better."* If he will not help poor people feed their families, *"maybe it's time to primary this guy."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="41:34" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="43:36" />
