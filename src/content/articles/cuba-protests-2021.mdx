**The 2021 Cuba protests** are the July demonstrations that dominated American television for several days, and which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) declined to treat as the start of anything.

## Not a revolution

Asked directly, his answer was unqualified: *"I agree completely. This is not a revolution. I think it's a test run as well."* What was being tested, on his reading, was the government's response — *"they're going to gauge the government's reaction. The government, if they're smart, they're going to try to institute some reforms — and then we see what happens six months from now."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="09:02" /><Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="12:07" />

## Against 'treading lightly'

A *New York Times* piece had argued that [Joe Biden](/wiki/joe-biden) and the Democrats would have to tread lightly in Florida because of the Cuban-American vote. Kiriakou's objection was that the advice was empty: *"that was part of the problem that I had with this piece — they didn't say what that's supposed to mean. You can't just throw this idea into the middle of the room and have everybody just nod their heads."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="06:29" /><Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="06:59" />

His alternative was simple and did not depend on the answer: *"if this is a legitimate uprising, if this is the beginning of a legitimate revolution in Cuba, well then that's good for everybody except maybe the Communist Party's leadership. But treading lightly? No — what you do is you make an announcement that you want the best for the Cuban people."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="06:59" />

The underlying difficulty he names is domestic: *"everything surrounding Cuba is just so politically loaded in the United States."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="07:31" />

## An aside on the coverage

Discussing how American media had handled the summit between Vladimir Putin and Joe Biden a few weeks earlier, Kiriakou agreed that the press had been *"so much more rabid"* about opposing [Putin](/wiki/vladimir-putin) than the president himself was — the same asymmetry he saw applied to Cuba.<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="09:32" />
