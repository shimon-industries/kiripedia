**Netanyahu in hiding** describes the period during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war) when [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) could not be located publicly. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) called it *"a kind of a mystery"*: there were reports that he was hiding in Germany, and later that he was hiding in Cyprus.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="02:36" /> His judgement was not about the location — *"Wherever he happens to be, he's hiding. What kind of wartime leader is that?"*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="02:36" />

## Iran's use of Israeli tactics

Kiriakou's explanation for the disappearance is that Iran had begun doing to Israel's leadership what Israel had done to its adversaries'. Netanyahu's house was struck; his brother was inside and was killed.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="03:06" /> [Itamar Ben-Gvir](/wiki/itamar-ben-gvir)'s house was also targeted, and as of the previous night's reporting Ben-Gvir was critically injured.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="03:37" />

*"They are employing Israel's tactics of taking out the heads, the leadership,"* Kiriakou said, and drew the obvious inference about how the Israeli government was taking it: *"this has to have the Israelis on their heel. I don't think that they expected any of this."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="03:06" /><Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="03:37" />

How Iran acquired the targeting to do it is treated at [targeting information between allies](/wiki/targeting-information-between-allies); Kiriakou raised the possibility that Russia was supplying satellite reconnaissance, or that Iranian agents had penetrated the Israeli state.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="03:06" />
