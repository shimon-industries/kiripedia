The **glass-eating prisoner** is a case [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has cited as the sharpest available illustration of what long-term [solitary confinement](/wiki/solitary-confinement) does to a person. He attributes it to an in-depth *New York Times Magazine* article on the federal Special Housing Units published in May 2015.<Cite s="2023-02-06-in-limine-podcast-in-limine-john-kiriakou" t="59:34" />

## The case

The man had been held in the SHU at the federal supermax at Florence, Colorado for something like eighteen years — eighteen years, in Kiriakou's summary, without human contact. He went insane.<Cite s="2023-02-06-in-limine-podcast-in-limine-john-kiriakou" t="59:34" />

Cells of that kind have a window two or three inches wide, barely enough to admit a little light. The prisoner broke his and ate the glass, so that the institution would be obliged to take him to a hospital where he could talk to another human being.<Cite s="2023-02-06-in-limine-podcast-in-limine-john-kiriakou" t="1:00:09" />

## What he draws from it

Kiriakou's response to the case is not analytical. *"Shame on us,"* he said of it, *"shame on us for pretending that we're better"* — and then, immediately, the consequence: *"and then we wonder why our recidivism rates are so high."*<Cite s="2023-02-06-in-limine-podcast-in-limine-john-kiriakou" t="1:00:09" /> His conclusion is that the system reforms nobody, because reform is not what it is doing. *"We're just torturing them."*<Cite s="2023-02-06-in-limine-podcast-in-limine-john-kiriakou" t="1:00:40" />

The case sits inside his broader position that solitary confinement is torture as a matter of settled international standard rather than opinion: the United Nations treats anything beyond fourteen days as a form of torture, and he invites doubters to test the proposition by locking themselves in a closet for a single day.<Cite s="2023-02-06-in-limine-podcast-in-limine-john-kiriakou" t="55:17" /> Eighteen years is roughly four hundred and seventy times the United Nations threshold.
