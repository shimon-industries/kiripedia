**The Colombian air bridge through Venezuela** is what [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he found when he examined the question on behalf of the [Senate Foreign Relations Committee](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee), where he served as senior investigator — a finding he raised in October 2025 against the American government's account of [the boat strikes in the Caribbean](/wiki/venezuela-boat-strikes).

*"The US has long accused Maduro of drug trafficking,"* he says. *"I did an investigation when I was the senior investigator on the Senate Foreign Relations Committee, and part of that investigation was drug trafficking through Venezuela."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="28:04" />

His first finding concedes the premise in part: *"there is some drug trafficking through Venezuela."* His second removes what the accusation depends on: *"that is not to say that Maduro controls it or even has any idea when and where it's happening."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="28:04" />

What he found instead was a transit route belonging to someone else. *"What it is, is Colombian drug planes landing in Venezuela to, you know, pick up, to drop off, unload, who knows, and then continuing on to Africa."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="28:04" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="28:34" />

His conclusion is about the certainty being claimed rather than about the trade: *"it's not clear-cut like the administration wants you to think it is."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="28:34" />
