**Taybeh** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s description, the only 100% Christian village in the West Bank and the site of the oldest brewery in the Holy Land.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="49:46" /> He says a camera crew of his was present when the IDF rolled into Taybeh's Marian festival with drones, helicopters, armored vehicles and stun grenades.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:02:16" /> On hidden-camera audio, Kiriakou says, an Israeli soldier with an American accent told an abused woman *"I'm so sorry you're having to go through this,"* only for his commander to yell, *"We don't talk to those animals like that. Don't show them any respect."*<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="1:02:46" /> In a separate account, Kiriakou situates a fire that struck Taybeh within a single week of other West Bank violence: the day before the fire, he says, a Palestinian infant was shot in the West Bank, and in the same week four Palestinian female students were kidnapped by the IDF.<Cite s="2026-07-07-covert-strategies-revealed" t="47:27" />
