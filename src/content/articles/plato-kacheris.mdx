**Plato Kacheris** is a transcription variant of the surname of [Plato Cacheris](/wiki/plato-cacheris), [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s lead defence attorney in the 2012–2013 prosecution. The automatic captions on which this encyclopedia's source corpus is built render the name inconsistently — *"Plato Kacheris"* in some episodes, *"Plato Cacheris"* or *"Plato Catereras"* in others — and the variant is retained here so that either spelling reaches the article.<Cite s="2021-05-13-green-socialist-notes-episode-056-whistleblowers-with-special-guest" t="29:36" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="45:16" />

The full account of his role — the descending plea offers, the ruling that ended the case, and the morning he drove to Kiriakou's house to tell him to take the deal — is at **[Plato Cacheris](/wiki/plato-cacheris)**.
