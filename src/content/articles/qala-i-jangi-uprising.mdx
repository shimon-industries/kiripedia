The **Qala-i-Jangi uprising** was a prisoner revolt at the Qala-i-Jangi fortress in northern Afghanistan in late November 2001, during which Taliban and al-Qaeda prisoners — who had been captured by the Northern Alliance — overwhelmed their CIA and military interrogators. [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia) officer [Mike Spann](/wiki/mike-spann) was killed in the uprising, becoming the first American killed in combat in Afghanistan after the September 11 attacks. The American al-Qaeda figure [John Walker Lindh](/wiki/john-walker-lindh) had just been captured at the same site and was present at the start of the uprising.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:36:36" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:37:09" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:38:09" />

## Why the date mattered to a Senate investigation

When Kiriakou tried to establish whether CIA officers had been present at the loading of the containers at [Dasht-i-Leili](/wiki/dasht-i-leili-massacre), the uprising gave him his one certainty about American presence. *"I know CIA officers were present 20 miles away, because the box-up was the same day as the uprising at Qala-i-Jangi."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="20:07" />

What he could not establish was the twenty miles in between — whether the two English-speaking men in blue jeans described by a witness at the container site were agency officers.<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="17:40" /><Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="20:07" />
