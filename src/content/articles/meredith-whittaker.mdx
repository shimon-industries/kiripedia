Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), **Meredith Whittaker** is working on a new business model for the encrypted-messaging app Signal, as part of what he frames as a necessary search for secure communications alternatives — and, in his assessment, "what she's doing is working."<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="01:21:44" /> His remarks came in response to a fellow panelist's question — itself prompted by [Andrew Feinstein](/wiki/andrew-feinstein)'s recent talk at the same venue on the multiple factors that ended apartheid — about whether activists should look beyond leaks and data toward other forms of resistance.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="01:19:40" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="01:20:10" />

He contrasts her effort with ProtonMail's own attempt at a new business model, saying ProtonMail was compromised through an intergovernmental agreement that allowed authorities to obtain information about who was communicating with whom.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="01:21:44" />
