**The CIA barber shop** is one of the amenities of the [headquarters building](/wiki/langley) that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has described, recalled in October 2025 in a digression about how paychecks used to be delivered.

## The paychecks

The recollection began with the observation that at the [CIA](/wiki/cia), as in the private sector at the time, *"they would just come with our paychecks and flip them upside down and leave them on our chairs."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="47:46" />

## Smoking

From there Kiriakou moved to a conversation he had had with a former colleague. *"When I started at the CIA, you could smoke at your desk. And then after a year, they made designated smoking areas."* The rule produced an immediate adaptation: *"so all of the managers made their offices designated smoking areas."* Asked whether smoking was really that prevalent, he confirmed it: *"it was that big, yeah."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="47:46" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="48:17" />

## The barber

*"There's a barber in the basement of the CIA,"* he says. *"When I first joined, I was like, oh, I might as well just go get a haircut at the CIA barber instead of going halfway across town."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="48:17" />

What he found there was not what the setting suggested: *"I went down there and they had Playboys and Penthouses all over the barber shop."* Inside the CIA.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="48:17" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="48:48" />

## The rest of the town

Agreeing that the compound is *"like a whole city in there"*, Kiriakou added the other services. Employees use the dry cleaner — *"oh yeah, yeah, everybody does"* — and *"there's even a little post office substation in there so you don't have to go into town to mail stuff."* He used it all the time.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="48:48" />
