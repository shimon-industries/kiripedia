The Chaos Computer Club, a group of hackers from Berlin, played an important role in the history of WikiLeaks. They were involved in the early stages of the organization. <Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="16:21:00" /> On the same 2026 Berlin panel that included [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), it was through the Chaos Computer Club that a journalist recounted meeting early WikiLeaks associate [Daniel Domscheit-Berg](/wiki/daniel-domscheit-berg), then introduced under the pseudonym "Daniel Schmitt."<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="16:53" />
