**Carter Page** was an adviser to the 2016 Trump campaign who became the subject of [FISA court](/wiki/fisa-court) surveillance warrants. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how those warrants were obtained is blunt: *"We know they lied multiple times to the FISA court to get warrants against Carter Page."* He names [James Comey](/wiki/james-comey), then director of the [FBI](/wiki/fbi), among those responsible.<Cite s="2019-12-21-fox-news-former-cia-whistleblower-on-durham-probing-br" t="02:07" />

The explanation he offers is institutional rather than conspiratorial. *"At that level, with everything being classified and with their arrogance at the same time, I think they think they can just get away with it — the truth isn't going to come out in the end."* His own view, given in the same breath, is that it does: *"It looks like it will come out."*<Cite s="2019-12-21-fox-news-former-cia-whistleblower-on-durham-probing-br" t="02:07" /><Cite s="2019-12-21-fox-news-former-cia-whistleblower-on-durham-probing-br" t="02:37" />
