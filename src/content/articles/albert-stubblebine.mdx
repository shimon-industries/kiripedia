John Kiriakou named General Albert "Bert" Stubblebine among the Army Intelligence figures behind the original remote-viewing program while discussing the CIA's decades-long, ultimately unsuccessful pursuit of remote viewing.

Kiriakou said the CIA "could never get the remote viewing thing to work," trying for 23 years before giving up, and that he had personally interviewed the top people at Army Intelligence involved in the original Project Stargate.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="01:01:09" />

Asked why figures associated with remote viewing don't simply come forward publicly with their findings, Kiriakou said that [Lynn Buchanan](/wiki/lynn-buchanan) had to sign non-disclosure agreements with the NSA and the CIA, "Bert Stubblebine, General Bert Stubblebine, and others" among them, and that they had been working on the subject for many years without being able to speak publicly about it.<Cite s="2026-06-02-it-s-rainmaking-time-john-kiriakou-unfinished-business-a-raw-exchange" t="01:01:41" />
