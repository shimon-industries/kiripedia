**Cost of the 2026 Iran war** is the running expense [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cited during the [war](/wiki/2026-iran-war): asked whether the figure was 800 million dollars a day or as much as 2 billion, he answered that Axios had reported it two days earlier at *"between 2 and 3 billion dollars a day."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="10:54" />

The figure prompted the recollection recorded at [the hundred-million-a-day briefing](/wiki/the-hundred-million-a-day-briefing). Kiriakou's own reconciliation of the two numbers is that they are closer than they look: *"just with inflation, it's going to be close to 2 billion."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="11:26" />

The cost sits alongside the physical damage catalogued at [strikes on US bases in the 2026 Iran war](/wiki/strikes-on-us-bases-2026).
