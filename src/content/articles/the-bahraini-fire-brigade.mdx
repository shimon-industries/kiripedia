**The Bahraini fire brigade** is a report from the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war) that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) declined to dismiss, and that he read as a sectarian signal rather than a lapse of discipline.

## The strikes

Kiriakou had been describing the effect of Iranian retaliation on the Gulf states, based on conversations with contacts there. *"Manama Airport in Bahrain is unusable. It has to be closed for a while and repaired. Kuwait International Airport in Kuwait has also been hit."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-react-as-iran-war-rage" t="05:15" />

## The report

His interlocutor then raised reports that the local Bahraini fire brigade had refused to fight the fire.<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-react-as-iran-war-rage" t="05:15" />

Kiriakou's answer went straight to the reason it would not surprise him. *"I would actually believe that because Bahrain is a majority Shia Muslim country just like Iran is Shia Muslim and Bahrain has a Sunni Muslim royal family, which can be a problem at times."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-react-as-iran-war-rage" t="05:45" />

He attached a consequence and a hedge in the same breath: *"Heads are going to roll if that's true. But I it doesn't surprise me."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-react-as-iran-war-rage" t="05:45" />

The underlying demographic arrangement, and the kingdom's response to it, is treated at [Bahrain's naturalisation policy](/wiki/bahrains-naturalisation-policy); Kiriakou served in the country and returns to its sectarian structure whenever Gulf loyalties are in question. The economic consequences of the same week's strikes are recorded at [Bahrain's force majeure](/wiki/bahrains-force-majeure).

## The wider judgement

The episode sits inside his general assessment of what Iran could and could not achieve. *"the Iranians can't win this thing, of course, but but they can put up a fight and they can they can, you know, put some hurt out on on the Gulf Arab states."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-react-as-iran-war-rage" t="05:45" />
