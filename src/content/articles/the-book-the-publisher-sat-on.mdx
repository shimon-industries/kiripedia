**The book the publisher sat on** is an unpublished manuscript John Kiriakou described on the DeProgram programme in August 2025, when a viewer asked what had become of his book.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:14:36" />

## The delay

Kiriakou's first answer was that he did not know: *"I don't know, man."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:14:36" />

He then gave the chronology. *"This book was supposed to be out. I sent them the draft of this book three years ago"* — and he fixed the month after a false start, settling on April.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:14:36" />

That places delivery in April 2022, and the conversation in August 2025, more than three years later, with nothing to show for it.

## The email

Kiriakou says he had by then abandoned patience and put it in writing: *"I sent a threatening email to my publisher saying it's time to fish or cut bait."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:14:36" />

He does not say what the threat consisted of, and does not name the publisher or the book.

## His position

The rest is short. *"So I've had enough of waiting. I'll let you know as soon as I hear. I'm tired of this."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:14:36" />

Kiriakou has published several books, including the prison memoir [Doing Time Like a Spy](/wiki/doing-time-like-a-spy), and refers on the same programme to *"my second book"* as containing a chapter about a fellow prisoner — so the manuscript in question is a later work whose subject he does not disclose here.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:15" />
