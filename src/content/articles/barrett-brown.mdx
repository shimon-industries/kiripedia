**Barrett Brown** is an American journalist about whom John Kiriakou was asked in January 2026 — specifically why, given that his information seems good and he is off heroin, he remains blackballed. Kiriakou answered as someone who had spoken to him two days earlier.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="48:28" />

## The assessment

Kiriakou's estimate of him is unusually high and unqualified: Barrett Brown is *"one of the most intelligent and best writers and analysts I've ever encountered in my life."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="48:28" /><Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="49:02" />

He pairs that with a second professional judgement that matters more in Kiriakou's vocabulary than talent does — Brown *"is very serious about his sourcing"* — and closes the passage with a recommendation: *"I would encourage everybody to look him up."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="49:02" />

## The difficulties

Kiriakou does not soften the rest of it. Brown has long-standing mental health problems and a two-decade-long heroin addiction, and has been off heroin for at least a year.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="49:02" />

He has also been expelled from the United Kingdom and relocated to Mexico, where a few weeks before their conversation he took what Kiriakou called *"a good hearty beating from the Mexican police."* Brown nonetheless told him that he loves it there and intends to stay.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="49:02" />

## The regret

What Kiriakou wanted from him was more writing. He told Brown he hoped he was writing again, *"because you're the most brilliant writer I I know"* — a man, in his summary, *"so smart and so analytic."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="49:02" /><Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="49:32" />

The exchange is a characteristic instance of Kiriakou's practice of vouching publicly for people whose reputations have been damaged, without editing out the facts that damaged them.
