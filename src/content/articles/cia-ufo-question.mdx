**CIA and the UFO question** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s consistent framing that unidentified craft and alien technology are a Pentagon matter, not a CIA one. On his first day he asked his boss *"where are the aliens?"*; the boss laughed that it was everybody's first question, but *"that's all at the Pentagon."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="53:47" /> Kiriakou adds that nearly every one of his former fighter-pilot friends, flying F-16s, F-18s and F-35s, *"has seen something,"* sometimes a ship emerging from the ocean, *"completely inexplicable."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="54:20" /> The CIA, he stresses, recruits spies and steals secrets; it is *"not beaming waves at your head."*<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="55:21" />

He has repeated the same first-day anecdote and its answer in numerous other interviews. His question was always some version of *"Where are the UFOs?"* or *"So, where are the aliens?"*, and the answer was always the same: everyone asks that on their first day, and *"there's nothing in the system"*, all such information sits at the Pentagon.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="06:10" /><Cite s="2026-05-20-covert-operations-insight" t="32:52" /> He says he agrees with a retired CIA officer's claim that the Pentagon, not the CIA, holds all UFO-related information.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="06:42" /> He recalls that when he started at the CIA the agency was still transitioning from typewriters to computers, with files kept in floor-to-ceiling rotating card files on 5-by-8 cards, and found nothing indicating the CIA held UFO information outside special compartmented channels.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="08:47" /> Kiriakou also says that within the CIA, UFO matters were always understood to be a Department of Defense issue, joking that the only things in the Langley basement are *"the gym, the boiler room, and the supply room."*<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="27:16" /> A retired CIA officer separately told him the agency once built an internal, Wikipedia-like intranet system, and that on the day it launched the number one search term was "UFOs and extraterrestrials."<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="06:10" /> He says the CIA would never lead a project to reverse-engineer a crashed UFO, DARPA would take the lead, bringing in the CIA and other, probably DOD, agencies to assist.<Cite s="2025-03-26-podcast-ufo-live-shows" t="46:50" />

## Kiriakou's own sighting (Jay Dyer)

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that at 17, driving through Pennsylvania Amish country with his father, they watched a silent orange trapezoid *"in the shape of a saucer"* hover 1,500 feet up, then shoot off *"at a fantastic speed that defied the laws of physics."* His father refused to report it, *"people are going to think we're insane."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:15:53" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:16:54" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:21:43" /><Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="28:19" /><Cite s="2026-05-20-covert-operations-insight" t="29:42" /> In a separate telling he places the family home in New Castle, Pennsylvania, with the restaurant they were driving to in nearby Sharon.<Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:21:43" /> Years later a cousin, an F-15 pilot, told him Air Force pilots *"see stuff like that all the time,"* sometimes *"coming up out of the water off the coast of Norfolk"*, filing a form the Pentagon never answers.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:17:57" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:24:17" /><Cite s="2026-05-20-covert-operations-insight" t="32:21" />

Kiriakou also says he personally saw a UFO alongside his father, an elementary school principal of 44 years, describing an object that flew in a way defying the laws of gravity.<Cite s="2026-02-09-epic-real-estate" t="53:53" /> He separately recounts that a Pentagon-frequenting F-14 pilot, at his uncle's Alexandria, Virginia restaurant, told him he once watched an unidentified craft dive into the ocean and shoot back out about ten seconds later.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="31:31" /> He also cites Harvard physicist Avi Loeb, who led an undersea expedition off Papua New Guinea and retrieved BB-sized metal fragments whose composition, Loeb says, does not match any known Earth alloy.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="30:29" />
