**Fridays at the agency** is a working observation [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he took from his time at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) and has kept since: that the last working day of the American week is almost always the emptiest.

*"I got this from the agency when I was at the agency,"* he says, *"Fridays at the CIA were always very, very slow."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="01:05" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="01:35" />

The reason he gives is not American at all. Business stops early because *"everything's closed in the Middle East on either Thursday and Friday or Friday and Saturday, depending on the country you're in. They have different weekends."* For an agency whose traffic in his years was dominated by the region, that meant the cables had already stopped coming in.<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="01:35" />

He treats the effect as having spread beyond the building. *"That sort of bled into the United States, too. It's usually quieter on a Friday."* [Congress](/wiki/congressional-intelligence-oversight) is generally not in session, and *"even when they're in session, they're going home on Thursday nights."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="01:35" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="02:05" />

He raised it on Halloween 2025 to note that the rule had failed: *"man, there is so much to talk about today. It's busy. And none of it is good news."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="02:05" />
