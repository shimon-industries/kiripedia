**Dr Oz and the Armenian hospices** is a short exchange from January 2026 in which John Kiriakou was asked whether he had seen a television segment by Mehmet Oz alleging fraud by Armenian-run hospices in Los Angeles.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="22:32" />

## The response

Kiriakou's answer opened with an admission of temper: he wanted to punch him in the face for that too.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="23:02" />

The reason follows immediately, and is the whole of the argument. *"Of course, Dr. Oz, who is a veteran of the Turkish special forces. Of course, he's going to zero in on Armenians in California."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="23:02" /> The unstated premise is the Armenian genocide and the century of Turkish denial that followed it, which Kiriakou treats as sufficient context for reading a fraud investigation as something other than a fraud investigation. His summary of the man was four words: *"I freaking hate that guy."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="23:02" />

## The pattern

Rall's contribution was to place it in a series — *"And Tulsi in Fulton County. Each appointee must have their own racial scandal"* — which Kiriakou accepted without correction before the conversation moved on to the seizure of Georgia voting records.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="23:02" />

The episode is a small instance of a habit that runs through Kiriakou's commentary: reading a public official's biography, and particularly their prior service in a foreign security establishment, as the operative fact about what they choose to investigate.
