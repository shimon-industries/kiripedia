**"Only two and a half more years"** is the consolation [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) offered in 2026 when asked about domestic policy, in this instance the rescinding of the greenhouse gas endangerment finding by a viewer who worked at the Environmental Protection Agency.

His first admission was that such questions cost him sleep — *"I lose sleep over these issues. I really do"* — and the only comfort he could name was arithmetic: *"There's only 2 and 1/2 more years of this. Even if a Republican wins, it likely won't be as bad."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="27:40" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="28:10" />

The list he assembled to justify the alarm ran across a single news cycle: wholesale, across-the-board deregulation; statues of slave owners announced for Washington; efforts touching on whether married women could vote; a murder charge brought over an abortion pill; the renaming of the Kennedy Center; and the minting of a gold coin bearing the president's face on both sides.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="28:10" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="28:41" /> His comparison for the last of these was a foreign one — *"It's Turkmenbashi all over again."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="28:41" />

## Why it will not be reversed

Put to him that even a Democratic successor would not roll most of it back, Kiriakou agreed and gave two reasons: *"they wouldn't, because they're cowards. And because they want the power and authority for themselves."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="28:41" />

His evidence is the last transition. [Joe Biden](/wiki/joe-biden) *"did not roll back much of the Trump first term stuff at all"* — and the example Kiriakou reaches for is the one closest to his own interests: *"in fact, Joe Biden never reestablished relations with [Cuba](/wiki/cuba-protests-2021)."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="29:11" />

He extends the same logic to Congress, expecting the legislature to concede whatever authority it retains the moment a Democrat wins the White House.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="53:12" />
