**Louise Mensch** is the former British parliamentarian whose attack on [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) produced the one occasion he describes taking legal action against a critic. He raises her as the precedent when asked about a later attack by [Laura Loomer](/wiki/laura-loomer): *"this has happened to me once before."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="22:22" />

His description of her is unsparing and includes her politics as the motive: she is, he says, *"like so 1,000% pro-Ukraine"* and *"irrational"* on the subject. He stresses that there was no prior contact of any kind — *"I've never met this woman in my life. I've never had an exchange with her in my life. And she just attacked me."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="22:53" /><Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="23:24" />

What he did next he frames through a phrase used against him by an opposing lawyer in one of his divorces: *"in the words of an opposing divorce attorney, John Kiriakou is a litigious prick."* He called his own lawyer, asked whether the statements were defamatory, was told they were, and answered: *"Then I want to sue."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="23:24" />

The case never reached a court. A letter went out demanding a full retraction on pain of suit, and *"she retracted everything."* The terms he records are specific — *"she had to pin an apology for 6 months on her Twitter"* — and his summary of the episode is one word: *"but I won."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="23:55" />

Kiriakou notes in passing that she has since dropped the name under which she was known, publishing as Louise Bagshaw.<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="23:55" />
