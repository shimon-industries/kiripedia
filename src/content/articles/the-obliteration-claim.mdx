**The obliteration claim** is [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump)'s statement, made the year before the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war), that the entire Iranian nuclear programme had been destroyed. *"That was his word, obliterated,"* [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) notes.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="01:34" />

Kiriakou reduces the claim to three possibilities: *"either he lied, he was tricked, or he's too stupid to know that he wasn't able to obliterate it."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="01:34" /> The reason the claim matters is that it does not survive contact with the justification later given for the war itself — that Iran was days away from producing a nuclear weapon.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="01:34" /> If the programme had been obliterated, there was nothing left to be days away from.

He places that justification in a longer sequence: a video circulated during the war compiling every occasion on which [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) had told the United States Congress that Iran was six months from a bomb.<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="02:05" /> Kiriakou's assessment of the public response is that the pattern had finally become visible — *"Americans are now coming to realization of this. We're aware of these lies."*<Cite s="2026-04-03-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-kiriakou-washington-has-no-say-in-this-war" t="02:05" />

The targeting basis for the original claim is treated separately at [Iranian underground missile silos](/wiki/iranian-underground-missile-silos).
