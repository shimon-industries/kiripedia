**Yves Engler** is a Canadian activist and writer based in Montreal whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has known for a long time and describes as a *"good guy."* Kiriakou recorded a podcast with him shortly before the March 2025 interview in which he gave this account.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="44:24" />

Engler wrote an article criticising a well-known Canadian Zionist, a woman Kiriakou says Engler did not threaten but criticised, on the ground that she does not respect the human rights of Palestinians. *"Next thing you know he's under arrest for hate speech, and all he said was that he supports Palestinian human rights. There was no hatred there."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="44:55" />

Engler spent five days in jail in a prison 40 miles outside Montreal before making bail, and was then set for trial.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="45:27" /> Kiriakou reports Engler's own expectation during those days, *"people are going to see how ridiculous this is; I didn't do anything wrong, I have freedom of speech, I'm a Canadian citizen, I'm talking about human rights"*, and the response instead: *"they're doubling down."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="45:27" />

## The additional charges

After his release, preparing for trial, Engler telephoned the arresting officer to ask for the documents and police reports his attorney would need. *"They charge him with extra crimes. They charged him with four counts of harassment of a police officer, because he sent a couple of emails and made a couple of phone calls."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="45:58" />

He was also suspended from Twitter and from Facebook. Kiriakou generalises the pattern, *"you use the G-word and you lose your access to these platforms"*, and cites the case as the concrete answer to what a censorship-resistant platform would be for.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="46:30" />
