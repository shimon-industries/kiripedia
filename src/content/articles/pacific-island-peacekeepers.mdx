**Pacific island peacekeepers** is an observation [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) offered in October 2025 while weighing [a proposal to send Pakistani troops into Gaza](/wiki/pakistani-troops-for-gaza), by asking who the alternative would be.

*"The alternative would have been troops from Tahiti, right? Or from Fiji,"* he said. *"These Pacific islands provide so many peacekeeping forces, and it's a major source of income for them. It makes up a great part of their budget."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="15:36" />

His objection to them is not to their conduct as soldiers but to the distance between them and the populations they are asked to police: *"they're cultural strangers, you know, the people from the South Pacific."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="15:36" />

The comparison he draws is specific about what cultural proximity would supply. Pakistani troops *"at least would understand Islam, would respect, you know, Palestinian women, all the stuff that non-Muslim peacekeeping forces over the years have been accused of ignoring."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="15:36" />
