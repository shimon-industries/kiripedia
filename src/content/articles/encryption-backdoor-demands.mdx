[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes the British government as *"practically at war with Apple"* in early 2025 over its demand for a back door into the company's operating system, and says Apple's answer was that it would rather shut down in the United Kingdom than give British intelligence access.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="34:23" />

He treats the episode as a template rather than an exception, and predicted the same demand would be made of any successful new platform under the second [Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) administration. Asked what happens when that administration comes knocking, he scripted the meeting: *"can you imagine a White House meeting where Kash Patel, the new FBI director, or John Ratcliffe, the new CIA director, go to Trump and say, 'Mr President, you know, terrorism, another 9/11 — so we need these back doors'?"* His answer to his own question is that Trump *"is not going to say 'oh no no no, I believe in transparency, I believe in freedom of speech, I'm not giving you any back doors that might save American lives' … he's going to give them what they want, just like every single president before him gave them what they wanted."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="42:19" /><Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="42:50" />

He extends the same scepticism to the administration's free-speech executive orders, which he says will not lead to more transparency: *"it's all about lawfare, it's all about going after your enemies."*<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="41:48" /> The countermeasure he identifies for a platform is jurisdictional — being registered in Iceland with staff distributed across every time zone means not dealing directly with the American or British governments or any of the Five Eyes.<Cite s="2025-03-01-truthwire-where-the-hell-is-panquake-surveillance-the-t" t="41:18" />
