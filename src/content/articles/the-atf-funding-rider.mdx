**The ATF funding rider** is the legislative device John Kiriakou identifies as the practical reason that a formally available restoration of firearm rights has never restored anyone's. He set it out in January 2026 while answering a question about what a presidential pardon would actually get him.

## The pardon question

Asked what he would receive if he succeeded in obtaining a pardon, Kiriakou's answer was one word: *"My pension."* Told that this was not a small thing, he agreed that it was the whole thing — twenty years of proud government service, and something he needs to live on in old age.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="33:25" />

He can already vote. Governor Terry McAuliffe, who had followed his case, pardoned him at the state level without his ever having applied, because elections are administered by the states.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="33:25" /><Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="33:57" />

## The split between state and federal rights

The consequence he finds absurd is that he has his state gun rights back and not his federal ones.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="33:57" /> Asked whether that means he can carry in Virginia, his answer was yes and no: he can carry legally in Virginia in the sense that he is not violating a state law, but he is still violating federal law.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="33:57" /> Rall's analogy — the position of a state-licensed cannabis dealer — drew his agreement: *"exactly the same thing."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="33:57" />

## The rider itself

The mechanism is the part Kiriakou says he and the constitutional lawyer Bruce Fein have discussed *ad nauseam*. Congress passed a law in the 1990s, under [Bill Clinton](/wiki/bill-clinton), providing that felons may apply to the ATF to have their gun rights reinstated if they were not convicted of a violent or drug-related felony.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="34:28" />

In the same law, Congress provided that no federal money shall be expended by the ATF to investigate any of the people who apply.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="34:28" /> The result, as Rall put it and Kiriakou did not dispute, is that no one has ever had their gun rights restored.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="34:58" />

Kiriakou's own response to the same problem in its modern form — a 2025 Justice Department restoration process announced and never built — has been litigation rather than another application.
