**The Vietnam speech** is a paid engagement [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described on 25 February 2026, when a viewer asked him to shed some light on it in the same message that asked about [Frank Snepp](/wiki/frank-snepp).

*"The speech in Vietnam is going to be on the morning of the 10th of August."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="47:17" />

## The booking

Kiriakou named the intermediary and the audience without prompting. *"I'm represented right now by a speakers bureau in New York called Gotham Artists and they booked this speech for me to give to 300 Australian investment bankers at their annual convention."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="47:17" /><Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="47:48" />

The pairing is an unusual one on its face, a former CIA officer prosecuted under the Espionage Act addressing a bankers' convention in Hanoi, and Kiriakou treated it as a windfall rather than an incongruity.

## The trip

He was taking family. *"My brother-in-law is so excited that he's going to go with me. We're going to meet in Washington. We're going to fly to Vietnam through South Korea. And take a couple of extra days for ourselves."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="47:48" />

The reason for the extra days is that he had never been. *"I've never been to Vietnam. I've always wanted to go. And uh I think this is going to be a blast."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="47:48" />

Asked whether the Hanoi Hilton or the tunnels were on his list, he took both: *"Yes and yes. Yes to both."* His only complaint was the length of the visit, *"I only regret that I don't have more than you know 5 days"*, because otherwise he would have had suits made, at about a hundred dollars apiece.<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="48:19" />

The corpus records the engagement as described rather than as completed; Kiriakou gave the date five and a half months in advance and no later account of the trip appears in the source material.
