**Israeli drone factories in Serbia** came to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) as new information during consultative talks with a Serbian delegation at the St Petersburg International Economic Forum.

He was told, he says, *"a shocking fact I wasn't aware"* of: that the Israelis are building five drone factories in Serbia.<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="57:38" />

What struck him was the delegation's attitude to it. They were pleased, on the reasoning that *"Bosnia are a bunch of Islamists"* and *"because we need to combat them and we see how well they're doing against the Islamists in the region, we want to work with them."* Kiriakou reports his own reply as *"Guys, this is crazy."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="58:09" />

He treats it as an instance of a general method rather than a bilateral arrangement: the same emotional play used on the Greeks and Cypriots — the neighbours are coming, arm yourselves, we will put the factories in your country — and answers it with a saying he attributes to his grandmother: *"better the Sultan's turban than the Frankish tiara."*<Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="58:09" /><Cite s="2026-06-08-ddgeopolitics-prison-for-truth" t="58:40" /> The Turkish version of the same dynamic is set out at [Israel, Turkey, and NATO Article 5](/wiki/israel-turkey-nato-article-5).
