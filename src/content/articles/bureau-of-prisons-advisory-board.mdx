The **Bureau of Prisons advisory board** is a body [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) joined after an unsolicited telephone call, and which he disclosed publicly for the first time in a July 2026 interview in Nicosia.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="13:30" />

## The call

Driving through Washington early on a Saturday morning, Kiriakou answered a number he did not recognise — "Chicago mobile" — on the reasoning that someone calling at that hour might matter. It was the Director of the [Bureau of Prisons](/wiki/bureau-of-prisons).<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="11:55" />

Asked to what he owed the pleasure, Kiriakou was told: "You've been talking about the Bureau of Prisons for the last 10 years." He corrected the figure to thirteen. The Director's reply was that this was the reason for the call — "You don't just talk about the Bureau of Prisons. You offer solutions."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="12:28" />

## The mandate

The Director then said the president had tasked him with tearing the Bureau of Prisons down and rebuilding it. Kiriakou's response was that the Bureau "is a failure because it's corrupt and everybody in it is corrupt," and that the conditions inside American prisons are third world and a matter for global shame. The Director said that was exactly why he was calling, and asked him to serve on an advisory board. Kiriakou answered: "I'm all in."<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="12:59" /><Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="13:30" />

Asked for further recommendations, he named two people and made the introductions.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="13:30" />

## Caveat

Kiriakou is not unreservedly optimistic about the outcome. Asked whether two and a half remaining years of a presidential term is enough to reform an entire prison system, his answer was no — while hoping whatever the commission produces is implemented.<Cite s="2026-07-09-part-2-how-well-do-you-really-know-him" t="18:14" />
