**Dimitris Koufodinas** was the primary assassin of [Revolutionary Organization 17 November](/wiki/revolutionary-organization-17-november), the Greek group that killed [CIA](/wiki/cia) station chief [Richard Welch](/wiki/richard-welch) and, a quarter of a century later, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s next-door neighbour [Stephen Saunders](/wiki/stephen-saunders). He was known as the Black Hand — *"because everything he touched died."*<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="29:04" />

When the group collapsed after one of its bombers was maimed by his own device and named every member to the police, Greek counter-terrorism officers broke the organisation within a day. Koufodinas briefly escaped. He was found, Kiriakou says, *"of all places, on a nudist beach on one of the Greek islands,"* and captured there.<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="28:33" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="29:04" />

Most members of 17 November have since been released from prison. Five of the primary assassins, Koufodinas among them, remain inside.<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="29:35" />
