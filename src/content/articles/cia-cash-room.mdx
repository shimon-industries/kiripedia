A **CIA cash room** is, per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), a small room at the larger CIA stations stacked to the ceiling with cash — *"50 million, 100 million, whatever you happen to need."* An officer signs out what an operation requires and goes.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="20:00" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="20:32" /> For the [Abu Zubaydah capture](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) he took $2 million, carefully counted into duffel bags, to buy two safe houses in Lahore and Faisalabad — mindful of the warning he got in his first week that finance, security and medical are the three offices that can *"send you to prison"* if a dollar goes unaccounted.<Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="20:32" /><Cite s="2025-08-18-hope-2025-kiriakou-talk" t="21:02" />

Kiriakou says that when he was in the CIA, intelligence work was fundamentally a cash business — officers carrying pockets or duffel bags of cash to pay sources, occasionally resorting to diamonds. He says that has changed: since almost nobody uses cash anymore, cryptocurrency such as Bitcoin is now the preferred way to pay agents discreetly, sent directly from a secure phone with no one the wiser.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="18:28" />

## The saying and the duffel bags

The operational premise Kiriakou states is that intelligence is a cash business and cash is not a problem.<Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-top-secrets-the-cia-torture-whistleblower-john-ki" t="34:05" /> The practical shape of that is a van on the road to [Faisalabad](/wiki/faisalabad) about two weeks before the [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) capture, carrying $2 million in two duffel bags, and a conversation between the two officers in it about why anyone would falsify twenty dollars of accounting paperwork when they were sitting on millions.<Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-top-secrets-the-cia-torture-whistleblower-john-ki" t="35:38" /><Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-top-secrets-the-cia-torture-whistleblower-john-ki" t="36:09" /> The mechanics of what happens to that money once it reaches an agent are set out at [agent payment mechanics](/wiki/agent-payment-mechanics).
