**Podcasters on foreign payrolls** is a claim [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he had not considered until roughly a year before recounting it, when an investigative journalist changed his mind. Asked how many prominent commentators he believes are paid or otherwise compromised, his answer was direct: *"You know what? I actually think it's a lot."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="25:29" />

The basis is second-hand and he presents it as such. A journalist *"whom I respect a great deal"* told him that people had independently come to her about four podcasters — *"kind of household names"* — who she was under the impression were taking [CIA](/wiki/cia) money. He asked whether she intended to publish. She would not: *"No, because I can't find anything to confirm it."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="26:02" />

What he does with the claim is extend it by inference rather than evidence: *"if podcasters have taken CIA money, you can bet your bottom dollar they're taking Israeli money, Russian money, maybe even Chinese money. Who knows? Depending on what the issue is."*<Cite s="2026-08-06-the-kiriakou-archive-john-kiriakou-on-netanyahu-s-private-meeting" t="26:32" />

## Who could actually find out

Asked separately what it would take to establish who is being funded by [Israel](/wiki/israel-united-states-relations), Qatar or anyone else, Kiriakou first moved the question to the right agency. It is *"an FBI issue"* — *"the CIA by law would be forbidden from doing that on Americans"* — though the agency could properly examine foreign nationals. On feasibility he was not pessimistic: *"you probably need a couple of months to really dig it up, but you could dig it up."*<Cite s="2026-04-25-valuetainment-valuetainment" t="00:30" />
