**Concealment devices** — *CDs* in [CIA](/wiki/cia) usage — are everyday objects modified to hide something inside them. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) confirms they are real, ordinary and widely distributed: *"certainly books like that exist. They're called CDs or concealment devices."* Some are handed out without a purpose attached — *"here are some CDs for you. Use them for whatever you want to use them for"* — and some are big enough to hold a gun. *"Absolutely they exist, and every CIA officer has access to them."*<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="28:41" /><Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="29:13" />

## How one is found

Reviewing the scene in *The Good Shepherd* in which a hollowed copy of *Ulysses* is detected on a shelf, Kiriakou calls the setup slightly *"Hollywood fabulist"* but the method sound. The character is looking for an anomaly in the placement of the book: it sits among classic literature, but its binding is newer and it stands ever so slightly higher than the others — the signature of a book that has been handled.<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="29:13" />

Taking it down, he is checking the hard cover for a second anomaly — whether something has been glued in, whether there is something inside — and he can feel it. Kiriakou's professional verdict on the device rather than the search: *"Frankly, it wasn't a very good concealment device if you can feel it with your finger."*<Cite s="2026-05-19-gq-ex-cia-officer-john-kiriakou-breaks-down-spy" t="29:45" />

## The Directorate of Science and Technology's old work

Kiriakou places concealment devices in the historical portfolio of the [Directorate of Science and Technology](/wiki/directorate-of-science-and-technology), which in his time was *"almost an afterthought"* responsible mostly for disguises and *"briefcases with secret pockets in them."* The directorate has since moved to what he calls big-ticket items and pushed the concealment and disguise work out to individuals and makeup companies.<Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="05:43" /><Cite s="2026-05-24-john-kiriakou-podcast-the-most-secret-cia-unit-ever" t="06:14" />
