**Kiriakou's skeet shooting** began as an accident of training. Having worked through pistols in the [shooting house](/wiki/shooting-house), the class moved to the Remington pump-action 12 gauge, a weapon [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) had never handled, having never fired a rifle or a shotgun in his life.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="29:34" />

The instructor was an old southerner near his eightieth birthday with a cheek full of chewing tobacco. *"Okay, youngster. So you're the one who ain't never touched a gun before."* He asked whether Kiriakou knew why they used the Remington pump action, and gave three reasons: buckshot will kill anyone breaking into your house; they are easy to load, fire and clean; and, racking it, *"it makes that bitchin' sound."* Kiriakou says he still laughs about it.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="30:06" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="30:36" />

## The plates

The drill was to knock down steel plates the size of a pie plate. Kiriakou hit both at seven metres, both at twelve, both at twenty-five. Told to try three at a time, he asked how one loads the third; the instructor showed him to hold a cartridge between two fingers of the left hand and feed it as the action opens. He did three, then four, and never missed.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="31:07" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="31:40" />

The instructor took him aside after dinner and said it would sound crazy, but he thought Kiriakou could be a competitive skeet shooter. *"And that is how I became a competitive skeet shooter. And I was darn good at it."* He shot at clubs around Washington and as far away as Warm Springs, Virginia on the West Virginia border, clay pigeons and, as he puts it, all kinds of stuff.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="31:40" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="32:12" />
