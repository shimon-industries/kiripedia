**Salt Typhoon** is the code name for a Chinese cyber operation against telecommunications companies, which John Kiriakou assessed briefly on the DeProgram programme in September 2025.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:06" />

## The attacker

Kiriakou's judgement of Chinese capability is unhedged. *"The Chinese are really good at computer attacks. They're just really good. And they've got this unit within the Chinese intelligence service that only does cyber warfare."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:06" />

## The operation

He describes the result as a windfall rather than a precise strike: *"they hit the jackpot with this thing, this Salt Typhoon, where they hacked the information of literally every American."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:06" /><Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:38" />

He reports, without endorsing it, a claim then circulating about the operation's origins — *"there are a couple of outlets today and yesterday that were saying that the real targets of this thing were Donald Trump and JD Vance"* — and gives the reasoning attributed to the operators for widening it: *"but then they thought, ah, no, we'll just cast a wide net and see how much success we can have."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:38" />

His summary of the outcome is that the wider approach worked: *"they had complete success, where they were able to collect data from every single American."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:38" />

## The verdict

Kiriakou's assessment is symmetrical and short. *"They're good"* — and, of the other side, *"our defences are not so good."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:03:38" /><Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:04:08" />

## The absence of a response

The host's complaint, which Kiriakou endorsed, was that no political response of any kind follows such breaches: that they appear in the newspaper, are read, and are forgotten, without a single politician proposing hearings or remedial action. Kiriakou's reply was *"yeah, you're absolutely right."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:05:12" />

The operation belongs to a category of intelligence activity Kiriakou treats as distinct from the human-source work he did himself, and about which his commentary is usually a straightforward assessment of relative competence rather than a moral judgement.
