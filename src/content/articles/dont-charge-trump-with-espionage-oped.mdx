When classified documents were found at Mar-a-Lago, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) wrote a column for [Consortium News](/wiki/consortium-news) whose headline was *"Don''t charge Donald Trump with espionage"* and whose subheading was *"and don''t charge anyone else either."*<Cite s="2024-02-19-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure" t="15:27" /><Cite s="2024-02-19-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure" t="16:00" />

## The alternative

The proposal in the piece was a return to the practice he had watched in the 1990s: *"we need to come up with something akin to what we had in the 1990s when classified information was mishandled, or allegedly mishandled"* — [an administrative penalty rather than a prosecution](/wiki/the-pillow-talk-leak-case).<Cite s="2024-02-19-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure" t="16:00" />

He applies the test consistently to both men. Of the box in [Joe Biden](/wiki/joe-biden)''s garage: *"who had access to that information? Who saw the information? Who was the information revealed to? Nobody. Maybe it went to his ghost writer. Okay — shame on you, you should have a strongly worded letter put in your personnel file. But espionage? Sorry."* Of Trump: *"Donald Trump has a problem — he has lots of problems — but in this case he had a problem with taking classified information with him in his retirement. Did he publish it? Did he turn it over to Fox News? Is society a more dangerous place because of it? No, not that anybody has proven. So let''s put a strongly worded letter in his personnel file or something. But to charge him with espionage, which in some cases can be a death penalty case? No. I''m sorry, it''s not appropriate."*<Cite s="2024-02-19-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure" t="17:36" /><Cite s="2024-02-19-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure" t="18:09" />

## What he wants instead

*"We need to revamp the whole thing. The [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act) needs to be scrapped and rewritten, so that it targets people who mean harm to our country — people who are working on behalf of foreign governments or foreign entities or dangerous terrorist groups or something like that. Not for some absent-minded politician who leaves boxes in his garage — and not for whistleblowers and other lower-level leakers who had their lives destroyed."*<Cite s="2024-02-19-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure-kevin-gosztola-unauthorized-disclosure" t="18:40" />
