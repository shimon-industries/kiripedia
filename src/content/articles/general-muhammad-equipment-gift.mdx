The story John Kiriakou tells about the equipment is a coda to the operation described in [Pakistan station 2002](/wiki/pakistan-station-2002), and he tells it as an illustration of what happens when nobody is accounting for anything.

## What was bought

As part of the operations to find [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah), Kiriakou says, the station bought more equipment than you can shake a stick at: everything from battering rams to bulletproof vests to night vision goggles, guns, ammunition, secure communications, encrypted walkie-talkies and satellite dishes. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="15:42" />

At the end of it, headquarters told him to just throw it away, or just give it to the Pakistanis, we don't want to bring it back. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="16:13" />

## The gift

So Kiriakou went to the Pakistani brigadier general he had been working with, General Muhammad, and said they would like to make a gift of all of it. The general's reaction, in Kiriakou's telling, was delight: oh my gosh, thank you so much, it's so advanced, my people don't have any of it. It went over as truckloads. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="16:13" />

A couple of weeks later, Kiriakou made conversation with the colonel who was his day-to-day contact, saying how glad he was that they were going to be so well equipped with everything that had been turned over. The colonel asked: what stuff? Kiriakou listed it, the guns, the ammunition, the battering rams, the walkie-talkies, the satellites. The colonel said: I don't know what you're talking about. The general just took it. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="16:44" /> <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="17:16" />

## What he thinks became of it

Asked whether the general had probably sold it to the Taliban, Kiriakou said of course, because he wants to retire in a nice big house someday too. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="17:16" /> He had already told his interviewer that he could tell her what the general had done with it. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="16:44" />

The episode is the mirror image of the discipline he says he imposed on his own station's books, described in [security, medical and finance](/wiki/security-medical-and-finance): every dollar the station spent, including every dollar given to the Pakistanis, had to be accounted for and the books balanced to the dollar when the operation closed. <Cite s="2025-11-06-elizabeth-lane-tv-co-branded-personal-channel-confirm-real-inte" t="15:10" />
