Kiriakou prefaces the finding with an admission about himself — "you know how obsessed I am with polls" — before describing this one as a global survey that ranked every country by whether its citizens were strongly pro-Israel, moderately pro-Israel, moderately anti-Israel or strongly anti-Israel. <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="08:18" />

## The result

Out of roughly two hundred countries, he says, only three came out pro-Israel by majority. One of them is Israel. One of them is the United States. "And the third, inexplicably to me, was Nigeria." <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="08:49" />

Everybody else, in his summary, is majority anti-Israel. "The world." <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="09:21" /> Asked whether the poll tracked change over time, Kiriakou said it had, and that the movement was dramatic. <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="09:21" />

## Turkey and Greece

Outside the Middle East proper — he flags the British usage — Kiriakou reports Turkey as the most anti-Israel country. <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="09:21" /> He treats this as a genuine reversal, agreeing that the two states used to be something close to allies, and recalling the Turkish doctors, rescue teams and trained dogs sent to Israel after the earthquake. <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="09:53" />

His explanation is Erdogan's drift "farther and farther toward the religious types over the years," with the decision to publicly support Hamas as the breaking point. <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="09:53" />

The consequence Kiriakou finds most striking is a swap. "That's when the Greeks moved in to become pro-Israel. It was the Greeks that were always pro-Palestinian. And when the Turks switched, the Greeks switched." <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="10:25" />

But the Greek public did not follow the Greek state. Combining strongly and moderately anti-Israel responses, Kiriakou says, Greece came out at 74 per cent anti-Israel — "despite the fact that the Greek government bows down before the Israeli government." He calls the gap fascinating. <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="10:25" />

## The lesson he draws

Kiriakou's conclusion is about the United States rather than about Israel: "the Israelis are dragging us down with them." <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="10:57" />

He pairs it with a complaint about the terms of American debate — that so many Americans believe being anti-Netanyahu means being antisemitic, and that one must be in lock step with the farthest-right elements of the Israeli government to escape the charge. His verdict on that proposition: "utter nonsense." <Cite s="2025-08-21-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="10:57" />
