**Haaretz** is the Israeli daily newspaper whose September 2026 investigation into [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu)'s handling of pre-[October 7](/wiki/october-7-attack) warnings [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has described as *"easily the biggest bombshell yet regarding whether Netanyahu let October 7th happen."*<Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="01:04" />

## Kiriakou's assessment

Kiriakou frames Haaretz as the class of source that shifts a story from claim to record. It is, he says, *"literally the most highly respected newspaper in all of Israel. A newspaper that has a gigantic budget for investigative journalism and they investigated the daylights out of this."*<Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="18:54" />

The 2026 investigation ran for two years and, per Kiriakou, was *"by a very well-respected reporter who is sourced to the teeth. He dotted all the eyes, crossed all the tees."*<Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="02:05" />

## The investigation

The findings, as Kiriakou summarises them from the report: Netanyahu received explicit warnings before [October 7](/wiki/october-7-attack) from [Mohammed bin Zayed](/wiki/mohammed-bin-zayed) and from Egyptian intelligence chief General Abbas Kamel; he told each *"he had the situation under control"*; and, per senior Shin Bet officials cited in the report, *"never shared details of those warnings with his intelligence services. If he had, it could have changed their entire threat assessment picture."*<Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="12:33" /><Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="13:04" />

## Netanyahu's response

Netanyahu *"just announced plans to sue Haaretz for libel and he blasted the investigation as false."* The Prime Minister's Bureau called the report *"an absolute lie"* and said Netanyahu *"did not speak to the president of the UAE during the period in question, and he received no warning from him."* Kiriakou's assessment of the suit is that *"the lawsuit will probably not go very far"* given the two-year sourcing.<Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="02:05" /><Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="13:04" />

## Corroboration

The story did not stand on Haaretz alone. Kiriakou notes that *"the Jerusalem Post and the Times of Israel have both confirmed the Haaretz article"*, and adds that *"the moderate newspaper, The Times of Israel, has now confirmed the details of the reporting"*, with *"multiple senior officials"* confirming the disputed call.<Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="02:05" /><Cite s="2026-09-10-john-kiriakou-bombshell-report-netanyahu-was-warned-before-october-7th" t="13:36" />
