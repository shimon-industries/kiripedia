**Bernard "Bernie" Kerik** is a former New York Police Department Commissioner. In [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, Kerik had emailed him not long after his release from prison to propose a joint college-campus speaking tour, an idea Kiriakou welcomed but which never came to anything. <Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="1:55:01" />

Kiriakou says that in 2018, when he decided to seek a pardon from Trump, he emailed Kerik knowing Kerik was still in touch with [Rudy Giuliani](/wiki/rudy-giuliani), and Kerik connected him to Giuliani's associate, an introduction that culminated in Giuliani's $2 million pardon-extortion attempt at the Trump International Hotel in Washington, D.C. <Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="1:55:32" />
