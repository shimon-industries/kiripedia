**Al Jazeera blocked in the United States** was the first thing [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) said on air on 28 February 2026, when [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) opened an unscheduled Saturday broadcast by apologising for the circumstances.

Kiriakou did not begin with the war. *"Ted, my friend Genie just texted me from Florida to say that the Dish Network has blocked Al Jazeera from broadcasting here in the United States."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="00:38" />

His gloss was one sentence: *"They don't want us to see what's happening in the Middle East."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="00:38" />

A second report followed from a viewer during the broadcast, Comcast Xfinity doing the same in the Boston area, with the observation that carriers do not do that on a whim.<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:42" />

## What he was watching instead

Having reported the blackout, Kiriakou sourced the morning's worst news to American outlets: *"We know from CNN and from other sources, the New York Times is reporting that an Israeli missile hit a girl's school in southern Iran this morning killing 57 children."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="00:38" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:10" />

His warning about the figure was that it was not an aberration. *"So, get used to that. That's going to be what this war looks like. The Israelis don't give a [expletive] about human life, about civilians, and we're going to see a lot of civilian casualties."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:10" />

## The precedent

The blackout was discussed on air as a repetition rather than a novelty, Al Jazeera America having been effectively shut down under the Bush administration, not by order but by leaning on the cable companies until it had no outlet.<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:13" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:44" />

What made the loss operational rather than symbolic, in the same exchange, was that no other broadcaster carries the same footage: on Gaza, the channel is *"the only game in town."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:13" />
