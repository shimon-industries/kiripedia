**The synagogue in Damascus** is a detail [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) offers as first-hand evidence for a claim about religious minorities in Syria, and as the ground for a forecast he states in the language of betting.

## What he saw

Discussing the fate of Syria's minority communities after the fall of [Bashar al-Assad](/wiki/bashar-al-assad), Kiriakou corrected an interlocutor on the Alawites — *"actually Bashar al-Assad was an Alawite Muslim"* — and gave a figure for the Christian population: *"there are some estimates that put the Christian community in Syria at more than 5%."*<Cite s="2025-11-14-the-deep-focus-show-taxpayers-against-genocide-w-seth-donnelly" t="36:31" />

The synagogue is introduced as personal knowledge rather than reporting: *"I've been to Syria. I went to a to an operating practicing Jewish synagogue in Damascus. There's still a Jewish community in Damascus."*<Cite s="2025-11-14-the-deep-focus-show-taxpayers-against-genocide-w-seth-donnelly" t="36:31" />

## The forecast

What follows is stated as a wager he would decline. *"if I were a betting man, I would not bet money on the safety of Christians or Jews or Alawites or any other religious minority in Syria. Not under this government or any other government."*<Cite s="2025-11-14-the-deep-focus-show-taxpayers-against-genocide-w-seth-donnelly" t="37:03" />

Kiriakou keeps the judgement on Assad and the judgement on minority protection separate, and refuses to let either soften the other. *"For for all of his faults, and Bashar al-Assad was a monster"* — and then, after his interlocutor agreed that Assad was a monster — *"But for all of his faults, he at least protected religious minorities. And that's that's all out the window now."*<Cite s="2025-11-14-the-deep-focus-show-taxpayers-against-genocide-w-seth-donnelly" t="37:03" />

Damascus recurs in Kiriakou's own account of his travels: he has also described viewing what is venerated as the head of John the Baptist, held in a silver coffin at the Umayyad Mosque, during his [CIA](/wiki/cia) career.<Cite s="2025-11-14-the-deep-focus-show-taxpayers-against-genocide-w-seth-donnelly" t="36:31" />
