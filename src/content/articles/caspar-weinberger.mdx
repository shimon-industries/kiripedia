**Caspar Weinberger** served as U.S. Secretary of Defense under President Reagan. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cites Weinberger's assessment of the [Jonathan Pollard](/wiki/jonathan-pollard) espionage case: that the damage was *"grave"* — which Kiriakou notes is the highest level of damage in the classification scheme.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="41:02" /> Kiriakou frames this as the reason Pollard served every single day of his thirty-year sentence with no time off for good behavior.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="41:35" />
