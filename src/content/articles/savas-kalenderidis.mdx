**Savas Kalenderidis** is a Greek intelligence officer whom John Kiriakou counts as a personal friend, and about whom he was asked directly in December 2025: was it true that the CIA, together with Turkish intelligence, had tried to kill a Greek intelligence officer in 1999 outside the Greek embassy in Nairobi?<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="12:57" />

## The denial

Kiriakou did not need the name supplied. *"I in fact he's a good friend of mine Savas Kalenderidis. No, they did not."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:29" /> Pressed with *"You would say,"* he repeated it: *"No."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:29" />

The denial is one of a small number of occasions on which Kiriakou has been asked to confirm a specific American assassination attempt and has refused the premise outright rather than declining to answer.

## The man

His description of Kalenderidis is warm and unqualified: an absolutely lovely man, a national hero in Greece, whose friendship Kiriakou says he thoroughly enjoys.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:29" />

He also recommends his work, with a practical caveat. Kalenderidis runs what Kiriakou calls an awesome YouTube podcast, sometimes as often as four times a day, jumping on to record whenever something even slightly interesting to him reaches the news, but he does not speak a word of English, so the recommendation only holds for people who speak Greek.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:29" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:59" />

## Nairobi, 1999

What Kalenderidis was actually doing, in Kiriakou's account, was taking part in an operation to help protect Abdullah Öcalan, the founder of the PKK.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:59" />

Kiriakou then corrected himself mid-sentence on the crucial point of who found out, *"And the Greeks became aware I'm sorry the Turks became aware that he was in the Greek Embassy in Nairobi"*, and described the seizure: the Turks worked with Kenyan intelligence to divert part of Öcalan's motorcade, grabbed him, and turned him over to the Turks.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="13:59" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="14:29" />

Öcalan has been in Turkish custody ever since. Kiriakou's account attributes the operation to a Turkish–Kenyan liaison rather than to any American hand, which is consistent with his denial of the assassination story that prompted the question.
