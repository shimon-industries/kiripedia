**Jake Tapper** is the CNN anchor who became, improbably, part of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s prison survival strategy.

## How the press found him

The chain began with a letter. [Jesselyn Radack](/wiki/jesselyn-radack) had told Kiriakou to write to her once he was inside and she would forward it to a mailing list of some six hundred people; what he wrote described two crimes committed against him by guards, naming staff malfeasance, which is not something prisoners do. Unknown to him, Radack was a friend of [Arianna Huffington](/wiki/arianna-huffington), who ran it as a banner headline on the Huffington Post. It drew two million hits in the first week.<Cite s="2021-10-17-la-progressive" t="26:02" /><Cite s="2021-10-17-la-progressive" t="26:33" /><Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="2:13:30" /><Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="2:14:00" /> The precipitating incident was that within four days of arriving he had been solicited into what the law library told him was a class D felony — conspiracy to promote violence in a federal facility — an approach he read as an attempt to get him to kill a prisoner, or get one to kill him, apparently for sport.<Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="19:50" />

Tapper drove to [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) to interview him for CNN, at the head of a wave that ran to NPR several times, Playboy, The Economist, the Atlantic Monthly, Time, the back cover of The Nation, ABC News, all four broadcast networks, CNN, Fox and MSNBC — and a steady stream of Greek newspapers, Kiriakou being a dual citizen and, in Greece, a star for having stood up to the American government.<Cite s="2023-02-08-fortress-on-a-hill-john-kiriakou-ep-133-probe-dup-vs-6kmi7tkpf7q" t="30:46" /><Cite s="2021-10-13-sharon-kyle-la-progressive-interview-with-john-kiriakou" t="26:33" /><Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="33:34" /><Cite s="2021-10-17-la-progressive" t="26:33" /><Cite s="2024-01-16-fortress-on-a-hill-henri" t="30:58" /><Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="2:14:00" /><Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="20:20" /> [Arianna Huffington](/wiki/arianna-huffington) came too, with two young journalists.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="36:49" />

Saying yes to every request was a deliberate position rather than vanity. Kiriakou had been ordered by the judge to a minimum-security work camp — no fences, no bars, doors never locked — and the Justice Department, furious at the ruling, sent him instead to the regular prison across the street on the grounds that a trained CIA officer was a national security threat.<Cite s="2024-01-16-fortress-on-a-hill-henri" t="29:55" /> Having been put there, he used the access as a soapbox on prison conditions, medical facilities and violence encouraged by guards and the administration. It drove the warden crazy, and it became his second book.<Cite s="2024-01-16-fortress-on-a-hill-henri" t="30:25" /><Cite s="2025-04-23-dr-phil-podcast-secrets-lies-price-of-truth-part2" t="20:51" /> Alongside it he answered more than seven thousand of the sixty to eighty letters a day that arrived, and wrote op-eds longhand on a legal pad for the Los Angeles Times, the Guardian and the Los Angeles Review of Books.<Cite s="2022-09-13-hr-news-channel-hr-news-channel" t="26:15" /><Cite s="2022-09-13-hr-news-channel-hr-news-channel" t="26:46" />

## Armor

That access became protection. When guards threatened him, Kiriakou's standing reply was: *"Yeah? Well, let's see what Jake Tapper has to say about it. How about that — why don't we give him a call and see what he has to say?"* And they would back off.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="35:09" /><Cite s="2018-06-26-rdap-dan-rdap-dan" t="02:16" />

The clearest measure of it reached him secondhand. A guard with a reputation as a psychopath — a man who enjoyed hurting people — never once touched Kiriakou. Assigned to Central One unit, he mentioned it to an Italian prisoner, who said his friend John was in that unit. *"The CIA guy?"* the guard asked, and explained why he left him alone: *"that's all I need. I work an eight-hour day and then I go out to the parking lot and CNN is next to my car."*<Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="08:16" /><Cite s="2019-06-14-slow-news-day" t="08:48" />

It did not stop the warden trying. Called in and told *"I'm going to put you in solitary and we're going to forget where you are and throw away the key,"* Kiriakou decided he had nothing to lose and answered that he had gone nose to nose with al-Qaeda, with Hezbollah and with the Iranians, and had lived in a great deal worse than Loretto's solitary.<Cite s="2025-06-01-not-silenced-part-1" t="2:14:30" />

## "Jake was different"

Kiriakou generally files CNN under the go-along-to-get-along mainstream.<Cite s="2022-04-11-katie-halper-katie-halper" t="32:34" /> But Tapper, he says, was different: Tapper asked President [Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) at a press conference why he had insisted on prosecuting Kiriakou when Kiriakou had exposed a crime — *"and Obama just walked away from the podium."* The two have stayed in touch since Kiriakou came home.<Cite s="2023-02-18-savage-joy-marie-mann-savage-joy-and-john-kiriakou" t="37:20" />

## The prison interview, and the friendship

Asked in 2026 to name journalists he respects, Kiriakou reached first for independent reporters — Max Blumenthal, Aaron Maté, Kevin Gosztola, and [Jason Leopold](/wiki/jason-leopold), whom he notes *"we might call mainstream. He's with Bloomberg"* — before conceding that his mind *"doesn't even go to the mainstream outlets."*<Cite s="2026-03-12-deprogram-ted-rall-a-super-dumb-mass-murder" t="22:53" /><Cite s="2026-03-12-deprogram-ted-rall-a-super-dumb-mass-murder" t="23:24" />

Tapper is the exception, and Kiriakou discloses the reason: *"As mainstream as I'm going to get, and I'm biased cuz I like him and he's a good guy, is Jake Tapper."*<Cite s="2026-03-12-deprogram-ted-rall-a-super-dumb-mass-murder" t="23:24" /> The acquaintance dates from his imprisonment — *"he came to the prison to interview me in 2013, and we've been friends ever since"* — and Kiriakou lets stand, without dissent, his co-host's qualification that Tapper *"doesn't have full freedom of movement"* at a network and would be different with a show of his own.<Cite s="2026-03-12-deprogram-ted-rall-a-super-dumb-mass-murder" t="23:24" /><Cite s="2026-03-12-deprogram-ted-rall-a-super-dumb-mass-murder" t="23:54" />
