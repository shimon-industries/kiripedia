**Why the UAE was targeted** is the question a viewer put to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) during the 2026 war, having noticed that the Emirates and Dubai were being hit disproportionately compared with the other Gulf states by the count of missiles intercepted.

## The explanation

Kiriakou's answer looks past the current war to the previous one. *"I think that the reason why the UAE has been so disproportionately targeted is that the UAE has been at the forefront of the attacks against the Houthis."*<Cite s="2026-03-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-iran-war-viewer-q-a-de" t="05:17" />

The implication is that Iranian target selection is settling an older account. The Emirates are not being struck as a proxy for the United States but on their own record.<Cite s="2026-03-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-iran-war-viewer-q-a-de" t="05:17" />

## The complication he acknowledges

Kiriakou immediately notes what makes the choice costly for Iran. *"And um you know, despite the fact that Dubai is is home to thousands of Iranians and Iranian businesses, I think the Iranian government just wants to bloody the Emiratis' noses as best they can."*<Cite s="2026-03-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-iran-war-viewer-q-a-de" t="05:17" />

That concession is important to his wider account, because elsewhere he argues that Iran has strong reasons *not* to damage Dubai: the city is [Iran's outlet to the Western world](/wiki/dubai-as-irans-window) and the mechanism by which it evades sanctions. The resolution he offers is one of degree — enough fire to injure the Emirates, not enough to close the window.<Cite s="2026-03-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-ted-rall-s-live-iran-war-q-a-de" t="03:06" />

## The pattern of the strikes

The form the attacks took is consistent with that reading. Kiriakou describes drones and missiles hitting hotels *"especially in Abu Dhabi and Dubai"*, chosen because striking a famous hotel damages the tourist economy in a way that striking a military installation does not.<Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="04:13" /><Cite s="2026-03-03-the-deep-focus-iran-escalation-captured-spies-what-really-happens" t="04:45" />

## The cost to Iran

Kiriakou's overall verdict on the Gulf campaign is that it made enemies Iran did not need. The Gulf states, he says, *"would have very nervously just sat the whole thing out"* — until the hotels, the airports and finally [the Omanis](/wiki/bombing-the-omanis) were hit.<Cite s="2026-03-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-iran-war-viewer-q-a-de" t="10:28" /><Cite s="2026-03-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-ted-rall-john-kiriakou-iran-war-viewer-q-a-de" t="10:58" />
