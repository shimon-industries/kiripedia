**Walter Pincus** appears in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the destruction of [Gary Webb](/wiki/gary-webb-kill-the-messenger) as the specific mechanism by which the [CIA](/wiki/cia)'s denial reached print.

Describing how the Washington Post and the New York Times responded to [Dark Alliance](/wiki/dark-alliance), Kiriakou says the two papers *"essentially said on their front pages that Gary Webb had made the entire story up"*, and names the reporter: *"Walter pinkus an esteemed reporter for the Washington Post got the side of the CIA and put that on the very front page of the Washington Post saying that none of this was true this was just an anti- CIA uh tie raade and that nobody should pay any attention to it."*<Cite s="2025-03-12-john-kiriakou-the-gary-webb-story-cia-cocaine-and-a-media-a" t="07:56" />

His judgement follows immediately, and distinguishes error from complicity: *"well Pinkus was wrong Pinkus probably knew he was wrong but published anyway the bottom line here is that both the Washington Post and the New York Times leapt as quickly as they could to the defense of the CIA."*<Cite s="2025-03-12-john-kiriakou-the-gary-webb-story-cia-cocaine-and-a-media-a" t="08:28" />
