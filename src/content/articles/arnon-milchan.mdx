**Arnon Milchan** is the Israeli-American producer behind films such as *Pretty Woman*, *Fight Club* and *12 Years a Slave*, whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes as an Israeli super-spy and arms dealer working for LAKAM.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="32:03" /><Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="32:34" /> Kiriakou says the FBI had known for two decades that Milchan was an Israeli spy; after an operation was blown and a Hebrew-language interview cost him his rare ten-year U.S. visa, Milchan went to [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu), who lobbied Secretary of State [John Kerry](/wiki/john-kerry) three times to restore it — the basis of one of Netanyahu's corruption charges.<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="33:06" /> Kiriakou adds that FBI documents obtained under the [Freedom of Information Act](/wiki/freedom-of-information-act) place both Milchan and Netanyahu personally in the smuggling of U.S. [nuclear secrets](/wiki/israeli-nuclear-program).<Cite s="2024-07-27-saltcube-cia-enemy-of-the-state" t="33:36" />
