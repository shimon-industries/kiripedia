**Pete Calabrese**, known as Peter the Rabbit, was a prisoner at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identifies as a senior member of the Bonanno crime family.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="50:51" />

He belongs to the group Kiriakou calls [the Italians](/wiki/italians-at-loretto) — the faction he describes as honest, honourable and generous, the one he was closest to inside, and the one whose association eventually gave him a measure of protection from both prisoners and staff.<Cite s="2026-05-18-dead-drop-s2e1-welcome-to-loretto" t="16:29" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="1:02:27" />

## The objection

Calabrese appears in Kiriakou's account at the point where prison loyalties collided. [Frank Russo](/wiki/frank-russo), the former Cuyahoga County auditor who shared Kiriakou's bunk, had testified against [Jimmy Dimora](/wiki/jimmy-dimora) as part of his own plea, and was therefore marked inside as a cooperator — barred from the television room and required to eat at the [rat and pedophile table](/wiki/rat-and-pedophile-table).<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="50:16" />

Kiriakou recounts Calabrese asking him directly why he hung out with that rat. Kiriakou answered that Russo was his bunkmate first of all, and second that he was a really great guy — adding that he knew what Russo had done and was making no excuses for it, and that he knew Russo had informed on Dimora.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="50:51" />

Calabrese's reply was that Russo brought dishonour to the Italians. Kiriakou did not dispute it. He agreed that Russo did — and said it did not make him any less of a sweetheart.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="50:51" />

## Standing

Kiriakou's account of the exchange is notable for what did not follow it. Russo, he says, had such a hilarious sense of humour and was such a nice man that he won everybody over — everybody except the Italians.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="50:51" /> Calabrese's objection stood, and so did Kiriakou's friendship with Russo, and Kiriakou describes his own relationship with the Italians as unaffected: he continued to eat with them, and his closest friend inside, [Mark Lanzalotti](/wiki/mark-lanzalotti), delivered hot meals to the made men at Loretto every night.<Cite s="2026-06-01-dead-drop-s2e3-circle-of-friends" t="58:01" />

Kiriakou notes that a half-dozen of the men at Loretto were made men, and that unlike every other faction the Italians did not have and did not need a [shot caller](/wiki/shot-callers), being tight enough as a group to deal directly and honestly with everybody else.<Cite s="2026-05-18-dead-drop-s2e1-welcome-to-loretto" t="17:35" />
