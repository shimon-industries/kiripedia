**Dr. Ahmed Khatib** was the Iraqi-installed occupation governor of Kuwait, announced by Iraq hours after the August 2, 1990 invasion. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), then an eight-month CIA analyst on Iraq, had written a classified profile of Khatib for the agency's internal "Leadership Review" a month before the invasion *"just because he was interesting to me,"* and presented him to President George H. W. Bush in the Oval Office that morning.<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="3:37" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="4:40" /><Cite s="2025-11-03-dead-drop-s1e3-golden-boy" t="20:28" />

## Biography per Kiriakou's Oval Office briefing

Per Kiriakou: Khatib was a black Kuwaiti and a medical doctor who *"hates the royal family because his mother was a slave in the household of the royal family. They had slavery until 1955 on the Arabian Peninsula."*<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="4:40" /><Cite s="2025-11-03-dead-drop-s1e3-golden-boy" t="20:28" /> In reaction, Kiriakou says, Khatib became a communist.<Cite s="2026-06-26-the-kiriakou-report-why-cia-torture-was-illegal-and-ineffective" t="1:01" /> The royal family, after slavery ended, sent Ahmed *"to the American University of Beirut. He trained there as a doctor. But his college roommate was [George Habash](/wiki/george-habash), and together they founded the [Popular Front for the Liberation of Palestine](/wiki/pflp)."*<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="4:40" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="5:10" /><Cite s="2025-11-03-dead-drop-s1e3-golden-boy" t="20:59" />

Kiriakou's account of the Oval Office reaction: *"And the vice president says, 'Jesus Christ!' like that. And the president just goes like this and he says, 'Gentlemen, thank you. That'll be enough. I need to think about this.'"*<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="5:10" /> Kiriakou has given a shorter account of the same briefing elsewhere, saying that as part of his briefing he told the room that the Iraqis had named Khatib — a man he had personally met — as Kuwait's occupation governor that morning; he traces the later mass expulsion of Palestinians from Kuwait after its liberation in part to the association between Khatib's PFLP co-founding and the occupation government.<Cite s="2024-12-21-not-a-grayman" t="14:40" /><Cite s="2024-12-21-not-a-grayman" t="15:45" />
