**The Cyprus fruit relabel** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s canonical description of how Gulf Arab states got around the Arab League boycott of Israeli produce for decades before the [Abraham Accords](/wiki/abraham-accords) formally removed the fiction.

## Kiriakou's account

Asked on Mario Nawfal's show why the UAE was unlikely to sever relations with Israel even over a suspected false-flag operation, Kiriakou reached for the piece of trade history he thinks explains the ceiling: *"For the Emiratis, they have to weigh the entire relationship. And that's why maybe you or I would go to the Israelis and say enough is enough, but the Emiratis wouldn't do that."*<Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="14:33" /><Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="15:05" />

## The mechanic

He gave the mechanic in one paragraph. *"Everybody knows, going back to the 1970s, all of the Arab countries in the Gulf were buying Israeli fruit. What the Israelis would do is they would harvest the fruit, send it to Cyprus, put it in new boxes, let's say 'produce of Cyprus', and then send it to the Arab countries."*<Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="14:33" />

## What the Abraham Accords changed

The Accords, in his rendering, are cost reduction, not moral awakening. *"Now they don't have to do the extra step, so it's cheaper, you can buy more things. They've got this economic relationship."* His scoreboard on which Gulf states have followed through on the trade the Accords opened: *"The Saudis are not there yet. The Kuwaitis are not there yet. The Bahrainis are kind of there but aren't really comfortable with it. The Qataris aren't going to do it. And the Omanis just want to be left alone."*<Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="14:33" /><Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="15:05" />

## Why the UAE cannot break the tie

*"For the Emiratis, they have to weigh the entire relationship."* Kiriakou piles on the strategic locks: the [Iron Dome](/wiki/iron-dome) sits on Emirati soil, the country depends on the U.S. dollar, and Dubai runs a robust trade with [Iran](/wiki/iran) that goes back at least to his 2009 Senate Foreign Relations Committee visit to Dubai to speak with business leaders, *"they were trading literally everything. Everything from food to, you know, toner cartridges to cars and everything in between."*<Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="15:35" /><Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="16:06" /><Cite s="2026-07-31-mario-nawfal-bibi-confronts-vance-behind-closed-doors-over-anti" t="16:40" />
