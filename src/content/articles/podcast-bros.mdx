Sohrab Ahmari, appearing alongside [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) on the same Megyn Kelly episode, described the podcast bros as a group of young men influencing politics through podcasts, speaking to 20-30 year old men who are fairly apolitical. <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="13:33:00" /> Kiriakou separately discusses their influence alongside that of talk-radio host [Mark Levin](/wiki/mark-levin-iran-policy) as part of the same broader media landscape.
