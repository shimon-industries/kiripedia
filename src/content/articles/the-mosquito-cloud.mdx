The **mosquito cloud** is the detail [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) returns to when describing the night he spent at [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah)'s bedside, waiting for him to come out of surgery after the capture.

## The phobia

Kiriakou has described the fear as long-standing and disproportionate. Discussing his astrophotography in 2025, he mentioned that *"for people who know me personally, they know that I have an utterly irrational fear of mosquitoes"*, which makes sitting outside for two hours at a telescope difficult — *"I freaking hate it. But, you know, we all have our cross to bear."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="1:13:41" />

In an earlier account: *"I have a thing about mosquitoes my wife laughs about me but I consider myself to be a pretty tough guy but I'm freaked out at the thought of something drinking my blood it just is from when I was a little kid it's always bothered me."*<Cite s="2016-04-25-fort-collins-community-act-john-kiriakou-cia-whistle-blower-part-1" t="21:37" />

## Why he was unprotected

That association carried him into the hospital story. *"There were mosquitoes all over the hospital and I I was freaked out by them because I couldn't take the chloricquin because um it gave me such terrible nightmares. So, I just stopped taking the chloricquin, which prevents malaria once the malarial mosquito bites you. So, I'm just taking my my chances with the mosquito."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="1:14:13" /> (The auto-caption renders the drug as *chloricquin*; the reference is to chloroquine.)

## The room

*"I go into Abu Zubeda's room to wait for him to come out of surgery and it's like a cloud of mosquitoes in there. I'm like, great."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="1:14:13" />

His earlier telling gives the reason they were concentrated there — *"swarms of mosquitoes are just feeding on people's open wounds"* — in a facility where a bar of Irish Springs soap with a dozen syringes stuck into it served as the sterile store.<Cite s="2016-07-11-adventures-in-the-free-sta" t="08:01" /> He has said elsewhere that he turned the ceiling fan on full blast *"to blow the mosquitoes away from his wounds and frankly to keep them off myself."*<Cite s="2016-04-25-fort-collins-community-act-john-kiriakou-cia-whistle-blower-part-1" t="21:37" />

## The FBI agent

The consequence was cold. *"So, I put the ceiling fan on high blast and this was March. It was a little bit cold, you know? So, I'm sitting there like this. I'm freezing and this FBI agent comes in. He goes, 'Dude, it's freezing in here. What are you doing?' I said, 'I would rather freeze to death than be bitten by a Pakistani mosquito.'"*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="1:14:43" />

Told in 2025 that mosquitoes will not fly above a certain temperature, and that the night ahead would be in the high fifties, Kiriakou said he had not known that, and thanked his host.<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="1:14:43" /><Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="1:15:14" />
