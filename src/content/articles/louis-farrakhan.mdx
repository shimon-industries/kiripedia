**Louis Farrakhan** is the leader of the Nation of Islam. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), while Kiriakou was imprisoned at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) Farrakhan issued *"this perfectly timed statement … saying that I was a hero of the Muslim people for standing up for human rights. It saved me from the Muslims."*<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="23:16" />

Kiriakou's prison-yard arithmetic at the time: the Italians had adopted him on the basis that he was CIA rather than FBI (*"FBI are cops and rats. The CIA protected us from the Muslims"*); the Aryans had adopted him on the basis of a rumor that he had been a CIA *"Muslim hunter,"* an assassin; Farrakhan's statement secured him with the Muslims; and a Mexican cartel prisoner whose appeal Kiriakou wrote pro bono secured him with the Latin population.<Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="22:44" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="23:16" /><Cite s="2026-04-27-carlos-watson-part-everyone-missed" t="23:47" /><Cite s="2015-04-02-vice-news-the-government-turned-me-into-a-dissident" t="6:51" />

## Statement on Kiriakou's arrival at FCI Loretto

On the day [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) arrived at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), the Nation of Islam newspaper carried a statement from [Louis Farrakhan](/wiki/louis-farrakhan) declaring Kiriakou a hero of the Muslim people for having stood up for Muslim human rights. The timing was, as Kiriakou described it, fortunate: it ensured that the Muslim inmate population at Loretto had no grievance against him.<Cite s="2026-04-23-doug-bopst-escape-system-stop-being-manipulated" t="11:20" />

## 'Hero of the Muslim people'

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that in prison a Nation of Islam member gingerly handed him a newspaper: [Louis Farrakhan](/wiki/louis-farrakhan) had declared, because of Kiriakou's stand against torture, that he was *"a hero of the Muslim people."* The man told him he would *"not have any problems with us,"* then never spoke to him again — *"they don't talk to the white devil."*<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="2:09:16" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="2:09:47" /> In a separate telling of the same delivery, Kiriakou describes the messenger as *"200 pounds of knotted muscle, this perfect human specimen,"* who gingerly handed him the newspaper carrying Farrakhan's statement.<Cite s="2024-11-16-the-team-house" t="1:33:51" />

Separately, when a neo-Nazi inmate asked Kiriakou point-blank whether he had been a CIA hitman killing Muslims, Kiriakou — having prepared for the question — deliberately gave an ambiguous answer: *"it was war time and we all did things we weren't proud of."*<Cite s="2019-03-14-salem-access-tv-public" t="40:06" />

## The handshake

A May 2026 account adds the gesture that closed the exchange. The Nation of Islam prisoner who brought the newspaper into Kiriakou's room on his first day at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) — one of the *"big ripped guys"*, and, Kiriakou is careful to note when the interviewer assumes otherwise, a man who read it perfectly well himself — delivered the substance of [Farrakhan](/wiki/louis-farrakhan)'s statement: that Kiriakou was *"the hero of the Muslim people."*<Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="14:53" />

Kiriakou then put his hand out. The reply was *"I don't shake hands with the white devil,"* to which his own response was *"that's cool."* He treats the refusal as entirely compatible with the protection the statement conferred: he had no trouble from the Muslim population at Loretto thereafter.<Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="15:25" />
