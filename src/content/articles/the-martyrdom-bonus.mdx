The **martyrdom bonus** is the term for a payment John Kiriakou says captured al-Qaeda fighters repeatedly described to him during the counterterrorist operations he led in Pakistan in 2002.

## The account

Kiriakou has said that so many al-Qaeda personnel were caught in these operations that a pattern emerged, and that at what he calls the grunt level, men of eighteen and nineteen, they all told basically the same story. <Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-likely-ingested-ev1ozulvly8" t="22:26" /> In the fullest version he puts the number at many dozens and the ages a year higher: as soon as he arrived they began busting down doors, and the shock for him was that *"to a man, they were all children, 18, 19, 20 years old, and they all told exactly the same story."*<Cite s="2025-06-30-jamarl-thomas-how-mossad-cia-recruit-in-iran" t="37:13" /><Cite s="2021-02-01-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="24:50" />

In that story the recruits were illiterate, lived in isolated villages in their home countries, Yemen, Algeria, Saudi Arabia, *"whatever the country was"*, and wanted to marry. The obstacle they described was that no man would give his daughter to someone who could not read or write, had no job prospects and no skills of any kind. The local imam would then approach them and tell them they should not stay in the village, that they should go to Afghanistan and make jihad against the Americans, and that they would be paid three hundred dollars a month, with five hundred dollars going to their parents as a martyrdom bonus if they were killed. <Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-likely-ingested-ev1ozulvly8" t="22:59" /><Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4510-cia-torture-network-whistleblowing" t="29:19" /><Cite s="2025-06-30-jamarl-thomas-how-mossad-cia-recruit-in-iran" t="37:44" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:52:37" />

Kiriakou's summary of the economics is blunt: they could not find the United States on a map, but three hundred dollars is better than no dollars, and so they made their way to Afghanistan. <Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-likely-ingested-ev1ozulvly8" t="22:59" />

## The figure varies; the bonus does not

The monthly wage is not stable across tellings. It is three hundred dollars in most of them.<Cite s="2025-06-30-jamarl-thomas-how-mossad-cia-recruit-in-iran" t="37:44" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="1:52:37" /> In 2018 he gave it as fifty dollars a month, and in 2021 as five hundred.<Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4510-cia-torture-network-whistleblowing" t="29:50" /><Cite s="2021-02-01-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:53" /> The death payment is five hundred dollars in every version.

## What he rules out

The point of the anecdote, in Kiriakou's hands, is negative: it is an argument about what did *not* motivate these men.

He rejects the religious explanation directly and in its familiar form. To a man, he says, their account was about poverty, *"this was not about, my imam promised me virgins in heaven if I only believe in radical Islam hard enough and kill you for being innocent."*<Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4510-cia-torture-network-whistleblowing" t="28:42" /> They went through training, and it was no great matter to them: they did not know the Quran, had never read it, did not know the prayers, and *"they weren't religious, really, I mean, they were Muslim."*<Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4510-cia-torture-network-whistleblowing" t="29:50" /><Cite s="2021-02-01-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:53" />

He rejects the political explanation just as flatly at this level of the organisation: *"These guys couldn't find the United States on a map. They had no idea what US policy was. There was no inherent or intrinsic hatred of the United States. It's that they were poor and they had no hope."*<Cite s="2025-06-30-jamarl-thomas-how-mossad-cia-recruit-in-iran" t="38:15" />

Offered the phrase *"an economic draft"* by an interviewer, Kiriakou accepted it twice over: *"that's exactly what it was, that's exactly what it was."*<Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4510-cia-torture-network-whistleblowing" t="29:50" />

## How they became motivated

The corollary he draws is that the hostility was manufactured after the fact. Even the ones who had been through training were not really motivated to fight the United States, *"but then we started killing their families with drones or with rocket attacks."*<Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4510-cia-torture-network-whistleblowing" t="30:21" /> In the 2021 telling the same sentence runs through the family tree: they had no beef with the United States until it bombed their village, *"until we killed their cousin or their brother or their grandfather"*, and then they were up for the fight.<Cite s="2021-02-01-the-bitter-truth-with-abe-the-bitter-truth-with-abe" t="25:53" />

## The distinction Kiriakou draws

He separates this level of the organization from its leadership. Of the camp commanders and al-Qaeda's senior figures he gives political motives, American military bases in Saudi Arabia, which he identifies as Osama bin Laden's personal objection, and American support for Israel, which he says other leaders saw as a betrayal of poor Arabs. <Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-likely-ingested-ev1ozulvly8" t="23:30" /> Put to him directly that the foot soldiers had no quarrel with America and were going for the job, Kiriakou agreed. <Cite s="2025-04-16-the-dr-phil-podcast-likely-ingested-ev1ozulvly8" t="24:01" />

## The policy conclusion

Kiriakou treats the recruitment offer as prescriptive rather than merely descriptive. Asked how to get out of the cycle of violence and terrorism, his answer is two words: *"education and economic development."*<Cite s="2025-06-30-jamarl-thomas-how-mossad-cia-recruit-in-iran" t="38:15" />

He says he gives the Israelis the same advice, and reports that they do not want to hear it: *"You can't kill everybody. You may think you can, but you can't."* The only way out is to give people a reason to lay down their arms, self-determination, an economy, the ability to raise happy families, something to live for. *"You can't just occupy their land, steal their homes, starve their children, shoot their fathers, blow up their hospitals. That's not tenable."*<Cite s="2025-06-30-jamarl-thomas-how-mossad-cia-recruit-in-iran" t="38:46" />
