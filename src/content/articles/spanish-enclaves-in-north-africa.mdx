Spain holds several small territories on the North African coast surrounded by Morocco, the largest of them **Ceuta**, opposite the Strait of Gibraltar, with further *"teeny tiny"* enclaves farther down the coast.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="01:01" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="01:32" /> Kiriakou observes that most people do not know they exist, and that they are *"remnants of colonial times"*: the Moroccans dislike the arrangement, the Spanish like it, and it remains an unresolved irritant between the two states.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="01:32" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="02:04" />

## The colonial argument

The enclaves give Israel a rhetorical opening against a country that has been among its sharpest European critics. As Spain condemns Israel as an occupying colonial power, Israel has *"been trolling Spain for its colonial settlements in Africa like Ceuta."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="15:31" /> Kiriakou grants the point rather than dismissing it, while noting the difference in vintage: Spain was given the territories in treaties more than 400 years ago, *"so a little less recent than Israel laid claims to places like Rafah and the West Bank. But you can see Israel's point."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="15:31" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="16:02" /> Spain's standing with Washington and Jerusalem is poor on other grounds too: it has refused the United States the use of Spanish airspace for military strikes, and Spanish public opinion has been among the most outspoken against Israeli conduct in Gaza.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="15:31" />

## American policy

Kiriakou flags a post by [Thomas Massie](/wiki/thomas-massie), whom he calls a friend of the programme, reporting that it had recently become official United States policy to oppose Spain's colonies in Morocco, and to describe them as an occupation — accompanied by $40 million given to the Moroccan government.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="16:32" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="17:05" /> Massie's question, which Kiriakou repeats without answering, is how such language found its way into American law.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="16:32" /> Kiriakou draws no direct link between this and the [2026 crossings](/wiki/ceuta-migrant-crossings-2026), saying only that he has seen no direct evidence tying Israel to the surge but *"would not be surprised at all"* if American and Israeli intelligence knew what was coming and neither acted nor warned Madrid.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="16:02" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="17:05" />
