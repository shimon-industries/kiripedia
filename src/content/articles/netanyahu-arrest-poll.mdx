The **Netanyahu arrest poll** is the survey [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) leads with in July 2026 as evidence that American opinion on Israel has broken. He is precise about the threshold: *"a shocking new poll showing that a majority, not a plurality, a majority of Americans support the arrest of [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu)."*<Cite s="2026-07-30-john-kiriakou-is-it-over-for-netanyahu-poll-shows-most-amer" t="02:05" />

Two comparisons give it scale. The number is *"significantly higher than the share of Americans who supported the arrest of [Nicholas Maduro](/wiki/maduro-capture)"*; and according to CNN *"it represents a 50% point move just over the last 5 years."*<Cite s="2026-07-30-john-kiriakou-is-it-over-for-netanyahu-poll-shows-most-amer" t="02:37" />

Returning to the figure later in the episode, Kiriakou breaks the coalition down and uses it to pose a longer question about the alliance: *"where in the world America's relationship with Israel is headed. Where will it be in 5, 10, 30 years?"* His answer to the present state of it is that *"the vast majority of Democrats, the vast majority of independents, and a sizeable number of Republicans think that Benjamin Netanyahu committed war crimes and that he should be prosecuted."*<Cite s="2026-07-30-john-kiriakou-is-it-over-for-netanyahu-poll-shows-most-amer" t="27:29" /><Cite s="2026-07-30-john-kiriakou-is-it-over-for-netanyahu-poll-shows-most-amer" t="28:00" />
