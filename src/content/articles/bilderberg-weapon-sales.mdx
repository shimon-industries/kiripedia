**Bilderberg and weapon sales** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s claim about what elite steering-committee gatherings are really for. He places groups like the Council on Foreign Relations and Bilderberg squarely within the *"deep state."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:33:01" /> Someone he was once close to (a former associate who attended both Bilderberg and Shangri-La), who began attending such meetings, told him that whether it is Bilderberg or the Shangri-La Dialogue, *"it's all about weapon sales"* — the presidents, prime ministers and billionaires may discuss big global issues, but *"at the end of the day it all comes down to which defense contractor made what deal."*<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:33:34" /><Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:34:06" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:43:36" /> More recently, he adds, the focus has widened to tech — central bank digital currencies and AI.<Cite s="2026-05-08-jay-dyer-real-cia-history" t="1:34:36" /><Cite s="2026-04-29-jay-dyer-they-really-did-this" t="1:44:38" /> Kiriakou has given a similar account elsewhere, saying a former close associate who attended Bilderberg, Davos, and Shangri-La told him that aside from a handful of prominent figures like Bill Clinton, Bill Gates, George Soros, and Tony Blair, everyone else at those conferences simply wanted to sell weapons.<Cite s="2026-02-09-epic-real-estate" t="51:47" />
