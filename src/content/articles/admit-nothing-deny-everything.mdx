*"Admit nothing, deny everything, make counter-accusations"* is one of the twenty life lessons [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says the [CIA](/wiki/cia) taught him in training — some of them serious, some tongue-in-cheek, all of which he found mattered later.<Cite s="2017-08-20-internet-party-internet-party" t="2:15:22" /><Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="57:34" /> Inside the agency it was also merchandise: the line was printed on coffee mugs and t-shirts in the headquarters gift shop. *"It was kind of a joke, but it was kind of not a joke — and they were by far the most popular coffee mugs in the whole place. Everybody had them on their desks."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="28:46" /><Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="57:34" />

## Among the twenty rules

Kiriakou lists it alongside the others in the same set — *recruit spies to steal secrets or anything else you need*; *if calm is not to your benefit, chaos is your friend*; *eliminate potential problems using dirty tricks*; *let others do your dirty work*; *trust no one*; *always maintain plausible cover for action*; *seek and utilize available cover*; *you're stronger than you realize you are*.<Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab" t="1:12:24" /><Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="58:05" /><Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4439-torture-program-his-time-in-prison" t="35:26" /><Cite s="2021-03-24-the-platform-talk-podcast-from-cia-whistleblower-to-convicted-felon-to" t="30:27" /> In his account of prison, the most practically important of them was the cover rule: at the first sign of trouble, get off the X, because everyone still standing there when the guards arrive goes to solitary while the investigation runs.<Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="58:05" />

Asked how a whistleblower squares an idealistic act with a rulebook this cynical, Kiriakou's answer is that the two coexist by being switched on and off: the ethical core is the constant, and the rules are what he reaches for when he is in danger.<Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab" t="1:12:54" /><Cite s="2017-06-06-disruption-network-lab" t="1:13:26" />

## The fight at Loretto

The rule earned its keep at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto). After a fight that ended up involving a dozen men and blood on the floor, Kiriakou was called down and asked about it. *"I thought to myself: admit nothing, deny everything, make counter-accusations. I said, 'What fight?'"* Told there were witnesses placing him at the start of it, he answered that maybe *they* had been there when it started, maybe they were the ones who started it — *"what the hell do I know about any fight? I think you're the only ones who pick fights around here."* After several minutes of this the guards became frustrated enough to tell him to get out. They got nothing.<Cite s="2021-03-24-the-platform-talk-podcast-from-cia-whistleblower-to-convicted-felon-to" t="29:19" /><Cite s="2021-03-24-the-platform-talk-podcast-from-cia-whistleblower-to-convicted-felon-to" t="29:51" /> He treats the outcome as double-edged: he came out ahead of the investigation, and the yard learned he was not a snitch — part of why, he says, the Italians adopted him.<Cite s="2021-03-24-the-platform-talk-podcast-from-cia-whistleblower-to-convicted-felon-to" t="29:51" />

The transfer from one institution to the other is the point he keeps returning to: a slogan the agency treated as an in-joke turned out to be *"a serious rule for me in prison. I used it a lot."*<Cite s="2017-05-31-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="57:34" /><Cite s="2018-02-06-scott-horton-ep-4439-torture-program-his-time-in-prison" t="35:56" /> Prison, in his summary, is a combination of seventh grade, *Lord of the Flies* and a mental institution — with eighty-five per cent of American prisoners suffering from some form of mental illness — leaving the rules as what keeps a person safe.<Cite s="2017-08-20-internet-party-internet-party" t="2:15:56" />

## Merchandise, again

In 2019 Kiriakou raised the slogan again while discussing merchandise as a fundraising route for [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange)'s legal defence, proposing that the CIA's own line could be printed and sold to pay for *"the best lawyers in Washington"* — *"there would be nothing better than to have that actually work."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="28:46" />
