**Jason Miyares** was the attorney general of Virginia from 2022 until his defeat in November 2025, and one of the very few Republican officeholders [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) praises without irony.

## The expectation

Kiriakou is explicit that he got the man wrong at the outset. *"When Jason Miyares, the Republican — he's Cuban-American, born in Cuba — when he first was elected attorney general four years ago, I thought: oh my God, there we go. He's a right-wing extremist. It's going to be terrible."*<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:30" />

## The correction

The verdict he actually reached is short. *"No, he's been a perfectly fine attorney general."* The host supplied the metric implied — never in the news, enforcing the laws — and Kiriakou let it stand.<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:30" />

On election night he put it more strongly, and flagged the effort it cost him. *"Miyares has been a terrific attorney general. And it's hard for me to say that about a conservative Republican, but he has been an excellent attorney general."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="04:16" />

## The defeat

Miyares lost to a Democratic challenger whose private text messages about violence against Republicans had leaked less than two weeks before polling day. Kiriakou's description of the outcome contains no satisfaction at all: *"and he was just unceremoniously thrown out last night."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="04:16" />

He had, two weeks earlier, called the race *"a dead heat"*, and had noted that the received wisdom was of large-scale ticket-splitting in Miyares's favour. Neither the polling nor the wisdom held.<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:57" /><Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="04:47" />

## Why he is in the corpus

Miyares is useful evidence against reading Kiriakou's political commentary as reflexively partisan. The pattern he describes here — an anticipated extremist who turned out to do the job soberly, and whose removal Kiriakou regrets — is one he applies in both directions, alongside his placement of the incoming Democratic governor as a 1970s moderate Republican.

The test he applies to both is the same, and it is not ideological: whether the officeholder is *"never in the news, enforcing the laws."*<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="36:30" />
