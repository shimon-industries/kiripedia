**Iranian oil sanctions relief, 2026** refers to the American move, during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war), to lift sanctions on Iranian oil at sea. The sequence was put to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) as an absurdity — that after years of sanctions the United States went to war against Iran and then had to un-sanction its oil because prices were rising — and he agreed there was something to it.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:09" />

What he added was the currency dimension. [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) had been *"talking tough about, you know, we're going to have to just tighten our belts, we're going to have to just pay a little bit extra, we're going to have to be all in this together."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:09" /> Then an Indian ship paid for its cargo in yuan, and the Iranians told it that it was free to pass.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:42" />

The American objection, in Kiriakou's rendering, was existential rather than legal — *"wait a minute, the oil has to be paid for in dollars, otherwise it's going to crash our economy"* — and the Iranian reply was *"tough luck for you."* His account of what followed is one sentence: *"So then we lifted sanctions on Iranian oil."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:42" />

The transit fees Iran charged shipping during the same period are treated at [Strait of Hormuz transit fees](/wiki/strait-of-hormuz-transit-fees).
