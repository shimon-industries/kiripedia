President Donald Trump privately queried cabinet members about replacing the Director of National Intelligence, expressing frustration that the DNI had protected former deputy [Joe Kent](/wiki/joe-kent). <Cite s="2026-04-02-megyn-kelly-trump-fires-bondi-cia-s-mk-ultra-history-and-two-weeks-sp" t="23:46" />

During the second-term transition, Kiriakou said, Elon Musk and Vivek Ramaswamy had not yet been given security clearances, a fact he inferred from Trump's own delayed first President's Daily Brief, which he says was holding up DOGE's access to CIA information pending clearance and a warning from the incoming CIA director designate, John Ratcliffe, to keep quiet about classified programs.<Cite s="2025-05-05-austin-and-matt" t="13:42" />
