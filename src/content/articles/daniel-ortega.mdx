**Daniel Ortega** is the president of Nicaragua, and the subject of one of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s sharpest distinctions between protests he regards as manufactured and protests he regards as earned.

Discussing [the 2021 Cuba protests](/wiki/cuba-protests-2021), which he had just called a test run, Kiriakou volunteered Nicaragua as the contrasting case. *"I've been following these demonstrations in Nicaragua. They started off because of a shortfall in the social security system, and the government raised the price of bread. And if you're poor and the price of bread goes up, that's a pretty big deal. But this social security thing was a very big problem, and it began to weaken the government of Daniel Ortega."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="11:06" />

## The arrests

What moved his assessment was the treatment of the opposition before the vote. *"So the election comes up — there's supposed to be an election this year — and so far eight opposition leaders, that is the leaders of eight opposition parties, have been arrested and imprisoned. So now there's nobody to run against Ortega."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="11:36" />

His conclusion is stated as a judgement rather than a prediction: *"now to me, that necessitates a real honest-to-god uprising."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="12:07" />
