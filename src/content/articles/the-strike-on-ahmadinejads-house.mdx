**The strike on Ahmadinejad's house** was reported mid-broadcast on 28 February 2026, and [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) interrupted the show to address it — *"Ted, may I add one thing? … this also just in from Al Jazeera."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:37" />

The report placed one of the targeted sites in Tehran at Narmak, in the east of the city, *"believed to be the residence of former populist president Mahmoud Ahmadinejad."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:37" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:08" />

## Why he singled it out

Kiriakou's objection rests on Ahmadinejad holding nothing that could be described as a war aim. *"Now, listen. He's the former president. He's not in any position of authority. He's retired."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:08" />

He then added a characterisation that runs against the man's Western reputation, and against Ahmadinejad's own record in office: *"He was a moderate, too."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:08" />

The verdict follows without qualification. *"Targeting him is simply an act of murder."* Pressed, he repeated the word twice: *"It's gratuitous. It's gratuitous."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:08" />

## The comparison

To make the category clear he moved the case to a war he knows better: *"It would be like going to war against Afghanistan and and killing [Hamid Karzai](/wiki/hamid-karzai)"* — who, he noted, is still living in Kabul.<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:08" />

The point of the analogy is that a retired head of state in his own house is not a military target under any reading, and that striking one is therefore not a proportionality question but a killing without a purpose attached.
