**Frances Haugen** is the former Facebook employee whose 2021 disclosures [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) declines to classify as whistleblowing, in an argument he makes as part of a broader claim that the term is being deliberately blurred.

## The question put to him

Kiriakou was asked whether there is an effort to smudge the definition of a whistleblower, given a run of figures coming forward with disclosures that happened to reinforce the state's preferred narrative that more censorship was required.<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="29:30" /> He called it *"a very important question, actually."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="29:30" />

## The test

He began from the statutory ground, noting that many people do not know one exists: whistleblowing is *"bringing to light any evidence of waste, fraud, abuse, illegality or threats to the public health or public safety."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="29:30" /> The fuller treatment is at [the legal definition of whistleblowing](/wiki/the-legal-definition-of-whistleblowing).

## The verdict

Against that test Kiriakou placed Haugen's motive rather than her documents. *"Francis Hagen, in my view, is not only not a whistleblower, she's just a corporate spokesperson. What was her her motivation for coming forward? Her motivation was to make Facebook a stronger company. Her motivation was to was to improve Facebook's bottom line. It wasn't to expose wrongdoing."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="29:30" /> (The auto-caption renders her surname as *Hagen*; the reference is to Frances Haugen.)

He applied the same conclusion to a Twitter figure raised in the same question, a hacker who, in Kiriakou's account, *"was working with officers of the CIA and the FBI and the NSA to squelch free speech on Twitter."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="30:08" />

## The missing evidence

The second half of his objection is about proof. Kiriakou said he repeats it *"like a mantra"*: allegations of wrongdoing arrive without access to the primary source documents.<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="30:08" /> *"Well, if you're going to throw a grenade into the middle of the room, don't expect people just to nod their heads and thank you for it. I want to see what the evidence is... If you're going to make an accusation and call yourself a whistleblower, let me decide that. Let me judge you. And that's just not what we're seeing."*<Cite s="2023-07-06-am-wakeup-false-flags-real-terrorists-w-john-kiriakou" t="30:41" />
