**The survival asymmetry** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s repeated formulation of what victory would require of each side in the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war). He states it as a pair of unequal conditions: the United States can only win by destroying the Iranian state, while Iran wins by remaining standing.

## The two victory conditions

Kiriakou set out the American condition first: *"what we have to do to win in Iran is we have to overthrow the entire Iranian government and impose a pro-American pro-Israeli government in Tehran, which will never happen in a million years."*<Cite s="2026-03-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:00" /> Against that he set the Iranian one: *"All the Iranians have to do to win is to survive."*<Cite s="2026-03-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:00" />

The conclusion he draws from the pairing is a binary. *"So either we're going to have to commit to killing half of the people in Iran over the next 20 years or just walk away"*, and he added, both he and his interlocutor knew which of the two would happen.<Cite s="2026-03-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="01:00" />

He put the same proposition to the Tehran academic Mohammad Marandi in March 2026, framing it as the question the administration had not answered: the administration in the United States *"is very clear that it wants nothing short of regime change. At the same time, all Iran has to do to win is to survive."*<Cite s="2026-03-06-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="26:03" />

## Why survival is available to Iran

Kiriakou grounds the Iranian side of the asymmetry in scale rather than in will. Donald Trump had said he could see the attacks running for weeks; Kiriakou's answer was that weeks of attacks change nothing, because *"Iran is an enormous country. It has 92 million people. Its economy is diversified even under sanctions. It's military is spread out. It has a navy."*<Cite s="2026-03-06-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="26:03" />

On the same reasoning he treats a ground invasion as unavailable rather than merely unwise. Asked whether American ground troops would stop the drone and missile attacks on the Gulf Cooperation Council states, he answered that ground troops *"would be the start of World War III"* and that the attacks would only continue or accelerate.<Cite s="2026-03-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="05:10" />

Asked directly whether the campaign could avoid ending in disaster, he allowed only a downgrade of the adjective. *"It could result in a partial disaster"*, before returning to the same verdict: *"It's not going to work. It's not. It can't."*<Cite s="2026-03-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="10:52" />
