The **shooting house** is the close-quarters decision range at [the Farm](/wiki/the-farm), and the part of his weapons training [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes with the most relish.

## Getting there

The course built up slowly. Trainees were issued heavy leather belts with holsters and a pistol — at first Brownings — and required to wear them at every moment they were not in bed or in the shower. Kiriakou, who had never handled a gun, found it exciting: *"this is gonna be some real James Bond shit."* Range work ran at seven metres, then twelve, then twenty-five, day after day, until he had a callus on his thumb from loading magazines. Then it was done at night, which he says exposes how bad a shooter really is — *"you're like at risk of shooting the guy standing next to you."*<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="26:25" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="26:56" />

## The house

In the shooting house the trainee moves through rooms, looking round corners and up, while figures appear at windows: a man with a machine gun, or a woman holding a baby. There are roughly three quarters of a second to decide before being shot with a paintball. Miss a hostile and you are dead; shoot the woman or the child who pops up to see what is happening outside and *"you might as well be dead."*<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="27:28" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="27:58" />

Kiriakou says he aced it every single time, and explains it in terms of temperament rather than skill: a very steady hand, half a second to classify hostile or civilian, and — in an explicit callback to his account of the [sociopathic tendencies](/wiki/polygraph) the agency recruits for — a heart that does not race in that situation at all.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="27:58" />

## From the car

The same exercise was then run from a vehicle. The instructors disable the trainee's car remotely; a van a hundred feet away slides its door open and a robot begins firing blanks. The drill is to duck below the robot's line of sight, unbuckle, crawl across to the passenger side, get out on your belly and disable it.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="28:31" />

Kiriakou could not get a clear shot, so he shot out the windows of his own car and hit the robot through them. The instructors ran onto the range — *"Damn it, Kiriakou! That was the last decent car we had"* — and he defended himself with their own doctrine: they had told the class to do whatever was needed to get the shot, and to *"get off the X"*; with the car disabled, the only way off the X was to kill the robot.<Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="29:03" /><Cite s="2025-12-29-dead-drop-s1e8-the-james-bond-academy" t="29:34" />
