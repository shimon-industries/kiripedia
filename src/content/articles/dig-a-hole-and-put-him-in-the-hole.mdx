**"Dig a hole and put him in the hole"** is the classified CIA cable [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has cited across years of interviews as the one that convinced him the American torture programme had crossed into murder. It sits, in his account, alongside the wider question no one at the agency ever answered, *"how's that legal? Nobody ever gave the CIA permission to torture people to death."*

## The cable

Kiriakou describes seeing the exchange during the torture programme's active phase. *"I remember seeing a cable, it was the first time I'd seen a cable like this, where the so-called interrogators went too far, which we all predicted they would. And they murdered a prisoner during the interrogation and said, 'What do we do?' And then the base office wrote back and said, 'Just dig a hole and put him in the hole.'"*<Cite s="2026-07-28-to-my-sons-and-daughters-truth-betrayal-and-the-cost-of-speaking-out" t="05:09" /><Cite s="2026-07-28-to-my-sons-and-daughters-truth-betrayal-and-the-cost-of-speaking-out" t="05:40" />

## The framing

Kiriakou treats the cable as the end of any hypothetical defense of the programme. *"Nobody ever gave the CIA permission to torture people to death. And we just dig a hole and pretend the guy never existed? But none of those people were ever prosecuted."*<Cite s="2026-07-28-to-my-sons-and-daughters-truth-betrayal-and-the-cost-of-speaking-out" t="05:40" />

He pairs it with the sequence he uses to describe the shape of accountability at the [CIA](/wiki/cia): the officer who exposed the programme went to prison; the officers who ran it *"all got away."* Speaking to Ben Kaplan on *To My Sons and Daughters*, he named the ones who appear on video in the Spy Museum's torture-programme exhibit, [Jose Rodriguez](/wiki/jose-rodriguez), [James Mitchell](/wiki/james-mitchell), and [Bruce Jessen](/wiki/bruce-jessen), as the ones who *"all got away."*<Cite s="2026-07-28-to-my-sons-and-daughters-truth-betrayal-and-the-cost-of-speaking-out" t="04:08" /><Cite s="2026-07-28-to-my-sons-and-daughters-truth-betrayal-and-the-cost-of-speaking-out" t="04:39" /><Cite s="2026-07-28-to-my-sons-and-daughters-truth-betrayal-and-the-cost-of-speaking-out" t="05:09" />
