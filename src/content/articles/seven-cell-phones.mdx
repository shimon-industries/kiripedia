[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes the failure of his first marriage as a structural feature of the job rather than a personal one, and calls the pattern "uniquely CIA."

## What she knew

His first wife was a ballet teacher. "She knew I worked for the CIA and that was it. She didn't know anything else."<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="44:06" />

He renders the daily conversation as a closed loop: they are overseas, he comes home from work, she asks how work was — great — what did you do — nothing — who did you talk to — nobody.<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="44:36" />

## The phones

Then the second half of it: "one of my seven cell phones would ring at night, at midnight, and I would speak in Arabic and then leave and not come back for a day."<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="44:36" />

The question that produced, in his telling, was: "what's her name?"<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="45:06" />

"And so fourteen years of marriage down the drain, because I couldn't tell her what I did for a living."<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="45:06" />

## The institutional answer

The remedy the agency effectively encourages is to marry inside it, which Kiriakou did. "So I married a CIA officer. Which happens. Which is encouraged, because you're both cleared. And so you can talk about work."<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="45:06" />

He describes the internal social apparatus that follows from the same logic — a softball league, a quilting club, a choir, "any club that you could possibly want they have" — and gives the reason: everybody is cleared, so people can talk about work without fearing they will accidentally reveal a secret.<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="45:36" />

It does not solve the underlying problem. Kiriakou puts the [CIA](/wiki/cia)'s divorce rate at eighty per cent, "the highest of anywhere in the U.S. government."<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="45:36" /> His own second marriage also ended, after prison. Asked in 2019 whether he had married again, he said no: "I think it's just not for me."<Cite s="2019-02-21-foresight-with-ken-weaver-john-kiriakou-whistleblower-at-the-cia" t="46:07" />
