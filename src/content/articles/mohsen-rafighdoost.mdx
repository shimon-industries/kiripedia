**Mohsen Rafighdoost** is an Iranian journalist and political figure whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identifies as a co-founder, alongside Ayatollah Khomeini, of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps after the 1979 Iranian revolution.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="10:49" /> Kiriakou describes him as the voice that recorded the intro to the pre-revolution propaganda tapes smuggled into Iran to turn the population against the [Shah](/wiki/reza-pahlavi).<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="11:22" />

On the PBD Podcast, Kiriakou notes, Rafighdoost — brought on as *"the propaganda guy"* — was asked point-blank whether the opposition's claim that SAVAK killed 100,000 people had been a lie. In Kiriakou's account of the exchange he heard on tape, Rafighdoost replied that *"many documents have come out"* since, and that *"it was only 3,000, 3,500"* political prisoners under the [Shah](/wiki/reza-pahlavi), not 100,000.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="11:54" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="12:24" /> Rafighdoost went further and offered his personal verdict that *"Shah was a better guy than Khomeini,"* and that the Shah's belief in modernisation of Iran was *"definitely better than [the] Islamic Republic."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="12:24" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="12:56" />

Kiriakou treats the admission as evidence that a founding figure of the Islamic Republic's security architecture had, four decades on and speaking against interest, effectively conceded that a foundational revolutionary talking point about the [Shah](/wiki/reza-pahlavi)'s prison population had been an exaggeration by roughly a factor of thirty.<Cite s="2026-04-23-pbd-podcast-ex-cia-officer-confronted-over-zionist-accusa" t="11:54" />
