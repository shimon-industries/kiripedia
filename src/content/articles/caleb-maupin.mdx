**Caleb Maupin** is an American journalist and political analyst, and one of the recurring guests on [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s radio programme [The Backstory](/wiki/the-backstory).

Kiriakou's standing introduction is a straightforward endorsement: *"journalist and political analyst Caleb Maupin — Caleb is one of the best informed people out there."*<Cite s="2021-03-13-caleb-maupin-sanctions-us-efforts-in-eurasia" t="00:12" />

## Agreement, with a boundary

After a long exposition from Maupin on capitalism, automation and the technological leap, Kiriakou endorsed the substance: *"I agree with Caleb's analysis. I think you're exactly right — and something that not a lot of people either understand or have put a great deal of thought into, but I think you've hit it on the head."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="19:53" />

He also proposed booking Maupin against the libertarian economist Mark Frost — as a conversation rather than a debate, on the grounds that they are *"two very smart, very reasonable people that we like."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="25:00" />

## On basic income

The same exchange produced Kiriakou's own comment on universal basic income, offered as political observation rather than economics. *"That argument didn't fly in the New York mayoral race when Yang brought it up. Just after he announced his candidacy the New York Times thought it was a great idea and said that he could actually win this thing — there was a fawning tribute to him about six months ago, and I think he ended up coming in fourth. People just weren't buying into it."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="23:58" />

His own position was procedural: *"it should be debated. You don't have to like it, but we should be talking about it."*<Cite s="2021-07-13-caleb-maupin-cuba-crisis-marxism-automation" t="24:30" />
