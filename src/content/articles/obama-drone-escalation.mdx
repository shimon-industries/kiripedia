**Obama's drone escalation** is the comparison [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) uses when the drone programme is discussed as something [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) inherited rather than something he enlarged. Rather than argue the point he states two months: *"In George W. Bush's last month as president, he killed 24 people with drones. In Barack Obama's last month as president, he killed 440 people with drones."* The question he attaches is *"Like, is that really the country we want to be?"*, and his interviewer's gloss — trial by drone — goes unchallenged.<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:02:08" />

He presents the figures as the second half of a pair. The first is the appointment: the president he had voted for, contributed to and taken his children to see inaugurated *"makes [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan) CIA director. John Brennan, who was the executive director of the CIA during the torture program, was one of the founders, the fathers of the torture program."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:01:38" /><Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:02:08" />

The escalation belongs, in his account, to the same disposition that produced his own prosecution — a president described to his lawyers by a National Security Council attorney as having *"a Nixonian obsession with national security leaks."*<Cite s="2023-09-22-ex-cia-officer-exposes-the-torture-program" t="1:23:29" />
