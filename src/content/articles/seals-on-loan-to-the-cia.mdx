A viewer asked why, if the [CIA](/wiki/cia) has its own tactical capability, the bin Laden operation was given to the Navy SEALs. John Kiriakou called it a good question and then rejected the premise.

## It was a CIA operation

"They did," Kiriakou said. "That Navy SEAL team was on loan to the CIA. So it was a CIA operation."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:06" /> He specified the chain of command: the raid "was directed by the CIA's Counterterrorism Center," and "all of the Navy SEALs were temporarily CIA contractors."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:37" />

The arrangement he describes is a legal one as much as an operational one. The military personnel executing the raid did so wearing, in effect, a different institutional hat for its duration, which places the operation under the agency's authorities rather than the Pentagon's.

## Whether capture was possible

Asked whether bin Laden could have been taken alive and brought to trial at The Hague or in New York, Kiriakou answered without qualification: "Yeah. Yeah, of course there was."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:37" /> The outcome was a choice, not a constraint. He attributed the choice to [Barack Obama](/wiki/barack-obama) directly, citing the president's own account: "Obama said in his memoir — one of his three memoirs — that he made the decision just to kill him. Just kill him."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="55:37" />

## The legal basis

Asked whether that was lawful, Kiriakou said it was, and set out the mechanism. Under US law, if "the president or the president's representative — who is usually the national security adviser or the CIA director — determines that a person is a clear and present danger to the United States, any American installation or any US person," meaning a citizen or green card holder, "you can kill that person legally."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:08" /> He added the condition: "But the president has to order it."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:08" />

When [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) restated this as a president holding the power of life and death over every human being on earth, like a feudal lord, Kiriakou agreed — "Correct" — and then volunteered his own view of it: "Isn't that awful?"<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:39" /> Told that it should not be so, he answered: "No, no, it shouldn't be."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="56:39" />

## What came afterwards

Kiriakou noted that the capability has since been broadened. There are now teams belonging to the Counterterrorist Center, teams belonging to the [Special Activities Division](/wiki/special-activities-division), and a further component he names as global resources.<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:10" /> Any of those three, or elements of them, can be sent "pretty much anywhere in the world to kill who you want to kill."<Cite s="2025-08-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:10" />
