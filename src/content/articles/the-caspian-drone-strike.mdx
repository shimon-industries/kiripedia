**The Caspian drone strike** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s name for the July 2026 incident in which Ukrainian drones, sent out under a declared 40-day drone campaign against Russia, hit an Iranian ship in the Caspian Sea, killed a sailor, and produced a brief window in which Kiev tried to turn the mistake into a joint U.S.–Ukraine casus belli against Iran.

## Kiriakou's read

Sitting on Judge Andrew Napolitano's intelligence-community roundtable at the end of July 2026, Kiriakou treated the pivot Zelensky attempted as behavioural rather than diplomatic. *"That's exactly how this played out. I'm too, this may have been in part as a result of let's say a suggestion from [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) that, you know, the conflict in Iran is expanding. It's now including the Houthis in Yemen, now the Egyptians, the Jordanians, the Iraqis. You want to be on the right side of this. Maybe you jump in on our side and help us out even symbolically."*<Cite s="2026-07-31-judge-napolitano-judging-freedom-intel-roundtable-weekly-wrap-31-july-4pm-and" t="03:39" /><Cite s="2026-07-31-judge-napolitano-judging-freedom-intel-roundtable-weekly-wrap-31-july-4pm-and" t="04:10" />

He read Zelensky's calculus as image, not policy. *"[Zelensky](/wiki/volodymyr-zelensky) looks like a tough guy. He hits an Iranian ship, doesn't make an apology, but there's no real follow-through. And all of a sudden it makes Ukraine a player in the Middle East at least for a minute."*<Cite s="2026-07-31-judge-napolitano-judging-freedom-intel-roundtable-weekly-wrap-31-july-4pm-and" t="04:10" />
