**Small-country intelligence services** are a category [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he has dealt with extensively and is rarely asked about. Presented in May 2026 with the question of what his experience was with the services of *"really small countries like Jamaica or Fiji or Bolivia or random sub-Saharan African nations,"* his reaction was that nobody had ever asked him that before.<Cite s="2026-05-28-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="34:53" />

## Djibouti

His illustration is a visit to the Djibouti intelligence service, and the measure he reaches for is his own house. *"I'm not exaggerating when I tell you that this very humble rental townhouse that I live in was twice the size of the Djibouti intelligence service."*<Cite s="2026-05-28-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="34:53" />

He narrows the comparison to the man at the top: *"my humble little living room here, which has room for a couch, a chair, a coffee table, and a TV — I'm looking at it — was twice the size of the office of the director of the Djibouti intelligence service."*<Cite s="2026-05-28-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="35:25" />

## The method

The lesson Kiriakou draws from the small services is about manner rather than resources. His practice, across many such countries, was to go in and *"treat them with respect"* — addressing the director as *"your Excellency."*<Cite s="2026-05-28-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="35:25" />
