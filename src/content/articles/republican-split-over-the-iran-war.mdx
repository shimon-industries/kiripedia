[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s assessment of the 2026 [war with Iran](/wiki/iran-12-day-war), delivered on a British panel programme a month into the campaign, was that [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) had made *"a disastrous miscalculation. Where do we even begin?"* The consequence he identified was domestic: *"there's a very significant split right now within the Republican Party here in the United States."*<Cite s="2026-03-30-piers-morgan-uncensored-you-don-t-stand-up-to-evil-you-lose-iran" t="31:11" />

## The broken expectation

The split, in his account, runs between the administration and the voters who elected it. *"There were a lot of Republican voters who voted for Donald Trump because they believed that he really was the no-foreign-wars president."*<Cite s="2026-03-30-piers-morgan-uncensored-you-don-t-stand-up-to-evil-you-lose-iran" t="31:43" />

Kiriakou grounds that expectation in a specific moment: *"You might recall February 14th, Valentine's Day 2016, during a Republican debate in which Donald Trump called George W. Bush a war criminal for getting the United States involved in the war in Iraq."* Against that record he sets the position a decade later: *"and now all of a sudden, it's as though it's [John McCain](/wiki/john-mccain)'s neoconservative Republican Party again."*<Cite s="2026-03-30-piers-morgan-uncensored-you-don-t-stand-up-to-evil-you-lose-iran" t="31:43" />

## The two wings inside the administration

Kiriakou describes the incoherence as staffed rather than merely rhetorical. Trump *"surrounded himself with some people like [Tulsi Gabbard](/wiki/tulsi-gabbard), for example, and [J.D. Vance](/wiki/jd-vance), who reportedly have been opposed to these foreign interventions — but then others like [Marco Rubio](/wiki/marco-rubio), who is sort of the successor to the John McCain wing of that party."* The result: *"the party and the president himself don't know which direction to go."*<Cite s="2026-03-30-piers-morgan-uncensored-you-don-t-stand-up-to-evil-you-lose-iran" t="32:14" />
