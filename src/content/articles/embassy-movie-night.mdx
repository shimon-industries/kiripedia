**Embassy movie night** is a detail [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) offered in July 2026, by his own description as an aside, about one of the very few bits of joy available during his posting to [Pakistan](/wiki/pakistan-station-2002) after September 11th.<Cite s="2026-07-28-john-kiriakou-shocking-new-footage-of-lindsey-graham-moments" t="16:42" />

## The routine

The station was working seven days a week and fourteen hours a day, which Kiriakou calls ridiculous. On Wednesday nights the ambassador hosted a film in the embassy's little mini theatre.<Cite s="2026-07-28-john-kiriakou-shocking-new-footage-of-lindsey-graham-moments" t="16:42" />

They watched whatever they could get their hands on, and the viewing became a running joke: the audience shouted at the screen and threw popcorn at it. The example he remembers is a film he gives as *How Shantae Got Her Man Back*, at which the room was screaming *"don't take him back, Shantae, he's no good."*<Cite s="2026-07-28-john-kiriakou-shocking-new-footage-of-lindsey-graham-moments" t="17:12" />

## Why he mentioned it

The memory arrived by association. Kiriakou had just said he would not want to watch the documentary footage of [Lindsey Graham](/wiki/lindsey-graham) then in the news, and then corrected himself, he would force himself to watch it, and would end up shouting at the screen.<Cite s="2026-07-28-john-kiriakou-shocking-new-footage-of-lindsey-graham-moments" t="16:42" />

He finishes the parallel explicitly: *"That's how I feel about Lindsey Graham. I'm going to shout at the screen."*<Cite s="2026-07-28-john-kiriakou-shocking-new-footage-of-lindsey-graham-moments" t="17:42" />
