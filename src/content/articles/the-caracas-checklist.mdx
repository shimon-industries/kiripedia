**The Caracas checklist** is the answer [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gave to a viewer who asked what the [CIA](/wiki/cia) would have had him do in Venezuela, an itemised account of what a regime-change operation consists of, followed by his standing objection to the whole enterprise.

## The involvement

He began by refusing to hedge on the premise. *"first of all, the CIA is 100% yes involved in whatever it is that our government is doing right now in Venezuela."*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="00:31" />

## The steps

The first is psychological. *"what you need to do is operationally put pressure on Maduro to to just run away. You want Maduro to run away."* The plan reported in the press, he notes, was to make him think he could, which is why *"we now know that the CIA tried to recruit his private pilot"* so that a flight to Havana would land instead in Key West.<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="00:31" /> Told that such a trick would mean no future target of American regime change could believe anything Washington promised, Kiriakou agreed: *"That's exactly right."*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="01:03" />

The rest is physical, and he numbered it himself. *"you have to secure the the presidential palace whether Maduro is in it or not. Um number number two, you have to um either co-opt the military or bombard the military if they can't be co-opted. Uh number three, you have to take control of the television and radio stations, at least the ones in Caracus."*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="01:03" /> Then: *"you have to take over the the main intersections of the capital."*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="01:34" />

Asked whether these are CIA or military tasks, he answered that the distinction has largely lapsed: *"It would be it would be joint operations … Since 9/11, most of these kinds of operations would be joint with special forces."*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="01:34" />

## The objection

The checklist exists in Kiriakou's telling mainly to be dismissed. *"like I've said in the past … overthrowing the government is the easy part."* When his interlocutor supplied the other half, *"It's governing"*, Kiriakou finished it: *"It's governing that's the hard part. What do you do the next day?"*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="01:34" /><Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="02:04" />

He endorsed two further measures put to him, cyber attacks on Venezuelan internet infrastructure and shutting the banking wire system so that regime officials could not move money abroad, with *"Exactly right."*<Cite s="2025-12-17-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-john-kiriakou-on-cia-coup-tactics-trump-s-wmd" t="02:04" />
