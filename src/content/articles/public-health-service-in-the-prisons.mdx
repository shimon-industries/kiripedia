**The Public Health Service in the prisons** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the same federal uniformed service seen from two sides of his life, and of what he concluded from the difference.

## Two encounters with one service

Over fifteen years at the [CIA](/wiki/cia) Kiriakou had occasion to deal many times with medical professionals from the United States Public Health Service, and found them *"just terrific — people, good medics, committed, patriotic."*<Cite s="2016-09-29-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="08:52" />

His experience in prison was the exact opposite. He was shocked to find that Public Health Service professionals were the physician assistants seeing patients every day, and that these were *"the dregs of the Public Health Service — these were the people who just couldn't cut it anywhere else in government, and so they were dumped into the prisons."*<Cite s="2016-09-29-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="08:52" />

## The doctor who left

His illustration is a doctor at [FCI Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto) of whom most prisoners he knew spoke highly. About six months into Kiriakou's sentence the man resigned — he did not ask for a transfer, he resigned and went into private practice — because the [Bureau of Prisons](/wiki/bureau-of-prisons) would not let him treat prisoners the way he wanted to, meaning send them to outside appointments and prescribe the medications he thought they needed.<Cite s="2016-09-29-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="09:23" />

The institution then went about six months with no doctor at all, and finally hired a failed child psychiatrist who had been asked to leave one of the major American teaching hospitals.<Cite s="2016-09-29-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="09:23" />

Kiriakou closes the argument with an observation he offers as arithmetic rather than insult: half of all doctors graduate in the bottom half of their class, somebody has to be ranked last in medical school, and frequently those doctors end up in prisons.<Cite s="2016-09-29-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="09:56" />
