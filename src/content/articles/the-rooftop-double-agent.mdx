Kiriakou gave this account to explain a clip of himself circulating without context, in which he insists a frightened man accompany him to a hotel roof. His summary of it is exact about the distinction: *"I wasn't going to throw him off the roof. I wanted him to think I was going to throw him off the roof."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="46:51" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="47:23" />

## The arrangement

In 2001, having been evacuated from Athens for his own safety and put on a job training Middle Eastern intelligence services that left him bored, he took a call from a friend who was a station chief in the Middle East.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="47:23" /> The friend had recruited a double agent and could not safely meet him: *"the guy's insisting on meeting only with the chief. Can you fly out here every month and pretend to be me?"* Kiriakou agreed on the spot.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="47:23" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="47:56" />

The agent was working for *"one of our gravest enemies in the world"* and did not know that Kiriakou knew it. After each meeting he went directly to that country's embassy and reported what had been said.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="47:56" />

## The order to kill

The hostile service eventually instructed the agent to kill Kiriakou at their final meeting. *"Headquarters freaked out and I was like, 'Relax.' I said, 'The guy's afraid of his own shadow. He's not going to kill me. He should be worried that I'm going to kill him, frankly.'"*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="47:56" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="48:28" />

What followed was, by his own description, largely mischief on top of a plan: *"I just kind of wanted to scare him a little bit."* He took the man into the stairwell and told him the meeting would happen on the hotel roof rather than in a room; the agent was *"genuinely petrified"*, and when Kiriakou finally laughed and told him *"I'm just pulling your leg. You don't really think I'm going to throw you off the roof"*, *"He started crying."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="48:28" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="48:59" />

He closes the account without elaborating on what came between: *"one thing led to the other to the other. And he's been in prison since the spring of 2001, and he's going to die there."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="48:59" />
