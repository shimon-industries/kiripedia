John Kiriakou spent part of a broadcast working through, in public, an engagement he was due to keep that same night and plainly wished he had not accepted.

## How it happened

The booking came through a friend. Kiriakou described a PR man who had run into a very bad period, his wife left him, he lost his job, former employees sued him, he was arrested, and said that he had stayed loyal through it: "I'm one of the most loyal people out there."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:11" /> The man had once done public relations for [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange), WikiLeaks and [Edward Snowden](/wiki/edward-snowden), and now works for the far right "because they pay him and the far left walked away."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:44" />

The request was simple: would Kiriakou give a speech on the anniversary of 9/11 about what the day meant to him? "I said, yeah, sure. He said, I'll give you $500. I said, okay, great. That was it. That was the extent of it."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:44" />

Only later did someone see a poster advertising the event under the banner of a 9/11 engineers-and-architects organisation, "featuring, you know, John Kiriakou and all these people who I've always considered to be like deeply insane."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:19" /> His complaint to the friend, "Dog gone it, Trevor, you put me with a 9/11 truth organization", drew the answer "didn't you do your due diligence?" Kiriakou's reply was that he had not: "I trusted you. We're friends. You asked me to do it."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:19" />

## The decision not to withdraw

His sister called and told him he had to pull out. Kiriakou refused: "I can't. I made a commitment. I have to stand by my commitments."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:49" /> He said he had thought about it for a couple of weeks before deciding to go.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:49" /> Dennis Kucinich, he noted, was introducing him.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:49" />

The fee was the whole of the original attraction. "It's an honorarium," he said, "which is the only reason I was doing it. It's $500 I didn't have yesterday", money earmarked, he admitted, for something he had seen on eBay.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="07:19" /><Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="08:24" /> His manager was less impressed, telling him his rate was $10,000 and that he would be strangled for taking $500; [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) said he was on the manager's side.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="07:19" /><Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="07:51" /> Kiriakou added a further grievance, the talk was at 9:45 at night, well past when he is usually wound down.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="07:51" />

## What he intended to say

Kiriakou set out his speech in advance, and it was not a concession. He planned to tell the room: "you and I will never ever agree on whether or not Building 7 was controlled demolition. I'll never believe that it was. I will never believe that such a thing as thermite paint exists and is suddenly somehow spontaneously explosive."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="59:49" /><Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:00:20" />

Having said that, he intended to give them the part he does believe: that he can "take a shit on the Saudis for hours without even taking a breath, and on the Israelis, both relative to the 9/11 attacks. And so for sure, that's what I'm going to do."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:00:50" /> Told the Pakistanis probably deserved some too, he agreed: "That's right."<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:00:50" />

## The lunch

Earlier that day a lunch accompanied the event. Kiriakou found himself beside a man who wanted to explain that the killing of Charlie Kirk had been a shot to the jugular from 200 metres, and to ask who is good at such precision, the Israelis, concluding it was a Mossad operation.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="05:46" /> Kiriakou cut him off. "I just said, 'Stop. Please just stop. I'm not going to have this conversation. Just stop.'"<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="06:17" /> He had already told Rall he had not realised what he had agreed to when he agreed to it.<Cite s="2025-09-11-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="05:15" />
