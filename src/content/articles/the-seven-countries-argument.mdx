**The seven countries argument** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s dissent from the position that the American wars of the past quarter-century, the list of seven countries whose governments [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) is said to have wanted removed, were fought on Israel's behalf. It was put to him during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war) that America had started those wars for Israel; he took the list apart country by country.<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="05:04" />

*"Look at them independently,"* he answered. *"Libya was no threat to Israel. Never. Never was, never would be. Libya was a thorn in the side of the United States. The US didn't overthrow and kill Gaddafi for Israel. We did it for ourselves."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="05:04" /> Pressed that Gaddafi's plan to decouple oil from the dollar was the real trigger, Kiriakou agreed and said that was his point exactly: *"That was a threat to the American economy. We didn't need the Israelis to egg us on to overthrow Gaddafi."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="06:08" /><Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="06:41" />

Somalia he treated the same way, *"no threat to Israel at all. Never was"*, but a threat to the United States, or at least to American commerce.<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="05:37" />

## Afghanistan

Afghanistan draws his one partial defence of an American war. *"For a while that was a righteous war. It was a war to destroy al-Qaeda."* What followed was not: *"It morphed into this never-ending war against the Taliban, who had never attacked the United States."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="05:37" /> Israeli preference was not the driver: *"It's not that the Israelis wanted us to. It certainly was in Israel's interest, but that was the United States all the way."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="06:08" />

## The conclusion

Kiriakou states his own position before drawing the moral, so that the argument is not mistaken for a defence: *"I am not a fan of Israel. I am even less of a fan of Israeli leadership, but we can't blame all of our problems on Israel. We can blame the United States for its mistakes, too."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="06:08" />

He does not dispute the influence itself. Told that Israel had held a strong grip on American politicians for thirty years, he agreed, *"I'm not denying that American and Israeli interests intersect"*, while maintaining that on those seven countries *"the US had in their own national interests reasons to overthrow them."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="07:18" />
