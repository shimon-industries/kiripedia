**Bezalel Smotrich** is an Israeli far-right minister who, with [Itamar Ben-Gvir](/wiki/itamar-ben-gvir), oversaw security before the [October 7 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls the two *"morons"* who — despite settlers warning Jerusalem for two or three weeks that an attack was coming — insisted it would come from the West Bank rather than Gaza, leaving Israel unprepared.<Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="39:47" /><Cite s="2026-03-01-the-inquiry-torture-assassinations-epstein" t="40:20" />

Elsewhere Kiriakou sets out Smotrich's portfolios and record as the reason he considers a durable settlement impossible while the present government stands. Smotrich is finance minister and — *"oddly"*, in Kiriakou's word — also holds jurisdiction over the West Bank; like [Itamar Ben-Gvir](/wiki/itamar-ben-gvir) he carries a felony conviction for anti-Muslim hate crimes, *"not for failing to fill out an internet form or something like that."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="27:29" /> That both men hold felony hate-crime convictions and are nonetheless *"in charge of the Palestinians"* is, for Kiriakou, the point: *"as long as those guys are in place, there's no possible way that there can be a lasting peace."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="28:01" /> He characterises the coalition they sit in as *"the most extreme right-wing government in the history of Israel."*<Cite s="2025-11-18-nuclear-war-vault-7-mossad-in-iran" t="27:29" />

## 'Our next great fear'

Asked in October 2025 what worries him most about the Israeli government, Kiriakou answered by naming its far right rather than its prime minister: *"not just Netanyahu, but the likes of Smotrich and Ben-Gvir and these other right-wing extremists — extremists who have been convicted of anti-Arab and anti-Muslim hate crimes, felonies, and who now lead the Israeli government. I think that they are crazy enough to use nuclear weapons. And so I think that should be our next great fear."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="32:03" />

He pairs it with a rule about taking such figures at their word: when they say they cannot lose a war, or threaten [Iran](/wiki/iran-nuclear-assessment) with nuclear war, *"we should believe them"* — and only American pressure will restrain them.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="32:33" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="33:05" />

## The conviction that preceded the ministry

Discussing [a column arguing that genocidal statements by Israeli officials were temper rather than policy](/wiki/the-brett-stephens-column), Kiriakou pointed to a fact about the two men usually cited. Smotrich and [Itamar Ben-Gvir](/wiki/itamar-ben-gvir) *"were successfully prosecuted for felony hate crimes against Palestinians years before ever becoming ministers in the Netanyahu government."* His conclusion: *"These are very bad people."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:16" />

He measured the record against the standard set at Nuremberg, where *"people went to prison for less"* — reaching for Julius Streicher, who was hanged for publishing a newspaper, however nasty a piece of work it was.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:16" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:47" />
