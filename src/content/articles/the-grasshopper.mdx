**The Grasshopper** is the cryptonym [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) uses when telling the story of a recruited source whose case turned, briefly, into a domestic emergency.

## The recruitment

Kiriakou describes the man's position as one that made recruitment straightforward. *"He's broke. He's just a terrorist. You can't make any money from terrorism. So I started paying him. And I was paying him pretty handsomely, too. And he was giving me good information, like operational information."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-part" t="06:42" />

The source's own affiliation shifted during the relationship. *"This terrorist group had sort of fallen out of fashion, and he became involved with a group of anarchists. And what they wanted to do was just blow [expletive] up for no reason other than to, you know, shout, 'Yay, anarchy.'"*<Cite s="2026-02-18-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-part" t="07:15" />

## The call

The contact procedure is the tradecraft detail Kiriakou lingers on. The man called around eleven at night on a telephone kept for him alone — *"it was him, cuz I had this phone that was just for him. It was a burner"* — and opened with the fixed nonsense phrase they had agreed. *"He says, uh 'Hi. The the rain in Spain falls mainly in the plain.' And so I said, 'Oh, ears eat oats and dosey doats and little lambs eat ivy.' And he laughed and I laughed and we hung up."* The exchange, Kiriakou explains, meant a meeting in the car park of a toy store at two in the morning — the same challenge-and-response discipline described at [the exchange of bona fides](/wiki/exchange-of-bona-fides).<Cite s="2026-02-18-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-part" t="07:47" />

## The jewellery

What the source wanted was not operational. *"he's like, 'I'm I'm desperate. I'm broke.' He goes, 'My my daughter's sick. We have these unexpected medical bills.'"* He had brought a paper bag full of gold jewellery — *"'It's all my wife's jewelry,' he said. 'Can you buy it from me?'"* Kiriakou refused on the ground that he could not value it: *"Dude, I'm not a jeweler. I have no idea what this stuff is worth."* They agreed to meet the following midnight behind a church in a northern suburb.<Cite s="2026-02-18-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-part" t="08:17" />

The resolution came from the chief of station the next morning. Told the sum needed was five thousand dollars, he answered: *"He's been good. Just give him the five grand. We'll call it a We'll call it an early Christmas bonus."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-part" t="08:47" />

Kiriakou met him behind the church, handed over the money, and had him sign the receipt. *"He was forever indebted to me. He actually cried when I when I left to return to headquarters. He's like, 'I'm not sure I can work with anybody else.'"* Kiriakou's reassurance was unsentimental: *"Oh, you're going to be fine. Everybody else has money that's just as green as the money that I carry."*<Cite s="2026-02-18-viral-plug-john-kiriakou-cia-story-meme-compilation-part" t="09:18" />
