**The denials after Dallas** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of how quickly foreign governments moved to clear themselves after the assassination of President [John F. Kennedy](/wiki/jfk-assassination), and the reason he gives for doubting that a foreign service would attempt such a thing.

## What the files showed

Kiriakou explains that the [CIA](/wiki/cia) keeps its historical documents open to junior analysts, who can simply go through them to get a sense of what a period was like.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:33:28" />

What those files recorded was a scramble. The Soviets came to the Americans within minutes of Kennedy being killed, saying *"we swear to God it wasn't us, we swear it wasn't us."* The Chinese did the same thing. The Cubans asked the Mexicans to tell the Americans that it was not them.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:33:28" />

## Why he tells it

He raised the episode in July 2024 when asked how much credit to give the idea that a foreign adversary, China, Russia or Iran, had been behind the shooting at [Butler](/wiki/butler-rally-shooting). His answer was that it was unlikely, and the historical record of the denials is the reason: killing an American presidential candidate is a trigger for world war, and no service considers the blowback worth it.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:32:56" /><Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:34:00" />

His second reason is professional. A foreign service that had decided to do it would not leave the work to a twenty-year-old, would not attempt it from 145 yards, and would not use an AR-15. It would use a professional sniper at five hundred or eight hundred yards, too far away to be seen, *"and he's not going to miss."*<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:34:00" />
