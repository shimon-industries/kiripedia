**Jerry Falwell Jr.** was an evangelical leader and president of Liberty University. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) recounts that Falwell called him shortly after he blew the whistle on the [CIA torture program](/wiki/cia-torture-program-whistleblowing) and offered him a post lecturing at the university's Jesse Helms School of Government.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="52:22" /><Cite s="2026-07-07-covert-strategies-revealed" t="49:31" /> Kiriakou says he warned Falwell they probably disagreed on 99% of the issues; Falwell answered, *"we want you because torture is not Christian."* Kiriakou took the job and says he loved every second of it, even as they argued over the role of evangelical Christians in the Middle East.<Cite s="2026-06-15-jason-jones-3-hours-kiriakou" t="52:52" /> He served as an adjunct professor of intelligence studies at Liberty University from 2008 to 2012; in that telling, he attributes the "torture is not Christian" line to the dean who called to offer him the job on Falwell's behalf.<Cite s="2025-07-14-danny-jones" t="1:50:47" />
