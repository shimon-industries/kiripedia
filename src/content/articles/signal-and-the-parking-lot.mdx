**Signal and the parking lot** describes the mechanics [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gives for contacting a clandestine source, offered in answer to a question about what being run by an intelligence service would actually have looked like for [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) day to day.<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="08:12" />

## The phone call that says nothing

If the source is handled normally, the contact is a conversation with no content in it:

> *"You get a phone call saying, hey, how's your mom doing? And the answer is, my mom is feeling much better, thank you. And that means: meet me at the parking lot of the yacht club at 2:00 in the morning. You have this all set up in advance. And then he goes and does his meeting, and gets his marching orders."*

The work is done beforehand. The call carries a single bit of information — that the arrangement already made is on — and nothing an interceptor could act on.<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="08:43" />

## Why there is no email

Asked directly whether there would be an email trail, Kiriakou's answer is categorical: *"oh, no, no, no. There would never be an email trail. This is all done verbally, or just using signals."*<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="09:13" />

The illustration he reaches for is domestic and famous. During Watergate, the source would react to Bob Woodward putting an American flag in a flowerpot on his apartment balcony — *"that triggered a meeting that night at 9:00 in a parking garage in Rosslyn, the Rosslyn neighbourhood of Arlington, Virginia."* The signal did not name the garage or the hour, because both had been agreed long before it was given: *"all of this is always set up in advance, so you never have to actually put anything in writing, or say, meet me at 8:00."*<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="09:13" />

The investigative consequence he leaves implicit but the hosts draw out: an absence of documents between a service and its agent is what competent handling looks like, not evidence that the handling did not happen.<Cite s="2026-02-08-how-epstein-was-used-by-mossad-cia-mi6" t="09:44" />
