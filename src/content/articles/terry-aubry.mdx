**Terry Aubry** is named by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) as one of two Intercept journalists he holds responsible for the prosecutions of both himself and NSA whistleblower Reality Winner. Kiriakou states: "The two Intercept journalists that were responsible for sending you to prison were the two Intercept journalists responsible for sending me to prison" — naming "Daniel Hail[e] and Terry Aubry."<Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="23:57" />

Separately, Kiriakou identifies Terry Aubry himself as an FBI whistleblower who exposed systemic racism at the FBI. Aubry was identified as the source, in Kiriakou's account, after the FBI reviewed security-camera footage from every FBI field office in the country until it found a shot of him photographing his own computer screen.<Cite s="2023-04-14-kevin-gosztola" t="26:45" />

Kiriakou says he tweeted at the Intercept after Winner's arrest, asking, "Are you guys just stupid, or are you working for the FBI?" — a question he says the outlet never answered.<Cite s="2025-09-30-deprogram-show-with-ted-rall-and-jamarl-thomas-deprogram-with-ted-ral" t="24:28" />
