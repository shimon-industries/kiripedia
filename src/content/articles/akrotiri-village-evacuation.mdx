## A 2026 drone strike on the base

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says a drone that hit the UK's Akrotiri base in Cyprus was likely an Israeli operation, staged to increase American, British, Cypriot, and Greek interest in the region, and initially, falsely, blamed on Iran: *"I make that the Israelis decided we need to get the Americans, the British, the Cypriots, the Greeks more interested. We're going to send a drone… and then we're going to blame it on the Iranians."*<Cite s="2026-05-31-lazaros-sideras" t="1:32:08" />

## The day before the war

Akrotiri appears once more in Kiriakou's account, as an indicator rather than a place. On the first morning of the 2026 Iran war he described a conversation from the previous day with a friend in Cyprus, who told him that something was going on at the British air base there.

He noted the secrecy of the arrangement, the British *"don't release any information, of course,"* and in a hundred years *"have never ceded any control to the to the Cypriots"*, but the friend was certain something had changed.<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:00:47" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:01:19" />

His answer, by his own account, needed no analysis: *"I said, 'Oh, I can tell you what's going on. We're getting ready to bomb Iran.' And there it is."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:01:19" />
