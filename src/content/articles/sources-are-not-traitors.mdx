**Sources are not traitors** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to a viewer who asked whether, inside the CIA, officers regarded the people they had recruited as traitors and held them in contempt.

Kiriakou called it *"an interesting question"* and answered it flatly: *"the answer is absolutely not."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="06:45" />

## The reasoning

The explanation he gives is not about the agent's conduct but about the officers' self-image. *"You know, we really really believed that we were the good guys."* Recruitment therefore functioned as an act of transfer rather than of corruption: *"And so if we were able to recruit somebody and make them one of the good guys, we were all one big happy family."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="06:45" />

## The exceptions he concedes

Kiriakou does not pretend that every recruitment was a friendship. *"There were there were a couple of sources that I recruited who in normal life I would not be friends with, right? We would not we would not have had contact. They were scumbags."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="06:45" />

One case he singles out: *"In one case they were he was a killer like a just a coldblooded stone cold killer."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="06:45" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="07:16" />

## The proportion

Against those he sets the majority, and gives a number for his career total. *"Um but seven of the nine people that I recruited over the years I would love to be friendly with them."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="07:16" />

## Trust and its verification

The follow-up put to him was the obvious one: whether an officer worried that a person who would betray one master would betray another — the doubt of someone conducting an affair with a married partner. Kiriakou conceded the worry immediately — *"Oh, sure. Sure"* — and named the machinery built to answer it: *"And that's what vetting is all about. So, you know, you've got to polygraph them. You have to what we call operationally test them."*<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="07:16" /><Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="07:47" />

His worked example of that testing — a source made to run a surveillance detection route while a full team followed him, then asked to describe what he had done — is set out in [the operational test](/wiki/the-operational-test).<Cite s="2026-02-10-deprogram-ted-rall-greek-spies-epstein-aliens-cia" t="07:47" />
