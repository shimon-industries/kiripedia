The theory behind the campaign is the one Kiriakou says [Benjamin Netanyahu](/wiki/benjamin-netanyahu) and other Iran hawks have argued for years: apply enough pressure, weaken the security apparatus, and the Iranian people will rise and overthrow the government.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="20:21" /> Trump's first speech announcing the war addressed the Iranian people directly — *"Rise up. Now is your time."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="20:21" />

Kiriakou sets that against what had happened weeks earlier. Iranian police had suppressed protests in January, killing by many estimates 30,000 civilians — *"The Israelis say it was 80,000"* — and the United States had condemned it.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="19:49" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="20:54" /> He states the sequence deliberately and then repeats it: *"We jumped up and down when the Iranians killed protesters… and we decided to start killing Iranian law enforcement officers, police, and domestic security."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="20:54" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="21:25" />

## Method and attribution

The method is targeted rather than aerial: *"We're not talking about bombing police stations. That's not happening. We're talking about assassins and saboteurs on the ground."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="21:59" /> His attribution rests on capability and on advertising — the operatives are *"likely Israelis, because the US doesn't have a presence inside Iran, and the Israelis brag about the extent of their presence, and they are assassinating individual policemen."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="21:59" /> He cites the Iranian president confirming attacks on individual officers in an address to the National Security Council, and reports the president's own comparison: that the Americans and Israelis did the same in Afghanistan and in Iraq, as *"part of the arsenal of operations"* both states carry out.<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="21:25" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="22:30" />

## The result

Kiriakou's assessment is that the campaign inverted its own purpose: *"instead of leading to the immediate collapse of this country, we have unified Iranians on the ground, whether they were pro-regime or anti-regime."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="22:30" /><Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="23:00" /> He files it under the conclusion he says he drew from the Iraq war while still at the [CIA](/wiki/cia): *"we are never seen as liberators. We're seen as attackers and occupiers."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="23:00" /> His verdict on the war follows directly: *"I think the time is beyond ready to end this thing."*<Cite s="2026-08-11-john-kiriakou-a-shocking-new-alliance-emerges-in-the-middle" t="23:32" />
