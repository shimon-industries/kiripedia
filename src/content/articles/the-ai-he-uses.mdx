**The AI he uses** came up as a deliberate change of subject on 28 February 2026, when a viewer paying for ChatGPT asked [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), in the middle of the first day of the Iran war, to explain which AI he uses and why.

His answer was immediate: *"Yeah, uh sure. I use Perplexity. I find it to be far superior to ChatGPT."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:01" />

## The complaint

The first reason is personal and specific. *"ChatGPT lies about me. It just makes stuff up. And um Yeah, and I don't like it."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:01" /><Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:35" />

For a subject whose public record is a contested one — and who has spent years correcting accounts of his own case — a tool that fabricates biography is disqualified on the first pass rather than the last.

## The preference

The second reason he offers without claiming to explain it. *"It's been my experience that Perplexity for whatever reason, reasons I don't fully understand, Perplexity offers much deeper analysis when I ask it to look at things."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:35" />

[Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) proposed that the difference is training data — that Perplexity is trained on academic papers — and Kiriakou accepted the explanation as fitting his use of it: *"That would make perfect sense because that's what I've used it for."*<Cite s="2026-02-28-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:35" />
