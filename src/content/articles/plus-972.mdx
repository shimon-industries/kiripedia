**+972** is an Israeli internet platform that, at a 2026 Berlin panel that included [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), was said to have published "quite a few really interesting" whistleblowers from the Israel Defense Forces — a remark made by the panel's moderator, not by Kiriakou.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="1:34:49" />

The moderator raised +972's coverage in the context of a broader discussion about the introduction of AI into modern warfare, suggesting further revelations along those lines are likely to surface.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="1:34:49" />
