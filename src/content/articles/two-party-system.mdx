**The two-party system**, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account, has been overtaken by ideology and is no longer the organizing fact of American politics: there are now more Americans who call themselves independents than there are Democrats or Republicans.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="19:17" />

## Kiriakou's own trajectory

Asked what party he belongs to, he answers *"I am an independent — independent truly"* — and immediately supplies the inheritance he left behind: *"I was a third generation Democrat. My grandparents came because of Franklin Roosevelt. Democrats, you know, through the Vietnam War. My parents were opposed to the war. They were supportive of civil rights. I left the Democratic party."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="19:17" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="19:49" />

## Why the parties cannot recover

Kiriakou says the parties want badly to restore a functioning two-party system *"but they don't know how to do it. And they don't know how to do it because they're unwilling to address the issues"* — the kind of issues, he adds, that [Charlie Kirk](/wiki/charlie-kirk) was raising.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="20:20" />

His stronger claim is that the distinction has already collapsed. *"A lot of people disagree with me when I say this, but I worked on Capitol Hill for years. I worked in the [CIA](/wiki/cia) for 15 years. I served overseas for my country. I've come to realize that there's really no major difference between the two political parties."* There are differences at the edges — *"maybe a little bit about abortion, maybe a little bit about the economy or taxes"* — but *"essentially these two parties are the same party, two sides of the same coin. And people are coming to realize that and they're tired of it."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="23:08" />

The practical consequence he names is disengagement: people are falling away from the parties *"because the political parties don't affect change in any way."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="22:34" />

## The realignment as evidence

He treats the recent right-wing break with Israeli policy as a demonstration of the same point — that ideology now cuts across party lines. He points to [Marjorie Taylor Greene](/wiki/marjorie-taylor-greene) voting with Democrats, and to [Tucker Carlson](/wiki/tucker-carlson), [Candace Owens](/wiki/candace-owens) and Steve Bannon making arguments about the Middle East that no party label predicts.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="23:39" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="25:14" />
