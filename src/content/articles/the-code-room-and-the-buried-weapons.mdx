The morning after [the broken side-view mirror](/wiki/the-broken-side-view-mirror) pitch, the target telephoned [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) and asked what he proposed.

## The meeting

Kiriakou told him to meet him in the lobby coffee shop of the Hilton hotel in two hours, alone and unarmed.<Cite s="2023-11-16-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="06:12" /><Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="04:11" />

The venue was chosen for exposure rather than privacy — "I wanted it to be in as public a place as possible so that if all hell were to break loose I might have a little bit of safety, safety in numbers." Six security officers were placed at tables around the café; Kiriakou says [CIA](/wiki/cia) people were at every table, all of them armed, and that he wore a bulletproof vest under his suit.<Cite s="2023-11-16-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="06:45" /><Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="04:11" />

That, he says, was itself part of the pressure: the target was a professional, "so he knew that every one of these white guys that's sitting at every table around him is armed to the teeth and is working with me."<Cite s="2023-11-16-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="08:17" />

## The demand

The man asked how it would play out. Kiriakou named two things: "I want access to your code room and I want all your weapons."<Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="04:41" />

The target answered that he had no idea how hard that was. Kiriakou's reply turned the difficulty into the argument for asking him specifically: "yes, I also know how important you are, and I know that of anybody in the embassy besides the ambassador, you have the access."<Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="04:41" />

## The terms

Asked what he would get in return, Kiriakou answered in order: "number one, you get to live. Number two, I can make you rich beyond your wildest dreams."<Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="04:41" />

In the English-language telling he is blunter about the balance of the negotiation: the target made a demand, Kiriakou made a counter-offer, and then told him, "you're not in a position of strength to negotiate here. I'm telling you what it is that you're going to do." Money could be discussed later; first he had to prove his bona fides.<Cite s="2023-11-16-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="07:16" /><Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="05:12" />

## What was taken

They negotiated a deal and made a plan. The codes were taken. The weapons were buried in an underground bunker outside the embassy; six men went in the middle of the night with shovels and took all of them.<Cite s="2026-02-08-arabi" t="05:12" />

Then the agent disappeared — into resettlement, and eventually into [seven restaurants](/wiki/the-restaurants-in-witness-protection).

Kiriakou's summary of the outcome is that the man "did literally everything that we wanted him to do," and that the operation shut down what he says would have been a real threat to Americans from that embassy.<Cite s="2023-11-16-dalton-fischer-podcast-dalton-fischer-podcast" t="07:46" />
