**Sports and the Steelers bar** records [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to a viewer who asked both hosts on 25 February 2026 whether they enjoy any sport, and what they most like to watch live.

[Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) handed it over and disposed of his own answer first — *"John, you better answer this cuz my question is short. The end. My answer is no."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="31:14" />

Kiriakou's was not short. *"I do. I love I love baseball and football. I watch both regularly."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="31:14" />

## The walk

What he described next is a preference for the room over the broadcast. *"Football is like such a big deal to me that even if the game's on here at home, I'll walk to a Steelers bar in Clarendon, Arlington, Virginia here, and um just so I can be surrounded by 500 screaming Pittsburgh Steelers fans."*<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="31:14" /><Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="31:46" />

The detail is a small piece of biography that the corpus otherwise carries only in fragments: a Pittsburgh allegiance kept up in Virginia, and a willingness to leave the house for the crowd rather than the game.

Rall's reply was that he envied people with that kind of passion, and named his own — buzkashi, which he said he would watch every day if it were available, and which requires a trip to Afghanistan or Central Asia to see.<Cite s="2026-02-25-deprogram-ted-rall-deprogram-ted-rall" t="31:46" />
