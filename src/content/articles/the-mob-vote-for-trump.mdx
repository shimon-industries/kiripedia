**The mob vote for Trump** is John Kiriakou's answer to a viewer who asked, in November 2025, whether he had discussed politics with the organised crime figures he served time alongside, and what their politics were.<Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="31:46" />

## The relationships

His answer opened with the fact that the acquaintances did not end at the prison gate: *"A lot. I I had dinner with two with two of them in uh in Philadelphia over the weekend."*<Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="31:46" />

## The politics

On their view of [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) he was emphatic and repetitive: *"They love love love Donald Trump. They love him."*<Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="31:46" />

## The explanation

What interests Kiriakou is the reason, which he treats as occupational rather than ideological. Being a real estate developer in New York of all places, he says, means daily contact with the mob, because *"They control everything."*<Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="31:46" /><Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="32:19" />

He then itemised what *everything* means in the building trade: the concrete union, the union that makes windows, the steelworkers union. And the conclusion: *"you've got to deal with these people if you want to build buildings."*<Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="32:19" />

Rall's inference, that this is why Trump had those connections, and that he had to, drew Kiriakou's agreement twice over: *"Yeah. Exactly. Exactly."* His closing summary was that they love Trump and there are no two ways about it.<Cite s="2025-11-13-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-snubs-us-workers-at-mar-a-lago" t="32:19" />

## Why it matters to him

The observation belongs to a body of material Kiriakou has produced about the people he met at [Loretto](/wiki/fci-loretto), for whom his consistent method is to report what they think and why, rather than to treat their opinions as disqualified by their convictions.
