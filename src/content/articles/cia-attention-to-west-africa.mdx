CIA attention to West Africa is John Kiriakou's short assessment of American covert interest in the Sahel, given in response to a question about whether the agency is likely to be involved in Burkina Faso because of what its leader Ibrahim Traoré is doing there.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="1:38:33" />

## The historical baseline

Kiriakou's first point is that the region was long a low priority. In all the years he was at the CIA, he says, the agency cared very very little about Africa, and especially about West Africa.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="1:39:06" />

## What changed

Two developments alter the calculation in his reading. The first is the emergence of what he calls a very healthy al-Qaeda or Islamic State presence in the region. The second is the drug trade, which he couples with it.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="1:39:06" /> He has described the trafficking route through the region separately in [the West Africa cocaine route](/wiki/the-west-africa-cocaine-route).

On that basis his answer is a qualified yes: it is possible, and he would guess that there is going to be CIA involvement and special forces involvement.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="1:38:33" /><Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="1:39:06" />

## The reporting gap

Kiriakou adds a rider that he treats as equally certain. The American people, he says, are not going to have any idea, because it is not going to be reported.<Cite s="2025-10-16-seymizzle-podcast-ask-ex-cia-agent-and-whistleblower-john-kiria" t="1:39:06" />

The observation matches his complaint elsewhere about undisclosed American spending in the Sahel, where he says billions of dollars are going to Niger, Mali, Burkina Faso, Guinea-Conakry and Guinea-Bissau as what is supposed to be a big secret.<Cite s="2023-08-24-london-real-ukraine-war-mass-surveillance-trump-ufos-cia" t="33:31" />
