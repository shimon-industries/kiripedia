**Kiriakou's grandmother arrested for espionage** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the 1934 arrest, in fascist Greece, of the woman who would become his maternal grandmother — and the specific reason his mother's side of the family became American.

## The arrest

*"My mother's parents came in 1934. The fascists had taken over in Greece, Greek fascists. And my Yia Yia was arrested because she was married to a Greek man who had become an American. They just called her 'I Amerikanida' and they arrested her."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-14-secrets-that-have-never-been" t="01:01" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-14-secrets-that-have-never-been" t="01:31" />

## The pretext

Asked on what grounds, Kiriakou's answer: *"For espionage. Just because she happened to be married to an — she got married when she was 14 years old. My Pappou was 18 years old."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-14-secrets-that-have-never-been" t="01:31" />

## The American-magazines bribe

*"My Pappou bribed the prison guard with American magazines and she got out. They snuck onto a ship and they came, in 1934."*<Cite s="2026-06-01-john-kiriakou-new-posdcast-14-secrets-that-have-never-been" t="01:31" />
