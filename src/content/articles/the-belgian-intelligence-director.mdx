**The Belgian intelligence director** is a figure John Kiriakou knows at one remove, described on the DeProgram programme in September 2025 when a viewer asked for his thoughts on Belgian intelligence. Kiriakou declined the obvious joke and offered a connection instead.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:02" />

## The friendship

*"This is one of those small-world stories,"* he began. *"When I was a senior in high school, we had a foreign exchange student from Brussels, Pete Jambon. And Pete and I are still close buddies. He comes back to the States just about every year and we're all in each other's weddings and we're godfathers to each other's kids — Pete and my three other closest friends."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:02" />

The brothers took different paths: *"Pete went into private business, became very wealthy. His brother went into government and became the director of the Belgian intelligence service."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:33" />

## The conversation in Atlanta

They met at a wedding. *"The last time he was here in the States, we met up in Atlanta to go to another one of our best friends' son's wedding."* Kiriakou asked after the brother, and learned the portfolio had grown: *"they made him intel director and deputy prime minister. It's the craziest thing."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:33" />

His follow-up — *"how's he fitting in with intelligence?"* — produced the part of the exchange Kiriakou tells the story for.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="24:33" />

## The prejudice, and the defence of it

*"He said, 'You know, I never realised that my brother had a hatred for Arabs.' And I'm like, 'What? You guys don't hate anybody.'"*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:05" />

The brother's reply, whenever challenged, was the appeal to privileged information: *"Oh, you don't see what I see. If you could see what I see..."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:05" />

Kiriakou recognised the formula. *"Oh, Pete, I hear that all the time"* — the claim being that if you had the access, *"you'd want to kill everybody."* His answer was his own service record: *"Well, no. I used to have access to what you had access to, and I never wanted to kill anybody."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:05" />

## His assessment of the service

Kiriakou's professional judgement of Belgian intelligence follows from the same conversation and is narrow rather than dismissive: *"the Belgians are very good when it comes to Arabs, and they pretty much ignore everything else. It's all about the Arabs."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:36" />

Asked what drives the focus, he pointed to a fear of the country's Palestinian refugee population — *"they're panicked at the idea that ... they might be terrorists"* — a preoccupation the hosts traced back to the bombing campaigns that hit Western Europe in earlier decades.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:36" /><Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:06" />
