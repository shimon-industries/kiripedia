**Ross Ulbricht** is the founder of the online marketplace Silk Road. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says Ulbricht received a sentence of *"life plus 75 years"* — which he calls *"some draconian sentence"* — before President Trump pardoned him.<Cite s="2026-02-27-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="18:39" />

Kiriakou says he met Ulbricht's mother and sister two weeks before the pardon and told them he wanted to have them on his podcast because *"this entire story is so sickening and it's such a miscarriage of justice."* He notes their advocacy campaign — t-shirts, hashtags, a sustained push across podcasts — culminated in the pardon, which gave Kiriakou hope for clemency in his own circumstances.<Cite s="2026-02-27-the-ripple-effect-podcast" t="18:39" />
