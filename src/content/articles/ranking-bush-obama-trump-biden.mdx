**Ranking Bush, Obama, Trump and Biden** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to a viewer who asked him and [Ted Rall](/wiki/ted-rall) to rank the four presidents and to say which they would live under for the rest of their lives.

His first pass was comparative and reluctant. *"I hated George W. Bush. I hated Barack Obama."* He nonetheless chose [Obama](/wiki/barack-obama), *"because he presented the least bad outward-looking face to the world, even though he was busily droning people in places like Pakistan and pissing people off all over the place that we didn't know about."* [Bush](/wiki/george-w-bush) he judged reckless.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:19" />

Then he reversed himself in the next breath: *"You know what? In good conscience, I just can't with Obama."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:19" /> The reason is personal and institutional at once, *"He prosecuted me, and not just me, but seven other whistleblowers"*, and his verdict on the record is categorical: Obama is *"the worst, harshest enemy of whistleblowers in American history."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:49" />

What compounded it, in his account, was the political cover it enjoyed: *"that was peak brunch Democrats, too. Everybody went home and put their protest signs in the closet because Obama was president."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:49" />

He added a foreign-policy indictment on top, that Obama destroyed Libya, and also Syria, part of Somalia and Honduras, and the appointments that went with it: *"he appointed not just [Hillary Clinton](/wiki/hillary-clinton) as Secretary of State, but Hillary Clinton and [John Kerry](/wiki/john-kerry). So, no thank you."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:20" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:51" />

## The remaining field

Bush was disposed of in a sentence: *"Bush was a war criminal. So that's a hard no."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:51" /> [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) he treated as two different presidencies that *"almost have to be separated"*, good on some measures in the first term, then reckless with COVID, though Kiriakou gave him credit for approving the vaccine quickly, doubting a President Hillary Clinton would have done so.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="31:45" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:51" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:21" />

That left [Joe Biden](/wiki/joe-biden), and the reasoning for choosing him is an absence rather than an achievement: *"Biden was having no president at all, basically. Everything was on autopilot."* Kiriakou's gloss on that is generous to the system if not to the man, *"I guess you could say it's a tribute to the American system that it survived that"*, and his conclusion is comparative: *"For me personally, the least damaging of all those presidents was Joe Biden."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="31:45" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:51" />
