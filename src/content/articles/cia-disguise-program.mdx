The **CIA disguise program** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the agency's disguise and forgery specialists. On his first day he sat between two new hires: a woman recruited from the Falls Church Academy of Beauty to be trained as a master disguise maker, and a man, Sean, recruited to be a master forger because he had drawn a two-panel cartoon in every issue of his college newspaper.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="52:09" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="52:40" /> He has told the same first-day story elsewhere, placing a hairdresser who trained at the Falls Church School of Beauty on one side of him — she became a master wig maker — and a college-newspaper cartoonist on the other, who became a master forger.<Cite s="2023-04-14-kevin-gosztola" t="41:50" /><Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="44:46" /> Twelve years later the disguise maker flew to Pakistan and spent six weeks building Kiriakou a bald-head mask, matching the color to his skin tone and inserting the hairs one at a time; clear-glass spectacles hid the single seam. The station chief and the ambassador, Kiriakou says, failed to recognize him until he pulled it off.<Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="53:12" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="53:42" /><Cite s="2025-10-02-truth-hurts-epstein-mossad-blackmail-playbook" t="54:12" /><Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="45:17" />

Kiriakou says officers under deep cover could be assigned any invented identity — a State Department officer, a Pentagon civilian, a wildlife photographer, a museum curator, a businessman — created and maintained by headquarters, which also supplies the training needed to sustain the cover.<Cite s="2017-06-29-rob-kall-bottom-up-show" t="15:18" /> He says that every time there was a walk-in to the American embassy where he was stationed, he had to wear a disguise — usually a fake mustache and a wig, sometimes clear-glass spectacle frames — and describes one overseas post experimenting with the first-ever bald head and comb-over disguise, which took six weeks to place the hair one strand at a time.<Cite s="2026-02-13-o-keefe-media-group" t="15:27" />

Asked about a viral photo showing a Fox News figure caught wearing a mask, Kiriakou said it was real but not representative of how CIA disguises are made: genuine CIA masks are glued prosthetics extending down to the mid-chest, not pull-on masks of the kind sold on Amazon.<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="43:44" /> He separately describes being recruited for a covert-action program because his height, weight, and body shape matched those of a foreign world leader the agency was targeting; the resulting mask required roughly six hours of daily work by two Emmy-winning Hollywood makeup artists.<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="47:24" />
