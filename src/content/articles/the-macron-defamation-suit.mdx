**The Macron defamation suit** is the libel action Emmanuel and Brigitte Macron brought against [Candace Owens](/wiki/candace-owens) in July 2025, discussed by [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) on the DeProgram programme the day it was reported.

## The claim and the warning

Owens had spent about three years asserting that Brigitte Macron was born male, and had made a documentary to that effect, arguing that no childhood history, documents or photographs existed.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="30:38" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="31:11" /> What was new, and had not been publicly known until days before, was that the Macrons had approached her through attorneys about a year earlier and told her to stop, on pain of suit. She did not stop; she doubled down, saying in part that her conversion to Catholicism required her to expose the person she described as masquerading as the first lady of France.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="32:47" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:19" />

## The question of venue

Asked where the suit should be brought, Kiriakou treated the choice of forum as the substantive question and answered it in one word: England.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:19" />

## The regret he attached to it

The answer led him directly to his own case. *"I really wish I'd sued the LA Times in England,"* he said. *"I could have, because of the internet. And so … I should have"* — acknowledging in the same breath that it *"would have required me to go to England quite a bit."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:19" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="33:50" />

The remark is one of the few occasions on which Kiriakou has named a legal step against a news organisation that he considered available to him and did not take.
