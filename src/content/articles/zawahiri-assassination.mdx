The **Zawahiri assassination** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of the drone strike that killed Ayman al-Zawahiri, al-Qaeda's number two. The missile, he notes, carried *no warhead*, chosen deliberately to avoid killing the women and children inside the apartment. When Zawahiri stepped onto the balcony to smoke a cigarette, the missile was fired and *"went completely through his body,"* with no explosion.<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="11:52" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="12:23" /> Kiriakou contrasts the care taken here with the [Obama-era drone strikes](/wiki/tuesday-morning-kill-list) that *"hit every wedding."*<Cite s="2026-05-29-jk-podcast-epstein-network-mossad" t="12:54" />

Kiriakou has separately noted, in passing, that a new al-Zawahiri podcast reportedly surfaced despite the U.S. having claimed to have killed him in a drone strike in Afghanistan the previous summer.<Cite s="2023-01-04-scott-horton" t="59:35" />
