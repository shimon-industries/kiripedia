**The Perplexity subscription** is a free lifetime account [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he was sent unsolicited by the company's chief executive, days after he complained about a competing product on [Joe Rogan's podcast](/wiki/joe-rogan). He told the story on DeProgram on 5 November 2025, prefacing it as *"apropos of nothing, but I thought people would get a kick out of it."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="20:25" />

## The machine that argued back

The complaint that started it concerned invented biography. *"When I was on Rogan a couple of weeks ago, we somehow got on to the topic of AI, and I said I didn't like ChatGPT because it lied about me. It just made up that I went to the University of Maryland and to some grad school in Belgium that I've never heard of."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="20:25" />

What he found notable was not the error but the refusal to concede it. *"I told ChatGPT, you're wrong. And it says, no, you're wrong."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="20:25" /> The exchange sits alongside his broader objection to the industry's preferred vocabulary, that a machine stating falsehoods is not hallucinating.

## Rogan's answer

Rogan, in Kiriakou's account, responded with a product recommendation and a disclosure attached to it: *"Oh, we use Perplexity here. They are advertisers on the show, but for our own use here at the studio, we use Perplexity."* Kiriakou's reply was immediate, *"I'm going to start using Perplexity"*, and on the podcast, that was the end of it.<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="20:57" />

## The email at ten o'clock

The consequence arrived weeks later and unbidden. *"Last night around like 10 o'clock, the CEO of Perplexity sent me an email with a lifetime subscription to Perplexity."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="20:57" />

Kiriakou quoted the message: *"Thank you for the conversation you had about Perplexity. Here's a link, it's for Perplexity employees. It's a free lifetime subscription to Perplexity."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="21:28" /> He accepted the point of the gesture without pretending to be above it: *"So now I'm going to be all about Perplexity."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="21:28" />

The episode is a small, unusually legible instance of a pattern Kiriakou has described elsewhere in his post-agency life, that a remark made on a large enough platform produces offers, invitations and gifts from parties he has never met, and that the offers arrive faster than any of them can be assessed.
