**National Bird** is an award-winning documentary about the United States drone program in which whistleblower [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale) appears openly, discussing the killing of civilians — [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) cites the film as evidence that Hale *"was pretty well out there"* about his objections, appearing on camera and on college campuses, rather than being outed by the press.<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="02:40" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="14:42" /> Kiriakou calls it *"an absolutely terrific… award-winning documentary"* that everyone must see, and says it addresses PTSD and what psychiatrists now call [moral injury](/wiki/moral-injury).<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="15:12" /><Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="15:44" /> He describes footage in which drone operators are heard on audio debating whether the people in their sights are children — *"I think the woman's holding an infant… oh are we supposed to shoot women too"* — before firing and *"just killing everybody."*<Cite s="2021-06-01-scott-horton-daniel-hale-persecution" t="17:48" />
