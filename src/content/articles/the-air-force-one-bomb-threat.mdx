**The Air Force One bomb threat** was a threat telephoned directly to President [Bill Clinton](/wiki/bill-clinton)'s aircraft as it took off from Athens at the end of his November 1999 visit to Greece.<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="22:50" />

The visit had ended in the basement of the Intercontinental Hotel, where Clinton gave a raucous and wildly popular speech to the Greek American Business Women's Association before driving to Ellinikon, the old Athens airport, and flying back to Washington.<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="21:48" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="22:19" /> [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), who had drawn the note-taker's job for the presidential meeting in the lottery run by Ambassador [Nicholas Burns](/wiki/nicholas-burns), returned to the embassy for the hot wash, the after-action review at which everybody involved goes through every detail of a visit, what went right, what went wrong and what was learned.<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="21:16" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="22:19" />

The ambassador opened it with a question: *"Do you want to hear something crazy?"* Just as the aircraft was taking off, he said, the telephone inside Air Force One rang. Somebody in Greece had obtained the top secret number for it, and the call was a threat to blow up the aircraft. Clinton did not answer; a low-level aide took it.<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="22:19" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="22:50" />

Kiriakou, who describes the episode as a side of the Athens story he had never told before, uses it as the argument against dismissing warnings out of hand. Discussing the Israeli intelligence behind [the catering truck transfer](/wiki/the-catering-truck-transfer), which he does not believe, he adds that *"you have to take every shred of information like this seriously just in case it's legit."*<Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="21:48" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="22:50" /><Cite s="2026-08-13-na-they-used-what-to-hide-the-president-ep-10" t="23:22" />
