The **cats of Athens** are the street cat population [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) credits with keeping the Greek capital free of rats — a point he had made on the show the previous week and was asked to repeat.

## The claim

*"There there are no rats in in Athens because there are, you know, 150,000 cats or whatever it is, a million cats, I don't know."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="39:15" /> He gives the figure loosely and twice, without settling it.

## What tourists do with them

*"And you know, tourists love the cats. They they'll come up to your to your table at a restaurant and you give them a little smelt or a shrimp or whatever and they purr and you know brush up against you. But as a result there are no rats in Greece."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="39:15" /><Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="39:45" />

## The contrast

What interests Kiriakou is the reversal of expectation for an American. *"It's just a completely alien thought to to you know think that oh you sit down at a restaurant you see a rat run across the uh the pavement."*<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="39:45" />

He moved directly from there to his own city, which he ranks second only to New York — see [Washington, rat central](/wiki/washington-rat-city).<Cite s="2025-09-08-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-fra" t="39:45" />
