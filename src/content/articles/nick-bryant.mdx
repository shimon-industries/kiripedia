**Nick Bryant** is an investigative journalist and activist, an officer and co-founder of Epstein Justice, and the author of *The Franklin Scandal: A Story of Powerbrokers, Child Abuse and Betrayal*.<Cite s="2026-07-13-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-nick-bryant-on-the" t="02:03" /><Cite s="2026-07-13-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-nick-bryant-on-the" t="44:33" />

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), who has hosted him more than once, introduces him as "someone who probably knows this issue better than anybody else in America" — the issue being the [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) files and the question of whether any such file exists.<Cite s="2026-07-13-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-nick-bryant-on-the" t="02:03" /> Kiriakou has said he would look forward to a further conversation on Bryant's research into Epstein's role in geopolitics.<Cite s="2026-07-13-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-nick-bryant-on-the" t="44:03" />
