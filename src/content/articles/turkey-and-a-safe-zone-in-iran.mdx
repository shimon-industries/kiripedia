**Turkey and a safe zone in Iran** is an exchange from January 2026, prompted by a viewer asking whether John Kiriakou had heard the Turkish president say that in the event of an attack on Iran the Turkish army would establish a safe zone inside Iranian territory.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="47:56" />

## The reading

Kiriakou did not treat the proposal as humanitarian. *"They would love that. They would love nothing more than to grab Iranian territory like they grabbed uh Iraqi territory and they grabbed Cypriot territory and they grabbed Syrian territory."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="47:56" />

The list is the argument: three cases in which, on his account, a stated security rationale produced a lasting Turkish presence on someone else's land. Rall supplied the label — *"Pan-Turkism gone wild"* — and Kiriakou answered with his own: *"The neo-Ottoman government in Turkey is what it is."*<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="47:56" /><Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="48:28" />

## The subject nobody discusses

Rall's closing observation was that pan-Turkism is a topic they should get into properly at some point, because most people are not aware of it — although, he added, a lot of people who watch the show certainly are.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="48:28" /> The conversation then turned to Barrett Brown, and the promised discussion did not take place in that episode.<Cite s="2026-01-30-deprogram-show-with-is-trump-bidening-out-deprogram-with-ted" t="48:28" />

## Where it sits

The exchange belongs to the same January 2026 conversation in which Kiriakou reported [the calls from Arab capitals and from Israel asking Trump not to strike Iran](/wiki/the-calls-not-to-attack-iran), and it supplies the regional argument those callers were making: that an attack would not stay bilateral, because too many neighbours have standing territorial appetites that a war would license.
