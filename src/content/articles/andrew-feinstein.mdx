**Andrew Feinstein** is mentioned at a 2026 Berlin conference panel that included [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou). A fellow panelist recalled that Feinstein had recently spoken there about the multiple factors that brought down apartheid in South Africa — citing popular uprising and academic divestment from military-industrial-complex ties among them — while posing a question to Kiriakou about resistance strategies beyond leaks and data. The recollection is the questioner's, not Kiriakou's own statement.<Cite s="2026-03-20-disruption-network-lab-war-military-exposing-crimes-is-not-a-crime-jo" t="01:19:40" />

No source in the corpus captures Kiriakou himself discussing Andrew Feinstein.
