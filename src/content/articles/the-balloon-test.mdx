**The balloon test** is the piece of evidence John Kiriakou puts at the centre of his argument that the Israeli government had advance knowledge of the [7 October 2023 attack](/wiki/october-7-attack). He set it out on the DeProgram programme in August 2025.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:42" />

## The excuse he is answering

Kiriakou frames the argument against an official explanation he considers weak: *"remember this weak-ass excuse that came out from the Israeli government in the days after October the 7th. The excuse was that, well, we knew something was coming, but Smotrich and Ben-Gvir told us it was going to be in the West Bank. We didn't know it was going to come from Gaza."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:42" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:13" />

## The two contradictions

His first objection is about who was reporting what. *"We know that's not true because settlers along the line with Gaza were reporting for months that there was training under way."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:13" />

The second is the balloons. *"And then remember, in the days before October 7th, Hamas sent balloons over as a test — and still the Israelis did nothing."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:13" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:45" />

The inference he draws is stated as a certainty: *"So that tells me that of course the Israelis knew it was coming."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:45" />

## The motive, and its limit

Asked why a government would let such an attack proceed, Kiriakou's answer is territorial: *"Why would they want it to happen? So that they could launch their drive for greater Israel."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:45" />

He is careful about how far this goes. The host had already drawn the same distinction in his own account of the day — pointing to a response time of seven and a half hours in a country too small for such delays, and to a breached smart fence that produced no arrival — and had put the limit explicitly: *"I don't think they told Hamas to do it. I don't think they planned it, but I think they were like, 'This is going to be our great opportunity.'"*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:42" />

Kiriakou's reply to that framing was *"Ted, I couldn't agree more"*, and his own account adds no claim of Israeli authorship — only of foreknowledge and inaction.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:42" />
