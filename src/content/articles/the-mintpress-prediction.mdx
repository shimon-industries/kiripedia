**The MintPress prediction** is a forecast [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) raises unprompted and against himself. Shortly after the 2016 election the outlet interviewed him, [Thomas Drake](/wiki/thomas-drake) and the Justice Department whistleblower [Jesselyn Radack](/wiki/jesselyn-radack), putting the same question to each: were things going to get better or worse for whistleblowers under [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump)?<Cite s="2022-05-04-covertaction-bulletin-julian-assange-and-the-u-s-war-on-whistleblow" t="22:37" />

He flags his own answer before giving it. *"I said, I was the only one who said this, and I was foolish and wrong in retrospect, but I said, I can't imagine a scenario where anything could be worse than Barack Obama. I can't imagine that Donald Trump would be worse."*<Cite s="2022-05-04-covertaction-bulletin-julian-assange-and-the-u-s-war-on-whistleblow" t="22:37" /><Cite s="2022-05-04-covertaction-bulletin-julian-assange-and-the-u-s-war-on-whistleblow" t="23:10" />

The correction is four words long: *"And then Donald Trump was worse."* What limited it, on his account, was arithmetic rather than restraint, *"the only thing that saved us from Trump was the fact that he was only in office for four years."*<Cite s="2022-05-04-covertaction-bulletin-julian-assange-and-the-u-s-war-on-whistleblow" t="23:10" />

He keeps the concession separate from his position on [Obama](/wiki/barack-obama), which the same conversation states at its harshest: eight [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act) prosecutions of people who spoke to the press, against three in the ninety-two years before, *"nearly three times the number of all US presidents combined. That was not an accident."*<Cite s="2022-05-04-covertaction-bulletin-julian-assange-and-the-u-s-war-on-whistleblow" t="20:58" />
