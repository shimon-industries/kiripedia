**The Melber walkout** was an incident during a recorded MSNBC segment in which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) removed his microphone and told the host he was leaving. It is the sharpest episode in the long dispute over whether he is described as a whistleblower or [a leaker](/wiki/leaker-versus-whistleblower).

The host, Ari Melber, introduced him not only as a leaker but as a convicted felon.<Cite s="2019-10-15-david-gornoski-a-neighbors-choice-john-kiriakou-on-the-cia-ukraine-whistleblowe" t="14:40" /> Kiriakou's reply was immediate: *"Okay, if you're going to play games like that, I'm walking"*, and he took his microphone off.<Cite s="2019-10-15-david-gornoski-a-neighbors-choice-john-kiriakou-on-the-cia-ukraine-whistleblowe" t="15:12" /> The segment was being taped in advance rather than broadcast live, and Melber apologised and asked what he objected to.<Cite s="2019-10-15-david-gornoski-a-neighbors-choice-john-kiriakou-on-the-cia-ukraine-whistleblowe" t="15:12" />

What Kiriakou objected to was the pairing of the framing with the subject matter. *"You brought me on here with Daniel Ellsberg of all people, the godfather of all whistleblowers, to talk about whistleblowing. And then you introduced me as a leaker and convicted felon."*<Cite s="2019-10-15-david-gornoski-a-neighbors-choice-john-kiriakou-on-the-cia-ukraine-whistleblowe" t="15:12" />

He places the episode within a wider pattern he has tracked across the networks: it took his attorneys a full year to get CNN to stop calling him a leaker, [Fox News](/wiki/the-fox-news-military-analyst) used the word whistleblower from the beginning, and MSNBC has never changed.<Cite s="2019-10-15-david-gornoski-a-neighbors-choice-john-kiriakou-on-the-cia-ukraine-whistleblowe" t="14:40" />
