The **Justice Department self-immolation attempt** was an incident at a demonstration outside the U.S. Department of Justice in support of [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange) and WikiLeaks, at which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was among the speakers. In his account a man walked up to the podium where the speakers were standing, removed all of his clothes, squirted lighter fluid over himself, and produced a lighter from the pocket of the trousers he had just put on the ground. Kiriakou and another demonstrator stopped him before he could use it.<Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="09:44" />

Asked why he intervened, Kiriakou's answer was about audience rather than about the man: the rally was one at which *"we were all standing there agreeing with each other,"* so the act would have been witnessed only by people already persuaded.<Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="09:44" /> He resisted the suggestion that the man had any political motive at all, offering instead that *"maybe the poor guy was just having a mental breakdown"* — and rejecting the proposition that he might simply have been cold, on the ground that the weather did not support it: it was, he recalled, a comfortable and *"warm day in October."*<Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="10:15" />

What happened after the police arrived is the part of the episode Kiriakou singles out approvingly. A woman at the rally who was an attorney ran to the officers as they made the arrest and told them *"I'm an attorney. He's clearly mentally ill. Don't hurt him."* Instead of taking the man to jail they took him to a hospital — an outcome Kiriakou called *"kind of nice,"* and one he attributes to her intervention rather than to the man having done anything chargeable.<Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="10:15" /><Cite s="2026-05-17-the-matan-show-matan-confronts-kiriakou-israel" t="10:45" />
