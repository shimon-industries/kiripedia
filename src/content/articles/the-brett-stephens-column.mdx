**The Brett Stephens column** is a New York Times opinion piece published in late July 2025 that [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) said he was *"still haunted by"* two days after reading it.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:14" />

## The argument in the column

As Kiriakou summarises it, the column conceded that people who call the war in Gaza a genocide point to the genocidal comments made by high-ranking Israeli officials in the days after [October 7](/wiki/october-7-attack), and answered that those officials *"were just being intemperate. They were being spicy. They were just angry about October 7th."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:14" />

## The test he applies

Kiriakou does not dispute the premise directly. He grants it for the sake of argument — *"let's just say that, you know, we're going to pretend here that Brett Stevens is telling the truth"* — and then applies a single test. If the remarks had genuinely not been the official policy of the Israeli government, *"those people would have been fired."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:44" />

He supplies the response a government that meant it would have given, in the voice of a hypothetical Israeli president: *"what are you doing? That's not who we are. Please leave. Leave your ID at the door."* Instead, he says, the government *"left them in there."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:44" /> His own view is that the policy is not in doubt in any case, *"because all the journalists have reported that it is."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="18:44" />

## The two ministers

The officials he has in mind are [Bezalel Smotrich](/wiki/bezalel-smotrich) and [Itamar Ben-Gvir](/wiki/itamar-ben-gvir), and the point he adds is a matter of record rather than of interpretation: both *"were successfully prosecuted for felony hate crimes against Palestinians years before ever becoming ministers in the Netanyahu government."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:16" />

His measure of that record is Nuremberg. *"People went to prison at Nuremberg for less,"* he says, and reaches for Julius Streicher as the example — a man who, however nasty a piece of work, *"just"* published a newspaper.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:16" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="19:47" />
