**The kafala sponsorship system** is the arrangement governing foreign workers in Saudi Arabia, which [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) confirmed and described in September 2025 when a viewer asked whether it was true that a worker is assigned a handler, has their passport taken and can leave only with permission.

His answer was one word — *"Yes, that is true"* — and both hosts agreed it is *"a serious human rights problem."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="11:32" />

## How it works

*"You have to be sponsored. So you have to say on your visa application who your sponsor is. A Saudi has to be the majority shareholder of whatever company you're working for."* There is no way around it: *"you can't just go to Saudi Arabia and say, I'm going to open an LLC and we're going to make widgets. Uh-uh. You're going to open an LLC that you own 49% of. Some random Saudi is going to own 51%."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:02" />

The leverage is documentary. *"The Saudi is going to hold your passport. And if he doesn't like your face, or you have a fight or a falling out over money, then you're essentially under house arrest because you can't go anywhere."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:02" />

## What Kiriakou says it is used for

He does not leave the consequence abstract. *"That's how they keep especially South Asian women as sex slaves. They hire them as domestics, nannies, English teachers, and then they arrive"* — the sponsor takes the passport, and the arrangement becomes something else entirely.<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:33" />

His assessment of the country that runs it is unqualified — *"I hated it there"* — and his aside on the relationship is a single line: *"good thing we're so close to them."*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:33" /><Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="13:03" />
