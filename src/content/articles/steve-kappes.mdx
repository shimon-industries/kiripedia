**Steve Kappes** is a former Deputy Director of the [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia). [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) encountered him in the lobby of the Marriott hotel in Sanaa, Yemen, during his final Yemen trip as a Senate Foreign Relations Committee investigator in approximately 2010. The presence of the *"package is moving"* protective-detail call-out alerted Kiriakou to the presence of a senior visitor; Kappes greeted him by name on emerging from the elevator.<Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:21:55" /><Cite s="2025-08-31-dalton-fischer-mossad-blackwater" t="3:22:57" />

## Delivering the ultimatum to Gaddafi

Kiriakou says that while he was serving as executive assistant, Kappes — then CIA deputy director — flew to Tripoli and personally told Muammar Gaddafi to give up his weapons of mass destruction programs; Gaddafi complied, giving up his nuclear energy program as well as his chemical and biological weapons programs.<Cite s="2026-02-09-epic-real-estate" t="22:51" />

## The Feinstein condition on Panetta's confirmation

Kiriakou describes Senator Dianne Feinstein as one of the CIA's foremost cheerleaders on Capitol Hill, and says that when Barack Obama struggled to find a CIA director willing to take the job, she told Leon Panetta she would vote to confirm him only if he chose Kappes — one of the "godfathers" and creators of the CIA torture program — as his deputy.<Cite s="2019-12-30-scott-horton" t="35:15" />
