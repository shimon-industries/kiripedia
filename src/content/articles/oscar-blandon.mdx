**Oscar Blandón** is one of the two men named in [Gary Webb](/wiki/gary-webb-kill-the-messenger)'s [Dark Alliance](/wiki/dark-alliance) series.

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) identifies him in his account of what the seven articles actually alleged: *"there were two people in particular one was named Oscar Blandon Oscar Blandon was a senior Contra leader who was in charge of this cocaine operation the other was a major drug importer based in Los Angeles."*<Cite s="2025-03-12-john-kiriakou-the-gary-webb-story-cia-cocaine-and-a-media-a" t="06:21" />

The route Blandón's operation served is the one Kiriakou traces from the mid-1980s: cocaine arriving in the United States *"ostensibly from Colombia but really making its way through Nicaragua"*, ending in Los Angeles, where *"it was turned into crack cocaine"* — a drug nobody had heard of before 1984, and which then *"took off like wildfire."*<Cite s="2025-03-12-john-kiriakou-the-gary-webb-story-cia-cocaine-and-a-media-a" t="04:15" /><Cite s="2025-03-12-john-kiriakou-the-gary-webb-story-cia-cocaine-and-a-media-a" t="04:45" />
