**The Epstein statute of limitations** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s explanation for why he expects no American criminal prosecutions to come out of the [Jeffrey Epstein](/wiki/jeffrey-epstein) files.

On the sex cases, he says, the limitation period is five years, *"and so we've missed it."* There will be civil suits, but nobody is going to be charged with a crime.<Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="28:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="39:19" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="31:07" /> He hates it, he says — *"I hate that very much"* — and adds a pointed suggestion about the delay: *"which, you know what, may have been a part of the plan. Why didn't Joe Biden release all this information?"* Every administration since Clinton, in his view, has had the chance and covered it up.<Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="39:19" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="30:37" />

He does hedge the scope of the claim. Speaking to LBC in February 2026, he said that *"for the most part"* the limitation periods appeared to have expired, and that it was therefore highly unlikely anybody would be brought to justice.<Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="00:00" />

## Confirmed from the top

The position was not left to inference. After the Justice Department released a further three million documents — the second tranche or the third, Kiriakou says, depending on how one counts — the deputy attorney general said over that weekend, on both Fox News and CNN, that there would be no prosecutions arising from them. Even though the documents clearly show crimes against children, the statute of limitations has apparently expired, *"which is terrible."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="01:04" /><Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="06:45" />

## What is left for the victims

What survives is civil. The victims are still able to file suits against the estate of Jeffrey Epstein — which, he notes, is very sizable — but he does not present this as adequate: *"that's not much solace."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="01:04" /><Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="06:40" /> Pressed on the political names in the files, he redirected the question to the same point: *"the issue is what about the women and the girls? Where's the justice for them?"*<Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="06:40" />

Nor, in his account, is there any residual criminal process to be hoped for. There will be no international arrest warrant and no grand jury impanelled; the men named are *"free to lick their wounds and go off into the sunset."*<Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="05:08" />

He is equally deflating about the idea of compelling testimony. Congress, he says, probably cannot demand that the former Prince Andrew appear — he cannot be subpoenaed because he is not an American citizen and is not located in the United States, and having worked for years on Capitol Hill, Kiriakou describes the mechanism as informal, a request that the witness do the committee the kindness of appearing. And in any event he doubts it would accomplish anything: the questions would be asked and the answers would either be refusals or things most people would find unacceptable.<Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="01:01" /><Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="01:32" />

## The one remaining route

Kiriakou says he put the question to a constitutional attorney, who told him a prosecution could go forward only if the Justice Department could successfully argue that the cover-up of the original crime continued and constituted a conspiracy — so that the charge would be conspiracy to commit the crime rather than the crime itself. *"But he said even that is a stretch."*<Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="38:16" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="38:48" />

## What that costs

His objection is not procedural but civic. What a prosecution would have shown the public of every country involved, he says, is that it does not matter how famous or how important you are — *"we're all equal under the law"* — and that is not going to be the case here.<Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="38:16" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="41:04" />

## The substitute: ruin instead of trial

Having ruled out prosecution, Kiriakou offers a consolation and is candid that it is one. Perhaps, he says, the public will have to be content with allowing these men to be social outcasts for the rest of their lives — and then reframes that as a heavier sentence than it sounds: *"in their obituaries, this is going to be the story. And maybe that's worse than what we might want to see them face right now."*<Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="00:30" />

His worked example is [Peter Mandelson](/wiki/peter-mandelson), who he says denied any connection to Epstein repeatedly, was not merely a regular on the island but a friend, and was shown in a Justice Department photograph in his underwear beside a sixteen-year-old girl, the two of them looking at a laptop. The camera, Kiriakou reasons, was clearly hidden, because no such man would allow himself to be photographed that way. Mandelson resigned from the Labour Party and from the House of Lords that weekend.<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="02:08" /><Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="02:40" /> *"Maybe that's some solace,"* he says. The man is personally ruined and socially an outcast, and he is not young: when he dies, his whole obituary will be about this.<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="03:12" /><Cite s="2026-03-05-the-paper-trail-whats-happening-in-america-after-u-s" t="08:22" /> Disappointed as he is that there will be no prosecutions, he concludes that the least that can be done is to get the information out and *"let people see the truth in broad daylight."*<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="03:12" />

## Where the scandal actually is

Kiriakou is careful to separate the volume of mentions from the substance of the crimes. [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) was named more than a thousand times in the release, which he says is no surprise given that the two men were close friends at one time — as could be said of [Bill Clinton](/wiki/bill-clinton), Bill Gates and the former Prince Andrew. The scandal, in his view, is not how many times a name appears but what the documents reveal about crimes.<Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="01:37" /><Cite s="2026-02-03-the-deep-focus-epstein-files-cia-asset-recruitments-worst-spies" t="02:08" />

He also warns against material that is not in the files at all. A great deal of AI-generated reporting had circulated saying Trump did this or that and giving ages for the girls, *"none of that appears in these documents."* Both Trump and Clinton have said they committed no crimes, and Kiriakou expected nothing more shocking to emerge than what already had.<Cite s="2026-02-02-lbc-cia-whistleblower-on-epstein-files-why-no-one" t="02:03" />

## The British contrast

He contrasts the American position with the British one, where he says the authorities have been more straightforward: the charges brought there concern documents rather than sex — Prince Andrew over passing Epstein a document relating to British business and trade negotiations, which he and his interviewer identify as the Al Yamamah deal, and Peter Mandelson rumoured to have handed over something from the Defence Ministry, potentially an Official Secrets Act violation. *"But none of this stuff has to do with sex."*<Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="28:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-29-john-kiriakou-podcast-john-kiriakou-podcast" t="29:00" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="31:07" /><Cite s="2026-02-23-epstein-lies-satanic-elite-mossad" t="31:38" />
