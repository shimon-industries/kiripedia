From 2020 the large social platforms began attaching warning labels to accounts identified as affiliated with foreign state media. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) has described the practice from the position of someone who expected to be caught by it.

*"I''ve gotten to the point where I have to go to my Twitter every day to see if they''ve put a little red tag saying that I''m an official mouthpiece of the Russian government, which I''m not."*<Cite s="2022-05-05-panquake-lee-camp-full-interview-kiriakou-sean-o-brien-talk-pan" t="05:14" />

The case he offers as proof that the labelling was not being done carefully is the broadcaster [Ed Schultz](/wiki/ed-schultz), who had presented a programme on RT America and died in 2018: *"poor Ed Schultz has been dead for how many years, and they made him, they put a tag on him saying that he''s a mouthpiece of the Russian government. So that was one thing that bothers me very much."*<Cite s="2022-05-05-panquake-lee-camp-full-interview-kiriakou-sean-o-brien-talk-pan" t="05:14" />

He raises it as the smaller of two grievances, the larger being [what had by then disappeared from his reading](/wiki/kiriakou-daily-news-diet) altogether.<Cite s="2022-05-05-panquake-lee-camp-full-interview-kiriakou-sean-o-brien-talk-pan" t="05:44" />
