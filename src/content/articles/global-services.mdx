**Global Services** is the name [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) attaches to the post-9/11 CIA cadre that operationalises the [Tuesday-morning kill list](/wiki/tuesday-morning-kill-list), the paramilitary teams the agency dispatches around the world to kill named individuals produced by a standing weekly meeting at the White House.

## The trainer

Kiriakou's most-repeated line about the cadre is that he knew the man who built it, *"Rick"*, an unassuming colleague ten feet from his desk in the Counterterrorism Center. *"Nicest guy. He'd come in every morning. He'd say, 'Hey guys.' We'd say, 'Hey, Rick.'"* Kiriakou did not know what Rick did. A neighbouring officer told him: *"John, he's the head of the [Special Activities Division](/wiki/special-activities-division)."*<Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="31:27" /><Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="31:59" />

The retrospective Kiriakou credits is a *Vanity Fair* piece: *"what qualified him to be the head of the Special Activities Division was that he had been a hitman for the Cali cartel when he was recruited into the CIA right at 9/11 because we needed somebody who knew how to kill people and not get caught. And so he trained an entire cadre that is now called global services."*<Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="31:59" /><Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="32:31" />

## The weekly calendar

The operating cycle Kiriakou describes is a calendar entry. *"With the technology that the CIA has now, it is able to carry out a meeting at the White House every Tuesday morning at 7:00 called the Tuesday morning kill list meeting. And what they do is they come up with a list of people to be executed that week. And then using technology that we learned about thanks to the Vault 7 revelations, global services sends its teams all around the world. They kill the people they're supposed to kill and then they come back for next Tuesday's meeting."*<Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="32:31" /><Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="33:02" />

## The toolkit

The technology Kiriakou attaches to Global Services is the toolkit the [Vault 7](/wiki/vault-7) leaks put in the public record: remote takeover of car computers *"to drive you over a bridge or into an abutment or into a tree"*; the smart-TV attack that turns the speaker into a microphone while the set appears off; the phone stack Kiriakou has stopped trying to defend against.<Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="33:33" /><Cite s="2026-08-22-2600-oak-lawn-both-sides-of-the-wire-surveillance-whistleblowing-and" t="34:03" />
