**Trump's NATO reversal** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s account of why [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump), having spent years attacking the North Atlantic alliance, came to embrace it in 2025.

His general observation about Trump's positions comes first: he is *"always flip-flopping like a fish on a dock."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:16:54" />

The specific explanation is about a failed assumption. Kiriakou believes Trump *"really believed that Vladimir Putin would do what he told him to do"*, and that he is *"just so angry that Putin won't just go to the negotiating table and settle this thing with Ukraine."* Having lost the bilateral route, Trump *"now has got to embrace NATO. He's got to. Otherwise, he's going to be out there all alone."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:16:54" />

The reversal, on this reading, is not a change of view about the alliance's merits but a fallback position produced by the failure of a personal approach — which returns Kiriakou to the point he had been making all afternoon about the Gaza talks: *"diplomacy takes work."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:17:25" />
