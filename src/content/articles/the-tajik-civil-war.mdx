**The Tajik civil war** is the conflict [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) reaches for when he wants a precedent for a war that kills enormous numbers of people without being reported, and which he raised in October 2025 as the closest analogue to [Sudan](/wiki/the-sudan-massacre).

## The scale

*"A lot of people died,"* he says. *"By some accounts, a quarter to a third of the population."* There were *"massacres, ethnic massacres all the time."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="08:48" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="09:19" />

The detail he singles out is a logistical one. At one point *"they ran out of bullets"* — and so *"they would tie people together with ropes and throw them into rivers and drown them all together. It was like one of their things."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="09:19" />

## Whose war it was

The similarity to Sudan that Kiriakou identifies is in the financing. These *"were proxy wars that were funded by outsiders. And as long as the money kept coming in, the war went on."* The sponsors in Tajikistan's case were an unlikely set to have in common: *"the Taliban and the US and Russia and China."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="09:19" /><Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="09:49" />

## How it stopped

It did not stop because anyone won. The sponsors *"basically all lost interest in Tajikistan"* and *"they stopped funding it"* — after which *"there was a power sharing agreement, and they had a fragile peace."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="09:49" />

Kiriakou's coda is the part that makes the comparison to Sudan bearable rather than only bleak: *"now they've really climbed out of the hole. It took a long time, but they were in a deep hole."*<Cite s="2025-11-01-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-john-kiriakou-gaz" t="09:49" />
