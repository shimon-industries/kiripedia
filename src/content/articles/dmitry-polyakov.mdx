**Dmitry Polyakov** was a colonel in the GRU, Soviet military intelligence, and an agent run by [CIA](/wiki/cia) officer [Sandy Grimes](/wiki/sandy-grimes). He was among the assets betrayed to Moscow by [Aldrich Ames](/wiki/aldrich-ames).<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="14:27" />

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) raises Polyakov to explain motive on the other side of the case. Grimes, he says, *"took Ames's crimes personally"* — Polyakov had been her agent, and he was one of the men Ames burned. The analyst who eventually correlated Ames's meetings with his KGB handler against his bank deposits was not working an abstract counterintelligence puzzle.<Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="13:55" /><Cite s="2026-02-13-dead-drop-mini-episode-5-betrayal" t="14:27" />
