**The Hajj ban as leverage** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s answer to a question asked during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war): with Saudi Arabia declaring that all trust with Iran was lost after strikes on Saudi facilities, what would a Saudi response actually look like?

His answer began with the conventional options — Riyadh *"could break relations again, or even just recall ambassadors,"* and he would not be surprised if it broke relations, the diplomatic relationship being only about three years old after decades without one.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="11:38" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:09" />

Then the real instrument: *"if you really want to hurt the Iranians, you forbid Iranians from making the Hajj, the pilgrimage to Mecca."* He prefaced it with a warning against underrating it — *"this is going to sound silly, but it's actually quite important."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:09" />

## The precedent

Kiriakou says it has been done: *"They did that for a couple of years, 1979, 1980, 1981. And it was a crisis, let me tell you."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:09" /><Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:41" /> The crisis escalated — *"to the point where the Iranians started carrying out terrorist attacks against Saudis,"* and, in his account, supported a takeover by Saudi radicals in which people were killed.<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:41" />

His forecast was that this, rather than a military reply, is where the Saudis would begin: *"My guess is that's what the Saudis are going to start with. Just ban all Iranians from the Hajj."*<Cite s="2026-03-20-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="12:41" />

Why a military reply is unlikely is treated at [Saudi military composition](/wiki/saudi-military-composition).
