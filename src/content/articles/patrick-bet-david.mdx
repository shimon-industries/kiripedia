**Patrick Bet-David** is the podcast host whose confrontation with [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) in April 2026 produced one of the few on-record retractions Kiriakou has made about a named person.

The dispute began with remarks Kiriakou had made on another programme, which Bet-David played back to him at the top of the interview. In the clip Kiriakou describes him as *"a hardcore Zionist"* and, more particularly, as *"a 1,000% backer of [Reza Pahlavi](/wiki/reza-pahlavi) to return to Iran and to assume the Peacock Throne"*; he had also attributed to Bet-David the claim that support for overthrowing the Iranian government stood not at 60 per cent but at 94 per cent.<Cite s="2026-04-23-valuetainment-valuetainment" t="00:31" />

Bet-David denied having made the 94 per cent claim. Kiriakou did not contest the denial, withdrew the characterisation, and repeated the withdrawal several times over the course of the exchange, *"No, I'm wrong and I apologize"*, closing with an unqualified version: *"you've shown me wrong and I'm sorry that I perpetuated an untruth."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-valuetainment-valuetainment" t="01:05" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-valuetainment-valuetainment" t="06:16" /><Cite s="2026-04-23-valuetainment-valuetainment" t="12:00" />

## The warning beforehand

Asked whether anyone had cautioned him about the appearance in advance, Kiriakou said yes but declined to name the person, describing him only as a big name: *"He said, oh, you're going on PBD? I said, yeah. He said, be careful. Patrick comes out swinging on Israel."*<Cite s="2026-04-23-valuetainment-valuetainment" t="05:13" />
