**Maria Butina** is a Russian gun-rights activist convicted in the United States of failing to register as a foreign agent. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says Butina founded a small Russian gun club, akin to a miniature version of the NRA, and reached out to the American NRA to build ties after moving to the United States for graduate school.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="00:31" /> He says he has spoken with her a number of times and is Facebook friends with her.<Cite s="2023-05-13-indie-news-network-inn" t="25:14" /> She was arrested and charged with failure to register as a foreign agent, which Kiriakou characterizes as a "process crime" rather than genuine espionage.<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="01:34" /> Kiriakou notes that Butina, a first-time offender for whom federal sentencing guidelines called for zero to six months, instead received 18 months in solitary confinement<Cite s="2023-12-11-danny-jones-whats-really-happening-in-israel" t="04:19" /><Cite s="2023-05-13-indie-news-network-inn" t="25:46" />, and that the United Nations deems any solitary confinement lasting more than two weeks to be a form of torture.<Cite s="2023-05-13-indie-news-network-inn" t="25:46" />
