**Charlie Kirk** was an American conservative activist whom [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes as the centre of the MAGA movement and one of the few figures of his generation with a genuine claim on mass political authority. Kiriakou disagreed with him on *"I don't know 80% of the issues,"* but treats that disagreement as beside the point: *"he was a born leader, and that was what his real service to the right and to the Republican party was."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="17:11" />

## The one meeting

Kiriakou met Kirk once, while working on the [Senate Foreign Relations Committee](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee). Kirk was seventeen or eighteen and arrived with a group of Tea Party people, which Kiriakou found funny, he watched them for a moment and thought *"you know what? It's that kid who's the leader. It's not any of the adults."* The group went office to office lobbying for tax cuts, and when it came time to leave there was a press conference, which the teenager gave himself. Kiriakou said to a colleague, *"Boy, that kid's going places, whoever he is"*, and had to ask who he was.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="16:09" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="16:40" />

## Political weight

On Kiriakou's account [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) said he would not have been elected to a second term were it not for Kirk, because it was Kirk who brought young people voting for the first time not merely to the Trump campaign but to MAGA as a movement.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="15:39" /> That is the basis for the comparison Kiriakou offers: *"Think of somebody like Martin Luther King or Robert F. Kennedy Senior. I would compare his political power, his authority to those people."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="15:39" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="16:09" />

He regards Kirk as unreplaceable within [Turning Point USA](/wiki/turning-point-usa) and the wider movement: the remaining figures, he names Ben Shapiro and Benny Johnson, *"are not Charlie Kirk. So this is going to hurt. This is going to do damage."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="15:07" />

## The turn on Israel

Kirk had been, in Kiriakou's description, *"one of the most pro-Israel, one of the most pro-Zionism figures on the American right until recently"*, and then began taking a stand against Israeli war crimes in Gaza.<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="11:50" /> Kiriakou sets that alongside the collapse of American public support for Israel to explain why some observers looked to [Mossad](/wiki/mossad) after the killing: a figure with Kirk's following switching sides *"would be a serious problem for the Israelis."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="12:22" /><Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="12:52" /> His own conclusion is a refusal: *"Did the Israelis have a reason for this to happen? You bet they did. Did they do it? I don't see it."*<Cite s="2025-10-11-dialogue-works-charlie-kirk-s-story-crumbles-iran" t="12:52" />
