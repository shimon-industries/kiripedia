**The National Guard in Washington** was the federalisation of the District of Columbia police force in August 2025, and the first subject John Kiriakou took up on the DeProgram programme that day.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:33" />

## His personal response

Kiriakou announced a decision before analysing the policy. *"I made a decision today when I heard that Donald Trump was federalising the Washington DC police force and taking over police duties in Washington with the help of the National Guard."* Because he lives *"three miles from the White House"*, he expected to encounter them: *"I'm going to go up to every National Guardsman that I see and I'm going to say, 'You should be ashamed of yourself. If there was ever a time to stand up and say no, now's the time, you gutless pigs.'"*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="02:33" />

## The stated justification

He disputes the premise of a crime emergency, and doubts it is sincerely held: Trump *"has gotten it into his head that crime is out of control. Actually, he probably hasn't even gotten it into his head. He's just faking it."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:06" />

The figures he gives run the other way: *"DC does not have a crime problem. Murders are down. Violent crimes overall are down 37%. We have no crime problem in Washington right now."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:06" />

He also notes who was left out of the decision: *"He didn't bother to inform Mayor Muriel Bowser. He didn't bother to inform the DC police chief. He just announced that he's taken it over and that's it."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:06" />

## The three-day limit

Kiriakou says he checked the authority, *"so I looked this up"*, and found it narrow. The president *"absolutely can do"* it, but only *"for a period of three days. He can take it over for three days and then he has to get congressional authorisation."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:06" /><Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:36" />

The obstacle was procedural. *"Well, guess what? Congress isn't in session. And so remember, they all ran away so they wouldn't have to vote on the Epstein release."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:36" />

He suspects the check was never made: *"I wonder if they didn't check that. I suspect they didn't check it. And so this is a publicity stunt. Literally nothing is going to come of it."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="03:36" />

## The homeless

On the accompanying promise to clear homeless people from the streets, Kiriakou's objection was that nothing had been said about where they would go: *"No word on what that means or where you're going to send them. You going to send them to Maryland? ... I don't know. And it doesn't make any sense."*<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="04:06" />

He is not, however, dismissive of the underlying conditions, having noted separately that homelessness in the capital is a real problem even as the response to it is a stunt.<Cite s="2025-08-12-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="04:06" />
