Speaking in April 2019 to supporters who assumed [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) would never move on [Julian Assange](/wiki/julian-assange), [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) argued the opposite, on the basis of what he said he had learned trying to reach the president himself: *"I actually have some access to the White House right now … it's quite expensive access, but it's access nonetheless."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="37:36" />

## The rule of three

*"The president really is malleable. The thing is — and this is just his personality — he needs to hear something from three different people."* One advocate is ignored; two are ignored; *"but if he hears it from the two of us and somebody that he plays golf with at Mar-a-Lago on the weekends, he might actually do something."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="38:09" /><Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="38:40" />

Kiriakou says he confirmed the rule by accident. An aide to Trump mentioned him to the president about six months earlier and *"he paid no attention whatsoever."* Then Rand Paul mentioned him roughly two weeks later, and Trump's response was to place the earlier one: *"you know, Joe Smith mentioned this guy to me a couple of weeks ago — so now that's two people."* *"So I know that this is true, what I've been told: that he needs to hear something from three different people, three different people he respects, and he's willing to change his mind."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="40:43" /><Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="41:14" />

## Why the neoconservative appointments were grounds for optimism

Asked to explain to Assange supporters why the appointments of [Gina Haspel](/wiki/gina-haspel), Elliott Abrams and [John Bolton](/wiki/john-bolton) should temper their hopes, Kiriakou inverted the question. Those appointments showed Trump had been *"clearly influenced by other neocons"* — *"and so if he can be influenced one way, I believe actually that he can be influenced in another way as well. It's just a question of getting to him."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="39:11" /><Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="39:42" /> He cited Trump's campaign-trail *"God bless WikiLeaks"* as proof the position had already moved once.<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="38:40" /><Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="40:43" />

## Who to reach, and what gets in the way

The route he proposed ran through civil-libertarian Republicans rather than the movement's own constituency: *"we need to get to the likes of Mike Lee, Republican senator from Utah, or Rand Paul, a Republican from Kentucky, or Mark Meadows, who's the chairman of the House Tea Party caucus — Republicans who believe that this is a civil liberties issue and not a criminal one. They're the ones who can really influence the president much more effectively than we can."* He added that he did not care why Republicans supported Assange — whether on civil-liberties grounds or because they saw him as an opponent of Hillary Clinton — *"so long as the outcome is right."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="39:42" /><Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="42:16" />

The obstacle he named on the same side was demobilisation by conspiracy: a quadrant of Trump supporters *"pacified"* into believing *"that they don't have to do anything, that Trump is in control and he'll save the day … that's just simply not true, and that weakens us."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="41:44" /><Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="42:16" />

## Sessions to Barr

Kiriakou said his own pessimism had lifted with the change of attorney general: *"I was very pessimistic for a long time, and then Jeff Sessions left. So I think that we've got a better chance now, because William Barr is a functionary — he's a bureaucrat and a paper pusher. He's not an ideologue like Jeff Sessions was. So maybe perhaps the guy might be willing to be convinced. We have to at least give it a try."*<Cite s="2019-04-07-unity4j-vigil-confirm-not-panel" t="43:18" />
