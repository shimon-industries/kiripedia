**The question about the showers** is John Kiriakou's account of how he prepared for imprisonment, and of what he actually wanted to know. He gave it on the DeProgram programme in July 2025 when a viewer asked how bad prison showers really were, prefacing the question with *"we've all seen the movies."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:11" />

## The lunch

Kiriakou's lawyers arranged a briefing. *"One of the things that my attorneys did for me was they scheduled a lunch a couple of weeks before I left for prison with a former professor from George Washington University who played fast and loose with the National Science Foundation credit card and got two years."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:11" />

He stresses how little he asked. The showers were *"one of the very few questions I asked my attorneys"*, and it was *"the very first question I asked him."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:11" />

## The answer

The professor dismissed the premise. *"Oh no, don't worry about that,"* he said. *"It's nothing like what you see on TV. You're going to go to a low or a minimum, and they're all individual showers, completely enclosed with shower curtains. Same with the toilets. They're all individual toilets with shower curtains. Don't worry about it at all."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:43" />

Kiriakou's verdict on the briefing is unqualified: *"he was exactly right."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:43" />

## The rule underneath it

He then generalises from the plumbing to the etiquette. *"I never saw a guy naked in prison,"* he says, and immediately corrects himself for accuracy, *"well, that's not true. We did have one strip search once."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="57:43" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:15" />

The correction aside, the point stands as a rule with an enforcement mechanism: *"you do not walk around exposing yourself unless you want to have every bone in your face broken."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:15" /> Kiriakou illustrated it in the same passage with the case of a prisoner who ignored the rule and was beaten senseless for it.<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="58:15" />

The episode belongs to a small class of Kiriakou stories in which the reality of the [federal prison system](/wiki/federal-prison-security-levels) turns out to be less lurid than its reputation, a corrective he applies as readily as the reverse.
