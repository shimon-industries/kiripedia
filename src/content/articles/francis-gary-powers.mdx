**Francis Gary Powers** was the CIA U-2 pilot whose 1960 shoot-down over the Soviet Union and subsequent capture the CIA initially denied. Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) the agency *"not only just denied that he was CIA, we denied that he was USG at all"*, until trading him back in a *"bridge-of-spies"* prisoner exchange. Powers is buried at Arlington National Cemetery.<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:49:42" />

Kiriakou: *"This poor guy, after piloting the U-2, ended up piloting a traffic helicopter in L.A., and then he crashed that and was killed in the crash."*<Cite s="2026-05-04-cleared-hot-446-cost-of-truth" t="1:50:12" />

## The traffic helicopter (Arlington tour)

Per [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou), [Francis Gary Powers](/wiki/francis-gary-powers), shot down in his U-2 over the Soviet Union in 1960, survived a parachute ejection that no one was sure was even survivable from that altitude, because U-2 pilots were expected to go down with their aircraft. Taken prisoner and paraded before Soviet media, he forced Eisenhower to admit the U.S. flew spy planes.<Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="10:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="11:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="11:30" />

When Powers returned home there was no hero's welcome; many Americans felt he should have gone down with the plane rather than be taken alive and embarrass the country. Unable to find work, he was eventually hired as a traffic-helicopter pilot by a Southern California television station and was killed in a crash while reporting on traffic. Kiriakou's assessment of his [Arlington](/wiki/arlington-cemetery-spies) burial: *"He is exactly where he belongs."*<Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="12:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-06-walk-with-history-spies-arlington-cemetery" t="12:30" />
