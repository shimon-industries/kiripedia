**PricewaterhouseCoopers** (**PwC**) is the Big Four firm that appears in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s story from three unlikely directions.

## Target, then audience

In his post-CIA corporate job, Kiriakou's assignment was to collect first-person human-source intelligence on his employer's competitors — Ernst & Young, KPMG, PricewaterhouseCoopers, and consulting rivals like IBM and Accenture.<Cite s="2015-05-06-the-real-news-network-why-i-was-targeted-by-the-cia-rai-9-10" t="02:52" /> The competitive stakes were concrete: a $50 million deal could turn on PwC undercutting a bid by four million.<Cite s="2024-11-07-julian-dorey-249" t="33:15" />

When his first book came out around early 2010, PwC hired him to keynote an offsite for 140 partners. He told them the story of the al-Qaeda fighter he recruited in Pakistan by treating him like a human being — and landed the moral: *"What I did and what you do really isn't that different, because it's all about the relationship. If I can convince somebody to commit espionage... just because he has a good relationship with me, you can certainly"* close a deal.<Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="08:53" /><Cite s="2025-03-29-lionel-nation" t="13:38" /><Cite s="2025-09-25-joe-mkhitaryan" t="41:11" /><Cite s="2025-09-25-joe-mkhitaryan" t="47:01" />

## The audits

PwC anchors two of Kiriakou's favorite indictments. First, the Pentagon: brought in to externally audit the defense budget, PwC tried for three years and gave up — *"we can't find where this money went."* For Kiriakou, the unaccountable trillion-dollar budget is one of America's major mistakes.<Cite s="2025-10-16-truth-hurts-show" t="32:46" /><Cite s="2025-09-25-joe-mkhitaryan" t="1:36:33" /><Cite s="2025-10-20-the-cia-is-the-greatest-threat-to-the-planet" t="16:36" /> Second, Greece: as the Greek government's auditor, PwC had been falsifying economic records for years — with Goldman Sachs involved — and in the end nobody from PwC, Goldman, or the government was ever prosecuted, while Greeks were left indebted for generations.<Cite s="2025-09-05-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="08:22" /><Cite s="2025-09-05-the-deep-focus-show-the-deep-focus-show" t="08:53" />
