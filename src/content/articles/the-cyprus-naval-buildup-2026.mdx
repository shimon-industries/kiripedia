**The Cyprus naval buildup, 2026** was the concentration of European warships and senior officials on Cyprus during the [2026 Iran war](/wiki/2026-iran-war), and the subject of an assessment [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gave when the details were put to him in an interview.

The deployments listed to him were Spanish, Italian and Dutch frigates; the French aircraft carrier *Charles de Gaulle* with a frigate escort and anti-missile and anti-drone systems; two Greek frigates together with four F-16s and a Hellenic Air Force C-130; and the British *HMS Dragon*.<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="09:33" /><Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="10:05" /> Alongside them came what the interviewer called the most high-level visits the island had seen in living memory, compressed into days: the Greek defence minister with the Greek army's senior officers, the British defence minister, the chief of MI6 at the presidential palace, and then Emmanuel Macron and the Greek prime minister Kyriakos Mitsotakis together.<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="10:40" /><Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="11:14" />

Kiriakou's explanation was neither military necessity nor conviction. *"As bad a policy as this is, the NATO militaries and even more broadly the European Union militaries feel compelled to at least have the appearance of being supportive of Israel and the United States."*<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="11:14" /><Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="11:50" /> What was said behind closed doors, he indicated, was another matter.<Cite s="2026-03-31-unfiltered-with-s-a-m-john-kiriakou-israel-s-samson-option-is-real" t="11:50" />
