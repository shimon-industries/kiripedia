**Kharg Island** is, in [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s description, *"where most of Iran's oil is refined."* Asked in January 2026 about the theory that a strike on it would destroy the world's oil economy, he treated the precedent as settled: it *"was bombed in the 1980s. It caused a global oil price shock."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="42:46" />

On whether it happens again he is conditional rather than predictive. It is *"possible that the West, meaning the United States and/or Israel, could bomb it again."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="42:46" />

What he will not accept is the premise that Iran would simply absorb the loss. *"If that were to happen, I think the Iranians, having learned their lesson in earlier decades, would come out swinging — and it would just be a crisis for oil prices."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="43:17" />

The assessment sits alongside his reading of the same month's [naval movements](/wiki/watching-naval-movements), which led him to conclude that no American strike on Iran was then possible at all.<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="21:23" />
