**The Jay Jones texts** were private messages from 2022, leaked in the closing fortnight of the 2025 Virginia campaign, in which the Democratic candidate for attorney general joked to a friend about violence against Republicans. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) was asked about them twice, and his answer moved.

## What was in them

A viewer put the question directly, characterising it as *"the Democrat talking about killing his opponent and his family."* Kiriakou agreed with the framing of its importance, *"that's become the biggest issue in the Virginia statewide races"*, and described the content more mildly: *"he was joking in a text exchange with a friend of his, and he said something about like target practice against Republicans, or some stupid thing like that."*<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:56" />

Corrected by the host that there had been more than one message, he accepted it: *"yeah, it was several. It wasn't just one. He said several different things."*<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="34:56" /><Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:27" />

## The reflex, and the revision

His first answer was condemnation. *"My knee-jerk reaction is: shame on him. He's advocating violence, even if he's doing it in what he says to be a joking manner"*, and as the host added and he repeated, in a private conversation.<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:27" />

The second answer arrived seconds later and reversed the emphasis. *"And I've gotten to the point where my position is: we all talk shit privately. The Republicans pull this shit all the time. I don't care."*<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:27" />

He supported the symmetry with an example from the previous week, a Young Republicans text scandal, and agreed with it twice.<Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:27" /><Cite s="2025-10-22-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="35:57" />

## The prediction that failed

On 5 November, with the result in, Kiriakou returned to the affair to note that the expected punishment had not materialised. The messages had been leaked on 22 October, *"less than two weeks ago"*, and concerned *"shooting Republicans in the head."* He set that against the standard he had already described: *"this is something Republicans have done time and time again, but this time, of course, they completely lose their minds because a Democrat did it. And nobody cared."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="04:16" />

The forecast everyone had made was of split tickets, voters backing Spanberger for governor and the Republican for attorney general. *"That didn't happen."* Jones won.<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="04:47" />

Kiriakou's closing note is agnostic about the man himself: *"now, we'll see what Jay Jones does in this position."*<Cite s="2025-11-05-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-1mebPn" t="04:47" />
