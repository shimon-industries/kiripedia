**"I should have gone to film school"** is one of two answers [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) gives to the question he is asked most often by strangers: knowing what he knows now, would he join the [CIA](/wiki/cia) again.

## Two answers

Kiriakou is unusually open about the fact that he has not answered it consistently. Sometimes he says yes, that the only way to change the CIA is from the inside, that nobody else blew the whistle on the torture programme, and that had he not, perhaps there would still be torture.<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="20:11" />

In other interviews he has given the other answer, and he does not want it taken lightly: *"I should have gone to film school. Um, I don't know. I I've I've told all of my children … it may have sounded like a joke, but I'm serious."*<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="20:11" />

What he has told his children is the harder version of the same thought: that he absolutely, positively never wants to hear the words *I want to work for the CIA* come out of any of their mouths. He thanks God that they do not.<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="20:41" />

## Where he lands

Pressed to settle it, he comes down on the side of doing it again, with one change. He would not have waited as long as he did to blow the whistle.<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="21:43" />

His stated reason is that somebody had to say something about *"a disgusting disgraceful crime against humanity that we were committing with smiles on our faces"*, and that he is glad it was him, and sorry that he waited.<Cite s="2026-07-03-the-jk-report-john-kiriakou-podcast-inside-fbi-recruiting-cia" t="21:43" />
