No verified John Kiriakou statement about Dr. Elizabeth Rouser was found in the source corpus. Her only mention occurs in a scripted narrator/producer voiceover segment appended to a Kiriakou interview episode, explicitly addressed to Kiriakou in the third person ("As John stated..."), and therefore cannot be attributed to Kiriakou himself.
