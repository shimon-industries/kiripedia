**CovertAction Magazine**, heir to the CovertAction Quarterly tradition of *"exposing covert action since 1978"*, is one of [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s regular publishing homes.

## Kiriakou's column

For years Kiriakou has described the same portfolio: a daily radio show, and three syndicated columns, one at Consortium News, one at ScheerPost out of USC, and one at CovertAction Magazine.<Cite s="2022-05-23-how-cia-s-rogue-agent-survived-prison-probe-v" t="46:15" /><Cite s="2022-07-23-the-darkened-hour" t="52:59" /><Cite s="2023-05-13-indie-news-network-inn" t="1:03:11" /> The magazine remained a fixture as the rest of the lineup evolved, through the Substack migration, the Rebel Media series, and the rest.<Cite s="2023-01-20-useful-idiots-gop-intel-oversight" t="31:47" /><Cite s="2023-06-26-kevin-gosztola" t="46:10" />

He also joined the magazine's podcast, the CovertAction Bulletin, introduced there as *"doing some work for CovertAction Bulletin and CovertAction Magazine"*, appearing on panels alongside Peter Dale Scott and Pepe Escobar, including one on the September 9, 2001 assassination of Ahmad Shah Massoud.<Cite s="2021-12-11-covertaction-bulletin-the-9-9-2001-massoud-assassination" t="01:40" /><Cite s="2021-12-11-covertaction-bulletin-the-9-9-2001-massoud-assassination" t="00:06" />

## The red check mark

Kiriakou tells of Pentagon-facing browser tools that flag disfavored outlets: red check marks popping up next to URLs, *"don't go to CovertAction Magazine."* In his telling, the same machinery labeled Consortium News Russian-adjacent; he wears the designation as proof of relevance.<Cite s="2023-04-12-danny-jones" t="34:39" />

## Named in the 2026 shortlist

In a January 2026 answer on which independent outlets he trusts, Kiriakou gave CovertAction Magazine a specific brief rather than a general endorsement: *"for historical analysis, CovertAction Magazine, Jeremy Kuzmarov is very good."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="07:21" />

It appeared in a short list headed by [Consortium News](/wiki/consortium-news) and also containing antiwar.com and *Reason*, of which he said that even where he disagrees, *"they argue it well."*<Cite s="2026-01-13-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-the-fix-is-in-deprogram-with-ted-rall-and-joh" t="07:21" />
