**Taking the ball and going home** is the shorthand for a position [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) took within days of the July 2024 shooting at [Butler](/wiki/butler-rally-shooting), when asked whether the attempt might have been deliberately enabled rather than merely botched.

## The objections

His gut answer was no, or likely no, and he gave three reasons. The first is scale: an operation of that kind would have so many moving parts that it would be virtually impossible to carry out.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:27:41" />

The second and third came from a conversation the same day with a friend who is a former FBI agent, and who had been giving it a lot of thought herself. If they were going to do that, she said, they would not have outsourced it to a twenty-year-old, *"just some local yokel from Bethel Park, Pennsylvania"*, and they would not have done it in front of thousands of people.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:27:41" />

Kiriakou is careful to mark what he is not saying. He is not claiming that such operations have never been carried out in the past; only that this is not how one would be carried out.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:28:13" />

## The decisive point

The argument he treats as conclusive is the outcome. Had it been the military or any American organisation, [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) would not be alive. A state operation would have provided secondary and tertiary layers precisely in case the first shooter missed, including a shooter tasked with killing the patsy.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:28:13" />

From that Kiriakou draws the behavioural rule his interviewers found most striking, and which they immediately contrasted with the prediction that a thwarted plot would double down: a state actor that has failed once does not try again. It takes its ball and goes home, as after the Bay of Pigs.<Cite s="2024-07-16-danny-jones-trump-assassination-attempt-john-is-co-guest" t="1:28:13" />
