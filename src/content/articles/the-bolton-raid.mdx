**The Bolton raid** was the FBI search of [John Bolton](/wiki/john-bolton)'s home and office on 22 August 2025, discussed by John Kiriakou on the DeProgram programme two weeks later.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="44:52" />

## What was taken

The host set out the reporting: agents took three computers, two iPhones and a quantity of old documents, and the investigation concerned classified material said to have been sent to family members.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="44:52" />

The warrant, as read out on the programme, described allegations that Bolton *"snuck national security files out of the White House during President Trump's first term by emailing them to family members on a private server"*, and cited two sections of the [Espionage Act](/wiki/espionage-act).<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="45:23" /><Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="45:54" /><Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:56" />

## Kiriakou's reading

His assessment was immediate and, unusually for him on such cases, unsympathetic to the accused. *"That is literally the definition of espionage in the Espionage Act."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="45:23" />

He attaches his own case to it. *"Remember, the definition of espionage was kind of mainstreamed in my case. It set a precedent. It is providing national defense information to any person not entitled to receive it."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="45:23" />

His conclusion is that the exposure is real: *"So yeah, this is real. He's in real trouble."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:56" />

## Why he thinks Bolton did it

Asked whether the transmission was for bragging or for convenience, Kiriakou's answer was neither: *"This is for his memoir ... And I think he probably did it just to make it easier for him to access later."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="47:27" />

On the method he is dismissive. Asked whether Bolton was stupid, he corrected the diagnosis: *"Arrogant. He's arrogant is what it is. This is arrogance."* The comparison he draws is with [Edward Snowden](/wiki/edward-snowden), who *"used thumb drives"*, a route that does not pass through a monitored server.<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="45:54" />

## The evidence sticker

The conversation turned to what the FBI habitually takes, and Kiriakou answered from his own experience: *"they took everything, which they normally do."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:25" />

The residue of that is still in front of him. *"In fact, on the back of my computer that I'm talking to you through, I still have an FBI sticker on the back that says evidence on it, that at first I couldn't get off, and now I just keep it as a souvenir."*<Cite s="2025-09-04-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="46:25" />
