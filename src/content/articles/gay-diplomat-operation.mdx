[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) described a [CIA](/wiki/cia) recruitment operation he ran against a foreign diplomat who was closeted and gay. The operation illustrates both the agency's approach to exploiting personal vulnerabilities and the unpredictable ways in which recruitment can succeed.

## The undercover approach

Kiriakou built a cover identity as a gay man and spent several months cultivating the target — lunches, dinners, theater performances. The investment of time was deliberate: the goal was to establish genuine personal rapport before any approach to the target's professional life.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="17:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="18:00" />

After several months, Kiriakou invited the diplomat to his apartment for dinner. At an appropriate moment during the evening, he leaned in to kiss him.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="18:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="18:30" />

## The target's response

The diplomat pulled back and said: *"I am gay. I'm just not into hairy guys."*<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="18:30" />

Kiriakou described this as a complete deflation of the planned approach. The romantic gambit had failed.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="18:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="19:00" />

## Recruitment succeeded anyway

Despite the failed personal approach, the operation ultimately succeeded — though through an entirely different mechanism. The diplomat had been passed over for a promotion he believed he deserved. That professional grievance proved more powerful than any personal leverage. Out of anger at his own government, he agreed to cooperate and provided everything Kiriakou was seeking.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="19:00" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="19:30" /><Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="20:00" />

Kiriakou used the account to illustrate the [CIA](/wiki/cia) doctrine that financial motivation and personal grievance — not coercion — are the reliable drivers of recruitment. The diplomat was not blackmailed; he was angry, and he acted on it.<Cite s="2026-05-05-morgan-nelson-cia-whistleblower-control-anyone" t="20:00" />
