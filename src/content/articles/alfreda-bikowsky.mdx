**Alfreda Bikowsky** is a senior [Central Intelligence Agency](/wiki/cia) officer who headed Alec Station, the CIA's Bin Laden unit, and before that served as its chief of operations, overseeing the agency's torture program. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) describes her as *"bad news from the get-go."*<Cite s="2023-01-04-scott-horton" t="34:39" />

## The San Diego cell

Kiriakou says Bikowsky and Rich Blee tried to turn the San Diego al-Qaeda cell into double agents but failed, and "dropped the ball."<Cite s="2023-01-04-scott-horton" t="35:11" />

## The clique of five

Kiriakou describes a group of five women in the [Counterterrorism Center](/wiki/counterterrorism-center) around whom the detention programme was built, and says [Jennifer Matthews](/wiki/jennifer-matthews) was the only one of them he did not hate; the others he calls bloodthirsty monsters.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="7:16" /><Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="7:46" /> Asked directly whether the officer he calls the red-headed devil had been trained in the torture techniques, his answer was that she had not merely been trained in them — she was the programme's founder and creator.<Cite s="2026-05-09-the-never-broken-podcast-the-never-broken-podcast" t="29:41" />

## At the July 2001 briefing

Kiriakou places her in the room for [Cofer Black's warning to the liaison class](/wiki/cofer-black-pre-9-11-warning) on 6 July 2001. He had booked a junior analyst; Black arrived instead and brought with him the head of operations for the bin Laden unit — identified in the telling only as *"a woman we used to call the red-headed devil"*, with the aside *"She's lucky we were that nice about it, too."*<Cite s="2026-07-06-the-jason-jones-show" t="52:03" />
