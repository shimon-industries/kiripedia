**The C-130 resettlements** is the name for a rule of American refugee geography that John Kiriakou and Ted Rall arrived at together in December 2025, when a viewer asked about immigration enforcement in Minnesota.

## The Minnesota question

Kiriakou began from family knowledge of the state: his brother lived there for nine years and his sister for about fifteen. There are large communities of Somalis, and of Hmong and Lao — and he said he had been unaware that a lack of documentation was a problem among them.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="51:22" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="51:53" />

Rall's reason for the same puzzlement was that these people had been brought in, especially the Hmong. Kiriakou completed the sentence: *"They were former CIA assets."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="51:53" /> Rall's confirmation followed — they were working for the CIA or for the military, and were brought over as refugees.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="51:53" />

## The rule

Rall then set out the mechanism as he understood it, and asked to be corrected. In these situations everyone packs into a C-130, the aircraft lands at whatever United States Air Force base it happens to land at, and *"that's kind of like where you live"* — which, he said, is how Dayton ended up with a large Vietnamese population.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="51:53" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="52:24" />

Kiriakou did not correct it. He extended it, supplying two further cases: *"And and Bismarck North Dakota and St. Louis with a ton of Kurds. Iraqi Kurds."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="52:24" /> Rall's response — *"So random"* — drew Kiriakou's agreement: *"Exactly right."*<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="52:24" />

## What it means

Rall's summing-up was that there is a certain beauty to it and that it is also very strange, and the conversation moved on without either man drawing a policy conclusion.<Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="52:24" /><Cite s="2025-12-10-deprogram-ted-rall-trump-to-europe-drop-dead" t="52:54" />

The rule matters in Kiriakou's commentary because it links two things he treats as connected: the American habit of recruiting local allies for a war, and the improvised, unplanned way in which those allies are absorbed into the country afterwards — the same failure he describes in far harsher terms in the case of [the Afghan Zero Units](/wiki/the-afghan-zero-units).
