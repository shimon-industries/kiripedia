**The Brennan felony referral** is [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s July 2025 account of where the criminal exposure of [John Brennan](/wiki/john-brennan) and [James Comey](/wiki/james-comey) then stood.

## Obama is out of reach

The discussion began with [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump)'s suggestion that Barack Obama could be arrested over the Russia election affair. Kiriakou dismissed it on Trump's own precedent: *"thanks to Donald Trump going to the Supreme Court, a president can't be arrested for anything."* All Obama would have to argue *"is that he did it in the exercise of his functions as president of the United States."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:22:04" />

## Brennan is not

*"Now, John Brennan is another issue,"* he said. What the Justice Department had been referred were misdemeanour charges against Brennan and Comey *"related to allegedly lying to Congress."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:22:36" />

Kiriakou had, immediately before going on air, interviewed Matt Taibbe for his other podcast, Deep Focus. Taibbe confirmed the misdemeanour referral and added to it: what he was hearing was that prosecutors *"are actively pursuing felony conspiracy charges against at least Brennan and maybe Brennan and Comey."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:22:36" /><Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:23:08" />

## What he would do about it

Kiriakou did not affect neutrality about the prospect. He had mentioned it to the host offline already: *"I will go to Brennan's trial every single day. I mean it."* Told he would be there with a grin on his face, he answered: *"Karma is real."*<Cite s="2025-07-25-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="1:23:08" />
