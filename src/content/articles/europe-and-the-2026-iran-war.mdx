**Europe and the 2026 Iran war** describes the collapse of allied confidence that the economist [Richard D. Wolff](/wiki/richard-wolff) reported to [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) in April 2026, a month or so into the fighting.

## Not told, then asked for help

The European governments, in Wolff's account, learned of the attack when everyone else did: *"The Europeans had not been told by the United States about the attack. They had not been warned. They had not had time to prepare."* They had assumed the United States would coordinate the war with them, and Mr Trump did not.<Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="10:29" /><Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="11:01" />

The sequence that followed is the one Wolff treats as decisive. Halfway through the war [Donald Trump](/wiki/donald-trump) asked the Europeans for help and *"they basically said no."* The single exception he identified was British: *"The British let them use the base in Cyprus, but mostly did not"* support the effort. Trump then attacked the Europeans publicly, *"furious that they weren't helping him, utterly oblivious to the reason why, which they told him."*<Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="11:33" />

## "NATO is dead"

Wolff's summary of European feeling was unhedged: *"the anger, the bitterness of the Europeans against the United States cannot be over stressed."* His verdict on the alliance followed directly, *"NATO is dead, the alliance is dead, and the blame for it is 100% on the US. That's the view in Europe."* He set it alongside the tariff dispute of the previous year as the second time Trump had declined to sit down with them.<Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="12:05" />

What made the failure to warn more than a diplomatic slight, in his telling, was range. The Iranian missiles that damaged American bases in the Gulf and penetrated Israeli air defences could travel hundreds of kilometres, and *"those missiles could go to Europe. They could hit European cities."* To be exposed to a war that way without having been consulted was, he said, *"driving them crazy with anger and bitterness at being treated in this way. And America are going to have to deal with that for years to come."*<Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="12:35" /><Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="13:08" />

## The domestic cost to European leaders

Wolff attached a political consequence to it. The leaders of Britain, France and Germany were polling *"between 10 and 20%,"* were *"not going to be in power much longer,"* and what was driving them out was *"a popular opinion that blames them for being lackeys of Mr. Trump."*<Cite s="2026-04-17-the-deep-focus-show-europe-is-running-out-of-fuel-w-richard-wolff" t="13:40" />

The immediate material consequence for Europe was fuel: see [Europe's 2026 jet fuel crisis](/wiki/europe-jet-fuel-crisis-2026).
