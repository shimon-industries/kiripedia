**The State Department cover investigation** was one of several inquiries [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) opened while serving as senior investigator on the [Senate Foreign Relations Committee](/wiki/senate-foreign-relations-committee), and one of several that were closed on him.

## The tip

*"I got a call from a reporter who told me that he had some information that seemed to indicate that the CIA was taking its torturers and putting them under State Department cover"*, which, Kiriakou notes, *"was a violation of the cover agreement between the CIA and the State Department."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="20:40" /><Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="21:11" />

## The letter

He did what the job allowed: *"I sent the State Department a letter asking for clarification."* Then nothing, for *"something like five, six weeks."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="21:11" />

## The answer

*"Finally a colleague of mine comes into my office and says, oh, you got a response to your letter from the agency. I said, I didn't get any response. He said, well, they classified it top secret and you're not cleared for it."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="21:42" />

Asked what the response said, the colleague smiled and gave the substance: *"they said go f--- yourself. So that was the end of that investigation."*<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="21:42" />

## In sequence

Kiriakou places this inquiry immediately after [the Dasht-i-Leili massacre](/wiki/dasht-i-leili-massacre) investigation, which the committee's own deputy staff director had ordered him to drop within days of his writing to the agency.<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="18:14" /><Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="20:40" /> He treats the pair as part of a pattern that ran for another three years, *"there were other investigations too, at which I was poking them"*, before the charges came.<Cite s="2023-04-25-real-news-reality-asserts-itself-why-i-was-targeted-rai-pt-9-probe-dup" t="22:13" />
