**Qasem Soleimani** was the Iranian general killed by an American drone strike at an airstrip in Baghdad in January 2020. [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) discussed the killing less than twenty-four hours after it happened.

## What was destroyed

His assessment of the target's standing was high: *"Donald Trump killing arguably the second most important person in Iran, a potential successor to the supreme leader."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="31:24" />

He relayed an exchange he had seen reported, in which an Iranian journalist asked officials whether the strike was equivalent to killing Petraeus or Mattis. *"The response was: no, this is far worse than killing Petraeus or Mattis. This is like killing Pence."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="31:54" />

His own forecast was short: *"there's going to be retaliation … everybody's talking about de-escalation. De-escalation is a pipe dream."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="31:24" /><Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="31:54" />

## The programme that produced it

Kiriakou treats the strike as the predictable end point of a policy rather than a departure. *"It's not legal to send drones to other countries and to just rocket people without due process, without the right to confront their accusers in a court of law before a jury of their peers. Whether we like their politics or not is irrelevant."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="31:24" />

He assigns responsibility across administrations: *"Barack Obama got away with it for so long, the inevitable continuation was going to be Donald Trump getting away with it."* And he credits the drone whistleblowers — naming [Daniel Hale](/wiki/daniel-hale), rendered "David Hale" in the auto-captions — with having warned that exactly this would happen.<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="30:53" /><Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="31:24" />

## The official explanation

Kiriakou singled out [Mike Pompeo](/wiki/mike-pompeo)'s appearance on *Fox & Friends* that morning, in which the Secretary of State said the strike had been made in order to help de-escalation and had saved untold numbers of Muslim lives. *"We're in Orwellian craziness right now."* What struck him as much as the claim was the reception: *"all the nattering nabobs on Fox just nodded their heads over and over, like that made total sense."*<Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="32:25" /><Cite s="2020-01-03-kevin-gosztola-kevin-gosztola" t="32:56" />
