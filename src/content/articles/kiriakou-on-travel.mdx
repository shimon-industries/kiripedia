Asked which country is the most beautiful, has the best food, is cheapest and is easiest to navigate, [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) calls it *"a wonderful question"* and answers each in turn.<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="34:29" />

## Greece

He declares his bias immediately: *"my favourite country is Greece. I go to Greece two, three times a year. I was there 2 weeks ago. I'm going again in 2 weeks. I'm going again in November. I was there in March, so four times this year."* Greece also takes the food category outright — *"hands down, no comparison. I eat like a pig when I'm there, and invariably, I always always lose weight."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="35:00" /><Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="35:30" />

The award is not unqualified. Speaking from [Cyprus](/wiki/kiriakou-cypriot-ancestry) earlier the same year he gave the island's cheeses the advantage over the Greek ones, and reported having been talked out of frappé by a cousin in Rhodes on the ground that it is made from instant coffee — both set out at [Kiriakou on Greek and Cypriot food](/wiki/kiriakou-on-greek-and-cypriot-food).<Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="24:09" /><Cite s="2026-06-01-giorgos-omirou-clickbait-title-personal-channel-probe" t="27:50" />

## Oman and Syria

For beauty he splits the question in two. Natural beauty goes to [Oman](/wiki/oman), on the south-eastern tip of the Arabian Peninsula: *"You've never seen landscape like this before. Thousand-foot cliffs going right to the ocean. Incredible."* He notes the local practice of camping at the top of the escarpments, where *"The view is unlike anything you've ever seen before in your life."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="35:30" /><Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="36:01" />

For the beauty of a civilisation and its culture, *"it's got to be Syria. You go to Damascus, and you're looking at stuff that's mentioned in the Bible, like by name. Damascus Gate, and there are cars driving through it."* The example that struck him hardest is domestic: *"there's this little house over here. That's the house where Saint Ananias lowered Saint Paul in a basket so he could escape from the Romans. It's just an incredible thing to see."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="36:01" /><Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="36:33" />

## Chile

The cheapest country produces the longest story. On honeymoon at a Chilean vineyard, going through a tasting, Kiriakou asked for fun what the most expensive wine in the house was and was told it was the director's special private reserve — expensive, and locked up. *"I said, 'Ah, we're on our honeymoon. Let's break open a bottle.'"* The man returned with a ring of keys, opened the room, opened the bottle and poured. *"we drink it and it's magnificent. And so I said to him, 'How much is it?' And he takes a calculator and he goes He goes, '$8.50.'"*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="37:03" /><Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="38:04" />

Kiriakou's reply was *"You guys have a really nice country here. You need to raise your prices."* He bought a case of the wine — Concha y Toro — and shipped it home, where *"The shipping cost more than the wine did."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="38:37" />

## Still going

He closes by noting he is not finished: he had visited Norway and Sweden for the first time two weeks earlier, is baffled to have missed Sweden for so long, and is going to Vietnam the following week — *"I'll do the show from Vietnam. So I'm still out there exploring the world."*<Cite s="2026-08-01-john-kiriakou-are-you-a-cia-plant-john-kiriakou-answers-mos" t="39:08" />

## What European airlines owe you

Kiriakou treats [an overnight in Dublin](/wiki/the-dublin-compensation) as the benchmark against which American practice should be measured. A mechanical fault in Madrid cost him his Washington connection; the airline had already booked him into a four-star hotel with a buffet *"that could feed an army"*, laid on a shuttle and expedited check-in, and refunded $600 of a $1,200 ticket — money that reached his checking account about six weeks later.<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="25:32" /><Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:04" /><Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:36" />

His question about it was short: *"How come we don't do that?"*<Cite s="2025-09-06-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra-deprogram-show-with-ted-ra" t="26:36" />
