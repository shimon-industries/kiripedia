**Mark Lanzotti** was [John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou)'s closest friend in prison and remains one afterward, seated on the Italian table within the [racially segregated](/wiki/prison-racial-segregation) prison cafeteria Kiriakou describes elsewhere. Lanzotti became his best friend after reading in the *New York Times* on a Sunday that Kiriakou was reporting to the prison the following Thursday; he took it upon himself to go to every one of the Italian inmates, men affiliated with the Gambino and Lucchese families, to vouch for Kiriakou, explaining, *"There's a CIA guy coming here on Thursday,"* not FBI, and that *"the CIA protected us from the Muslims."*<Cite s="2026-05-20-the-devory-darkins-intervi" t="28:05" /> An Italian who, Kiriakou says, went to every made man at FCI Loretto to explain the difference between a CIA officer and an FBI agent, Lanzotti had drawn triple life without parole for a first-time, nonviolent meth conspiracy, one his stepfather ran, and which Lanzotti was the *only* participant to voluntarily leave. When the others were caught, they informed on each other and on him; they got five and a quarter years, Lanzotti got triple life.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="33:50" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="37:29" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="38:32" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="39:04" /> Suffering bone cancer, he had surgery in a converted motor home in the prison parking lot because he was deemed too dangerous to move.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="39:34" /> Kiriakou wrote his clemency appeal; Lanzotti's sentence was commuted, and after Kiriakou's release Lanzotti's mother called, weeping, to say he had won.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="40:38" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="41:09" /> He later got a real-estate license after writing to Governor Chris Christie, and became the top-selling realtor on the Jersey Shore.<Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="43:11" /><Cite s="2025-08-04-tegan-broadwater-from-the-inside-part-2" t="43:44" />
