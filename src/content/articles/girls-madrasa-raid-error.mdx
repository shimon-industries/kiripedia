The **girls' madrasa raid error** was a mistaken raid carried out by the CIA on the night of the [Abu Zubaydah](/wiki/abu-zubaydah) operation, targeting the home of an elderly Pakistani man running an unlicensed school for girls out of his house. Most families in the area did not want their daughters educated; this man's household happened to hold the only telephone in the neighborhood, and al-Qaeda fighters had been using it to place the calls the CIA was intercepting.<Cite s="2026-03-09-dead-drop-s1e18-cross-currents" t="13:50" />

[John Kiriakou](/wiki/john-kiriakou) says he personally apologized to the old man on behalf of the President of the United States after the mistake became apparent, and arranged for the CIA to pay roughly $500 to buy the family a new door and shoes.<Cite s="2026-03-09-dead-drop-s1e18-cross-currents" t="14:56" />
