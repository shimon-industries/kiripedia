Prompted by the [Bernie Moreno](/wiki/bernie-moreno) affair, Kiriakou puts a general question: *"it makes one wonder too how many politicians, senior politicians, national politicians, do you think are closeted in some way and are being blackmailed for it?"*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="30:09" /> The interest is professional as much as political, the leverage a concealed private life gives is the same mechanism the CIA relies on in [recruitment](/wiki/blackmail-in-recruitment).

## The roll-call

His examples come from his own college years in the 1980s, when two Democratic congressmen from Massachusetts were outed at the same time.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="30:09" /> Barney Frank, who Kiriakou notes had recently died, was having an affair with a man who turned out to be a prostitute; Frank apparently did not know, and let him move into his house on Capitol Hill, where the man *"was turning tricks in the congressman's house"* while Frank was at work. Frank was forced to acknowledge that he was gay, and in Kiriakou's summary, *"nobody cared."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="30:39" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="31:10" />

The comparison he draws is with Gerry Studds, who *"was having sex with underage male pages, 16 year olds"* and who, *"believe it or not"*, was re-elected and never charged with a crime.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="31:10" /> A Mississippi Republican, whom he names as *"John Henson"*, was caught in a House bathroom with his male chief of staff and did not survive the scandal.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="30:39" /><Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="31:42" /> His last case is the Idaho senator arrested in a Minneapolis airport men's room for a foot-tapping signal, whose defence was that he had *"a wide stance"* and was tapping his toes at random rather than soliciting the man in the next stall; the senator's wife stayed with him.<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="31:42" /> The auto-captions render the Mississippi congressman as *"John Henson"* and the Idaho senator as *"Mccclure"*; both are recorded here as spoken.

Kiriakou's summary of the pattern is that it is not exceptional: *"this kind of thing happens all the time."*<Cite s="2026-08-04-john-kiriakou-who-is-really-behind-the-migrant-crisis-in-ce" t="31:42" />
