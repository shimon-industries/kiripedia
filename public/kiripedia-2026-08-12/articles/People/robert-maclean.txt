Robert MacLean
==============

Robert MacLean is a TSA whistleblower. Per John Kiriakou he is "an awesome guy ... a serious, serious whistleblower."

Role in the Giuliani exposé

On the evening of the 2018 Rudy Giuliani pardon-extortion meeting, Kiriakou attended a Republican National Committee book launch in Washington at which MacLean was also present. Kiriakou described the meeting to MacLean. MacLean immediately called the FBI to report the attempted bribery; the FBI declined interest. MacLean then called the New York Times, which dispatched reporter Mike Schmidt to Kiriakou. The story ran on the Sunday front page.
