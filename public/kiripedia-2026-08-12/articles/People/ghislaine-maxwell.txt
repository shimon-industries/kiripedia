Ghislaine Maxwell
=================

Ghislaine Maxwell is a British socialite and convicted sex offender who served as the principal associate of Jeffrey Epstein. John Kiriakou's assessment of the Epstein operation centers on her family background: "Ghislaine Maxwell's father was a prolific Mossad spy. That's a historic fact."

The Mossad connection

Kiriakou holds that Maxwell, or her father, Robert Maxwell, introduced Epstein to MOSSAD: "It stands to reason that she would have introduced him to the Mossad or even her father would have introduced Epstein to Mossad." He has separately described her father as "a very well-known Mossad asset," and elsewhere put the case even more directly, saying he believes "the evidence is pretty solid that he was a spy for Mossad." Kiriakou adds that it is not unusual for parents in Robert Maxwell's position to offer up their own children once those children gain access to valuable information: "It's not at all unusual for parents to offer up their children."

In Kiriakou's framing, the entire Jeffrey Epstein operation bears the hallmarks of a MOSSAD access operation, using Epstein to cultivate, and where necessary compromise, high-value figures such as Bill Clinton, Prince Andrew, and Bill Gates, and the Maxwell family connection is the structural explanation for how it was assembled: "They kept it all in the family." Kiriakou has floated the specific mechanism more than once: he speculates that Robert Maxwell, as a Mossad agent, may have told the service his daughter was friendly with Epstein and suggested they "take a look at him", the seed of Epstein's eventual recruitment as an access agent.

The 2021 trial

Kiriakou has noted that Maxwell's federal trial was unusual in one specific respect: it was not videotaped, owing to a federal court rule against cameras in the courtroom.

Post-conviction custody

Kiriakou says an internal Bureau of Prisons regulation bars anyone convicted of a child sex crime from serving a sentence at a minimum-security work camp, yet Maxwell was transferred to one on Donald Trump's orders after she agreed to meet with investigators, a step he says the administration did not have to take. He notes work camps have no bars on the windows and unlocked doors, with inmates free to come and go on their honor; most work in town as janitors or gardeners, which Kiriakou says also gives them access to children, "the whole point," in his framing, of the ordinary ban on housing such offenders there.

He has since put the anomaly at its sharpest, and explained the placement rule it breaks. Convicted child-sex offenders are barred from minimum-security camps, and are also kept out of penitentiaries and medium-security prisons for their own safety, "they'll be killed there. Everybody beats on pedophiles in prison", which is why they are concentrated in low-security facilities, with the concertina wire and controlled movements still in place. On that reading Maxwell is a category of one: "She's the only pedophile in the entire federal prison system who's in a minimum security work camp. And she was transferred there by the president. Why? Why would he do something like that?"

The designation anomaly

John Kiriakou treats Maxwell's placement as a straightforward violation of Bureau of Prisons practice, and explains the practice in order to show it. Sex offenders are not permitted in minimum security, because minimum-security camps have no fences, no bars on the windows and unlocked doors, inmates are on their honour not to abscond, which he notes they do all the time anyway.

The usual placement for paedophiles is low security, and Kiriakou says the logic is sound: unless they killed the children they molested, they go to lows because in a maximum they would not last fifteen minutes, in a medium they would probably be killed or at least regularly beaten, and in a minimum they would be an escape risk. Maxwell was moved not to a low but to a camp, a transfer he calls "absolutely unheard of" and "yet another sweetheart deal that just defies any kind of logic."

What he finds most perplexing is not the transfer itself but the silence around it: neither the president nor anyone around him nor the Justice Department has bothered to explain to the public why this prisoner is receiving special treatment.

The comparison he draws is with his own case, in which a judge granted him a camp with the prosecution's consent and the Bureau put him in the prison across the street regardless.
