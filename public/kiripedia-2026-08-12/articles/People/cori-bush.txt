Cori Bush
=========

Cori Bush is a former U.S. Representative from Missouri's 1st congressional district (St. Louis). She is cited by John Kiriakou, alongside Jamaal Bowman, as one of the two named cases of AIPAC successfully primarying an incumbent House member after the member publicly characterized Israel's prosecution of the Gaza war as a "wholesale massacre of Palestinian civilians and women and children."

> Cori Bush, for example, in St. Louis, they threw her out on her ass. ... Why? Because they said, you know, maybe the Israelis shouldn't be massacring all these women and children.
