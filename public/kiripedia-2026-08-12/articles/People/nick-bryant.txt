Nick Bryant
===========

Nick Bryant is an investigative journalist and activist, an officer and co-founder of Epstein Justice, and the author of The Franklin Scandal: A Story of Powerbrokers, Child Abuse and Betrayal.

John Kiriakou, who has hosted him more than once, introduces him as "someone who probably knows this issue better than anybody else in America", the issue being the Jeffrey Epstein files and the question of whether any such file exists. Kiriakou has said he would look forward to a further conversation on Bryant's research into Epstein's role in geopolitics.
