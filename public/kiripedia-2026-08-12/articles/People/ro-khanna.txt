Ro Khanna
=========

Ro Khanna is a Democratic member of the United States House of Representatives from California. Kiriakou states a personal liking, "I like Ro Khanna very much", before grading him A in the case-officer rankings.

The reasoning turns on the two things he looks for in an operations officer. The first is motive: "I think Ro Khanna is one of these very few selfless people. Ro Khanna cares about the greater good." The second is the quality he treats throughout the exercise as the operational one, "he has the kind of personality where people want to be around him. And I think that that would make him a successful case officer." He places Khanna alongside Matt Gaetz, a former Republican congressman he assesses on the same personability grounds despite the distance in politics.
