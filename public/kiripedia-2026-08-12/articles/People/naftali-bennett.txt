Naftali Bennett
===============

Naftali Bennett is a former prime minister of Israel whom John Kiriakou cites for the proposition that the 2026 Iran war was not intended to be the last one.

Discussing which states obstruct the Greater Israel project, Kiriakou named two: "Iran and Turkey, which is why Naftali Bennett, the former Israeli prime minister, is also now saying after Iran, it's Turkey. Turkey's next."

Kiriakou's own forecast is that neither objective is achievable, "I don't see them succeeding. There are too many Arabs. There are too many Muslims. There are too many people who oppose this naked Israeli aggression."
