Mack McLarty
============

Mack McLarty is a U.S. political consultant who served as White House Chief of Staff to President Bill Clinton. He is the founder of McLarty Associates, formerly Kissinger McLarty, and is identified by John Kiriakou as one of the figures in Washington who provided him with material support in the period after his departure from the Central Intelligence Agency and before his Senate Foreign Relations Committee position.

In 2008 McLarty "very generously offered me an office and a part-time secretary in McLarty Associates." Kiriakou: "Back then it was Kissinger McLarty. I was very, very indebted to him. He was very kind to me." It was through the McLarty Associates office that Kiriakou first encountered Bill Richardson, then on McLarty's board of directors.
