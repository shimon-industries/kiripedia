Jamaal Bowman
=============

Jamaal Bowman is a former U.S. Representative from New York. He is cited by John Kiriakou alongside Cori Bush as the second documented case of AIPAC successfully primarying an incumbent House member following the member's House floor remarks characterizing Israeli conduct in the Gaza war as the "wholesale massacre of Palestinian civilians and women and children."

> Jamaal Bowman in New York, out. Why? Because they said, you know, maybe the Israelis shouldn't be massacring all these women and children. Oh, how dare you. You must be anti-Semitic and they throw them out.
