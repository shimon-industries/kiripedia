Jim Jordan
==========

Jim Jordan is a Republican member of the United States House of Representatives from Ohio, whom Kiriakou calls powerful and identifies as the chairman of a major committee while declining to be certain which: "the Judiciary Committee or the Homeland Security Committee in the House. Something like one of the very big committees."

His view is explicitly divided, "I sort of have a love-hate thing going with Jim Jordan", and the first criticism is one of effectiveness: "He talks a tough game. But he doesn't really get a whole lot of things done."

What decides the grade is older. Before Congress, Jordan was the wrestling coach at Ohio State University, and "wrestlers repeatedly went to him and said, 'Coach, we're being sexually abused by the team doctor.' And he did nothing to help them. Nothing." Kiriakou converts that into the operational judgement the segment calls for: as a case officer "he probably couldn't be trusted to live within the confines of the rules," and the grade is a D.
