Brett McGurk
============

Brett McGurk is a senior U.S. diplomat. John Kiriakou cites reporting that McGurk was caught on top of a U.S. embassy, "the whole Middle East knows," he says, while McGurk was in charge of the war against ISIS. Kiriakou offers the episode as a further example of the CIA and diplomatic adultery culture he considers a genuine counterintelligence vulnerability, calling it "quite a vulnerability" and "a problem that needs to be fixed."
