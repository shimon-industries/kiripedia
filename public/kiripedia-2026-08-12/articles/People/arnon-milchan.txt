Arnon Milchan
=============

Arnon Milchan is the Israeli-American producer behind films such as Pretty Woman, Fight Club and 12 Years a Slave, whom John Kiriakou describes as an Israeli super-spy and arms dealer working for LAKAM. Kiriakou says the FBI had known for two decades that Milchan was an Israeli spy; after an operation was blown and a Hebrew-language interview cost him his rare ten-year U.S. visa, Milchan went to Benjamin Netanyahu, who lobbied Secretary of State John Kerry three times to restore it, the basis of one of Netanyahu's corruption charges. Kiriakou adds that FBI documents obtained under the Freedom of Information Act place both Milchan and Netanyahu personally in the smuggling of U.S. nuclear secrets.
