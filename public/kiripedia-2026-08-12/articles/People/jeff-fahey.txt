Jeff Fahey
==========

Jeff Fahey is an American actor and the star of the Tyrell Vanto-written and -directed film in which John Kiriakou made his acting debut, filmed in Mexico during October 2025.

Kiriakou describes Fahey as "a really great guy, very generous with his time and his advice" and credits him with off-camera coaching that allowed Kiriakou, a first-time actor, to remain on-screen credibly: "He would stand just off camera and whisper directions to me and it made me, I guess, believable enough that I didn't make an ass of myself in this movie."
