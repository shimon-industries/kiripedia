Caspar Weinberger
=================

Caspar Weinberger served as U.S. Secretary of Defense under President Reagan. John Kiriakou cites Weinberger's assessment of the Jonathan Pollard espionage case: that the damage was "grave", which Kiriakou notes is the highest level of damage in the classification scheme. Kiriakou frames this as the reason Pollard served every single day of his thirty-year sentence with no time off for good behavior.
