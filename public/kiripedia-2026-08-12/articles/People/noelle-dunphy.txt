Noelle Dunphy
=============

Noelle Dunphy was an aide to Rudy Giuliani at the time of his 2018 $2 million pardon-extortion attempt against John Kiriakou. Per Kiriakou, on the evening of the meeting, back in New York, Giuliani told Dunphy that he had "tried to get $2 million out of this guy for a pardon today." Dunphy retained the information until Giuliani's subsequent sexual harassment of her, at which point she contacted the New York Times and corroborated Kiriakou's account in full.

'That fucking Kiriakou ratted me out'

John Kiriakou says he was "heartened" to learn from Noelle Dunphy, former head of business development for Giuliani Partners, that when the New York Times exposed the pardon shakedown, Rudy Giuliani fumed, "That fucking Kiriakou ratted me out." In the same conversation, Kiriakou recounted the Taliban embassy raid in Peshawar.
