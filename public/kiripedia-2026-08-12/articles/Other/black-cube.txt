Black Cube
==========

Black Cube is an Israeli private intelligence firm staffed predominantly by former MOSSAD personnel. The firm was reportedly engaged by attorney David Boies in connection with the Jeffrey Epstein litigation. John Kiriakou cites the firm as representative of the no-compunction operating posture he attributes to Israeli intelligence broadly: "The Israelis have no compunction at all about violating any law that gets in their way. And then they say, 'What are you going to do about it?'"
