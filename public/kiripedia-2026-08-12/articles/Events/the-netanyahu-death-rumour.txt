The Netanyahu death rumour
==========================

The Netanyahu death rumour was a claim circulated during the 2026 Iran war that Benjamin Netanyahu had been killed and his death concealed. John Kiriakou recounted it as an object lesson in how quickly credibility could be spent during the war.

The case, made by "a couple of podcasters who until yesterday were highly respected," ran that Netanyahu had not been seen; that his son had gone quiet on Twitter for exactly seven days, which was read as sitting shiva; and that the son's return to posting marked the end of the mourning period. Kiriakou's account of the refutation is one word long: Netanyahu gave a press conference. "Oops."

His own explanation for the absence was mundane, "There are plenty of bunkers in Israel. They're all over Israel. Everybody has a bunker." A fortnight later he was tracking reports placing Netanyahu abroad instead; see Netanyahu in hiding.

The real decapitation campaign

The rumour sat alongside a genuine sequence of killings. The United States announced that it had killed the IRGC's spokesman, following the head of the IRGC and the minister in charge of it.

Kiriakou's forecast for the war as a whole, given in the same passage, was that "it's worse than it was yesterday. This is not going to end quickly. It's not going to end easily or nicely. It's going to be hideous." The theory behind the killings is treated at the house of cards theory.
