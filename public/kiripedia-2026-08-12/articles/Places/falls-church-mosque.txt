Falls Church mosque
===================

The Falls Church mosque is a large mosque located on Route 7 in Falls Church, Virginia. In 1993, the imam was Anwar al-Awlaki. John Kiriakou visited the mosque that year as part of a CIA Arabic-language-training field trip, a day-long itinerary of Arab-American businesses in Falls Church under a no-English-permitted rule, that ended at the mosque for a tour and Q&A with the imam.

Eight years later, the September 11 hijackers prayed at the same mosque the night before the attacks.
