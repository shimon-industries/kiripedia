Fort Gordon
===========

Fort Gordon is a U.S. Army installation in Georgia. NSA whistleblower Reality Winner, discussing her case on John Kiriakou's show, says she was working at Fort Gordon in what she considered a temporary position, taken to renew a security clearance that was set to expire after she left the U.S. Air Force.

Winner says she took the next available linguist position without knowing whether it would be at Fort Meade or Fort Gordon, because recruiters for a position in Bagram, Afghanistan would not hire her without an active clearance. She describes working at Fort Gordon "in the giant, shiny NSA building that was brand new at the time" as an NSA government contractor. In the same conversation, Winner describes the printer watermark that ultimately let the NSA trace her leaked document back to a specific office.
