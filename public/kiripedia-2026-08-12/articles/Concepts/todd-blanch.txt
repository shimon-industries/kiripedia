Todd Blanch
===========

Megyn Kelly, reading a Trump statement live on air during her interview with John Kiriakou, reported that Todd Blanch is the incoming acting Attorney General, replacing Pam Bondi, and is a respected legal mind and well-liked within the Department of Justice.  Kelly, in her opening monologue on the same episode, had reported that EPA administrator Lee Zeldin had also reportedly been considered as a possible replacement for Bondi.
