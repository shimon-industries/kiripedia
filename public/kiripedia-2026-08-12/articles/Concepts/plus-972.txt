Plus 972
========

+972 is an Israeli internet platform that, at a 2026 Berlin panel that included John Kiriakou, was said to have published "quite a few really interesting" whistleblowers from the Israel Defense Forces, a remark made by the panel's moderator, not by Kiriakou.

The moderator raised +972's coverage in the context of a broader discussion about the introduction of AI into modern warfare, suggesting further revelations along those lines are likely to surface.
