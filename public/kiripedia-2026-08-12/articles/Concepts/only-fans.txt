OnlyFans
========

Per John Kiriakou, the owner of OnlyFans, who has since died, was Ukrainian-American rather than Israeli, but was, in Kiriakou's telling, a big supporter of and donor to Israel.

Kiriakou raised OnlyFans' ownership in the context of a broader claim that Israelis have "been the kings of the porn industry for decades," pointing to Brooklyn and Hawthorne, California as the industry's two main hubs and calling it "almost exclusively Israeli."
