Veterans' benefits for IDF service
==================================

Veterans' benefits for IDF service is a proposal John Kiriakou raises as the sharpest available illustration of what he considers divided loyalty in Congress.

"Just in the last 48 hours a Republican congressman from Pennsylvania and a Republican congressman from Ohio sponsored a bill in the House of Representatives to give US veteran veterans benefits to any American who joins the IDF." His response is immediate: "That is a [expletive] outrage. As an American, I consider that to be treason. If you If you're If you're fighting on behalf of a foreign military you shouldn't be an American citizen. Period."

He sets it among related conduct: "We have members of Congress like this this fine of Florida who show up on the floor of the House of Representatives wearing an IDF uniform. We have members of the US Senate including the chairman of the Senate Intelligence Committee, Tom Cotton, who have Israeli flags outside their offices on Capitol Hill. What is that?"

The underlying position is the one he opens and closes the interview with: "Either we're Americans fighting on behalf of America and American ideals or we're not. We're Israeli puppets and stooges and I'm unwilling to be an Israeli stooge."
