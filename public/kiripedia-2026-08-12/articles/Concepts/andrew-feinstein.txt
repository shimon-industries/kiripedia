Andrew Feinstein
================

Andrew Feinstein is mentioned at a 2026 Berlin conference panel that included John Kiriakou. A fellow panelist recalled that Feinstein had recently spoken there about the multiple factors that brought down apartheid in South Africa, citing popular uprising and academic divestment from military-industrial-complex ties among them, while posing a question to Kiriakou about resistance strategies beyond leaks and data. The recollection is the questioner's, not Kiriakou's own statement.

No source in the corpus captures Kiriakou himself discussing Andrew Feinstein.
