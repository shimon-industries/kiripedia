Eric Swallwell
==============

John Kiriakou names Eric Swalwell only once in the corpus, in a passing aside contrasting the partisan Congress of today with the 1975 Church and Pike Committees: he says the only participants from the other party on a House "weaponization" committee were Swalwell and Cheney.  In the same conversation, Kiriakou notes that a proposed agreement for 9-month work visas for Mexican workers was never implemented because of the September 11 attacks.
