Chaos Computer Club Wikileaks
=============================

The Chaos Computer Club, a group of hackers from Berlin, played an important role in the history of WikiLeaks. They were involved in the early stages of the organization.  On the same 2026 Berlin panel that included John Kiriakou, it was through the Chaos Computer Club that a journalist recounted meeting early WikiLeaks associate Daniel Domscheit-Berg, then introduced under the pseudonym "Daniel Schmitt."
